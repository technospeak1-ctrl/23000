## 一、编排逻辑

本主题是"机械"，名词密集、术语（部件名与工具名）成堆，最容易写成一张"机器零件清单"。我用"年轻工程师本·卡特在老机修工萨姆带领下，亲历一台新发动机从装配到运转再到排障的一天"为主线，按"车间清晨 → 工作台上的零件 → 装配 → 运转 → 小故障"五幕推进，让每个部件、工具与动作都落到具体的装置和操作上。

| 幕 | 场景 | 承载语义场 | 叙事功能 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The workshop at dawn 车间清晨 | 场地身份 + 安装陈设词 | 建立人物与场景 |
| 2 | The parts on the bench 工作台上的零件 | 零部件 + 工具 + 材质词 | 铺陈零件谱系 |
| 3 | Assembly on the floor 装配现场 | 装配/吊装/加工动词 + 搭配 | 用动作串起组装 |
| 4 | The machine wakes up 机器运转 | 动力/效率/产出词 + 状态词 | 展现运作因果 |
| 5 | The small malfunction 小故障 | 故障/维修词 + 收束搭配 | 排障点题、回收词汇 |

**设计要点**：`constituent / component / combination / mechanism` 组成"零件—构成—组合—机构"的认知链；`piston / gear / pulley / ratchet / screw / wedge / hook / reel / dial / gauge / filter / fuse` 分别贴到具体部件上；`chisel / hatchet / punch / vice` 收进工具架；`serrated` 写螺钉的锯齿头、`particular` 写老技工的较真。8 条搭配全部嵌入动作——`detach…from`（拆旧件）、`be comprised of`（由百余部件组成）、`combine with`（部件两两配合）、`insert…into`（把销子插入孔）、`crane hoist`（起重机吊起）、`generate electricity`（发电）、`harness the power of`（利用风力）、`vary from`（读数逐小时变化），使搭配成为"他做了什么"的一部分，而非孤立罗列。

## 二、英文故事　The Machine in the Workshop

### 1. The workshop at dawn

Ben Carter arrived at the **industrial** **mill** before sunrise, carrying a worn **kit** of tools and a notebook. The town had grown around the factory, and the company had helped **industrialize** the whole valley, where it began to **manufacture** engines. Inside, the **workshop** was already awake: an old **apparatus** hissed in one corner, and a strange new **device** stood under a sheet in another. Sam, the veteran **mechanic** who had kept the **machinery** running for thirty years, greeted him. Sam was **particular** about order and had a **mechanical** mind that noticed every loose bolt. The **installation** of a new production line was nearly complete, and everywhere Ben looked he saw the **outfit** of a busy shop — a **tackle** hanging from the beam, spare **accessory** parts, and a heavy iron **fixture** bolted to the floor. "Everything here earns its place," Sam said.

### 2. The parts on the bench

On the bench lay the pieces of the new **engine**. A single **component**, Sam explained, was only one **constituent** of the whole; the **combination** of many such pieces formed a working **mechanism**. He showed Ben the **piston**, the toothed **gear**, the **pulley**, the **ratchet**, and a long **screw** with a **serrated** head. A **wedge** and a **hook** lay beside a **reel** of wire, while a round **dial** and a glass **gauge** sat near a **filter** and a spare **fuse**. In the tool rack hung a **chisel**, a short **hatchet**, a **punch**, and a **vice**. "Good **stuff** makes good work," Sam said, running his hand over a block of raw steel.

### 3. Assembly on the floor

The real work began at nine. First they had to **detach** the old drive **from** the frame: Sam loosened the bolts and pulled the worn part away. The new machine **was comprised of** over a hundred pieces, and each had to **combine with** the next perfectly. Ben learned to **insert** a pin **into** its hole before the gear slid home. A **crane** **hoisted** the heavy engine block into place, and together the two men **assembled** the frame. Sam used a torch to **weld** a crack in the support. By noon the line was ready to **launch**, and they **streamlined** the last steps of the work so nothing would jam.

### 4. The machine wakes up

When Sam turned the switch, the engine woke. It ran on **gas**, and its first job was to **generate electricity** for the whole shop. "We **harness the power of** the wind to **generate electricity**," Sam said, "and this engine does the same with gas." Before the fuel reached it, a small unit **purified** the gas, for its **purity** mattered. Sam **lubricated** every moving part so nothing would grind. Now the line was fully **operational**: the **dial** on the panel flickered and the **gauge** climbed. The **efficiency** figures looked good; within an hour the **output** of finished parts doubled, and Ben calculated that **productivity** had risen sharply. The whole **process** now ran with little human help — this was **automation** at its best. Sam **polished** a brass rail until it shone and warned that damp air would **tarnish** it again. "We **preserve** what we build," he said. Even the readings **varied from** hour to hour, he noted, and a good engineer watched them all.

### 5. The small malfunction

Late in the afternoon a faint knock betrayed a **malfunction** in the **piston** assembly. Sam shut the line down at once and showed Ben how daily **maintenance** prevents such trouble: he cleaned each **filter**, tightened every **screw**, and **maintained** the moving parts with care. The fault was nothing serious. Once it was fixed, the veteran **mechanic** smiled at the young engineer who had worked so hard to **integrate himself into the community** of the mill. "Machines are only as good as the people who **maintain** them," he said. Ben wrote the lesson in his notebook: a shop is more than steel and **machinery** — it is the **combination** of care, skill, and the will to keep improving.

## 四、学习建议

1. **一条装配线记动词**：`detach…from`（拆下）→ `be comprised of`（由……组成）→ `combine with`（配合）→ `insert…into`（插入）→ `crane / hoist`（吊装）→ `assemble`（装配）→ `weld`（焊接）→ `launch`（投产），正好是一台机器的组装顺序。
2. **零件谱系分层**：`component / constituent / combination / mechanism` 是"零件—构成—组合—机构"的抽象层；`piston / gear / pulley / ratchet / screw / wedge / hook / reel / dial / gauge / filter / fuse` 是具体部件层，分两层记忆不易混。
3. **通电后的因果链**：`gas → purify / purity → generate electricity → lubricate → operational → efficiency / output / productivity → automation`，把"动力—净化—发电—润滑—运转—产出"连成一条线。
4. **一词多义对照**：`gear`（齿轮/传动装置）、`dial`（表盘/拨号盘）、`gauge`（仪表/规格）、`stuff`（原料/材料）、`mill`（磨粉机/作坊）都是书中多义词，故事里都给了具体装置作锚点。
