## 一、编排逻辑

本主题是"工作表现"，词项几乎全是评价人物的形容词与副词，天然分成褒贬两端：一端是 `assiduous / conscientious / dedicated / meritorious / surpassingly` 之类的好评词，另一端是 `inept / overdue / platitudinous / sycophant / malinger` 之类的差评词。若按词表顺序铺开，就是一张"褒义词表 + 贬义词表"。我用"公司首次年度考评"作主线，让检查官 Ms. Voss 用一张 chart 逐个评估员工，按"检查官到场 → 模范员工 → 麻烦制造者 → 岗位调整 → 考评结论"五幕推进：正面词全部落到 Ada 身上，负面词全部落到 Ray 身上，两人同场对照，形容词因此变成有血有肉的人物评价。

| 幕 | 场景 | 承载语义场 | 叙事功能 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The inspector arrives 检查官到场 | 考评框架（inaugural / evaluation / chart / authoritative） | 建立场景 |
| 2 | The model worker 模范员工 | 正面表现（conscientious / diligent / capable / meritorious） | 立正面样板 |
| 3 | The trouble-maker 麻烦制造者 | 负面表现（inept / overdue / sycophant / malinger） | 立反面样板 |
| 4 | The reshuffle 岗位调整 | 处置动作（dislodge / nominate / authorize / toil） | 推进转折 |
| 5 | The verdict 考评结论 | 主题升华（achievement / self-knowledge / pursuit） | 点题收束 |

**设计要点**：褒贬同框对照是本篇核心——`conscientious / diligent / capable / assiduous / meritorious` 属于 Ada，`inept / overdue / critical / platitudinous / devotional` 属于 Ray，中间用检查官的 `chart` 与 `evaluation` 串起。常用搭配全部进入真实评价场景：`a conscientious worker / be adequate for / be capable of / be critical of / be liable for / quote authoritative works / an arduous task / be authorized to do sth. / deputize for / endeavor to do sth. / be in a tight squeeze`。原词表被例句吞掉的 `fag / toil` 也分别补进第 4、5 幕，让"苦干"与"装病"形成呼应。

## 二、英文故事　The Annual Review

### 1. The inspector arrives

On the morning of the **inaugural** review, an **inspector** from head office arrived with a **chart** and a thick **memo**. She was **authoritative** but not **critical**; she would quote **authoritative** works only when she had to. The **officer** in charge **fetched** a chair, and an **observer** took notes for the **evaluation**. "This will take an **hour**," she said, and began with the easy **regulation** questions. Nobody expected an **anomaly** that day.

### 2. The model worker

First came Ada, a **conscientious** worker who was **committed** to her **duty** and **diligently** kept the books. She was **assiduous** and **capable** of any task, **adequate** for every deadline, and **versant** in the new system; her **ability** was **sufficiently** proved by the way she **fulfilled** each target. She had a **canny** eye for a **typo**, was **chary** of easy answers, and took a **strenuous** pride in **drudgery** that others skipped. She had once **endeavored** to master the whole **tack** of the department, and her **presentation** that morning was **surpassingly** clear. Her **fealty** to the firm was no **equivocation**; she would **voluntarily** stay late rather than waste an afternoon in **dalliance**.

### 3. The trouble-maker

Then came Ray, the office **trouble-maker**, whose **assertiveness** often turned into **importune** nagging. He was **inept** at his job, **critical** of everyone else, and **overdue** with three reports; his **absenteeism** was a standing **anomaly**, and he was **liable** for the missing files. Keeping those files was an arduous task he had never finished. He tried to **malinger** through the interview, **nitpicked** the **chart**, and **squeezed** every small grievance into the record; by the end he was in a tight **squeeze**. A **sycophant** by nature, he poured **platitudinous** praise on the inspector and called it **dedicated** service; even his **devotional** emails sounded **platitudinous**. When she asked about his **placement**, he gave an **equivocation** instead of an answer.

### 4. The reshuffle

The inspector had to **dislodge** Ray from a comfortable **sinecure** he had held for years. There was no **redundancy** package for such a post, only a **redistribution** of his work among the willing. Ray now had to **toil** over the backlog he had left and even **fag** at the simplest filing. Ada was **nominated** for a new **opportunity**, and a junior was **authorized** to **deputize** for the supervisor and sign the **requisition** for new desks. The **take-up** of the training scheme was high; the **spunk** of the younger staff impressed everyone. The **inept** habits of the old office gave way to a **far-sighted** plan, and the **hour** ended with an honest **evaluation**.

### 5. The verdict

By evening the **evaluation** was done. Ada's **achievement** was recorded in **fractional** detail, and the inspector wrote that her **self-knowledge** made her **meritorious**; her **voluntary** help and **assiduous** care were noted, and the **pursuit** of **enough** was not for her—she always gave **sufficiently** more. Ray, by contrast, had fagged at the edges of his **duty** and **toiled** only when watched. "A **conscientious** worker needs no **observer**," the inspector said, and **seriously** advised him to study the **chart** on the wall. He left with a **memo**, a **sinecure** gone, and the first honest **self-knowledge** of his career. The whole office had worked **strenuously** through an arduous week, and everybody agreed the **inaugural** review had been worth the **drudgery**.

## 四、学习建议

1. **褒贬两栏对照记**：褒义 `assiduous / conscientious / diligent / capable / meritorious / assiduous / surpassingly`；贬义 `inept / overdue / platitudinous / devotional / sycophant / malinger / nitpick`。第 2、3 幕分别把两栏全部安到 Ada 与 Ray 身上，人物对照记忆最牢。
2. **"能力—尽责—结果"三级辨析**：`capable / adequate`（有能力、能胜任）→ `conscientious / diligent / assiduous`（态度尽责勤勉）→ `achievement / meritorious`（结果与嘉奖）。故事第 2 幕与第 5 幕完整呈现了这条链。
3. **介词搭配整体背**：`be capable of`、`be adequate for`、`be critical of`、`be liable for`、`deputize for`、`quote authoritative works`、`be authorized to do sth.`、`endeavor to do sth.`、`in a tight squeeze`、`an arduous task`、`a conscientious worker`。
4. **名词化的抽象词**：`dalliance`（虚度）、`drudgery`（苦工）、`equivocation`（托词）、`fealty`（效忠）、`self-knowledge`（自知之明）都较书面，正文各出现一次，注意它们的评价色彩。
5. **排版遗漏词留痕**：`fag`（苦干）与 `toil`（苦力活）在原词表中被例句吞掉，这里分别落在 Ray"被拆穿后干最琐碎的归档"与"只在被监督时卖力"，与 Ada 的 `voluntarily stay late` 形成鲜明对照。
