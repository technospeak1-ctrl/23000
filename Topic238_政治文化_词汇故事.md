## 一、编排逻辑

本主题是"政治文化"，立场、风气与制度评价词高度密集，92 词极易写成宣言式清单。我用"一位技术官僚在旧君主出逃后参与起草新政治准则、平息派系冲突"作为主线，按"临时政府 → 首都风气 → 派系冲突 → 行为准则 → 清算与和解"五幕推进，把 `radical / pragmatic / neutral / tyrannical` 等抽象立场词人格化到具体人物的言论与行动里，并按语义场拆成多个子场景：

| 幕 | 场景 | 承载语义场 | 叙事功能 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The provisional government 临时政府 | 过渡/政体/仪式名词与搭配 | 建立场景 |
| 2 | The culture of the capital 首都风气 | 公共生活/风气/经济形容词 | 铺陈氛围 |
| 3 | The factions 派系冲突 | 激进/冲突/镇压词族 | 制造危机 |
| 4 | The code of conduct 行为准则 | 廉洁/中立/禁令搭配 | 提出规范 |
| 5 | The reckoning 清算与和解 | 裁断/和解动词与抽象名词 | 点题收束 |

**设计要点**：`provisional / interim / momentous / reigning / sovereign / crown` 集中在临时政府一幕，配合 `provisional government`、`in the interim`、`momentous decision`、`an irresistible force`；`parade / publicity / informative / conspicuous / pointed / predominant / strategic / stable / standing` 落在首都风气一幕，配合 7 条制度性搭配；`maverick / partisan / radical / militant / provocative / intransigent / traitor / repression / reprisal` 组成"派系冲突"链，正反立场同框；`incorruptible / neutral / pragmatic / definitive / servile / tyrannical / unilateral` 与 `remain neutral`、`a tyrannical political system`、`concerted efforts`、`unilateral trade` 等搭配落在准则一幕；`absolve / abstain / reconcile / stabilize / cope` 等动词收束于清算，避免清单式罗列。

## 二、英文故事　The Code of the Interim

### 1. The provisional government

The old **sovereign** had fled, and a **provisional government** took power in the **administration** building. A **technocrat** named Halden was asked to **convene** the first meeting of the **provisional** council. **In the interim**, the **reigning** ministers kept the **crown** on the desk and asked Halden to read the **protocol** and the **procedure**. "This is a **momentous decision**," he said, and the room fell silent. Outside, a crowd pressed toward the palace with **an irresistible force**, but the **minister** would not open the gate.

### 2. The culture of the capital

Public life returned quickly. A **parade** filled the square, and **publicity** about the new **minister** was everywhere. An **informative** leaflet explained the **basis** of the new **standing committee** and the **portion** of tax each **constituent** would pay. The **institutional hobby** of the old families — long lunches and quiet votes — seemed out of place. A **conspicuous** banner praised **strategic investment**, while a **pointed** cartoon mocked the **predominant** party. Every **inhabitant** felt the **interrelationship** of **stable prices** and political trust, and a **short-term** rent rise caused more **dependence** on the council than anyone admitted.

### 3. The factions

The calm did not last. A **maverick** general gathered **partisan** supporters and spoke of **radical ideas** in the barracks. His **militant** deputy staged **a provocative act** at the gate, and **tension** turned to **friction** in every office. Soldiers began to **patrol** the streets; **unrest** spread when a **traitor** in the council was said to **conspire** with the old guard. Rivals **collided** in the hall, and the **repression** that followed led to a **reprisal** the next night. The general's **intransigent** stand made every **tactic** of compromise fail, and Halden feared the state would **disintegrate** or **rupture** before dawn. He asked the assembly to **intervene** at once.

### 4. The code of conduct

At the emergency session, a **definitive** code was read. It demanded that every official **remain neutral** and be **incorruptible**, and that a **pragmatic** majority reject **a tyrannical political system**. **An anonymous letter** had accused the general of taking **confidential information**, and a clerk was declared **ineligible for** office. The code imposed a **ban** on **unilateral trade**, a **prohibition** on secret deals, a **cancellation** of old privileges, and a **constraint** that would **constrain** even the general's power to **declare** war. A **referendum** would grant **remission** of debts to loyal regions, and **concerted efforts** would **safeguard** the peace. Even an **internationalist** minister and a supporter of **gynaecocracy** agreed that **servile** obedience had no place in the new order.

### 5. The reckoning

The **deliberation** lasted three days. Halden faced a **quandary**: the **impracticality** of the plan was plain, yet the **significance** of a free vote was greater. He would not **predetermine** the outcome, nor **disclaim** his own doubts. Some members **despised** the general and **reprehended** his allies; others **deprecated** haste and urged **reconciliation** in a **reunion** of the old families. A **milestone** was reached when both sides agreed to **abstain** from violence and to **converge** on a single **resolution**. The **interplay** of fear and hope **beget** a strange calm, and a speech meant to **beguile** the crowd instead **accentuated** the need for care. Halden **impugned** no one; he sought to **absolve** the innocent, **restore** order, **stabilize** prices, and **cope** with what came next. In the margin of his notebook he had marked the word **interplay** "v./n.", for it served as both verb and noun. "Political culture," he wrote, "is not what people say in a **parade**; it is what they do when the lights go out."

## 四、学习建议

1. **过渡与政体词**：`provisional`（临时）、`interim`（其间）、`momentous`（重大）、`reigning`（在位）、`sovereign`（君主）、`crown`（王冠）围绕"旧王出逃、临时政府成立"记，配合 `provisional government`、`in the interim`、`momentous decision` 三条搭配。
2. **首都风气词**：`parade`、`publicity`、`informative`、`conspicuous`、`pointed`、`predominant`、`institutional`、`strategic`、`stable`、`standing` 落在公共生活场景，配合 `institutional hobby`、`strategic investment`、`stable prices`、`standing committee` 一次记全制度性表达。
3. **派系冲突链**：`maverick`（特立独行）→ `partisan`（党羽）→ `radical`（激进）→ `militant`（好战）→ `provocative`（挑衅）→ `intransigent`（不妥协）→ `traitor`（叛徒），配合 `radical ideas`、`provocative act` 与 `repression / reprisal / unrest` 的因果。
4. **准则与禁令**：`incorruptible`、`neutral`、`pragmatic`、`definitive`、`servile`、`tyrannical`、`unilateral` 对照 `remain neutral`、`a tyrannical political system`、`unilateral trade`、`concerted efforts`，把政治立场词与固定搭配绑定记忆。
5. **清算动词**：`absolve`（赦免）、`abstain`（弃权）、`reconcile`（和解）、`stabilize`（稳定）、`cope`（应付）、`restore`（恢复）按"惩前—和解—维稳"顺序记，配合 `reconciliation`、`resolution`、`milestone` 等名词收束全篇。
