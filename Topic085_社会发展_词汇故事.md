## 一、编排逻辑

本主题围绕"社会发展"，词可归为三类：发展性质形容词、社会结构名词、发展行动动词。我用"项目主管娜迪亚主持渡口区社区发展计划"作主线，按"摸底 → 规划 → 行动 → 成效 → 回望"推进，让破败街区、新移民、志愿者、自由派议员在因果链中依次出现：

| 幕 | 场景 | 承载语义场 | 叙事功能 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The survey 摸底 | 问题名词 + 交往动词 | 呈现困境 |
| 2 | The plan 规划 | 发展形容词 + 制度搭配 | 设计蓝图 |
| 3 | The work 行动 | 变革动词 + 冲突名词 | 落实项目 |
| 4 | The results 成效 | 成果名词 + 秩序词 | 阶段收成 |
| 5 | The legacy 回望 | 剩余词 + 升华 | 收束主题 |

**设计要点**：`abandonment / disconnect / obstacle` 写旧困境，`cohesion / consolidation / transmission` 写新成果；`alienate / condone / degrade` 与 `broaden / mitigate / strengthen` 构成"破坏—建设"动词对照；`an autonomous legislature`、`a communal toilet`、`a cosmopolitan gathering`、`innovative spirit`、`a large-scale revolution`、`personal freedom`、`for successive years` 七条搭配全部嵌入规划与回望场景。

## 二、英文故事　The Ferrybank Project

### 1. The survey

Nadia arrived in Ferrybank to direct a **community** development project. The old docklands showed **abandonment** everywhere: empty houses, a **disconnect** between the **urbanite** families and the **migrant** newcomers, and **obstacles** to even simple repairs. The **odds** against success were high and public **order** was fragile. A **baby-sitter** she met complained that the **behavior** of idle youths frightened her charges. At her first **encounter** with residents, voices rose; the **commonplace** complaints about **discrimination** made Nadia choose to **converse** with people rather than lecture them. An early **investigation** revealed that local **politics** had reduced **liberty** to a slogan. The **popularity** of the old **reformism** had faded, and no **stimulus** had reached the area **for successive years**.

### 2. The plan

Her first report had to be **applicable** to real conditions. She proposed **an autonomous legislature** for the district, **a communal toilet** and kitchen built with new funds, and **a cosmopolitan gathering** where newcomers and old residents could meet. **Innovative spirit**, she argued, mattered more than **prohibitive** rules; the plan was nothing less than **a large-scale revolution** in local habits, and **imminent** change was already in the air. She wrote that **personal freedom** must be joined to **equity**, and that **ethics**, not **accord** alone, should guide the work. A **volunteer** offered to help, and an **innovator** agreed to design the first workshop.

### 3. The work

The work began with small, practical acts. A clerk tried to **alienate** the newcomers **from** the project, but Nadia would not **condone** it; she chose to **broaden** the circle instead. When a shopkeeper proposed a **boycott** of the new kitchen, she invited him to the table. New arrivals agreed to **conform to** the shared rules, yet nobody asked them to **degrade** their own language or customs. When a few neighbours **diverted** repair funds, Nadia acted to **mitigate** the loss and **secure** the workshops. She asked the council to **exempt** the poorest households from fees, and spent evenings helping them **socialize** with longer-term residents. It **occurred** to her that a **revelation** about hidden debts would **diminish** trust, so she **dilated on** the figures at a public meeting. A rumour **spread** that the project would **repulse** investors, but a small grant **stimulated** new repairs. Volunteers helped **populate** the empty houses, and the first families to **succeed** in finding jobs **strengthened** the whole district. Older residents who had fearfully **demobilized** from public life began to take part again; their return **portended** a quiet **reunion**. The **modernization** of the water system would come later, but **collaboration** was already growing.

### 4. The results

By the third year the results were visible. Full **affluence** had not arrived, but **accord** between neighbours replaced suspicion and **cohesion** grew. The **consolidation** of small workshops created jobs, and the **transmission** of skills from older to younger workers became routine. A **liberal** on the council argued that **liberty** meant more than a **succession** of official visits; he wanted equity written into the budget. The **urbanization** of the docklands proceeded carefully, with a garden on every street. Small grants **stimulated** shops that had been closed for years.

### 5. The legacy

On the last page of her report Nadia wrote that development is not a single **revelation** but a long **succession** of patient choices. The **ethics** of a **community** are measured by how it treats the weakest, and the **equity** of its **accord** is the real test. She remembered the **odds** and the early **disconnect**, the **baby-sitter** and the **migrant** families, and the **innovator** who had designed the first workshop. The **modernization** was unfinished, but the **volunteer** spirit had **spread** and the **urbanization** of the docklands was no longer a fear but a shared project.

## 四、学习建议

1. **困境与成果对照**：`abandonment / disconnect / obstacle` 写起点，`cohesion / consolidation / transmission` 写结果——按"问题—解决"成对记忆。
2. **破坏与建设动词**：`alienate / condone / degrade / divert` 是"拆台"动作，`broaden / mitigate / strengthen / secure` 是"建设"动作，可放在同一场景对照。
3. **制度搭配**：`an autonomous legislature`、`a communal toilet`、`a cosmopolitan gathering`、`personal freedom` 是描述社区治理的常见短语，建议整串记忆。
4. **"联合/抵制"立场词**：`boycott`（抵制）与 `collaboration`（合作）、`liberal`（自由派）与 `reformism`（改革主义）在故事中分别代表不同立场，注意区分。
5. **易混词**：`stimulus`（名词，刺激物）与 `stimulate`（动词，刺激）同根；`urbanite`（都市人）与 `urbanization`（都市化）也是名词同源，注意词性差别。
