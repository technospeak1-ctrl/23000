## 一、编排逻辑

本主题是"政治话题 2"，海战、选举与官僚词交错，最忌各自成篇。我用"一名年轻书记员记录沿海省份的海战、后方的选举、信仰之争与财政部查账"作为主线，按"总部下令 → 后方选举 → 信仰冲突 → 账房查账 → 战后余波"五幕推进，让军事词、选举词、意识形态词分别落到具体场景人物的行动与言论上：

| 幕 | 场景 | 承载语义场 | 叙事功能 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Orders from headquarters 总部下令 | 海军/军衔/作战名词与形容词 | 建立冲突 |
| 2 | The campaign at home 后方选举 | 选举/官僚/舆论名词与动词 | 切换场景 |
| 3 | A clash of creeds 信仰冲突 | 意识形态/仪式名词 | 激化矛盾 |
| 4 | The accountant's ledger 账房查账 | 财务/行政搭配与动词 | 揭示内幕 |
| 5 | After the smoke 战后余波 | 收束性形容词与动词 | 点题收束 |

**设计要点**：`naval / bloody naval battles / carnage / marine / veteran / colonel / commander / headquarters / operation / major` 集中在海战一幕；`candidate / electorate / polling / canvass / magistrate / bulletin / commentator / political climate` 落在后方选举一幕；`revolution / theocracy / communism / jingoism / nonviolence / emperor / empire / theocracy` 组成"信仰与政体"链，通过传教士、鼓动者、老兵的人格化对立呈现；`official / unofficial`、`authoritative`、`upper class`、`chief accountant`、`official confirmation` 等官方—非官方对照词贯穿查账一幕；`pending`、`rehabilitate`、`remain` 收束于余波，避免清单式罗列。

## 二、英文故事　The Ledger and the Landing

### 1. Orders from headquarters

At the **headquarters** in the coastal **province**, a **colonel** and a **commander** studied the map. A **major** brought the order: the **naval** **operation** would begin at dawn. The **chief** of staff, an **authoritative** officer, spoke with **official** confidence, though an **unofficial** rumor already said the **foe** had landed. A **veteran** marine shouldered his **knapsack**, and young soldiers muttered about the **carnage** of **bloody naval battles** past. When the guns began to **fire**, nothing could **budge** the line. The engineers moved in to **demolish** the bridge and to **raze** the old fort, and the **authority** of the crown was at last enforced along the shore.

### 2. The campaign at home

Far from the coast, the election drew near. A young **candidate** asked the **electorate** for trust, and **polling** showed a tight race. Volunteers went out to **canvass** the villages while the **minister** and the **consul** argued inside the **bureaucracy** of the capital. A local **magistrate** announced the **result**, and a **commentator** described the **political climate** as tense. The **counterpart** of the minister across the aisle demanded a **session** to debate **responsibility** for the war. Officers came to **recruit** students, and the party machine continued to **run** the campaign. The **remainder** of the day went to counting ballots from the **minority** districts; every **announcement** was read twice. A **bulletin** from the front changed nothing, for the voters **remained** skeptical.

### 3. A clash of creeds

In the square the argument turned to creeds. An old **revolution** had promised **inalienable** rights, but now a **theocracy** preacher and a **communism** agitator fought for the same crowd. A wave of **jingoism** swept the port, and men who had praised **nonviolence** began to shout for blood. An **aviator** dropped leaflets over the rooftops, and an **assassin** was said to be watching the minister. The **league** of veterans recalled an old **exploit** of the army, but the **prestige** of the old **empire** was fading; even the **emperor** had lost his **tenure** on power. A **procession** marked the **inauguration** of the new assembly, and the **induction** of a **collective** of ministers followed at dusk. The **tactic** of the reformers was to seize the **target** of public opinion before the old guard could **incite** the crowd.

### 4. The accountant's ledger

At the treasury a **chief accountant** checked the books. He had **devoted friends** in the **upper class**, and they told him an **incongruous story** about missing funds. The minister asked for **official confirmation** before any name was printed, and demanded a **political perspective** that rose above party. To **centralize** control, the crown would **counterbalance** local taxes, and a clerk was told to **decipher a code** in the ledgers. The accountants **contrived** to hide the discrepancy for a while, but the **incongruous** figures would not balance, and the **devoted** clerk would not let the matter rest.

### 5. After the smoke

**Pending** the final report, the new government moved to **rehabilitate** the soldiers blamed for the old **carnage**. A young clerk named Ansel watched the **procession** pass and wrote in his diary that **authoritative** men rarely change, though **unofficial** histories are kinder. The **major** who had first **fired** was quietly **rehabilitated**, and the **remainder** of the file was sealed. Ansel thought of the **devoted friends** and the **incongruous story** they had told, and of the **prestige** that had once held the old **empire** together.

## 四、学习建议

1. **海战词群**：`naval`、`bloody naval battles`、`carnage`、`marine`、`veteran`、`colonel`、`commander`、`major`、`headquarters`、`operation` 围绕"登陆作战"场景一次记全，配合 `fire`、`demolish`、`raze`、`budge` 四个动作动词。
2. **选举词群**：`candidate`、`electorate`、`polling`、`canvass`、`magistrate`、`bulletin`、`commentator`、`political climate` 落在"后方竞选"场景，与 `minister`、`consul`、`bureaucracy`、`counterpart` 一起记。
3. **意识形态词**：`revolution`、`theocracy`、`communism`、`jingoism`、`nonviolence`、`inalienable` 通过传教士、鼓动者、老兵的立场对立呈现，避免抽象堆砌。
4. **官方与非官方**：`official` / `unofficial`、`authoritative`、`upper class`、`chief accountant`、`official confirmation`、`political perspective` 形成"官方—非官方"对照轴，配合 `centralize`、`counterbalance`、`decipher` 的查账动作记忆。
5. **收束动词**：`pending`（未决）、`rehabilitate`（复职）、`remain`（仍然是）、`run`（经营）散布于战后余波，把"命令—作战—选举—查账—平反"串成完整时间线。
