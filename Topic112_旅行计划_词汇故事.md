## 一、编排逻辑

本主题是"旅行计划"，机场、行李、路线与住宿名词密集。我用"年轻女孩 Clara 策划一次海外长途旅行"作主线，按"桌上规划 → 机场延误 → 乡下短游 → 登船航行 → 落脚收束"推进，让每件行装、每种交通方式都在她的计划与见闻里出现：

| 幕 | 场景 | 承载语义场 | 叙事功能 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | At the kitchen table 家中 planning | 规划心理 + 地图/精度/费用名词 + 决策动词 | 建立动机 |
| 2 | A delayed departure 机场 | 机场/行李名词 + 天气形容词 + 延误动词 | 制造波折 |
| 3 | A jaunt in the countryside 乡下 | 乡野/天气/临时遮蔽名词 + 地方口音 | 情节展开 |
| 4 | The voyage 码头登船 | 船型/铺位/港口名词 + 航行动词 | 情节推进 |
| 5 | A place to stay 落脚 | 住宿名词 + 节日/伴随动词 | 收束回望 |

**设计要点**：`at home and abroad`、`an improvised shelter`、`a provincial accent`、`choose from` 等搭配自然嵌入；`atlas / cartographer / precision / distance` 落在"她照着地图、跟当制图员的舅舅一点点量距离"的动作里；`baggage / camera / cash / bunk / lodging` 随她收拾、登机、住店依次出现；`suspend / delay / shuffle / fasten / steer / sail` 等动词按"延误—系安全带—掌舵—航行"的时间链铺开，避免清单式堆砌。

## 二、英文故事　The Map Clara Made

### 1. At the kitchen table

Clara had always been **avid** about travel. For months she had spread her **atlas** across the kitchen table, tracing routes with the **precision** of her uncle, an old **cartographer** who drew coastlines for a living. "Before you go **abroad**," he said, "you must **consider** the **distance**, the **expense**, and the **fatigue** of a long journey." Clara nodded. At home and **abroad** alike, she believed, a careful traveler plans **correctly**. She had to **choose from** three routes before her **choice** was final, and at last she decided to travel **overseas**. She checked her list **daily**, counting her **cash** and crossing off each small **choice** she had made. Her uncle teased that she was already tired before she had packed a single bag.

### 2. A delayed departure

At **dawn** they reached the **airport**. Clara **fastened** her seat belt, but the **passenger** beside her looked worried: the flight had been **suspended** without warning. A **delay** of three hours meant a long **layover**, and a **shuttle** would carry them to a waiting room. Through the window she watched a plane roar down the **runway**, then a **barge** creeping along the river far below. Her **baggage** sat at her feet — a **camera**, a warm coat, and a wooden **lance** she had bought as a curiosity in the market. She was still **sopping** from the morning rain, and the **frigidity** of the terminal made her shiver. She opened the **atlas** again and waited.

### 3. A jaunt in the countryside

When the **shuttle** finally released them, Clara took a short **jaunt** into the **countryside**. A friendly **motorist** offered her a lift along the **footpath** that led to the river **floodgate**. Beyond it lay a **caravan** of travelers and an **improvised shelter** of branches where a family spoke in a soft **provincial accent**. Nearby stood a **jade**, an old packhorse too tired to lift her head. Clara's **camera** would not work in the harsh **daytime** glare, so she simply watched the water slip through the gate. The **distance** to the **dock** was still long, but she felt the **fatigue** of the morning begin to lift.

### 4. The voyage from the dock

At the **dock** a **ship** waited beside a **barge** and a small **rowboat**. Clara would **sail** on the **voyage**, and an old sailor agreed to **steer** for her. He asked her to **fasten** the rope and to **correct** the course whenever the wind pushed them off. She had never **steered** a **ship** before, but the **management** of the little vessel was simpler than it looked. During a **layover** at a village port, a **hotelier** invited her to a **festal** dinner, and a fellow traveler agreed to **accompany** her back to the **ship** before dark. She slept in a narrow **bunk** and woke to the sound of water.

### 5. A place to stay

For **lodging** Clara stayed first in a **dormitory**, then in a small **condominium** whose stairs were always spotless. The **management** there was kind, and the **hotelier** who ran the place had a **provincial accent** and a warm smile. On her last morning she stood at the **dock** with her **camera**, photographing the **rowboat** and the **ship** in the pale **dawn** light. She had traveled far from home, yet the **distance** no longer frightened her. She had learned to **choose** well, to **consider** carefully, and to **correct** her course **correctly** — and she knew that at home and **abroad**, the wide world was hers to see.

## 四、学习建议

1. **规划链**：`consider`（考虑）→ `choose from`（从中选择）→ `choice`（选择）→ `correct`（修正）→ `precision`（精确），按"做计划"的动作顺序记，比孤立背单词更牢。
2. **出行方式谱系**：`shuttle`（摆渡车）→ `runway`/`airport`（机场）→ `barge`/`rowboat`（内河船）→ `ship`/`voyage`（远洋航行）；`layover`（中转停留）与 `delay`（延误）是旅行中最常见的两种"等待"。
3. **行李与花费**：`baggage`（行李总称）里装着 `camera`、`cash`、`lance`；`expense`（开支）要先算 `cash`，`atlas` 与 `cartographer` 帮你规划 `distance`。
4. **天气形容词对照**：`sopping`（湿透）与 `frigidity`（寒冷）常一起出现；`festal`（节日的）是正向气氛词，可与前两个负面感受词放在同一场景对比记忆。
5. **易混词**：`dock`（码头）与 `runway`（跑道）分属水、空两种交通；`abroad`（在国外）与 `overseas`（在海外）近义，但 `at home and abroad` 是固定搭配，不能换成 `overseas`。
6. **词形提示**：`correct` 既是动词"改正"也是形容词"正确的"，副词 `correctly` 由它加 `-ly` 构成；`improvised` 来自动词 `improvise`（即兴），`suspended` 来自 `suspend`（暂停）。
