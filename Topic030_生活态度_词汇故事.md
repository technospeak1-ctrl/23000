## 一、编排逻辑

本主题词量全章最大，围绕"生活态度"，可按情绪与立场分为 4 个语义场：正面态度词、负面态度词、情绪与品格名词、态度动作动词。我用"年轻人莉娜求职受挫后重建人生态度"的成长主线，按"受挫消沉 → 自省转念 → 重拾行动 → 豁达收获"推进，让褒贬态度词随主人公心境变化成组出现：

| 幕 | 场景 | 承载语义场 | 叙事功能 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The setback 受挫 | 负面态度形容词 + 消沉名词 | 建立低谷 |
| 2 | A change of heart 转念 | 中性思考词 + 自省动词 | 展示反思 |
| 3 | Back in action 重新行动 | 正面态度形容词 + 行动动词 | 展示振作 |
| 4 | A wiser life 更智慧的生活 | 剩余褒贬词 + 哲理名词 | 收尾升华 |

**设计要点**：正面态度（positive、chipper、hilarious、exultant、thoughtful、lofty、unshaken）与负面态度（depressed、desperate、exhausted、annoyed、mournful、vapid、turbulent）沿主人公心境交替；13 条常用搭配如 `lofty ideals`、`unshaken faith`、`positive role`、`transient happiness` 集中嵌入，动词搭配 `aspire to`、`rebuke sb. for doing sth.`、`simmer with` 等自然呈现。

## 二、英文故事　Lena's Change of Heart

### 1. The setback

Lena had lost the job, and at first she was **depressed**, **desperate**, and frankly **exhausted**. She felt **poorly** and **annoyed about** the whole business; her **expectation**s had been **unrealistic**, and the **disappointment** left her **mournful**. For days she was **shy** of friends and **halting** in speech, her **behavior** **overwrought**, her **attention** wandering. A **hypochondriac** neighbour would **sympathize** in a **patronizing** way; a **demanding** visitor once **embarrass**ed her in front of friends and left her in **consternation**. A **namby-pamby** card full of **sentimentality** only made her **sensitive** response worse, and the **frustration** of small tasks nearly made her **collapse**. She **regret**ted nothing, yet her **compunction** grew; she would **simmer with** resentment and **shilly-shally** over every small decision. "It is all so **vapid**," she said, **openly** weeping—a **transient** **shock** that felt permanent.

### 2. A change of heart

Then a **thoughtful** friend, **deferential** but **serious**, came to see her. With **deferential bearing** he spoke **modestly**, not with **flattery** or **complimentary** words, but with **truth** and **sensitivity**. He did not **disparage** her nor **rebuke** her **for** **shilly-shallying**; instead he would **admire** her **inflexible courage** and ask what she **aspired to**. His **consider**ed question **arouse**d something in her. She began to see her **complacency**, her **shortsighted** fears, her **jealousy** of others' **gallantry**, and how **unrealistic ideas** had shaped her. Was her **fatalist** outlook **favorable** or **mournful** self-pity? She **apologize**d to herself for the **connivance** in her own **indifference**, and felt a **thrill** of **recognition**. The **disapproval** she had feared from others was mostly her own **mourning** for a lost version of herself.

### 3. Back in action

That week Lena chose an **alternative** path. She became **chipper** and **positive** again, **hilarious** with old friends, **exultant** when a small **contender** role came her way. She would **behave herself**, **further** her skills, and **commend** itself to the new team; her **lofty ideals** and **unshaken faith** **propel**led her forward. She was **deliberately** kind, **openly** grateful, and took **satisfaction** in honest work rather than **flattery**. She declined to **brag** or **glorify** herself, nor would she **idealize** the **perfection** of others. A **positive role** at the office and **sensitive response**s from colleagues earned her **congratulation**s. "I **deserve** this," she said, **unrestricted** by doubt—her **mad** **time** of creation had begun.

### 4. A wiser life

In time Lena learned **tolerance** and **mercy**, even toward a **lax** boss and an **overconfident** rival whose **droll** **eclecticism** amused her. She no longer **connived** at **unsatisfactory** work, nor let **turbulent** moods destroy her quiet hours; she kept **inflexible** principles but a **lofty**, **unshaken** heart. She would **refuse** unfair terms and even **refuse draft** on a dishonest bill, **pique** no one's pride, and **apologize** when wrong. She knew **transient happiness** was not the whole of life, nor **undeserved** praise its goal. With **gallantry** she **consider**ed the **avail** of **attention** well spent, and **sympathize**d with those still **get depressed**. Her life, once **namby-pamby** and **vapid**, was now **serious**, **thoughtful**, and **favorable**—an **alternative lifestyle** built on **lofty ideals**, **inflexible courage**, and **unshaken faith**, to which she **aspired** every day.

## 四、学习建议

1. **按心境曲线记褒贬**：低谷（depressed、desperate、exhausted、mournful、vapid、turbulent）→ 转念（thoughtful、deferential、serious）→ 振作（chipper、positive、hilarious、exultant、lofty、unshaken）→ 豁达（tolerance、mercy、gallantry）。
2. **情绪名词成组**：disappointment、frustration、compunction、jealousy、indifference ↔ satisfaction、congratulation、recognition、thrill、gallantry。
3. **动词搭配优先**：`aspire to`、`behave oneself`、`commend itself to`、`rebuke sb. for doing sth.`、`simmer with` 五个高频搭配整块记忆。
4. **常用搭配成串**：`lofty ideals`、`unshaken faith`、`inflexible courage`、`positive role`、`transient happiness` 是本主题考查重点。
5. **词形意识**：过去式与被动（apologized、regretted、aroused、aspired、commended、propelled、refrained）、以及 shilly-shally→shilly-shallied、simmer→simmered 等变形要会认。特别注意 refuse draft（拒付票据）是商业语境的固定搭配。
