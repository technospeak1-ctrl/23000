## 一、编排逻辑

本主题是"企业性质与行为"，名词多为商业机构与产权词，动词多为经营决策词，极易写成"经济学词条表"。我用"玛拉·维尔接手家族私营商号，在国有化与私有化的风波中守住小店"作主线，按"接手商号 → 市场与货品 → 国有化浪潮 → 伪造危机 → 回望"五幕推进，让每个词落到具体的人、合同或制度上：

| 幕 | 场景 | 承载语义场 | 叙事功能 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | A private house 一间私营商号 | 企业身份 + 租赁/贷款词 | 建立人物 |
| 2 | Goods and a rival 货品与对手 | 市场/商品/企业规模词 | 铺陈环境 |
| 3 | The state factory 国有工厂 | 产权变革 + 投资动词 | 情节推进 |
| 4 | The forged signature 伪造签名 | 违规/损害/风险词 | 制造冲突 |
| 5 | The house that stayed 留下的商号 | 剩余词 + 收束 | 点题升华 |

**设计要点**：`private / individual / unauthorized / unavailable` 四个形容词分别贴到人物身份（私营、个人票据）与风险状态（未授权、无从获得）；`nationalization / privatization / denationalize / privatize` 一组词集中在"国有化浪潮"一幕，形成政策对照；`lessor / lessee / accommodator / loan / license` 落在开店的具体手续上；`forge / harm / hamper / weaken / scatter` 等动词在"伪造危机"一幕串成因果链；`black market price / dirty look / individual bill / private business` 四条搭配全部成为人物说出口或亲眼所见的具体事物。

## 二、英文故事　The House on the Square

### 1. A private house

Mara Vale ran a **private** trading **house** on the town square. It was a **private business** her father had left her, and every **individual** decision was hers alone. When she opened her first **individual bill** at the counter, she still signed it by hand. The stall belonged to a **lessor** named Cal Rusk, and Mara was the **lessee** who paid him rent each month. To buy more cloth she took a **loan** from an old **accommodator** who trusted her, and she hung a carved wooden **logo** above the door. A **newsagent** on the corner sold her the forms she needed, and at the county office she collected the **license** that let her trade legally.

### 2. Goods and a rival

The town also had a **hypermarket** that sold cheap **commodity** goods, and a **conglomerate** that owned half the shops. Mara watched **ballooning** prices on the exchange sheet and feared a sudden **downturn**. Her little store had no real **capacity** to compete, but she believed the **embodiment** of good trade was trust, not size. One winter a stranger offered her **counterfeit** handbags at a **black market price**. Mara gave him a **dirty look** and sent him away. "A **nonentity** with a false label is still a fraud," she told her clerk, and she locked the door.

### 3. The state factory

Behind the town stood a huge state-owned shoe factory. The council spoke of **nationalization** as if it were progress, but the workers said the **disintegration** of its management had already begun. Mara argued that it made no sense to **denationalize** a factory nobody could run, and that a real **privatization** needed **investment**, not slogans. She decided to **invest** in the plant and to **invest in** new machines, hoping to **privatize** the failing unit herself. A rival tried to **undercharge** her customers by two coins, and one day he even **undercharged** Mrs. Pike by a full pound to win her trade. Mara would not follow him down that road.

### 4. The forged signature

Trouble came from an **unauthorized** clerk who dared to **forge a signature** and move money out of the account. The **harm** was done before anyone noticed, and fresh **loan** papers were suddenly **unavailable**. Investigators said the missing files would **hamper** the case. Rumours began to **scatter about** the market, and Mara was tempted to **scatter** her efforts across a dozen problems at once. An old accountant warned her never to **scatter** her strength so thinly. She called the whole **adventure** a hard lesson and refused to let one fraud **weaken** the firm she had built.

### 5. The house that stayed

In the end the factory was sold, the **conglomerate** moved on, and the **hypermarket** cut its prices again. Mara kept her **private business** and her **license**, and the wooden **logo** still hung above the door. She had learned that a **commodity** is easy to copy, but a name built on honest **investment** is not. The **newsagent** still sold her the morning paper; the **lessor** still called for rent. "I began with one **individual bill**," she said, "and I will finish the same way — a **private** trading **house** that answers only to itself."

## 四、学习建议

1. **产权一条线**：`nationalization`（国有化）→ `denationalize`（非国有化）→ `privatization`（私有化）→ `privatize`（使私有化），四个词描述同一场政策拉锯，放在一段里对比记忆最牢。
2. **开店手续词**：`lessor`（出租人）对 `lessee`（承租人），再加 `accommodator`（贷款人）、`loan`（贷款）、`license`（执照），是"租铺—借钱—领证"的固定流程。
3. **机构规模词**：`house`（商号）→ `hypermarket`（大超市）→ `conglomerate`（企业集团），按规模递增排列；`capacity`（产能）与 `ballooning / downturn` 一起记，是经营景气词。
4. **风险与损害动词**：`forge`（伪造）、`harm`（损害）、`hamper`（妨碍）、`weaken`（削弱）、`scatter`（分散）在"伪造危机"一幕形成因果链，配套记比孤立记有效。
5. **搭配整串记**：`black market price`（黑市价格）、`dirty look`（厌恶的目光）、`individual bill`（个人票据）、`invest in`（投资于）、`scatter about`（四处散布）、`undercharge sb. by`（少收某人钱）都要连成短语背，不要拆成单词。
