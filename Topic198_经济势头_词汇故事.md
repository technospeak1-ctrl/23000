## 一、编排逻辑

本主题是"经济势头"，讲经济的兴衰循环，天然适合因果叙事。我用"经济顾问索菲亚亲历富图纳国从停滞、刺激、繁荣到泡沫破裂、再建平衡的完整周期"作主线，按"停滞 → 刺激 → 繁荣 → 泡沫破裂 → 新平衡"五幕推进，让每个词落在"政策做了什么、经济如何回应"的具体因果上。

| 幕 | 场景 | 承载语义场 | 叙事功能 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | A stagnant economy 停滞的经济 | 停滞/赤字/预测词 | 建立背景与问题 |
| 2 | The stimulus 刺激政策 | 刺激/预测/复苏词 | 政策介入 |
| 3 | The boom 繁荣 | 繁荣/猛涨/泡沫词 | 矛盾积累 |
| 4 | The bubble bursts 泡沫破裂 | 危机/衰退/暴跌词 | 冲突爆发 |
| 5 | A new balance 新平衡 | 均衡/稳定/活力词 | 点题升华 |

**设计要点**：`stagnant / stagnation / stagnate / dull / deficit` 集中在"停滞"一幕；`stimulus / accelerate / boost / stabilize / renewal / forecast / predict` 落在"政策介入"；`brisk / prosper / skyrocket / flourish / thrive / boom / bubble / foam economy / disproportionately` 沿"繁荣—泡沫"链集中在第三幕；`crisis / recession / deflation / crumble / plunge into / repression` 集中在"破裂"；`equilibrium / stability / standardized / vitality / steady` 在结尾收束，形成"停滞—繁荣—平衡"的完整经济周期。

## 二、英文故事　The Rise and Fall of Fortuna

### 1. A stagnant economy

Sofia Vance advised the government of Fortuna. She opened her **annual** report on a **dull** spring morning. For five years the economy had **stagnated**; the **stagnant economy** showed clear **stagnation**, and the **trend** was flat. **Current** figures put **current expenditures** above **annual income**, so a **deficit** remained. Prices were only **moderately** steady, demand barely a **steady demand**, and no real **momentum** had built. The **prospect** for **prosperity** looked poor, and every **prediction** failed. Within a **scope** this narrow, she saw one **tendency** above all: an **inconsistency** in the official books and a **false account** hidden inside the **economic climate** report. "This economy needs a **stimulus**," she concluded.

### 2. The stimulus

The cabinet acted at once. It would **accelerate** public works, **boost** confidence, and **stabilize** the currency. A **renewal** fund was created and the **budget** revised; Sofia was asked to **forecast** the outcome. She would **predict** a slow recovery: output would not **decrease to** the old lows, and the **depreciation** of the currency would stop. Data would **fluctuate** for a while — the **fluctuation** was **predominately** seasonal — but she urged the **steering** committee to stay the course. Spending rose **substantially**, **economical activity** picked up quietly, and the government's **credibility** and **competitiveness** slowly returned.

### 3. The boom

By the third year Fortuna was booming. Trade was **brisk**, and **a boom in car sale** led the rise. **Prosperity** spread; firms would **prosper**, shares would **skyrocket**, and new industries would **flourish**. Workers **thrived on** overtime, and **blue chip shares** became the favorites of **acquisitive** buyers. Years of work had let Sofia **accumulate experience** about bubbles, but even she joined the **boom**. A **bubble** was forming and the market began to **foam**; this **foam economy** rested on credit, and prices rose **disproportionately** to real output. Critics warned of a new **plutocracy** built on easy money.

### 4. The bubble bursts

Then credit tightened and the **bubble** broke. Share prices would **plunge**, and the **plunge into** panic was swift. A full **crisis** followed, then a deep **recession** with a **slowdown** in every sector. Prices fell into **deflation**, factories began to **crumble**, and the **repression** of doubt turned to open fear. The central bank tried to **depress** borrowing costs, but confidence was gone. Fortuna's **credibility** collapsed as fast as it had risen.

### 5. A new balance

Recovery came slowly, but it came. Sofia's successor built a new **equilibrium** on **stable prices** and prudent rules. Reporting became **standardized**, and the **nicety** of honest accounts returned. **Economical activity** among ordinary firms grew again, output regained its **vitality**, and a quieter **prosperity** took hold — not the foam of before, but the steady result of work. Looking back, Sofia wrote that a **prospective** age of **prospective wealth** depends less on a **boom** than on balance. The **termination** of the old bubble had been painful, but it had restored the economy's **stability** at last.

## 四、学习建议

1. **周期主线**：`stagnation / stagnate / stagnant` → `stimulus / accelerate / boost / renewal` → `boom / prosper / skyrocket / flourish / thrive` → `crisis / recession / deflation / crumble / plunge` → `equilibrium / stability / vitality`，按"停滞—刺激—繁荣—衰退—平衡"五阶段记整条经济周期。
2. **泡沫词族**：`boom`（繁荣）→ `bubble`（泡沫）→ `foam / foam economy`（泡沫经济）→ ` disproportionate`（不成比例地上涨）→ `crisis`，"泡沫从形成到破裂"一条线。
3. **政策动作**：`stimulus / accelerate / boost / stabilize / steer / forecast / predict` 是政府与顾问的动词链，配合 `budget / renewal fund` 记政策工具。
4. **状态名词**：`momentum / tendency / trend / fluctuation / equilibrium / stability / vitality` 描述经济"势头与状态"，注意 `tendency`（倾向）与 `trend`（趋势）的细微差别。
5. **对照记忆**：`prosperity / prospect / prospective wealth`（正面）与 `deficit / crisis / recession / depression`（负面）同框；`standardized / nicety / stable prices` 写"规范化与稳定"，是复苏阶段的标志。
