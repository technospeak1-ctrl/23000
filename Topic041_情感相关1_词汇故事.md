## 一、编排逻辑

本主题围绕"情感"，可归为 4 个语义场：情感状态形容词、情感名词、情感动作动词、情感搭配。我用"演员娜塔莉在首演日经历大起大落的一天"作主线，按"爱与期待 → 喜悦与感动 → 忧怒交加 → 平静收场"推进，让情感词随剧情起伏成组出现：

| 幕 | 场景 | 承载语义场 | 叙事功能 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Love and anticipation 爱与期待 | 关爱形容词 + 期待名词 | 建立基调 |
| 2 | Joy and acclaim 喜悦与喝彩 | 喜悦形容词 + 欢乐名词 | 情绪高潮 |
| 3 | Resentment and dismay 怨恨与沮丧 | 负面情感词 + 冲突动词 | 制造低谷 |
| 4 | A calm resolution 平静收场 | 剩余词 + 升华 | 情绪归于平静 |

**设计要点**：正面情感（affectionate、ecstatic、elated、engaging、beloved）与负面情感（resentful、discontented、distraught、aggravating、defiant）在一天内交替；`desperate for`、`emotional state`、`a transparent attempt`、`be afflicted with/by`、`applaud sb. for sth.` 等搭配自然嵌入。

## 二、英文故事　An Emotional Day

### 1. Love and anticipation

Natalie was an **affectionate** woman, **beloved** by all who knew her. She was **crazy** about the stage and **desperate for** a chance to prove herself. On the morning of the premiere she felt **anxious** yet full of **expectancy**; her **emotional state** was a mix of **desire** and **amazement** at how far she had come. She **adore**d her mother, who watched with **affection** and **deference**, and she **anticipate**d the **cheer** of the crowd; her **appreciation** of their support was deep. Her friend Mark was **bashful** but **engaging**, and his **alacrity** in helping calmed her. An **apologetic** stagehand was **beset** with nerves, but she **astonish**ed everyone with her calm. Yet a **resentful** rival, **avaricious** for fame, watched from the wings with a **transparent attempt** at a smile. Natalie **attach**ed her **affection** to the work itself, not the **acclaim**; she would **approve** of a **fair** judgment whatever came.

### 2. Joy and acclaim

The performance was **ecstatic**, **elated**, alive with **exhilaration**. The audience broke into **acclaim** and **jubilation**; their **applause** and **mirth** filled the hall. Natalie **effusive**ly thanked them, her **emotional state** one of pure **delight** — she had never felt such **exaltation**. Mark **applaud**ed her **for** her courage, and even the critic **extol**led her **transparent**, honest **sentiment**. She **gratify**ied her **taste** for hard work; the **flame** of her passion **flicker**ed and then **flare**d bright. There was **conviviality** in the dressing room, **compassion** in every **consolation**, and a **upsurge** of **cheer** that swept them all. She **appreciate**d every kindness and **exclaim**ed with joy. For one shining hour her **anxiety** and **discontent** vanished, and a **respectable** future seemed **decisive** — **be decisive of** her fate.

### 3. Resentment and dismay

Then came the **aggravating** news: a rival's **flaunt**ing **disdain** in the press. Natalie was **distraught**, her **defiant** composure cracking; **resentment** and **disgust** warred with **dismay**. She **begrudge**d the rival her **excitement**, and **bother**ed no one with her pain. A **discontented** understudy began to **gripe**, to **disagree with** the director, and to **disregard** the **rigorous** plan — **a rigorous program** that had held them together; his **discourteous**, **irritating** remarks would **bore** no one more than Natalie. The rival would **gloat** over every stumble. Mark tried to **commiserate** and to **concede** nothing of his loyalty; he would **confess** his own **anxiety** and **afflict** himself with guilt. But the rival's **dispute** **enflame**d old **alienation**; **exasperate**d, Natalie **detest**ed the **avaricious** intrigue and **exclaim**ed **against** the **heartrending** unfairness of it. The **deadpan** producer **disregard**ed her **despair**, and her **disobedient** mood **flare**d **out**.

### 4. A calm resolution

By evening the **whim** of fame had passed. Natalie **deplore**d the **apathy** of the critics but **strengthen**ed her resolve; she would **afflict** herself no more, nor **be afflicted with** doubt. The **aversion** she felt **baffle**d her at first, then **puzzle**d into clarity: she **detest**ed only the game, not the art. She **confess**ed to Mark that her **emotional state** had been a **dispute** between pride and love; he **gratify**ied her **taste** for honesty. The **deadpan** face of the producer **disagree**d with her **discontent**, but she **concede**d that **compassion** mattered more than **acclaim**. She **attach**ed **to** the theatre her **affection** once more, **anticipate**d tomorrow's **delight**, and slept — **elated**, **respectable**, and at last at **ease** with her **sentiment**.

## 四、学习建议

1. **情感两极**：正面（affectionate、ecstatic、elated、engaging、beloved、fair）↔ 负面（resentful、discontented、distraught、aggravating、defiant、heartrending）。按"首演日大起大落"记。
2. **喜悦名词成组**：acclaim、cheer、jubilation、mirth、conviviality、delight、exhilaration、exaltation、elation——程度与场合各异。
3. **负面情感动词链**：gripe（抱怨）→ begrudge（嫉妒）→ detest（厌恶）→ deplore（悲痛）→ exasperate（激怒）→ flare out（发怒），情绪逐步升级。
4. **搭配优先**：`desperate for`、`emotional state`、`a transparent attempt`、`be afflicted with/by`、`applaud sb. for sth.` 是高频搭配。
5. **词形意识**：过去式与被动（adored、affected、afflicted、anticipated、applauded、approved、astonished、attached、baffled、begrudged、beset、bothered、commiserated、conceded、confessed、deplored、detested、disagreed、disregarded、exasperated、exclaimed、extolled、gratified、irritated、puzzled、strengthened）要会认。
