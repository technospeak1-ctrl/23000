## 一、编排逻辑

本主题是"医疗保健"，核心是健康观念、生活习惯、疾病预防与保健名词。若逐个罗列必然枯燥，我改用"一场社区医疗保健讲座"作主线：开场讲体检与体质，接着讲好习惯，再讲常见病与药物，然后讲人体与病理，最后讲长寿与养生观，让保健词、生活名词与动词沿讲座流程自然出现：

| 幕 | 场景 | 承载语义场 | 叙事功能 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The lecture 开场 | 健康形容词 + 体检名词 | 开讲 |
| 2 | Good habits 好习惯 | 养生名词 + 生活方式动词 | 正面保健 |
| 3 | The remedies 常见病与药 | 疾病/药物名词 + 治疗动词 | 预防治疗 |
| 4 | The human body 人体与病理 | 身体/病理形容词 + 器官名词 | 科学解释 |
| 5 | The long view 长寿观 | 剩余词 + 升华 | 收束主题 |

**设计要点**：`genetic code`、`medical insurance`、`sanitary facilities`、`health care`、`health regimen`、`physical examination` 等搭配写进讲义与讲稿；`be conscious of`、`physical therapy` 落在讲座内容上；健康形容词（`robust / sturdy / healthy`）与病理形容词（`autoimmune / senile / vegetative`）放进相邻段落形成对照，构筑"保健—防病—治病—长寿"的完整链条。

## 二、英文故事　A Lecture on Health Care

### 1. The lecture

Dr. Noble stood before the community hall. "**Health care** begins with **physical examination**," he said, **emphatic** about prevention. He worked at the local **clinic** and knew that a good **check-up** and an early **diagnosis** saved lives. A strong **constitution**, he explained, came from **healthy** habits and a sound **lifestyle**; the **genetic code** you inherit is not your fate. "Be **healthy**, stay **robust**," he said, "and you will be **sturdy** into old age." In the front row sat a healthy baker and his **sun-bronzed** wife; a child waved. The **health professional** beside him handed out leaflets on **medical insurance** and **health certificate**s. He was **conscious of** the crowd's questions before they were asked.

### 2. Good habits

Noble spoke of **sanitary facilities** and clean water; a **salubrious** climate helped, but **avoidance** of **germ**s mattered more. "Take a deep **breath**," he said, and the room breathed. **Aerobics** kept the heart young — a **mettlesome** old man in the third row still did them daily. **Abstinence** from drink helped; a man who would **tipple** every night was slowly harming himself. **Vitamin**s and **calcium** built bone; **pabulum** — plain, simple food — and the **ingestion** of clean water mattered. A **sauna** relaxed the muscles, and the **salinity** of a sea bath soothed the skin. "**Nourish** the body," Noble said, "and give it time to heal." He added that **melatonin** helped a sound night's sleep, so a restless patient would not lie awake in a **confining** room.

### 3. The remedies

Then came the remedies. In winter the **flu** was **rife**, and **vaccination** was the best defence. A **germ** could cause **inflammation**; a **bandage** and a **tourniquet** belonged in every home. At the **pharmacy** a **prescription** brought a **tablet**, whose **therapeutic** effect would **assuage** the pain and bring **appeasement** and quiet **healing**. "Do not feed **anxiety**," Noble said; "**physical therapy** and a **health regimen** of rest do more than any pill." A patient who had long suffered began to **recover** under his care.

### 4. The human body

Noble drew a figure on the board. "We are **spined** creatures," he said; "an **autoimmune** reaction can turn the body against itself." A **peptic** ulcer flared with **remittent** pain; a **senile** aunt forgot names; a **lifelong** smoker coughed. A patient in a **vegetative** state lay still, while another waited **ambulatory** and eager to walk. An **unwitting** joke in the back row made the crowd smile. A **donor** had given an organ; a donor card, Noble urged, belonged in every wallet. The **carcass** in the lab reminded them of mortality, and a sudden **hiccup** in the audience broke the spell. "**Physical health** is not everything," he said.

### 5. The long view

The hall grew quiet. "**Longevity** is a gift," Noble said; "the **stimulus** of good habits offsets the **strain** of modern life, and both are a **necessity** we must understand." He would not **coddle** his listeners: "Do not spoon-feed children; let them **fit** themselves to the world." He would **liquefy** a **tablet** in water for a child, and he warned that a patient could **backslide** into old ways. The hall had been **soundproof**ed so his voice carried to the back; he cleared his throat and **recover**ed his breath. He spoke **faintly** now, but everyone leaned in. "**Health care**," he concluded, "is a **lifelong** practice, and **physical health** is the ground of a long life." Before he left, he walked to the hospital **ward** to see a patient who had followed his advice.

## 四、学习建议

1. **正向vs反向健康词**：`healthy / robust / sturdy / salubrious / sanitary` 属正向保健词，`autoimmune / senile / vegetative / peptic / remittent` 属病理词——故事第 2 幕与第 4 幕形成对照。
2. **预防三部曲**：`vaccination`（接种）→ `avoidance`（避免细菌）→ `sanitary facilities`（卫生设施）——按预防手段记忆。
3. **药物链**：`pharmacy`（药店）→ `prescription`（处方）→ `tablet`（药片）→ `therapeutic effect`（疗效）——第 3 幕按取药流程串联。
4. **构词法**：`养生`相关名词多含抽象后缀，如 `abstinence`、`avoidance`、`longevity`、`necessity`、`salinity`、`security`；形容词 `genetic / therapeutic / sanitary / salubrious` 多含 `-ic / -ous` 后缀。
5. **复述练习**：`confining`、`melatonin`、`ward` 已在故事中出现，建议用自己的话复述，例如 "a **confining** room"、"**melatonin** helps sleep"、"the doctor visited the **ward**"。
