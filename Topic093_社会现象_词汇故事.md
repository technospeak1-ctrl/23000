## 一、编排逻辑

本主题是"社会现象"，词可归为四类：描摹社会状态与性质的形容词、指称现象与机构的名词、观察与改造社会的动词、固定搭配。我用"退休社会学者 Elias 用一本笔记观察城市、调查慈善黑幕、推动改革"作主线，按"观察城市 → 发现问题 → 调查取证 → 温和改良 → 收束"推进，让正面价值词（`altruistic` / `vibrant` / `triumphant`）与负面现象词（`crooked` / `degenerate` / `inequitable`）在相邻场景里形成对照：

| 幕 | 场景 | 承载语义场 | 叙事功能 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The old observer 老观察者 | 状态形容词 + 城市名词 | 建立视角 |
| 2 | Beneath the surface 表象之下 | 负面现象形容词 + 问题名词 | 发现问题 |
| 3 | The inquiry 调查 | 取证动词 + 文书名词 | 调查取证 |
| 4 | A tentative remedy 初步改良 | 改良动词 + 价值名词 | 温和改良 |
| 5 | The last page 最后一页 | 剩余词 + 升华 | 收束主题 |

**设计要点**：`a crooked businessman`、`in a smooth way`、`transient happiness`、`traditional view`、`a transient worker`、`a triumphant smile`、`a vague answer` 等搭配自然嵌入；`camouflage / fabrication / embezzlement / obscurity` 构成"掩盖真相"的语义链；`authenticate / verify / accredit / circumscribe / invoke` 落在"调查取证"的具体动作上。

## 二、英文故事　The City in the Notebook

### 1. The old observer

Professor Elias Vane was an **emeritus** scholar who had watched the city for most of a **century**. From his high window he saw the **populace** hurry to work and the **inhabitants** of the old quarter follow their **habitual** routines. In his **traditional view**, a city was a living body: it might lie in **slumber** for years, then wake with **vibrant** energy. He insisted on reading it **holistically**, never judging one **case** in isolation. "Things **appear** simple," he wrote in his notebook, "but the simplest answer is often **simplistic**." Each **remark** he recorded held a **plenitude** of meaning. He had learned to mark the **demarcation** between public order and private wish. The street below ran **smooth**, and in a **smooth way** the market opened at dawn: carts rolled, the trams **clattered** over the rails, and a quiet kind of joy hung in the morning air.

### 2. Beneath the surface

Yet Elias knew that a **smooth** surface could hide deep **distension**. A **beggar** sat by the market gate, too proud to **beg** aloud, while a **crooked businessman** counted his gains behind closed doors and the **bourgeois** families along the avenue lived in ease. He had proved that **embezzlement** was **endemic** in the city's charities, and that a single **fabrication** could travel faster than any truth. The **obscurity** of the ledgers was deliberate **camouflage**, and the **insulation** of the rich from the poor was no accident. A local **crank** loved to **heckle** every public meeting; his words stirred a **disruptive** mood, and he shouted that all reform was **contrived** and the whole system **degenerate**. The **incongruity** was plain to anyone with eyes: a city so rich, yet so **inequitable**. People spoke of **transient happiness** and of the **transient worker** who never stayed long enough to belong. Elias would **ruminate** for hours on why **patronage** and poverty grew side by side.

### 3. The inquiry

Elias would not accept a **vague answer**. He began to **authenticate** every document and to demand **verification** of every claim. He refused to **accredit** any report without evidence, and he worked to **circumscribe** the power of the corrupt council. When threatened, he would **invoke** the law, for a **statutory** inquiry had already begun. Few could **foresee** how fast the truth would spread, but his **penetration** into the mystery grew day by day: the inquiry would **encompass** every district, and he could **encapsulate** a whole scandal in a single sentence. No official was **invulnerable** to it. He **despatched** assistants to the archives, where the false figures **disorientated** more than one volunteer. The clerks **repacked** dusty boxes, and slowly he **unwrapped** the layers of deceit. "I must **impress** on you," he told the council, "that numbers can lie." He **prepared** a careful report, noting one **rarity**: a missing ledger that had vanished without a trace.

### 4. A tentative remedy

The first reform was **abortive**, but from its failure a better plan began to **germinate**. Elias proposed the **substitution** of honest officials for the old guard, a wiser **utilization** of public funds, and the **fulfillment** of promises long broken. He argued that charity must be **altruistic**, not a tool of **patronage**, and that the city needed a **tenable** policy rather than grand promises. A **hypothetical** question in the seminar — would honesty ever pay? — drew **respondent** after respondent to their feet. An **inventive** young clerk suggested new rules; the debate grew more **sophisticated**, and even the old parties began to **westernize** their habits. The city **reinvigorated** its schools and hospitals. One afternoon a boy was caught trying to **scrump** an apple from a stall, and Elias used the small incident to argue that poverty, not wickedness, caused such acts.

### 5. The last page

By spring the city had changed **tangibly**. The new council kept its word, and the coalition for reform proved **unbeatable**. Elias closed his notebook with a **triumphant smile**, though he knew that no single victory ended a city's troubles. A **vibrant** crowd filled the square, and the old man watched in a **smooth way**, content that his **traditional view** of a living city had, at last, been proved **tenable**.

## 四、学习建议

1. **表象与实质**：`smooth`（表面顺利）与 `distension / embezzlement / camouflage`（内部张力与掩盖）在同一场景对照；`vague / simplistic` 是"含糊、简单化"的认知陷阱，`holistically / penetration` 则是看透现象的能力。
2. **"掩盖真相"语义链**：`obscurity`（隐晦）→ `fabrication`（捏造）→ `embezzlement`（贪污）→ `camouflage / insulation`（伪装与隔绝）——按"现象如何被藏起来"串记。
3. **调查动词群**：`authenticate`（鉴定）→ `verify / verification`（核实）→ `accredit`（采信）→ `circumscribe`（限制）→ `invoke`（诉诸法律），是"调查取证"场景的高频词。
4. **改造社会的动词**：`germinate`（萌芽）→ `substitution / utilization`（替换与利用）→ `reinvigorate`（重振）→ `westernize`（西化），按"从想法到制度"排列。
5. **社会现象的固定表达**：`a crooked businessman`、`a transient worker`、`a triumphant smile`、`a vague answer`、`in a smooth way`、`traditional view`、`transient happiness` 都是描述社会人事的地道短语，建议各造一句。
