## 一、编排逻辑

本主题是"公司管理"，词表里既有大量形容管理失当或得法的形容词，也有组织、人事、制度名词和整肃动词。我用"一家混乱的公司来了一位有魄力的新主管，她查明积弊、整肃人事、重建制度，最终让公司恢复和谐运转"作主线，按"混乱的现状 → 新主管到任 → 整肃人事 → 重建制度 → 重归和谐"五幕推进，让每个词落到具体人物动作或制度上：

| 幕 | 场景 | 承载语义场 | 叙事功能 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | A topsy-turvy office 混乱的办公室 | 失当形容词：topsy-turvy, shiftless, blustering, long-winded, discursive, dissonant, gauche, impolitic, complaisant + 组织名词 | 建立问题场景 |
| 2 | A chief with charisma 有魅力的主管 | 领导/才能类：charisma, knack, judgement, leadership, mastery, direction, proprietor + executive board 等搭配 | 引入变革者 |
| 3 | Cleaning house 整肃人事 | 整肃动词：defalcate, peculate, resign, rescission, depose, proscribe, deregulate, devolve, designate, indenture, constitute | 展现纠偏动作 |
| 4 | Rebuilding the system 重建制度 | 建设动词：allocate, allot, ameliorate, approach, attest, mend, outshine, require, superintend + optimization, welfare, precedent 等 | 推动制度重建 |
| 5 | A congruent whole 重归和谐 | 收束词：congruity, concinnity, conduct, colleague, recapitulate | 点题升华 |

**设计要点**：第 1 幕把 `blustering / long-winded / discursive / dissonant / gauche / impolitic / complaisant` 七个"负面管理相"集中在一个会议场景，形成鲜明群像；第 3、4 幕用 `defalcate / peculate / depose / proscribe` 与 `allocate / allot / ameliorate / mend / superintend` 形成"破"与"立"的对照；正负词同篇对照出现，避免词典式罗列；结尾以 `congruity / concinnity` 点出"管理出和谐"的主题。

## 二、英文故事　The Art of Order

### 1. A topsy-turvy office

The company was in a **topsy-turvy** state. A **shiftless manager** presided over a **blustering** meeting, and his **long-winded**, **discursive** speech only added to the **dissonant** noise. A **gauche** clerk made an **impolitic** joke, while a **complaisant** secretary agreed with everyone. Even the **chief executive** could not see the **overall** picture. The **administration** had become a **system** of habits: each **section** guarded its own files, the finance **sector** hid an **error**, and **decision-making** had turned into **escapism**. Worker **welfare** was forgotten.

### 2. A chief with charisma

Then a new **executive** arrived. She had real **charisma** and a **knack** for hard **judgement**. She called the **executive** board together, demanded a **confidential** document on the current year accounts, and spoke of managerial talent as the company's greatest need. The **proprietor** watched her every move. "**Leadership** is not a title," she said. "It is **mastery** of the details." Her **direction** was clear: no more **complaisant** nodding.

### 3. Cleaning house

She acted fast. A treasurer who had **defalcated** and another who had **peculated** chose to **resign**; their **resignation** letters were accepted and the **rescission** of their contracts followed. She **deposed** a **shiftless** head, **proscribed** the old favouritism, and **deregulated** the rigid rules — the **deregulation** surprised everyone. She gave **permission** for every change. Power was **devolved** to the section heads, and a new treasurer was **designated**. Older clerks were **indentured** to train the newcomers. The old guard fought back, but the new **system** **constituted** a fresh start.

### 4. Rebuilding the system

Next she **allocated** funds fairly and **allotted** each **section** a share. She set out to **ameliorate** the **welfare** of the staff and **mended** a broken **precedent**, **approaching** every problem with patience. To **attest** that the books were clean, she hired auditors. The **optimization** of the workflow will **outshine** the old routine. She **required** every **recipient** of a budget to report back, **superintended** the changes herself, and promoted a **superintendent** who **attested** to the new **disposition** of duties. No clerk was **inferior** to another, and everyone had to learn the **institutionalized** rules. The **overall** balance of payment improved at last.

### 5. A congruent whole

At last the company found its **congruity**. The once **dissonant** departments now worked in **concinnity**, and the new **conduct** of business earned praise. Her **colleagues** on the **executive** board listened closely as she would **recapitulate** the plan in a single sentence. The **executive** who had arrived in doubt now led with **charisma** and calm **judgement**. "A good **manager** knows the art of making every **section** pull together," she said. The **proprietor** agreed: the board had never been stronger. The **knack** she brought — the **mastery** of people and numbers — had turned a **topsy-turvy** firm into a model of order.

## 四、学习建议

1. **负面管理形容词成组记**：`blustering / long-winded / discursive / dissonant / gauche / impolitic / complaisant / shiftless / topsy-turvy` 全在第 1 幕的会议群像里，按"一场糟糕的会议"整体记忆。
2. **"破"与"立"两组动词对照**：`defalcate / peculate / depose / proscribe / deregulate / rescission` 是"破除积弊"，`allocate / allot / ameliorate / mend / superintend / optimize` 是"重建制度"，第 3、4 幕形成清晰对照。
3. **"-tion/-sion" 名词后缀**：`administration / deregulation / optimization / rescission / resignation / disposition / direction` 多为"动词 + 名词后缀"，记住后缀就能推测词义。
4. **Management 搭配**：`executive board`（董事会）、`confidential document`（机密文件）、`current year`（本年度）、`managerial talent`（管理人才）、`inferior to`（次于）、`overall balance of payment`（收支总差额）都在故事里有真实场景，注意整体记忆。
5. **同义微辨**：`defalcate` 与 `peculate` 都指挪用公款，`resign` 与 `resignation` 是动词与名词，`depose`（免职）与 `resign`（辞职）方向相反——结合第 3 幕的人事整肃体会差别。
