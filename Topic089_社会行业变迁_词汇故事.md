## 一、编排逻辑

本主题围绕"社会行业变迁"，词可归为四类：变迁形容词/副词、旧行当与新职业名词、变迁行动动词、搭配。我用"记者玛拉回到故乡、记录一个渔业小镇在十年间变成现代城镇"作主线，按"旧秩序 → 新力量涌入 → 人口外流 → 重建 → 落成记录"推进，让老匠人与新职业者在因果链中依次登场：

| 幕 | 场景 | 承载语义场 | 叙事功能 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The old circle 老行当 | 旧职业名词 + 基础/区域形容词 | 建立旧秩序 |
| 2 | The new forces 新力量 | 新职业名词 + 全球/功利形容词 | 外力涌入 |
| 3 | The exodus 外流 | 负面变迁名词 + 阻碍/恶化动词 | 代价显现 |
| 4 | The new cornerstone 新基石 | 建设动词 + 搭配 | 重建新生 |
| 5 | The record 记录 | 剩余词 + 升华 | 收束主题 |

**设计要点**：`go downtown`、`departmental library`、`regional accent`、`global warming`、`in advance`、`existed on`、`far removed from`、`in the distant future` 等搭配自然嵌入；`blacksmith / carpenter / craftsman / workman / herdsman / peasant / pedlar / porter / postman / factotum` 构成"旧行当网"，`electrician / technician / designer / dietitian / optician / instructor / tutor / scientist` 构成"新职业网"；`alter / build / develop / acclimatize / tinker` 等动词落在"改造与重建"的具体动作上。

## 二、英文故事　The Town That Changed

### 1. The old circle

Mara was a **writer** in **journalism**, and she had come home to report on a town that no longer **existed** as she remembered it. She would **go downtown** in the early morning, past the **departmental library** where she had once studied, and stand on the **cornerstone** of the old market square. Her hometown's **birth** had been as a **piscatorial** village; **approximately** a century ago, **sailors** and **pedlars** traded here. In those days a **servant** or a **factotum** could be found in every big house, and a quiet **acolyte** swept the church steps. The **circle** of old shops was small: a **blacksmith** hammered iron, a **carpenter** shaped wood, a **workman** carried bricks, and a skilled **craftsman** repaired the boats. Beyond the town, a **herdsman** tended cattle and a **peasant** grew the grain, while a **porter** met the train and the **postman** delivered the letters. Their tools were **rudimentary**, their speech carried a **regional accent**, and their whole **existence** turned on the **trade** of fish and timber. Mara had **presupposed** that these ways would last, but now she had returned to **discern** the truth.

### 2. The new forces

By the time she was a girl, **external** pressure and **global** markets had reached the town. **Global warming** was changing the fish runs, and the catch that had fed generations began to **drop**. A new generation of **venturesome** builders arrived with **adjustable** machines and **utilitarian** plans; they would not **settle** for the old ways but wanted to **alter** every street. An **electrician** strung the wires, a **technician** installed the pumps, and a **designer** drew new clothes for the shop windows. A **dietitian** opened a clinic, an **optician** fitted glasses, an **instructor** taught at the new school, and a **tutor** gave private lessons in the evenings. A **scientist** came to study the river, while a great **migration** of workers **immigrated** from the distant provinces. The **predominant** mood was progress, though a **bandit** or two appeared in the unsettled years. A **mediator** was hired to keep the peace, a **solicitor** drew up the contracts, and a **senator** spoke at the new hall. A **sponsor** paid for a **ballerina** and a **diva** to perform, and the old fish market was **changed into** a theatre. Nothing had been planned **in advance**.

### 3. The exodus

Yet progress could **impede** as well as help. Tasteless new blocks began to **disfigure** the riverfront, and the smoke seemed to **burn** away the old memories. The factories **impoverished** the peasants who had once **existed on** their small fields. A quiet **disillusionment** spread among the older craftsmen, and the **diffusion** of cheap goods drove the **embroidery** sellers from the square. Young people, eager to **advance**, joined the **exodus** to the cities, and this **migration** emptied the lanes. The **departure** of the last **sailor** marked the end of an era. The council tried to **prohibit** the worst excesses and voted to **remove** the derelict workshops, but nothing could stop the **change**.

### 4. The new cornerstone

Then the town began to rebuild itself. The council voted to **build** a new hall and to **develop** a training centre for the new trades. Newcomers had to **acclimatize** to a different rhythm of life; an old **workman** who had once carried bricks learned to **tinker** with electric motors instead of carts. To write honestly, Mara tried to **objectify** the past rather than romanticize it. The glass **designer**'s studio and the **mediator**'s office now stood where the fish market had been, and the new railway station **epitomized** the age. The town was **far removed from** the village of her **birth**, yet the old **cornerstone** was still set into the square. **In the distant future**, the elders hoped, the town would remember its **provenance**.

### 5. The record

Mara filed her story at dawn. She wrote of the **accountancy** classes that had replaced the evening songs, of the **philatelist** who still met collectors in the new library, and of the **police** station and the **policeman** who now directed traffic where the blacksmith's forge had been. The old **artillery** barracks on the hill had become apartments. "Nothing stands still," she concluded. The **predominant** truth of her town was written in the **decade** of change she had witnessed: the **diffusion** of new trades had not erased the old **circle**, only redrawn its **cornerstone**. She thought of her first **departure** and of all those who would **immigrate** after her, and understood at last that **existence** is a series of **adjustable** rooms we **settle** into, one after another.

## 四、学习建议

1. **新旧行当对照**：`blacksmith / carpenter / craftsman / workman / herdsman / peasant / pedlar / porter / postman / factotum` 是旧行当，`electrician / technician / designer / dietitian / optician / instructor / tutor / scientist` 是新职业；按"谁消失了、谁出现了"成对记忆。
2. **变迁动词链**：`alter`（改）→ `disfigure`（破坏外观）→ `impoverish`（使贫困）→ `impede`（阻碍）→ `remove`（拆除）→ `build / develop`（重建）→ `acclimatize / settle`（适应），正好是故事推进的顺序。
3. **人口流动词**：`migration`（迁徙）与 `exodus`（大批离去）都写人口移动，但 `exodus` 强调"集体离开某地"，`immigrate`（移入）是反向动作。
4. **全球与区域**：`global warming`、`external pressure` 是宏观外力，`regional accent`、`departmental library` 是本地细节；故事用它们把"大趋势"落到"小地方"。
5. **易混词**：`predominant`（主要的）与 `rudimentary`（基本的）都以 -d 结尾但义不同；`utilitarian`（功利的）与 `venturesome`（好冒险的）在故事中代表新建设者的两种面孔。
