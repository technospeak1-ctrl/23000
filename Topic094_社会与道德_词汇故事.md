## 一、编排逻辑

本主题围绕"社会与道德"，词可归为四类：社会状态形容词、社会与道德名词、社会行动动词、制度搭配。我用"市政道德委员会主席 Mara 主持一场关于虐待指控的听证会"作主线，按"建立原则 → 接到投诉 → 听证争辩 → 真相大白 → 新宪章"推进，让正面价值词（`virtue` / `equality` / `harmony` / `liberty`）与负面现象词（`harassment` / `abnormal` / `persecute` / `uproar`）在同一场听证里形成对照：

| 幕 | 场景 | 承载语义场 | 叙事功能 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The committee 委员会 | 状态形容词 + 制度名词 | 建立原则 |
| 2 | The complaint 投诉 | 负面现象词 + 文书名词 | 接到投诉 |
| 3 | The hearing 听证 | 立场动词 + 道德名词 | 听证争辩 |
| 4 | The turning point 转折 | 转折动词 + 和解名词 | 真相大白 |
| 5 | The new charter 新宪章 | 剩余词 + 升华 | 收束主题 |

**设计要点**：`an anonymous letter`、`abnormal behavior`、`moral concepts`、`municipal buildings`、`stable prices`、`initial impression` 等搭配自然嵌入；`denounce / reveal / evoke / interfere / intervene / persecute / reconcile` 等动词落在"投诉—听证—和解"的具体动作上；`equality / liberty / harmony / virtue` 构成"道德价值网"。

## 二、英文故事　The Moral Hearing

### 1. The committee

Mara Bach chaired the city's **municipal** committee on **moral** affairs in a **metropolitan** district that had changed beyond recognition in the **contemporary** era. Meetings were held in the **municipal buildings**, where the members debated **moral concepts** and the meaning of a decent life. Her **initial impression** of each case was recorded before evidence arrived, and she taught the young members never to trust a first **image**. She opened each hearing in the **customary** way, with a **parade** of facts rather than speeches. The committee's **basis** was simple: every citizen **responsible** for a wrongdoing must answer for it. In an **epoch** of quick judgments, Mara wanted **stable prices**, **secure** streets, and a rule of law just as **stable**. "A **principle** is not a fashion," she said. "It is the **fabric** of a decent city." She believed **action**, not words, revealed **virtue**.

### 2. The complaint

The case began with **an anonymous letter** pinned to the door of the hall. It described **abnormal behavior** in a city shelter and accused a keeper of **harassment** of the weakest residents. The **incidence** of such complaints had risen, and every **occurrence** was logged with care. Mara knew the problem was **complex** and would **complicate** the hearing, for an **impulse** of anger can easily be mistaken for proof. A public **row** broke out when the letter was leaked; the **uproar** in the press grew louder. Mara refused to **denounce** anyone before the facts, and she would not **evade** the hard questions. "We must **reveal** what happened," she said, "not hide it." She **inspected** the shelter herself, **classified** each complaint by **scale** and seriousness, and chose to **handle** the case personally.

### 3. The hearing

At the hearing, voices clashed. A **peer** of the accused spoke of **prestige** and **hierarchy**, while an **immigrant** family asked whether **equality** and **liberty** could survive in a city of such gaps. The old **ideology** of the district valued **heritage** and order, but the **norms** were changing. The stories **evoked** memories of earlier migrations. Mara asked the clerk to **organize** the files, to **mobilize** volunteers, and to **establish** a fair **procedure** for questioning. She would **intervene** if a witness tried to **intimidate** another, and she would not let the committee **interfere** in matters beyond its power. When a member tried to **isolate** the family, Mara **resisted** the move and met **resistance** with calm; she was **resistant** to any attempt to **persecute** the weak. Some children would **tease** the newcomer, but she urged tolerance. Several problems **underlie** the complaint: fear and poverty **affect** every answer. She asked the council to **ensure** that both sides were heard, and she would not let a single shout **disrupt** the proceeding. To her **feedback** file she added one line: the committee must **resolve** to act. The final session would **coincide with** the spring festival.

### 4. The turning point

It **emerged** that the letter was false, and a **miracle** of sorts followed: the two sides agreed to talk. The **knot** of distrust began to loosen, and no one would **lapse** back into silence. The trouble had **originated** from a single **lapse** of judgment, but the **inquiry** traced it to its **origin**, and the **influence** of one honest witness changed everything. A **banner** was hung outside the hall, and an **improvement** in trust could be felt across the district; **harmony**, once a distant word, returned to the streets. Some residents chose to **emigrate** to other countries, others would **migrate** to distant towns, but all carried the lesson with them. Each night Mara would **meditate** on the case, and she urged the city to **reinforce** its rules and **reconcile** the two families. Above all, the committee worked to **secure** justice for the weak.

### 5. The new charter

At the **advent** of spring, the city passed a new charter. It was **realistic** rather than grand, built on the **basis** that no one stands above the law. In a new **era**, the meaning of **moral** life was clearer: **equality** before the rules, **liberty** to speak, and a **harmony** that lasts. The **improvement** came on a small **scale** at first, but it spread. Mara looked at the **fabric** of the city she had served and wrote one last line in her notebook: "A decent society is not a **miracle**; it is the daily **action** of people who refuse to look away."

## 四、学习建议

1. **道德价值网**：`virtue`（美德）→ `equality`（平等）→ `liberty`（自由）→ `harmony`（和谐）→ `principle`（原则）——这是讨论社会道德的五个核心抽象名词，故事把它们放在同一场听证里。
2. **投诉与听证动词链**：`denounce`（指责）→ `reveal`（揭露）→ `inspect / classify`（检查分类）→ `intervene / interfere`（干预）→ `reconcile`（和解），按"接到投诉到结案"排列。
3. **正反对照**：`harassment / abnormal / persecution`（骚扰、反常、迫害）与 `virtue / harmony / liberty`（美德、和谐、自由）形成道德两极；`resist / resistance / resistant` 一族分别从动词、名词、形容词出现。
4. **社会变迁词**：`emigrate`（移出）、`migrate`（迁移）、`immigrant`（移民的）、`origin / originate`（起源）——写社会流动时高频，注意 `emigrate from` 与 `immigrate to` 的方向差别。
5. **制度搭配**：`municipal buildings`、`stable prices`、`an anonymous letter`、`abnormal behavior`、`initial impression`、`moral concepts` 都是讨论社会与道德的地道短语，建议各造一句。
