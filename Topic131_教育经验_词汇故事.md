## 一、编排逻辑

本主题是"教育经验"，核心是描写**教师品质与学习状态**的抽象形容词（36 个）以及一批**教育心理名词**，极易写成"术语清单"。我用"一位退休老教师在家接待返校学生和一名青年教师，借告别谈话回顾一生教学经验"作主线，按"相聚 → 回忆教室 → 我的教法 → 临别赠言 → 夜里独省"五幕推进，让每个抽象词落到具体人物的动作与因果上：

| 幕 | 场景 | 承载语义场 | 叙事功能 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The gathering 门前的相聚 | 教师品质形容词 + 聚会名词 | 建立人物与氛围 |
| 2 | The schoolroom 教室的回忆 | 学生性情（正反对照）+ 心理名词 | 群像铺陈、对照 |
| 3 | The method 我的教法 | 教学动词 + 动词搭配 | 点明教育经验 |
| 4 | The farewell 告别赠言 | 演讲名词 + 褒义形容词 + 搭配 | 情节高潮 |
| 5 | The evening 夜里的独省 | 内省名词 + 剩余抽象词 | 收束与升华 |

**设计要点**：`commonsense / thorough / cultivated / literate / peerless / tip-top` 等褒义词落在老教师与优秀学生身上，`frightened / maudlin / malevolent / fanatical / misguided / ersatz` 等贬义词落在问题学生或错误做法上，同框对照；`a comprehensive survey / compulsive eating / a cultivated girl / instructive lessons / an integral number / mental fatigue / a passionate speech / a peerless performance / a theoretical background` 九条常用搭配，以及 `amend one's ways / attach importance to / earn a living / consult sb. about sth. / terrify sb. into doing sth.` 等动词搭配，全部嵌进老教师的原话；`elicitation / introspection / enlightenment / paranoia` 等心理名词集中在他的自述里，紧扣"教育经验"主题。

## 二、英文故事　The Old Pedagogue's Farewell

### 1. The gathering

On a **calm** autumn afternoon the old students **congregated** at the little house beside the school, and with them came a **rookie** teacher who had been sent to learn from the **pedagogue** they had come to honour. Mr. Hale was a **tidy**, **commonsense** man in his seventies, **disposed** to talk and not **averse** to a long **conversation**. His **curiosity** about their lives was undiminished. It seemed **advisable** to begin with the past, so without **delay** he unlocked a drawer and spread out his **credentials**, a **complete** record of a working life. "I have made **a comprehensive survey** of every class I ever taught," he said. His voice was **calm**, his verdicts almost **absolute**, and his **thorough** notes told a **psychological** story the diplomas could not tell. Two empty chairs marked the **absent** ones. The **educated**, **literate** guests listened; the young man kept a **lively** face though he felt **mentally** weary. Mr. Hale had never **distracted** himself with self-pity. "A teacher **inured** to routine turns **ersatz**," he said, "so I stayed **fancy-free** and changed my ways when I was wrong."

### 2. The schoolroom

He remembered a **frightened**, **maudlin** boy who had sat in the back row, and **a cultivated girl** who read poetry while the rest whispered. There had been a **fanatical** pupil whose **vanity** curdled into **paranoia**, and a **malevolent** one who twisted a harmless **brainstorm** into a cruel **distortion**. Mr. Hale felt **chagrin** at his own first term and **grief** when a **misguided** parent turned a small **pitfall** into a formal **infliction** of punishment at home; yet the boy's **obedience** was never forced. "I never **terrified** any child **into doing** anything," he said. "I would **forswear** the rod before I **mislead** a mind, and I **amended** my **ways** whenever I erred." He had an **aversion** to loud noise and no taste for **careerism**. He laid **emphasis** on **concentration** and would not **distract** a class with idle talk. His **instructive lessons** were built on **elicitation**, not on fear; a **sequence** of small questions produced an **ecstasy** of understanding in the **disciples** who sat at his feet. One girl showed real **flair** and gave **a peerless performance** in the final play; the memory was a **delight** that time could not dim. He allowed no **compulsive eating** of facts, no rote that caused **mental fatigue**, and he drilled **an integral number** on the board until every child grasped it.

### 3. The method

"People think **a theoretical background** is enough," he continued. "It is not. You must **comprehend** the child as well as the subject." When a case baffled him he would **consult** a colleague **about** it, then **consult with** the parents before he **advised** a change. He had **admitted** his own errors in public and **suggested** that a teacher should **attach importance to** small acts as much as to marks. He never **earned** good money, but he **earned a living** honestly and **attached himself to** the school until it became his home. To **exemplify** his rule he took a wrong answer and showed how one false figure could **distort** a whole sum. He would **emphasize** the need to rest, for a tired mind cannot learn. "I **imprinted** one maxim on every class," he said: "teach the whole child, and be **courageous** enough to say 'I was wrong.'"

### 4. The farewell

At the gathering he rose to make **a passionate speech**. It was **a peerless performance** for a man of seventy, and the room fell silent as he traced the **far-reaching** consequences of a single kind word. He spoke of **enlightenment** as a slow growth, of **guidance** as the quiet art of **elicitation**, of the **temptation** to judge a child by one bad day. He warned that a **tip-top** student could still be **fallible**, and that an **exciting** idea could turn **fanatical** in the wrong hands. "Keep your **sanity**," he told the young teacher, "and never confuse **absolute** certainty with **complete** truth." He urged them to **amend** their **ways** each year, to **attach importance to** patience, and to let **introspection** be a daily habit.

### 5. The evening

In the evening, after the guests had gone, Mr. Hale sat alone with his thoughts. The house was **tidy** and **calm**; the day's **conversations** played over in his mind. He had given his **guidance** freely, and if a word of advice had gone unheard, that was **destiny**, not **vanity**. He thought of the **absent** chairs, of a **grief** he had never fully **comprehended**, and of the **psychological** cost of a **misguided** correction. He wrote one last line in his notebook: "A teacher's work is **far-reaching**; a life is **complete** only when it is **thoroughly** examined." Then he set the book down, **fancy-free** as a boy and **calm** as the autumn night, and let the **lively** voices of his old students finally be still.

## 四、学习建议

1. **品质形容词分正反记**：褒义 `commonsense / thorough / cultivated / literate / peerless / tip-top / courageous / educated`；贬义 `ersatz / malevolent / fanatical / maudlin / misguided / averse / absent`。放在"老教师与问题学生"的同一场景里对照记忆，效果最好。
2. **"易错/狂热"词族**：`fallible`（易错的）→ `fanatical`（狂热的）→ `misguided`（被误导的）→ `paranoia`（偏执）→ `sanity`（理智），程度与侧重点不同，正好串起教育心理线索。
3. **教育心理名词链**：`elicitation`（启发）→ `concentration`（专注）→ `enlightenment`（启迪）→ `introspection`（自省）→ `destiny`（命运），串起老教师的教育思路。
4. **搭配聚焦**：`attach importance to`（重视）、`consult sb. about sth.`（向某人请教某事）、`terrify sb. into doing sth.`（恐吓某人做某事）都是高频考点，注意介词 `about / with / into` 不要混用。
5. **冷词留痕**：`ersatz`（代用的）、`maudlin`（爱哭的）、`pedagogue`（教师）、`careerism`（追名逐利）、`infliction`（强加的痛苦）较书面，已在正文自然出现，注意其贬贬色彩与搭配。
