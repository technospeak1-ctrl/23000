## 一、编排逻辑

本主题是"债券、股市"，证券与交易术语密集。我用"经纪人克拉拉陪伴客户哈罗先生经历一轮牛市转熊市、遭遇违约、最终回暖"作主线，按"开户 → 持有证券 → 市场转冷 → 违约风波 → 回暖收尾"五幕推进，让每个词落在"经纪人做了什么操作、客户承担什么后果"的具体动作上。

| 幕 | 场景 | 承载语义场 | 叙事功能 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The newcomer 新客户开户 | 券商/委托/交易词 | 建立人物与业务场景 |
| 2 | Bonds and shares 债券与股票 | 债券/票据/估值词 | 铺陈持仓 |
| 3 | The market turns 市场转冷 | 牛熊/暴跌/投机词 | 冲突爆发 |
| 4 | The default 违约风波 | 债务/支付/票据词 | 矛盾激化 |
| 5 | Recovery 回暖收尾 | 猛涨/期权/预测词 | 点题升华 |

**设计要点**：`broker / brokerage / client / fiduciary / assurance / deed / transaction` 集中在"开户"一幕；`bond / debenture / bondholder / bearer / convertible bond / face value / intrinsic value` 集中在"持仓"一幕；`bull market / bear market / bearish / slump / fluctuate / declining stage` 与 `insider / black market / predatory pricing` 集中在"市场转冷"制造冲突；`creditor / debtor / debit / payment / foreclose / bounce / sight draft` 落在"违约"；`soar / surge / share option / rights issue / forecast` 在结尾收束。

## 二、英文故事　The Broker's Cycle

### 1. The newcomer

Clara Vance worked as a **broker** at a small **brokerage** near the **bourse**. Her first **client**, Mr. Harrow, wanted to open an account and make a **transaction**. She handed him a **quotation** and explained the **quoted price** of every **security** on her screen. As a **fiduciary** she owed him **assurance** that his **deed** of ownership was safe; the **factorage** fee was modest and the **currency** was stable. The **stock exchange** itself felt far away from this quiet office, but Clara knew that every **market** begins with one order. "Tell me what you want," she said, "and I will find it."

### 2. Bonds and shares

Within a month Mr. Harrow held a **bond** and a **debenture** issued by a **listed company**. As a **bondholder** he received interest, and the **bearer** of the paper could claim its **face value** at maturity. He bought a **convertible bond**, a **blue chip** share and an **A share**, then studied the **dividend per share** before the stock went **ex-dividend**. The **quoted share** looked sound: the company's **market share** was rising and its **intrinsic value** was strong, even at a **stock discount** to par. He kept his spare cash in a **current account**. There were no **additional charges** at first, and Clara's **authorization of resources** for the account was quickly approved.

### 3. The market turns

Then the **bull market** broke into a **bear market**. Clara turned **bearish** as prices began to **fluctuate** and then **slump** into a long **declining stage**. The mood was **bleak**, and the **bleak future** of several firms frightened small investors. "This is a **risky business**," she warned, but Mr. Harrow kept **speculating in the stock market**. A rumor of **predatory pricing** and a **black market** in shares spread through the trading floor. When an **insider** was accused of leaking news, confidence collapsed and the losses began to **spiral**.

### 4. The default

The trouble reached Mr. Harrow when one **debtor** failed to pay and the **creditor** demanded **payment**. A **draft** he had accepted and a **sight draft** from abroad would both **bounce**; the **sight draft** would also **expire** if not honored. With an **authority to purchase** stalled and no new **authorization of resources**, the lender threatened to **foreclose** on the collateral. Mr. Harrow had to **pawn** his remaining shares, and a **debit** note arrived with an **extra charge** and a demand for **indemnity**. **As a general rule**, Clara said, read the **authentic text** of every contract before you sign.

### 5. Recovery

A year later prices began to **soar** and trading would **surge** again. Mr. Harrow, more careful now, used his **privilege** as a long-standing client to gain a **share option** and a **stock option** in a new firm; a **rights issue** followed as a **side benefit**. Clara kept the signed records in a **strong room** and issued a fresh **forecast** with modest expectations. "The **market** rewards patience," she told him, "not the hope of a quick **extra** gain." The cycle, she had learned, turns on discipline rather than luck.

## 四、学习建议

1. **开户链**：`broker / brokerage / client / fiduciary / assurance / deed / transaction / quotation / quoted price` 串起"经纪人—客户—委托—报价—成交"的完整场景。
2. **持仓词族**：`bond / debenture / convertible bond / bondholder / bearer / face value` 是债券端；`blue chip / A share / quoted share / dividend per share / ex-dividend / intrinsic value / market share` 是股票端，对照记忆。
3. **牛熊对照**：`bull market` 对 `bear market`，`bearish / bleak / slump / fluctuate / declining stage / spiral` 是下行词，`soar / surge` 是回暖词；`black market / predatory pricing / insider` 写市场乱象。
4. **票据风险**：`draft / sight draft / authority to purchase` 是正常票据，`bounce / expire / foreclose / pawn / debit / indemnity` 是违约动作，配合 `extra charge / additional charges` 一起记。
5. **期权收尾**：`share option / stock option / rights issue / side benefit / privilege / strong room / forecast` 是回暖后的品种与凭证，注意 `as a general rule`（一般来说）是阅读高频固定语。
