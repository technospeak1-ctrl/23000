## 一、编排逻辑

本主题是"宗教信仰 1"，教义、人物、仪式与态度词并行，最容易写成宗教名词表。我用"一位年轻学者到修道院访问一周，经历辩论、异端传说、节庆礼仪，带着问题离开"作主线，按"抵达 → 神学院之辩 → 圣地与异端 → 节庆礼仪 → 离别"五幕推进，让每个词落在"读经、争论、祈祷、行礼、祝福"的具体动作上：

| 幕 | 场景 | 承载语义场 | 叙事功能 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | A scholar at the cloister 修道院的访客 | 信仰/经典/修士/伦理词 | 建立人物与氛围 |
| 2 | The argument in the seminary 神学院之辩 | 神学/哲学/态度/意识形态词 | 思想冲突 |
| 3 | The heretic at the shrine 圣地的异端 | 奇迹/迷信/伪经/崇拜词 | 悬念与反思 |
| 4 | The feast day 节庆之日 | 教阶/礼仪/清真寺/祝福词 | 仪式高光 |
| 5 | At the gate 在门口 | 祈祷/欢庆/赠言词 | 点题收束 |

**设计要点**：`religion / faith / belief / tenet / Bible / scripture / hallowed / eternal` 集中在第一幕的"读经"；`theological / metaphysical / existential / ideological differences / moral concept / scrutable / gnomic` 落到第二幕的辩论；`chauvinistic / nationalism / racism / patriotism / propaganda` 作为被批评的态度同框出现，避免立场失衡；`heretic / superstition / miracle / apocrypha / witch / idolatrize / benison` 串成第三幕"圣地传说"的因果；`chant / chime / bishop / cardinal / primate / laity / etiquette / sermon / mosque / minaret` 集中在第四幕的仪式动作；`bless with / chant sb.'s praises / enshrine / glorify / whoop it up` 等搭配分别嵌进祝福、颂扬与欢送，整串加粗。

## 二、英文故事　A Week at St. Oran's

### 1. A scholar at the cloister

Miriam Vale, a young scholar of **religion**, arrived at the **cloister** of St. Oran with a **faith** she would not name and a **belief** that every **tenet** deserved a question. The **monastery** stood beside a narrow **chapel**; inside, a **monk** swept the steps and a **nun** carried bread to a **hermit** in the woods, and behind the chapel lay a **cemetery** of flat stones. Brother Ansel, the **pastor** who ran the **seminary**, was **conservative** in dress but **customary** in kindness. As was his **wont**, he offered her water and an **abstemious** meal — no meat, no wine. On the table lay a **Bible**, a page of **scripture** copied in a **hallowed** hand, and a small **shrine** with a candle. "We keep **the eternal love** at the centre," he said. Miriam had come as an **advocate** of **altruism**, and she admired the **humanitarian** work of the **humanist** who had built the school; their **ethics** were plain, their **ethos** quiet. A disputed **value** still troubled her **conscience**, and she wrote it in her notebook.

### 2. The argument in the seminary

In the library the argument turned sharp. Ansel was **theological** by training, but a visiting **philosopher** pressed him on **metaphysics** and on a **metaphysical**, **existential** question no prayer could settle. A young monk spoke of **the supernatural powers** and of a **preternatural** light seen at the shrine; Miriam answered with a **scrutable**, almost **gnomic** sentence about **ideological differences** and the **moral concept** of duty. The **chauvinistic** visitor from the city, a loud **patriot**, called the old **naturalist** a fool. His **patriotism** and **propaganda** about **nationalism** and **racism** made the room cold. Ansel was **iconoclastic** about images but not **sacrilegious** about them, and he valued a **discerning** mind above a loud one. His **humanistic** learning was **extant** in three thick notebooks. "The **valuable antiques** in our vault are not the truth," he said. "Truth is a **fortunate** accident of **fortune** — and an **augury** only fools trust." The **epicurean** merchant who funded the school laughed; the **hedonic** life amused him, yet he paid for the lamps.

### 3. The heretic at the shrine

At dusk a crowd gathered at the shrine. An old woman whom the village called a **witch** knelt there; a **heretic** once burned at that spot was now spoken of as a saint. A **miracle** was said to have happened when a **pilgrim** touched the stone, and **apocrypha** in the side chapel told the story of **Jesus** walking these roads toward **Heaven**. The crowd began to **idolatrize** the statue, and Miriam saw how quickly **superstition** becomes **observance**. A **benison** was promised to all who stayed quiet. She wrote one line in her notebook: "A sign is not a proof."

### 4. The feast day

On the feast day the **chant** rose at dawn and the **chime** answered from the tower. A **cardinal** in red, a **bishop** with a tired face, and the **primate** of the province took their places; the **catholic** families of the town filled the **laity** benches. Even a **conformist** cousin who never argued about **etiquette** bowed when the **sermon** began. Across the valley a **mosque** sent up the call from its **minaret**, and the two sounds met in the wind. Ansel **blessed** the table and asked God to **bless with** peace the visitors who had fasted. In his speech he would **glorify** the plain virtue of service, **enshrine** the memory of the heretic in a new niche, and **chant the nun's praises** in the evening hymn. "I will not **discriminate** against anyone at this table," he said.

### 5. At the gate

Before she left, Miriam **prayed** in the little chapel and then, to the horror of the quiet cousins, **whooped it up** with the young novices in the yard — a joy that needed no augury. Ansel walked with her to the gate. "Keep the **eternal** light," he said, "and remember that a **benison** is only a word unless you feed someone." Miriam looked back at the monastery and the cemetery behind it, and understood that her **religion** of questions had found a house large enough to hold them.

## 四、学习建议

1. **信仰名词网**：`religion / faith / belief / tenet / Bible / scripture / shrine / sermon / seminary` 以"读经—讲道—祈祷"为轴串起来，比单背"宗教、信仰"两个中文词清晰得多。
2. **修会人物**：`monk / nun / hermit / pastor / bishop / cardinal / primate / laity / conformist / pilgrim` 按"修士—教阶—信众—朝圣者"排成一张人物表，第四幕的仪式场景里一遍记全。
3. **态度反差**：`conservative / customary / abstemious` 与 `iconoclastic / epicurean / hedonic` 在修道院与商人之间形成对照；`chauvinistic / nationalism / racism / patriotism / propaganda` 作为被批评的一组同框出现，注意它们与 `altruism / humanitarian / humanistic` 的对立。
4. **神秘与怀疑**：`supernatural / preternatural / miracle / augury / apocrypha / superstition / idolatrize` 是"信"的一边；`scrutable / gnomic / discerning / metaphysical / existential` 是"思"的一边；`heretic` 站在两者之间，正是故事的张力所在。
5. **仪式动词搭配**：`bless / bless with / chant / chant sb.'s praises / glorify / enshrine / pray / whoop it up` 都对应一个可见的动作或声音，务必整串记；`eternal / moral / valuable` 这三个常被夹在释义行里的形容词，结合 `the eternal love / moral concept / valuable antiques` 一起记。
