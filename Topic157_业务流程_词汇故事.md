## 一、编排逻辑

本主题是"业务流程"，词表由一批组织、审计、生产策略词和大量业务流程搭配构成。我用"三家公司合并后，一位大人物带领团队重整组织、审计账目、改造生产线、理清人事并完成一次投标"作主线，按"新组织结构 → 内外审计 → 生产策略 → 人事与成长 → 化解危机"五幕推进，让每个词和搭配都落到具体流程或人物动作上：

| 幕 | 场景 | 承载语义场 | 叙事功能 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | A new structure 新结构 | 组织/人物类：corporation, combine, enterprise, partnership, directory, mogul, lead, orientation + able, equal, resourceful, assistant, acting, corporate, nominal | 建立公司场景 |
| 2 | The audit 审计 | 审计/记录类：evaluator, record, overestimate + external/internal audit, direct tax, nominal wages, permanent income, final demand, track record, card index | 展现核查流程 |
| 3 | The production line 生产线 | 生产/制度类：short-staffed, vacant, practicable, inner + make-to-order/make-to-stock strategy, mass production, bill of materials, TQC, IT, fail-safe system, critical path analysis | 推进改造流程 |
| 4 | Staffing and growth 人事成长 | 人事类：cultivate, entrant, presentation, shortlist, progression, deliberate, belong + temporary post | 铺陈人事流程 |
| 5 | Averting the crisis 化解危机 | 决断类：avert, award of bid, portfolio management, cardinal rule | 点题收束 |

**设计要点**：`corporation / combine / enterprise / partnership / directory` 集中在第 1 幕，附着于"合并、画组织机构图、发通讯录"的动作；`external audit / internal audit / direct tax / nominal wages / permanent income / final demand` 等审计搭配串成第 2 幕的查账链；`make-to-order / make-to-stock strategy / mass production / Total Quality Control / fail-safe system` 在第 3 幕形成完整的生产流程；`short-staffed / vacant / temporary post / progression` 落在第 4 幕的人事变动；结尾以 `avert / award of bid / cardinal rule` 收束"流程为王"的主题。

## 二、英文故事　The Business of Order

### 1. A new structure

When three firms merged into one **corporation**, the **mogul** who ran them called it a true **combine**. On **orientation** day he showed the new **enterprise** on an organization chart and handed out a staff **directory**. "This is a **partnership** of equals," he said, "and every **able** person here will be treated as an **equal**." A **resourceful assistant** took the **lead** in arranging the desks, while the **acting** head of finance checked the **corporate** accounts. A **nominal** chairman was named for show, but real power lay with the board. "Do nothing for private gain," the mogul warned; "work for **corporate** good."

### 2. The audit

External auditors came first. The **evaluator** looked through every **record** and kept a track **record** of past deals. She asked for a card index of suppliers, took a carbon copy of each **direct** tax form, and filed everything in triplicate. Her external audit found an **overestimate** in the budget; a second internal audit confirmed it. She noted that **nominal** wages had risen while **permanent** income had not, and that final demand for the product was still strong. "The **record** must be clean," she said, "and the orders from the board must be **direct**."

### 3. The production line

On the factory floor the manager faced a **short-staffed** team and a **vacant** supervisor's chair. "We need a **practicable** plan," he said. He compared a make-to-order manufacturing strategy with a make-to-stock strategy, then chose mass production for the basic models. A bill of materials lay on his desk, and Total Quality Control was posted on the wall. Information Technology linked the machines, and a fail-safe system stopped any faulty part. Critical path analysis showed where the line slowed, and better working conditions lifted output. New safety **procedures** were posted beside the clock. "In the **inner** city, space is tight," he noted, "so every inch must count."

### 4. Staffing and growth

HR had to **cultivate** new talent. A young **entrant** impressed them in her job **presentation**. They drew up a **shortlist** for the **permanent** post and offered a temporary post to another candidate. "Career **progression** matters," she said, "as much as pay." The **deliberate** pace of promotion was fair, and no one **belonged** to a favoured clique. "A **deliberate** plan beats a hasty one," the manager agreed, and the whole team felt they **belonged**.

### 5. Averting the crisis

When a rival bid threatened the deal, the mogul acted at once. "We must **avert** a takeover," he said. He made a **deliberate** choice, won the award of bid, and kept risk low through portfolio management. "The **cardinal** rule is simple," he told them: "know your numbers." The **evaluator**'s **resourceful** work had saved the **enterprise**, and everyone now **belonged** to a stronger **partnership**. "Business is a chain of small, **direct** steps," he said, "done for the common good."

## 四、学习建议

1. **组织词成组记**：`corporation / combine / enterprise / partnership / directory` 都出现在合并场景，配合 organization chart 与 staff directory 的画面，按"一家公司从合并到建档"的流程记忆。
2. **审计搭配集中背**：`external audit / internal audit / direct tax / nominal wages / permanent income / final demand / track record / card index / carbon copy / in triplicate` 全在第 2 幕的查账链里，成组出现便于整体掌握。
3. **生产策略术语**：`make-to-order manufacturing strategy`（按订单生产）与 `make-to-stock strategy`（按库存生产）是一对反义策略，配合 `mass production / Total Quality Control / fail-safe system / critical path analysis` 理解整条产线。
4. **"-ent/-ant" 人物后缀**：`assistant / entrant / evaluator` 分别指"助理/新进入者/评估者"，第 1、2、4 幕各有其人，注意 `-ant/-ent` 常表"做某事的人"。
5. **易混形容词**：`nominal`（名义上的）与 `permanent`（固定的）、`acting`（代理的）与 `assistant`（辅助的）都在故事里形成对照，体会"名义 vs 实质""代理 vs 正式"的差别。
