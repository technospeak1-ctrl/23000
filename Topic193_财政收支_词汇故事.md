## 一、编排逻辑

本主题是"财政收支"，与上一篇"财政事务"相连，但重心落在"收支如何计算、如何平衡、如何由亏转盈"上。我用"新任司库玛塔用两年把小岛国从危机带到偿付能力"作主线，按"危机接手 → 追查账目 → 勒紧开支 → 开辟财源 → 恢复稳健"五幕推进，让收支、税收、核算类词落在具体的财政决策动作上。

| 幕 | 场景 | 承载语义场 | 叙事功能 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | A crisis on the desk 危机中的账本 | 财政形势 + 收入/税种词 | 建立人物与危机 |
| 2 | Following the money 追踪资金去向 | 成本核算/支出/账目词 | 揭出做假账 |
| 3 | Tightening the belt 勒紧裤带 | 紧缩/市场/可持续词 | 应对危机 |
| 4 | New sources of money 新的资金来源 | 融资/税改/外汇词 | 重建财源 |
| 5 | Solvent again 重获偿付能力 | 结果/品格词 | 点题升华 |

**设计要点**：`revenue / tax / taxation / tariff / gross income / taxable income / disposable income` 沿"收入从哪来、税怎么收"的因果链集中在前两幕；`costing / direct cost / variable costs / cash flow / balance / gross margin / gross profit` 集中在"追查账目"一幕；`retrench / tighten / tight market / sustainable growth / steady demand` 集中在"紧缩"一幕，形成"紧缩—可持续"的决策逻辑；`floatation / financial backing / managed float / direct debit / bailment` 落在"开辟财源"的融资操作上；`solvent / steadfast / residual` 等结果与品格词在结尾收束。

## 二、英文故事　The Treasurer's Two Years

### 1. A crisis on the desk

Marta Reyes became **treasurer** of Costana in the middle of an **economic crisis**. The **government** had asked her to rescue the **treasury**, and every **financial** report on her desk told the same story: **revenue** was falling while **expense** kept rising. The nation's **finance** was **financially** weaker than anyone admitted. Workers' **disposable income** had shrunk, so **taxable income** was **small**, and even **gross income** from the **tariff** on imports looked **huge** only on paper. The **tariff rate** was too high to lift **tax** collections, and **taxation** was criticized as unfair. "Our **financial situation** is worse than the figures show," Marta told her team. She had to **calculate** how much the **economy** could bear before the next **fiscal year** began.

### 2. Following the money

Marta ordered a full **costing** of every ministry. She wanted the **direct cost** of each project separated from **variable costs**, and she studied the **cash flow** line by line. The **balance** was positive on paper, but an **abatement** here and a **relief** payment there hid the truth. **Gross margin** and **gross profit** at the state port looked healthy, yet the **spillover** from one bad loan troubled her. She closed a loophole that let firms label private luxuries as a **tax-deductible expense**. An auditor warned that someone had tried to **manipulate** the accounts; the false entries were **calculated to** conceal a **pelf**-filled slush fund. Marta refused to **manipulate public opinion** with rosy **valuation** reports. She reviewed the **accessions tax** on inherited land and the **value-added tax** on goods; both needed reform if the **tax yield** was to recover.

### 3. Tightening the belt

With the **fiscal year** half gone, Marta **retrenched**. She would **tighten** every line of spending, and the **tight market** for credit gave her no choice. Aid to the **internal** regions could not be cut, however, for **abject poverty** there was real. She looked for **preferable** ways to save: buying **preferably** from local suppliers, sustaining only a **steady demand** for essentials, and aiming for **sustainable growth** rather than a one-year spike. The **tax relief** and **tax allowance** she had granted earlier looked **steady**, and she promised **sustainable profit growth** in the next plan. Even a **flat** levy, she argued, was fairer than a sudden **tight** squeeze.

### 4. New sources of money

To fill the gap, the **government** backed a **floatation** of shares in the state railway and sought **financial backing** from friendly banks. A **managed float** of the currency was **interlinked** with a new **direct debit** system for **fund** transfers, and a **bailment** of gold was arranged as collateral. Marta worked to **cement** ties with foreign lenders, knowing that **wax and wane** in commodity prices could wreck the plan. Every **financing** deal had to **mature** on schedule, and the **residual** cash was placed in a reserve.

### 5. Solvent again

Two years later Costana was **solvent**. The **government** was **steadfast** in its new discipline, and Marta's **financial** reports were **financially** sound at last. The **economy** grew again, and the **treasury** posted a real surplus. She had proved that a **small** island could be **steadfast** and **sustainable** without **huge** borrowing. On her final desk lay a note: "Public **finance** is not **pelf** for the clever; it is **calculated to** serve, and it **matures** only when leaders are **steadfast**."

## 四、学习建议

1. **收入链**：`revenue`（收入）→ `gross income`（总收入）→ `taxable income`（应纳税收入）→ `disposable income`（税后收入），沿"毛收入—计税—税后到手"顺序记，配合 `tax / taxation / tariff rate` 理解税怎么收。
2. **核算对照**：`costing`（成本核算）下分 `direct cost`（直接成本）与 `variable costs`（可变成本），与 `cash flow`（现金流量）、`balance`（收支差额）、`gross margin / gross profit`（毛利/总利润）一起记，是财报阅读核心。
3. **紧缩词族**：`retrench`（紧缩开支）、`tighten`（收紧）、`tight market`（紧张市场）与正面目标 `sustainable growth / sustainable profit growth / steady demand` 同幕对照，理解"短期紧缩换长期可持续"。
4. **融资操作**：`floatation`（发行股份）、`financial backing`（财政支持）、`managed float`（管理浮动）、`direct debit`（直接借记）、`bailment`（委托）串起"怎么找钱、怎么划钱"的场景。
5. **警示与收束**：`manipulate`（篡改）、`pelf`（不义之财）、`manipulate public opinion`（操纵舆论）写反面，结尾用 `solvent / steadfast / sustainable` 点题；`be calculated to do sth.`（目的在于）与 `mature`（到期）在首尾呼应。
