## 一、编排逻辑

本主题围绕"正面态度"，可归为 3 个语义场：赞许的性情形容词、嘉奖与认同的名词、表达肯定的动词。我用小镇"善行节"颁奖典礼作主线，按"提名 → 候选人 → 评议 → 典礼 → 尾声"推进：先由委员会物色人选（性格形容词），再逐一列举受奖者（正面品质），接着是 ↑ 表决与嘉奖（动词与认同名词），最后以誓言与掌声收束。

| 幕 | 场景 | 承载语义场 | 叙事功能 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The nomination 提名 | 性情形容词 + 礼仪副词 | 建立情境（筹办善行节） |
| 2 | The candidates 候选人 | 正面品质形容词 | 群像（受奖者的美德） |
| 3 | The debate 评议 | 嘉奖动词 + 认同名词 | 冲突与表决（从质疑到一致） |
| 4 | The ceremony 典礼 | 赞誉名词 + 誓言名词 | 情感高潮（掌声与誓言） |
| 5 | The closing 尾声 | 剩余词 + 升华 | 收束主题（善意的回响） |

**设计要点**：把 `an agile mind`、`a daring airman`、`an outstanding success`、`a plausible excuse`、`resolute refusal` 等搭配直接写进情节；`consensus / conviction / persuasion / veneration / vitality / pledge / vow` 等名词成为颁奖词中的"关键词"。正面态度词在多幕中反复出现，形成"由个人品性到集体认同"的递进。

## 二、英文故事　The Festival of Good Deeds

### 1. The nomination

On a **brisk** morning Mrs. Cora took a **brisk walk** to the town hall to plan the Festival of Good Deeds. As an **affable** chair with an **affectionate** manner, she greeted everyone **courteously** and was **acquainted** with most of the nominees and **conversant** with their stories. "It is **advisable**," she said, "to begin with **civility** and end with **certainty**." Mr. Hale, a **plainspoken** clerk and an **accommodating person**, agreed **absolutely**. He **formulated** a short list and **highlighted** the **favorable** reports, but no one **alleged** any favoritism; **doubtless** the choice would be fair. Their **convivial meeting** was warm, and even the **hypersensitive** poet smiled. Cora **clapped** her hands to call for order.

### 2. The candidates

The nominees were a remarkable lot. There was Dr. Vale, an **acclaimed** surgeon and an **accomplished musician**, whose **agile** hands and **agile mind** had saved lives, and who was as **intrepid** as any **intrepid explorer**. Then came Mrs. Lin, a **charitable** woman with a **congenial** soul and a **courtly** grace; she was **outgoing**, had an **outgoing personality**, and was **supportive** — the **favorite** of every child. A **daring airman**, **aspiring** and **enterprising**, had flown into a storm; a **disinterested** adviser gave **a disinterested advice**; an **objective** engineer, **resolute** and **thrifty**, had rebuilt the bridge with **resolute refusal** of all shortcuts. The **passionate** teacher delivered a **passionate speech**, **zealous** and **vibrant**, and offered a **plausible excuse** for every late pupil; the **faithful** old gardener, **humorous** and **glamorous** in his own way, kept the park green.

### 3. The debate

The committee debated long. Mr. Hale **asserted** that the airman deserved **recognition**; the mayor **consented** to the plan; another member **approved** it, and Cora **confirmed** the list. She **commended** the gardener, **acclaimed** the surgeon, and **applauded** the teacher; the whole room **applauded** too. A quiet man **implied** that the engineer had cut corners, but Cora **handled** the matter gently. "Do not **generalize**," she warned; "**identify** the facts." She **convinced** the doubters with **persuasion** and **devotion** to duty, and rose to **recognize** an old friend in the crowd. She **granted** a request to speak, **intended** to **enhance** the festival, said the town **needed** every hand, and noted that the rules **permitted** it; her sincerity **inclined** everyone to trust her. They reached **consensus** on her **cue**.

### 4. The ceremony

At the ceremony the hall was **vibrant** with **applause**. The mayor, an **optimist** by nature, spoke of **admiration** and **veneration**, of **gentility** and **vitality**, and saw a **favorable** future for the town. The honorees took a **pledge** and made a **vow** to serve; their **conviction** was plain to see. Cora **clapped** again, and the **outstanding success** of the evening was already certain.

### 5. The closing

As dusk fell, Cora **recommended** the list to the council and **affirmed** that the festival had been **absolutely** fair. The **objective** truth was that goodwill had **enhanced** the town. "An **outgoing** person," she said, "is a **favorite** everywhere; a **supportive** neighbor is a **vow** kept." The crowd gave a final **applause**, the **pledge** was signed, and **doubtless** the memory of that **convivial** day would last for years.

## 四、学习建议

1. **正面词按"人—事—言"归类**：`affable / affectionate / congenial / outgoing / supportive` 形容待人；`agile / intrepid / daring / resolute / zealous` 形容做事；`plainspoken / passionate / plausible / courteously` 形容言谈。
2. **嘉奖动词链**：`acclaim → applaud → clap → commend → confirm → approve → consent → grant → recommend` 是"从称赞到批准"的完整链条，故事把它们串在评议幕里，建议整体记忆。
3. **认同名词链**：`admiration → recognition → applause → veneration → conviction → pledge → vow` 从"内心钦佩"走向"公开承诺"，是正面态度的情感梯度。
4. **搭配优先**：`an agile mind`、`a daring airman`、`an outstanding success`、`resolute refusal` 是高频考点，建议整块背诵。
5. **动词 need 补句**：`need` 在故事中以过去式 needed 之外的形式出现较少，可另造 "The town felt a **need** for celebration." 与 "We **need** every hand." 加深印象。
