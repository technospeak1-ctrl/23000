## 一、编排逻辑

本主题是"环境保护"，词量超大（100 词），涵盖荒芜地貌、污染毒害、灾害气候、保护修复、生态物种五大语义场，最易写成环保术语表。我用"一位生态学家调查并拯救一条污染河谷"作主线，按"荒谷调查 → 河流中毒 → 洪灾与预报 → 协同修复 → 河谷复生"五幕推进，把每个词与搭配落到生态考察的具体动作上：

| 幕 | 场景 | 承载语义场 | 叙事功能 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | A valley in decline 荒谷调查 | 荒芜/贫瘠/聚落词 | 建立场景与危机 |
| 2 | The river turns sour 河流中毒 | 污染/毒害/腐蚀词 | 矛盾激化 |
| 3 | The floods and the forecast 洪灾与预报 | 灾害/气候/预测词 | 灾难高潮 |
| 4 | A cooperative restoration 协同修复 | 保护/修复/资源词 | 应对危机 |
| 5 | The valley heals 河谷复生 | 生态/物种/结语词 | 点题升华 |

**设计要点**：`desolate / barren / sparse / meagre / scant` 与 `a desolate landscape / barren land / sparse population / meagre resource / be scant of` 集中在"荒谷调查"一幕，铺陈贫瘠底色；`pollutant / contaminant / poison / taint / corrode / erode` 与 `noxious gas / poisonous gas / sticky climate / unpredictable weather` 落在"河流中毒"的具体污染动作上；`disastrous flood / landslide / inundate / devastation / havoc / jeopardy` 集中在"洪灾与预报"，配合 `climatologist / forecast / cyclic` 形成因果；`conserve / preserve / reserve / recycle / aseptic / nontoxic / spotless / pristine` 放进"协同修复"；`ecological balance / endangered species / symbiosis / viability` 在结尾点题。超大词量按"地貌—污染—灾害—保护—生态"五条语义线分层承载，避免清单化。

## 二、英文故事　The Valley That Healed

### 1. A valley in decline

Dr. Lena Vale, an **ecologist**, arrived to survey the **environment** of the **desolate** valley. The **ecology** of the place had collapsed: where a **precious** forest once stood, **a desolate landscape** of **barren land** now stretched to the horizon. **A nomadic tribe** with a **sparse population** had long survived on **meagre resource**, and the camps began to **be scant of** clean water. A **gloomy** sky hung over **deserted** farms, and the **season** felt like **a severe winter** that would never end. Lena had come for **environmental** research and to plan **conservation**. She knew that **ecological balance** could be broken in a single year.

### 2. The river turns sour

The **contamination** began upstream. A factory started to **emit** **a noxious gas** and **a poisonous gas**; the air grew **noisome** and the **ozone** overhead thinned. **Pollutant** after **pollutant** entered the stream, and a **contaminant** settled on the **surface** of the water. Workers feared the waste would **contaminate** the springs, and it did: the river was **polluted**, its **pollution** plain to see. The **poison** spread, and the **poisonous** algae **tainted** every **frond** and every **underwater** shoot. **Underwater plants** that had thrived now **vanished**. The **degradation** was **evident**, and **deterioration** set in as water quality **deteriorated**: **acid** rain began to **corrode** the stone and **erode** the banks. The **erosion** exposed the roots, and a **shortage** of clean water became a real **dearth**. Even the **sticky climate** turned **unpredictable**, with **unpredictable weather** arriving without warning.

### 3. The floods and the forecast

Then came **a disastrous flood**. A **landslide** blocked the valley and the river **overran** its banks to **inundate** the farms; the whole **environment** was **devastated by** the flood. The **devastation** was **ruinous** and **havoc** reigned. Officials said the valley itself was in **jeopardy**; a second storm would **imperil** every home and **endanger** the last **endangered species** of the region. The **destruction** included a **decimation** of the **scrub**, and **deforestation** had already **deforested** the slopes, so nothing held the soil. A **tsunami** of mud seemed possible. The **disaster** had been **forecast** by a **climatologist** who studied the **climate** and the **cyclic** pattern of floods. She had tried to **forecast** it, but her warnings were ignored. The **circumstance** was clear and the **condition** urgent: the **expanding** town had treated the forest as **inexhaustible**, leaving **untapped market potential** unused and no **technological innovation** to speed the **completion** of the plan.

### 4. A cooperative restoration

Lena knew the **preservation** of what remained demanded **adaptive**, **cooperative** work. The village agreed to **conserve** the **fen** and **preserve** the **sanctuary** from grazing; they would **reserve** the upper slope for the children and **recycle** waste instead of burning **trash**. A local **pioneer** who remembered the old **symbiosis** between people and forest taught the young. Workers wore **aseptic** gloves to handle the **nontoxic** seedlings, and the nursery became **spotless**. Slowly the **pristine** quality of the spring returned, and the **viability** of the water plants improved. Some **unexplored** corners of the **scrub** were left for wildlife. They worked hard to **preserve** the young trees from the **overrun** of goats, and they guarded **precious resources** for the future.

### 5. The valley heals

Within two years the **ecosystem** began to recover. Water **consumption** fell as the village **encompassed** every stream in its plan, and no one would **deplete** the springs again. Clean water was no longer **scarce**. The **ecological balance** returned; a few **endangered species** came back to the **sanctuary**, and the **precious resources** of the **fen** were safe. Lena wrote her report on the **completion** of the project: "A valley is not a warehouse of **untapped market potential**. Its **viability** depends on **conservation**, not **consumption**." The **desolate** slopes turned green, and the **pristine** spring ran **spotless** once more.

## 四、学习建议

1. **污染链**：`contamination`（污染）→ `contaminant`（污染物）→ `contaminate`（污染）→ `pollutant`（污染物）→ `pollute`（污染）→ `taint`（污染）→ `poison / poisonous`（毒），按"来源—物质—动作—结果"一条线记，配合 `noxious gas / poisonous gas / acid / corrode / erode` 一起记。
2. **灾害链**：`disastrous flood`（水灾）→ `landslide`（滑坡）→ `inundate`（淹没）→ `devastation / havoc / ruinous`（毁坏/浩劫/破坏性）→ `jeopardy / imperil / endanger`（危险/危及），是环保新闻的高频词族。
3. **荒芜词组**：`desolate / barren / gloomy / deserted / sparse / meagre / scant / severe` 与搭配 `a desolate landscape / barren land / sparse population / meagre resource / be scant of / severe winter` 成对记忆，描述"贫瘠荒凉"最地道。
4. **保护动作**：`conserve / preserve / reserve / recycle` 是四个核心动词；`preserve ... from ...`（保护……免受……）、`reserve ... for ...`（为……保留）两个搭配配合 `aseptic / nontoxic / spotless / pristine` 的"洁净语义"一起记。
5. **生态结语**：`ecology / ecosystem / ecological balance / symbiosis / viability / endangered species / conservation` 是环保阅读的点睛词，结尾 `consumption / encompass / deplete / scarce` 把"可持续"主题收束。
