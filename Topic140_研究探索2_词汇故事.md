## 一、编排逻辑

本主题是"研究探索 2"，词表有三条主线：一是"提出假设"的认知词（`hypothesis / assumption / postulate / deduce / infer / substantiate`），二是"测量与编码"的仪器词（`chronograph / transducer / interface / numerical code / infrared radiation`），三是"一波三折的验证"动词（`probe / correlate / subtract / surpass / quest`）。若按词性分栏会变成半张实验报告。我以"研究所接到农民关于夜空不明光点的报告，萨拉博士带队用仪器排查，最终证明它只是一只反光气球"为主线，按"报案 → 仪器 → 野外之夜 → 分析 → 结论"五幕推进，让每个术语都成为一次假设、一架仪器或一轮计算：

| 幕 | 场景 | 承载语义场 | 叙事功能 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The call 报案 | 假设词 + 起源词 + 评估名词 | 建立悬念与科学态度 |
| 2 | The instruments 仪器 | 光学电子名词 + 数字编码 + 测量动词 | 铺陈实验室装备 |
| 3 | The field night 野外之夜 | 探测动词 + 推断/计算词 + 搭配 | 用一次观测串起动作 |
| 4 | The analysis 分析 | 认知动词 + 结论名词 + 搭配 | 把"证伪"写成推理链 |
| 5 | The verdict 结论 | 剩余抽象词 + 求证收束 | 收束、点题、回收搭配 |

**设计要点**：12 条搭配全部进入情节——`UFO (unidentified flying object)` 是报案原话，`approach to science` 是萨拉的口头禅，`infrared radiation / numerical code / dilute hydrochloric acid` 是实验台上真实存在的东西，`infer from / correlate with / subtract...from / beyond compute` 构成夜间推算的四步，`facilitate one's work / illustrate the point / reflect on` 落在带新人与写报告的场景。`unsubstantiated` 与 `substantiate` 同框对照，`unidentified` 首尾呼应，避免把科技术语写成词条清单。

## 二、英文故事　The Light over the Fields

### 1. The call

At the institute the call came at midnight: a farmer had seen an **unidentified** light over the fields. He called it a **UFO** — an **unidentified flying object** — but Dr. Sara Vale refused to call it a **fantasy**. She had spent her career studying the **genesis** of strange lights, and she knew the **originator** of any good report was usually an ordinary witness. A flicker the farmer described as a **precursor** had come ten minutes before the main glow. "We need a **hypothesis**, not an **assumption**," she told the **panel**. She **hypothesized** that a weather balloon or an **aviation** beacon was behind it, and asked a young colleague to **approach** the problem calmly. "That is our **approach to science**," she said. A **chronograph** on her desk would time the flashes, and she trusted its **accuracy** more than the farmer's memory. Her first **prediction** was simple: if it returned, it would return at the same **frequency**. The farmer's **error** about the colour, she said, was a small **error** that could be checked.

### 2. The instruments

In the optics lab Sara **utilized** every tool at hand. A **laser** beam, a **microscope** slide, a **transducer** that turned sound into voltage, and a bank of **infrared** sensors formed the **configuration** of the rig. **Infrared radiation** from the object, if it existed, would leave a trace. The **interface** on the screen showed a **numerical code** in rows; Sara **encoded** the coordinates and asked a programmer to **decode** them. The team had **computerized** the old **database** and kept a **clone** of it for safety, so that every reading could be logged. They prepared **dilute hydrochloric acid** to clean a small metal fragment. Sara checked the **graduation** of the scale and **magnified** the trace until the **frequency** line was clear. "The **interface** is only a window," she said; "the **module** behind it does the work." A **refinement** to the **optics** — a better lens — cut the noise. She **updated** the software and wrote a one-page guide to **facilitate** their work.

### 3. The field night

On the third night the team **explored** the field on foot. They **probed** the darkness with a **laser** rangefinder and **attempted** to **obtain** a clean sample of the residue; under a hedge they **unearthed** a thin strip of foil. A second flicker **preceded** the main flash, just as the farmer had said. **Correlating** the **chronograph** marks **with** the **infrared** readings, Sara began to **infer** that the object was not flying at all. **Inferring from** the slow drift, she concluded it was a rising balloon. She had to **subtract** the wind's effect **from** the raw speed: "**Subtract** the wind **from** the reading," she wrote. The corrected number only **approximated** to the facts, but it was close. "This is **beyond compute** if we guess," she said, "so we **compute** it." She **simulated** the flight a hundred times on her laptop, and each **simulation** **surpassed** the last in detail.

### 4. The analysis

Back at the institute the **discovery** was written up. Sara would **concoct** no story; she would **construe** the data honestly. A colleague tried to **devise** a clever **fusion** of radar and **infrared** readings, but Sara **contrived** a simpler test. The light did not **possess** any engine of its own. The farmer had **imitated** the flash with a pocket mirror, she **deduced**, and she **postulated** that the "UFO" was a balloon reflecting a car's headlights. To **illustrate** the point she drew a diagram: "Let me **illustrate the point**," she said, and asked the team to **reflect on** their own eagerness to believe. The **refinement** of the **prediction** came from **utilizing** all the old logs. Her report would **substantiate** the quiet explanation, and she knew the figures **surpassed** anything a **fantasy** could offer. The **advance** was small but real: a method, not a myth.

### 5. The verdict

The final report contained no **unsubstantiated** claims. Sara liked to **quest** for the dull truth: "We **quest** for facts, not wonders," she said. The **error** in the first **assumption** had been corrected; the **accuracy** of the **chronograph** settled it. The **unidentified** light was now a balloon, the **prediction** confirmed, the **discovery** logged in the **database**. For the **originator** of the report, the farmer, Sara left a copy. The **eternal** sky still held **unidentified** things, but our **approach to science** needs no **fantasy** to **illustrate the point**. She **updated** the file, **utilized** every **specimen**, and went home under a sky that looked, at last, ordinary.

## 四、学习建议

1. **"假设—验证"动词链**：`hypothesize → postulate → deduce → infer from → correlate with → subtract from → substantiate`，正是萨拉排查不明光点的全过程；把 `assumption / hypothesis / prediction` 三个名词挂在这条链上一起记。
2. **仪器一条线**：`chronograph → laser → microscope → transducer → infrared radiation → interface → module → numerical code`，按"计时—照射—观察—转换—感光—显示—处理—编码"的顺序走一遍实验室，比背单词表牢得多。
3. **代码与计算词族**：`encode / decode / numerical code / compute / computerize / beyond compute / subtract / approximate` 都跟"数字"有关；注意 `compute`（计算）与 `computerize`（计算机化）词性不同。
4. **正反对照**：`unidentified / unsubstantiated`（未确认、未证实）与 `substantiate / prediction confirmed`（证实、确认）首尾呼应；`fantasy` 与 `approach to science` 代表两种态度，故事里形成鲜明对照。
5. **12 条搭配按幕归堆**：报案（`UFO / approach to science`）→ 仪器（`dilute hydrochloric acid / infrared radiation / numerical code`）→ 野外（`infer from / correlate with / subtract...from / beyond compute`）→ 分析（`facilitate one's work / illustrate the point / reflect on`），按画面 recall。
