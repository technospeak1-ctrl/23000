## 一、编排逻辑

本主题围绕"反面论调"，可归为 4 个语义场：否定批判形容词、争议与不满名词、反驳反对动词、负面评价搭配。我用"小镇议会围绕一项计划的激烈辩论"作主线，按"质疑开场 → 激烈交锋 → 反驳与抵制 → 不欢而散"推进，让反面词在攻防中成组出现，与 Topic038 的正面论调形成镜像：

| 幕 | 场景 | 承载语义场 | 叙事功能 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Doubt and doubt 质疑开场 | 怀疑/否定形容词 + 怀疑名词 | 建立质疑基调 |
| 2 | Bitter exchanges 激烈交锋 | 敌意形容词 + 不满名词 | 展示冲突升级 |
| 3 | Rebuttal and resistance 反驳抵制 | 反驳动词 + 反对名词 | 展示攻防动作 |
| 4 | A bitter aftermath 苦涩收场 | 剩余负面词 + 升华 | 收尾反思 |

**设计要点**：把"怀疑"类（doubtful、dubious、sceptical、questionable、plausible）与"敌意"类（hostile、bitter、nasty、ruthless、wanton、provocative）分层铺陈；`a controversial question`、`defective goods`、`hostile attack`、`be jealous of sb.`、`be skeptical about`、`wanton destruction` 等搭配承担关键表达；反驳动词按 disagree → dissent → dispute → refute → disprove → demolish 形成递进。

## 二、英文故事　The Bitter Debate

### 1. A questionable plan

The council faced **a controversial question**: a **controversial** plan to rebuild the market. From the start the mood was **doubtful** and **dubious**. "I am **be doubtful about** its costs," said one member, "and **sceptical** of its promises — **be skeptical about** the numbers." Another called it a **plausible** but **improbable** scheme, a **questionable** use of funds, and an **unfair** deal — **disproportionate** to the need and **disparate** from the town's real **diversity**. The **discrepancy** in the budget was **obscure**, the **diversity** of opinion **divergent** — a **diverse** and **adversarial** room. A **meticulous** clerk noted that the **objection**s were **disputable** at best and called the figures **defective**, **incompatible** with the town's accounts. The mayor, **reluctant** to speak, made **a dubious reply** and gave **a negative answer/reply** to the request for more time. The **denial** **disappoint**ed everyone — a **disappointing** start. Already the **backlash** was forming, with a **murmur** of discontent and an **injustice** no one would **deny**.

### 2. Bitter exchanges

The debate turned **bitter** and **hostile**. A **bibulous** councillor known for **excessive drinking** made **bitter remarks**; a **pompous** one gave a **facetious** speech that was **pointless** and **embarrassing**. The noise **annoy**ed and **disturb**ed everyone. Another's **ill-mannered**, **inconsiderate** outburst was **uncalled-for** — **an uncalled-for criticism** that left a young member **embarrassed**. There were **hostile attack**s and **provocative comment**s; a **jealous** rival — **be jealous of sb.** — spread **envy** and **spite**. A **braggart** boasted, a **serpent** of a man whispered **wanton destruction** threats, and a **nasty** joke about **a nasty room** silenced the gallery. The **controversy** grew; **criticism** and **complaint** piled up, and **discord** filled the hall. When a **disaster** of a decision was proposed, the **paradox** was plain: a plan meant to help had become **detrimental**, with a **detrimental effect** on morale. The **disaster** of mismanagement **deteriorate**d the mood further.

### 3. Rebuttal and resistance

Members began to **disagree with** the plan and to **dissent** openly. Their opinions **diverge**d — **diverge from** the official line — and a **disparate** coalition formed. One **criticize**d the design, another **damn**ed it outright; a third would **deceive** no one and refused to **deceive oneself**. They **boycott**ed the vote, **oppose**d the measure, and **reject**ed **an offer** of compromise — **reject** an offer flatly. A careful member tried to **refute** the claims and **refute an opponent**, then **disprove** the costings and **demolish** the whole argument; he **defy**ied the chair, **decentralize**d the committee's power, and **underestimate**d no opponent. But a **ruthless ruler** of a chairman — **a ruthless ruler** in manner — would **damn** every **dissent** and **blame** the critics for the **neglect** of the town. He **vex**ed them all, **humiliate**d the young member, and **frown**ed at every **objection**. **Otherwise**, he warned, the plan would **disrupt the agricultural economy**. The **resistance** hardened; a **spurn** of his authority was in the air.

### 4. A bitter aftermath

The motion failed, and the **opposition** claimed **victory** — a **negative** sort of triumph. The **controversy** left **disparate subjects** scattered across the table and **divergent opinions** unresolved. A **disaster** for the mayor, the defeat was **detrimental** to his standing. In the corridor a **spurn**ed ally refused to speak; a **serpent**-tongued adviser **suspect**ed everyone — **suspect sb. of (doing) sth.** — of **deceit**. The critic who had **disprove**d the budget walked away **uncalled-for** criticism behind him. It was a **wanton** waste of a morning, an **absurd**, **unthinkable** way to run a town. As the **diversity** of views slowly settled, one thing was **unlikely** to change: the **contempt** and **scorn** in which the plan was held. "We **disagree with** the method, not the town," a member said at last — and even that **plausible** excuse sounded **unlikely** in the cold evening air.

## 四、学习建议

1. **怀疑 vs 敌意分层**：怀疑（doubtful、dubious、sceptical、questionable、plausible、unlikely）↔ 敌意（hostile、bitter、nasty、ruthless、wanton、provocative、vicious）。辩论从怀疑升级到敌意。
2. **"分歧"词族**：disparate（毫不相干）、disproportionate（不均衡）、divergent（有分歧）、diverse（多样）、discrepancy（差异）、discord（不和）——都含"不同"语义但侧重各异。
3. **反驳动词递进**：disagree → dissent → diverge → criticize → damn → refute → disprove → demolish，力度逐步加强，是辩论攻防的动词链。
4. **搭配优先**：`a controversial question`、`hostile attack`、`be skeptical about`、`an uncalled-for criticism`、`wanton destruction` 是高频搭配。
5. **词形意识**：过去式与被动（disagreed、deteriorated、demolished、decentralized、defied、disproved、diverged、refuted、rejected、suspected、underestimated、vexed、humiliated、opposed）要会认；注意 disprove→disproved、refute→refuted 的拼写。
