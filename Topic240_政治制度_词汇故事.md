## 一、编排逻辑

本主题是"政治制度"，政体名称、统治者和官僚程序词密集，最容易写成政治学名词表。我用"灰厅帝国在宪政改革中的一年"为主线，按"旧秩序 → 议会辩论 → 法令与官僚机器 → 军队与代价 → 新宪章"五幕推进，让每个词落在政体转型的具体环节上：谁掌权、谁进议会、谁起草法令、谁执行、谁付出代价。

| 幕 | 场景 | 承载语义场 | 叙事功能 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The twilight of the old empire 帝国暮色 | 政体/统治者/贵族形容词 | 建立旧秩序 |
| 2 | The parliament convenes 议会开幕 | 议会/政体方案/煽动政治 | 引出改革冲突 |
| 3 | Edicts and paperwork 法令与文书 | 法令/机构/官僚动词 | 推进制度改革 |
| 4 | The field and the price 军队与代价 | 军队/战利品/衰败词 | 揭示改革成本 |
| 5 | A charter at last 新宪章 | 认同/献身/收束动词 | 点题完成 |

**设计要点**：`empire / imperial / king / throne / princess / aristocracy` 集中第一幕铺陈帝制；`despot / dictator / autocracy / oligarch / gerontocracy / potentate` 沿"暴君—独裁—寡头—老人政治"的权力退化链分布；第二幕把 `parliament / president / confederate / Marxist / capitalism` 放进议会辩论，`demagogue / candidacy / xenophobe` 落在竞选煽动场景；第三幕让 `edict / fiat / assize / affidavit / bureau / functionary` 沿公文流转，`enjoin / exile / gerrymander / immure / conscript` 落到执行环节；`degradation / deformation / infelicity / nadir / juggernaut` 等负面词集中在第四幕的衰败现场；`habituate / devotion / nationality` 在收束幕完成"从臣民到公民"的语义转折。

## 二、英文故事　The Last Edict at Greyhall

### 1. The twilight of the old empire

For three centuries the **empire** had been ruled from Greyhall. Every **imperial** chronicler told the same tale: a **king** upon the **throne**, a **princess** at his side, and an **aristocracy** that still swore by old **feudal** oaths. Chancellor Vane had once knelt as a **knight** in the royal guard; now he served a **gerontocracy** of tired men who could agree on nothing. The **potentate** whom the people had once praised as a stern **despot** had slowly become a **dictator**, and his **autocracy** leaned on six **oligarch** houses. Their **clannish** doors were closed to talent, and their **overbearing** manners made every guest feel small. "This **congruous** order has outlived its purpose," Vane said **distinctly** to the council. A **wry** look passed around the table. A **vitriolic** leaflet called the whole court **inane**, a **vindictive** cousin muttered about old grudges, and the **sexual** gossip of the palace, together with the **vicarious** loyalty of hired champions, only hastened the decay. The **interrogative** look on the princess's face told Vane that she had already made up her mind to **abjure** the old ways.

### 2. The parliament convenes

In April the **parliament** was summoned to debate the future. A **Marxist** delegate rose first and demanded an end to **capitalism**; a merchant answered that free trade was the engine of **improvement**. Others wanted a **confederate** republic with a **president** chosen by the counties. Into this argument stepped Corbin, a **demagogue** whose **pretension** to speak for the poor drew cheers from the gallery. He announced his **candidacy** with another **vitriolic** speech against the old houses. A **xenophobe** in the back row shouted that **colonist** settlers from abroad would steal their land. The debate reached its **nadir** when a **pyromania** scare was used to frighten the voters, and Vane saw that fear, not reason, had become the **catalyst** of the whole crisis.

### 3. Edicts and paperwork

The council worked quickly. A royal **edict** was read aloud, followed by a **fiat** issued at the **headquarters** of the guard. Clerks in the **bureau** copied every **affidavit**; a **functionary** in the **department** of justice carried the papers to the **assize**. The old **consul** arrived from the **municipality** to act as **intercessor** between the two sides. Vane urged the council to **enjoin** any further violence and to **exile** the worst offenders. When a map of the voting districts was **gerrymandered** to favour the oligarchs, he warned that the generals would **mishandle** the election. A **conscript** army was ordered to **march** at dawn; men who refused were **immured** in the **jail** at Greyhall and their families held under **duress**. The **divestiture** of the great estates was demanded, but the **decision** was delayed: would one signature **suffice** to make the transfer legal? The uncertainty began to **unsettle** the city, and the **confirmation** of the new charter had to wait.

### 4. The field and the price

At dawn the **battalion** moved out, every **weapon** inspected twice. The soldiers spoke little of **booty**; they had seen too much **deviltry** in the last campaign to expect glory. Along the road they passed villages in **degradation**, their walls showing the **deformation** of years of neglect. A sergeant noted the **infelicity** of the orders: no one had said what to do if the crowd resisted. The rumour of a **juggernaut**, a huge machine rolling towards the capital, spread through the ranks, and in the **depth** of night even brave men grew quiet. Vane understood the **vicissitude** of fortune better than any of them; he had been a favourite of the court and now was its gaoler. When a **deserter** was brought back in chains, he did not shout. He only asked whether the new charter would remember men like him.

### 5. A charter at last

By summer the charter was signed. The **coronation** of the young **princess** was quietly cancelled; in its place came the inauguration of a **president** chosen by the **parliament**. The streets, which had flirted with **anarchy** in April, were calm, and the **fustian** of the old court was heard no more. Vane now argued that civic **devotion**, not **sex** or birthright, should be the test of **nationality**: anyone who swore to the charter belonged to the commonwealth. He had watched a whole city **habituate** itself to fear under the old **despot**, and he wanted its people to **habituate** themselves to law instead. In his last report he wrote that the **improvement** was real, that the **vicissitude** of politics had left deep scars, and that a just **decision** had at last been **congruous** with the patience of ordinary men.

## 四、学习建议

1. **权力退化链**：`king` / `aristocracy` → `despot` → `dictator` → `autocracy` → `oligarch` → `gerontocracy`，按"君主—暴君—独裁—寡头—老人政治"的顺序记，正好是一条政体衰变主线。
2. **公文流转链**：`edict` / `fiat`（命令）→ `affidavit`（宣誓书）→ `bureau` / `department` / `functionary`（机构与官僚）→ `assize`（裁判）→ `confirmation`（批准），把冷冰冰的行政词串成一条看得见流程的线。
3. **议会与煽动**：`parliament`、`president`、`candidacy` 是制度词；`demagogue`、`pretension`、`xenophobe`、`pyromania` 是竞选乱象词，正反同框记忆最牢。
4. **执行动词**：`enjoin`（禁止）、`exile`（流放）、`conscript`（征兵）、`gerrymander`（操纵选区）、`immure`（监禁）、`mishandle`（搞砸）都落在第三幕的法令执行环节，动作感强，不易混淆。
5. **形容词成组**：`clannish` / `overbearing` / `vitriolic` / `inane` / `vindictive` / `wry` 写权力人物的嘴脸；`degradation` / `deformation` / `infelicity` / `nadir` 写制度衰败，按"人—制度"两组分别复习。
