## 一、编排逻辑

本主题围绕"经验教训"，可归为 3 个语义场：描述状态与性质的形容词副词、教训中的抽象名词、反思与改进的动词。我用老检验员 Ada 给学徒写"经验笔记"作主线，按"深夜 → 隐患 → 苦干 → 教诲 → 结语"推进：先写下开篇的心境（形容词），再复盘被掩盖的隐患（名词），继而回忆艰辛与改进（动词），最后升华出教训。

| 幕 | 场景 | 承载语义场 | 叙事功能 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The notebook 笔记 | 状态/性质形容词副词 | 建立情境（深夜写教训） |
| 2 | The hidden dangers 隐患 | 欺诈与约束名词 | 复盘事故（谎言与暴露） |
| 3 | The struggle 苦干 | 艰难与力量名词 | 群像与冲突（艰辛与阻挠） |
| 4 | The lesson 教诲 | 反思改进动词 | 核心动作（检查与克服） |
| 5 | The closing 结语 | 剩余词 + 升华 | 收束主题（胜利与教训） |

**设计要点**：把 `an anonymous letter`、`a foolproof scheme`、`insurmountable difficulty`、`a permissive attitude`、`traditional view`、`valuable merchandise` 等搭配直接写进情节；`exposure / falsehood / fraud / misrepresentation / constraint / regulation / standards / insight / strength` 等名词成为笔记中的"关键词"。动词按"适应 → 检查 → 消除 → 确保 → 克服 → 解决"链条串联。

## 二、英文故事　The Inspector's Notebook

### 1. The notebook

Old Inspector Ada stayed **awake** late, writing lessons for her apprentice in a **designed** notebook. **An anonymous letter** had arrived that morning, and she read it **deeply** troubled. The **outward** calm of the factory hid an **impenetrable** tangle of lies. "Nothing here is **foolproof**," she wrote, "and no **foolproof scheme** survives a **functional** machine's **endless** wear — not even **a functional organ** in a healthy body." She thought of the **prenatal** care a mother gives and of how a **grown-up** child should repay it. **As far as I know**, she added, no lesson is learned **for sure** until something **goes wrong**.

### 2. The hidden dangers

The first lesson was about **exposure**. A **hazard** had been hidden under a **disguise** of fresh paint; a **falsehood** in the ledger, a **fraud** in the accounts, and a **misrepresentation** of the risks had **endangered** every **laborer** in the hall. The **management** had **inadvertently** ignored the **constraint** of the budget, the **regulation** that set **standards**, and the **strict inspection** that might have caught the fault; **opposing** interests had been papered over. Much was done **in secret**. When Ada **checked** the records, the **insight** was plain: **falsehood** breeds **fraud**, and **clear profit** bought with lies is no profit at all. **Valuable merchandise** was being shipped from a rotten store. She would **disparage** no one, but she would not **confuse** a lie with a mistake, nor **leave** the matter **alone**.

### 3. The struggle

The work had been hard. Heavy crates **encumbered** the hall and **dragged** at every step; the **hardship** of those weeks **emaciated** the men and **exhausted** their **strength**. Ada remembered how a **villain** of a foreman would **override** every safety rule and **overdo** the pace, until a **frustrated** worker cried out that the difficulty was **insurmountable**. The **traditional view**, **stiff** and slow, had nevertheless held; the **permissive attitude** of the owners was the true **villain**. "A **singularly** foolish man," she wrote, "mistakes a **foolproof scheme** for safety. **Sooner or later** he pays."

### 4. The lesson

"Now listen," Ada told her apprentice. "First **adapt** to the facts; do not **mix** them up with wishes. **Check** every figure, **eliminate** every error, and **ensure** that every **constraint** is honest — **make sure** of that before you sign. A problem will **occur** where you least expect; an accident does not **exist** in the abstract. **Explore** every corner, **overhaul** every machine, and **escape** from the habit of **overdoing** things. **Overcome** your fear of hard work; **settle** the account before you sleep. **Most of all**, be **awake** to what is in front of you."

### 5. The closing

In the end the **victor** was patience, and the **villain** was haste. The ledger, **unblemished** and **unaffected** by fear, stood as **valuable** proof; the **unfounded** rumors died, and even the **unregenerate** foreman **settled** down. Ada had no patience for **an endless speech**. She wrote **phenomenally** in the margin that **overwhelmingly** the greatest lesson was this: a **confined** mind cannot **overcome** an **insurmountable difficulty**, but an **awake**, **grown-up** worker can. She read the **anonymous** letter once more and laid it down, **unaffected** by praise and **unblemished** in purpose — the quiet **victor** of a hard-won day.

## 四、学习建议

1. **教训主题按"发现—反思—改进"记**：`exposure / disclosure`（揭发）→ `falsehood / fraud / misrepresentation`（谎言欺诈）→ `insight / strength`（洞见与力量）→ `adapt / check / eliminate / ensure / overcome / settle`（改进动作）。
2. **改进动词链**：`adapt → check → eliminate → ensure → explore → overhaul → escape → overcome → settle` 是"从适应到解决"的完整链条，故事已按此顺序串联，建议整链背诵。
3. **状态形容词成组记**：`foolproof / functional`（可靠）对 `impenetrable / insurmountable / confined`（难以逾越）；`unblemished / unaffected / valuable`（正面）对 `unfounded / unregenerate`（负面）。
4. **不规则变形提醒**：`overcome → overcome / overcoming`（不用 overcame）、`overdo → overdo / overdoing`（不用 overdid）、`override → override / overriding`（不用 overrode），故事已刻意采用可识别形式。
5. **易忽略的副词与搭配**：`inadvertently`（无意中）与 `opposing`（对立的）在故事第 2 幕 "had **inadvertently** ignored…; **opposing** interests had been papered over" 中自然入文；`strict inspection`、`valuable merchandise`、`make sure of` 也已在正文出现，可直接复述巩固。
