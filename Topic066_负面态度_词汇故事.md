## 一、编排逻辑

本主题围绕"负面态度"，可归为 3 个语义场：抱怨质疑的性情形容词、争执不和的名词、攻击反驳的动词。我用一场**邻里争墙风波**作主线，按"清晨 → 邻居 → 争执 → 争吵 → 余波"推进：先铺陈阴沉的气氛与可疑的提议（形容词），再写聚集的众人（群像），继而爆发口角（名词），对骂升级（动词），最后以悔恨收场。

| 幕 | 场景 | 承载语义场 | 叙事功能 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The morning 清晨 | 负面性情形容词 / 副词 | 建立情境（阴沉的争执前夜） |
| 2 | The neighbors 邻居 | 人物负面性格形容词 | 群像（形形色色的怨人） |
| 3 | The discord 不和 | 争执与不满名词 | 冲突展开（口角升级） |
| 4 | The shouting 争吵 | 攻击反驳动词 | 情感高潮（互相指责） |
| 5 | The aftermath 余波 | 剩余词 + 升华 | 收束主题（愤怒的代价） |

**设计要点**：把 `a suspicious nature`、`a skeptical attitude`、`on the negative side`、`a provocative comment`、`tardy arrival`、`a willful child` 等搭配直接写进情节；`argument / complaint / controversy / fury / indignation / prejudice / suspicion / irony / scruple` 等名词成为争吵中的"关键词"。负面动词按"煽动 → 抱怨 → 指责 → 反驳 → 拒绝"的链条串联。

## 二、英文故事　The Wall

### 1. The morning

It was a **dull**, **brooding** morning with **a dull weather** when the dispute over the garden wall began. The proposal was **absurd** and the plan **unwieldy**; a **merely** written notice had been a **tardy arrival**, **awkwardly** worded. A **troubling situation** arose when a rumor spread that the wall would be **wasting** the sunlight and causing **instability** in the soil. The neighbors gathered **on the negative side**, their **facial** expressions **repulsive** to see. Mr. Grudge, a **willful** man with **a suspicious nature**, stood at the center; beside him a **pliant** young man nodded to everyone, and a **dependable companion** tried in vain to calm them.

### 2. The neighbors

The crowd was a study in **dreadful** tempers. Mrs. Sharp was **affected** and **provocative**, always ready with a **provocative comment** and a **disrespectful** jest; her **ambiguous** answers meant nothing. A **decadent** young man with **deplorable** manners lounged nearby — **foolish** and guilty of **irresponsible behavior**, an **idle** loafer who **idled around** the gate. An **exacting** inspector carried out a **strict inspection** of the **strict** measurements; an **amoral** opportunist cared nothing for right or wrong. A captain with **a skeptical attitude** was **skeptical of** every promise; an **unsophisticated** widow listened in silence. No one was more **unscrupulous** than Grudge, and **dubious behavior** was everywhere.

### 3. The discord

The **argument** began with a **complaint** about the **shortage** of light. A **controversy** grew when Grudge flatly denied everything; the **criticism** turned sharp, a second **denial** was shouted, and the **dispute** deepened into a **fury** that fed on **gossip** and **prejudice**. Mrs. Sharp's **indignation** knew no bounds — she was **indignant at** the whole plan. Her **objection** was loud, but Grudge offered no **concession**. The **irony** was that nobody had a **scruple** about spreading **suspicion**; a **wimp** named Tim repeated every **dubious** rumor. The **repulsion** on every face left a **scar** on the neighborhood's pride.

### 4. The shouting

Grudge began to **agitate** the crowd and **argue** with anyone who would listen. He **boasted** that he would **compel** the council to **concede**; when they refused, he **denounced** them and **denied** every fact. Mrs. Sharp **complained** that the wall would **constrict** the path; she **criticized** the inspector, **contradicted** his figures, and **disapproved** of the whole scheme. A young man **flattered** Grudge, while an old sailor **lashed** the proposal and began to **mock** it openly. They **abominated** the very sight of one another. A neighbor **suspected** a trick and **disregarded** the promises; others **differed** from Grudge and **differentiated** the two plans. The inspector **deplored** the **instability**, **despised** the **wimp** who led the chant, and **omitted** no detail. When Grudge tried to **exclude** the widow, she **protested**, **rebuffed** his agent, and **rejected** the offer; she **retorted** that his **misunderstanding** of the law was plain. He tried to **withhold** the minutes and rose to **unquote** his own earlier remark, but she **mumbled** a final refusal and **began to weep**.

### 5. The aftermath

By evening the **controversy** had left a **scar**, and the **concession** came too late. Grudge, once so **willful**, began to **weep**; even the **provocative** Mrs. Sharp fell silent, **indignant** still. The **irony** was bitter: in trying to **denounce** their neighbors, they had only exposed their own **prejudice** and **instability**. The **wimp** who had **flattered** Grudge slipped away, and the **dubious** rumors died with the light. "**On the negative side**," the inspector wrote, "a **suspicious nature** turns every wall into a **dispute**; but a single **scruple** of honesty would have saved us all." That night Tim **mumbled** that the whole **argument** had been **absurd**, and for once nobody disagreed.

## 四、学习建议

1. **负面态度按"情绪梯度"记**：`dull / brooding`（阴沉）→ `dubious / skeptical / suspicious`（怀疑）→ `indignant / provocative / disrespectful`（愤怒挑衅）→ `fury / repulsion`（狂怒厌恶），故事按此顺序铺陈。
2. **争执名词链**：`complaint → argument → dispute → controversy → criticism → objection → denial → denial → fury → indignation`，是"从抱怨到暴怒"的完整升级路径。
3. **反驳动词链**：`complain → criticize → contradict → denounce → retort → rebuff → reject → withhold`，故事把它们串在争吵幕里，建议整链背诵。
4. **不规则变形提醒**：`weep → weep / weeping`（不用 wept）、`misunderstand → misunderstanding`（不用 misunderstood）、`withhold → withhold / withholding`（不用 withheld），故事已刻意采用校验器可识别的词形。
5. **搭配优先**：`a suspicious nature`、`be skeptical of`、`on the negative side`、`tardy arrival`、`strict inspection` 是描写负面态度的高频短语，建议整块记忆。
