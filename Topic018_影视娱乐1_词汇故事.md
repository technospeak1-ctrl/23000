## 一、编排逻辑

本主题围绕"影视与音乐娱乐"，可归为 4 个语义场：声音与表演评价形容词、戏剧影视名词、音乐名词、表演动作。我用"一部音乐剧从排练到首映"作主线，按"筹备 → 舞台与演员 → 音乐 → 首映"推进：

| 幕 | 场景 | 承载语义场 | 叙事功能 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The production 制作筹备 | 评价形容词 + 戏剧名词 | 建立情境（versatile 演员加盟） |
| 2 | The cast 演员阵容 | 演员、角色、剧情名词 | 展示人物（演员与角色） |
| 3 | The music 音乐 | 乐器、乐曲名词 | 展示配乐（器乐与歌声） |
| 4 | Opening night 首映之夜 | 剩余影视名词 + 表演动词 | 收尾升华（娱乐的超越性） |

**设计要点**：把声音类词（dulcet、euphonious、musical、boisterous）与影视制作链（cast、script、scene、shot、screen、preview、rating）放进同一条制作线，形成"从剧本到银幕"的完整流程。

## 二、英文故事　Opening Night

### 1. The production

The **theater** announced a new **production** for its **centenary**. A **versatile actor** — a **virtuoso** of the stage — joined a **well-known** **actress**; a **cineaste** called it a **mysterious** **conundrum** of a **storyline**. The **setting** was a **mainstream** city, but the **narrator** promised **countless** twists. A **program** and a **poster** went up; a **photographer** devoted to **photography** took a **photo** for the **profile** in the **journal**. The **reputation** of the **cast** was **immense**, and the **nominee** list was long. "We want **euphonious music** and a **musical performance** that is **dulcet**," the director said, "not a **boisterous** din." The **magic** was in the details: a **costume** here, a **curtain** there, a **scene** that would **echo** in memory.

### 2. The cast

The **cast** was remarkable. The **actor** played the **hero**; the **actress** took the **role** of an **idol**. A **jockey**, a **substitute**, and a **narrator** filled the **segment** between **scene** and **scene**. The **libretto** and the **script** — the **storyline** in **print** — were adapted by a **nominated** writer. An **epilogue** and a **coda** were added; a **conundrum** in the **libretto** puzzled the **offstage** crew. A **magic** trick with a **wand** delighted the children; **fame** and **fun** followed the **hero**. A **pirouette** by a **ballet** dancer and a **jockey**'s leap made the **gamut** of the **show**. The **photo** of the **cast** ran in every **picture** paper, and the **profile** of the **virtuoso** **idol** drew a crowd.

### 3. The music

The **composition** was a **musical** marvel. A **quartet** of a **piano**, a **harpsichord**, a **bassoon**, and a **carillon** played; a **percussionist** kept time. A **duet** and a **tenor** **song** rose, followed by an **elegy** and a **psalm**. A **carol** and a **hymn** — **paean** and **ode** — celebrated the **hero**; the **note** of the **tenor** was **dulcet** and **euphonious**. The **coda** **echoed** the **opening**; the **music** **transcended** the **setting**. A **record** of the **score** and a **tape** of the **rehearsal** were sold in the lobby. Even the **offstage** **carillon** and the **cinematograph** of the old **centenary** played a **song** of **imagination**. The **composition** and the **chord** of the **quartet** made the **musical performance** **euphonious** indeed.

### 4. Opening night

On **opening night** the **curtain** rose. The **screen** — actually a painted **backdrop** — showed a **shot** of the **setting**; the **scene** was **mysterious**, the **storyline** **countless** in its turns. The **actor** and **actress** **transcended** themselves; the **narrator**'s **commentary** **echoed** through the hall. A **nominee** was **nominated** for best **actor**; the **cast** was **recast** when one **substitute** fell ill. The **preview** had promised **versatility** and a **rock** concert, and the **rating** soared. The **film** was **screened** in the **theater**, and the **picture** of the **idol** hung on the **poster**. I thought of the **imagination** behind the **magic**, the **euphonious** **music**, the **dulcet** **coda**, and the **fame** that **transcends** the **role**. The **entertainment** was **countless** small **pleasures** — a **wand**, a **pirouette**, a **note**, a **curtain** — woven into a single **transcending** night.

## 四、学习建议

1. 按制作流程记：筹备（production、poster、program、reputation）→ 演员（cast、actor、actress、hero、role、substitute）→ 音乐（quartet、piano、bassoon、duet、tenor、coda）→ 首映（screen、shot、scene、preview、rating）。
2. 声音词成组：dulcet、euphonious、musical（悦耳）↔ boisterous（喧闹）。
3. 搭配优先：`a versatile actor`、`euphonious music`、`musical performance` 与动词 `echo`、`transcend`、`nominate`、`recast`、`screen` 是高频考点。
4. 词形意识：动词过去式/被动要会认（nominated、recast、screened、echoed、transcended）。
