## 一、编排逻辑

本主题是"合作谈判"，82 个词项横跨谈判人物、往来文书、冲突状态与交涉动作四个层面。若按词性平铺，必然沦为"谈判术语清单"。我以"一家小企业的女创始人安娜，与巨头哈洛集团围绕净水器合作展开一场从紧张对峙到握手签约的谈判"为主线，按"会前准备 → 首次会谈 → 陷入僵局 → 密室转机 → 落笔签约"五幕推进，让每个词都成为谈判桌上的一个人、一份文件或一句话：

| 幕 | 场景 | 承载语义场 | 叙事功能 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The eve of the parley 会前准备 | 谈判人物 + 文书物品 + 目标与准入词 | 建立人物、任务与筹码 |
| 2 | The first dialogue 首次会谈 | 交涉动作 + 冲突/含糊状态形容词 | 用一次交锋带出负面状态词 |
| 3 | The logjam 陷入僵局 | 升级/僵局名词 + 强硬动作动词 | 把冲突推到顶点 |
| 4 | The backroom brainstorm 密室转机 | 协作动词 + 联合/一致名词 + 活泼形容词 | 由对抗转向合作 |
| 5 | The engrossment 落笔签约 | 收束动词 + 准入/结论名词 | 点题并完成契约 |

**设计要点**：人物词 `entrepreneur / deputy / coordinator / proxy / profiteer` 分别落到安娜、黑尔、克拉拉、派克与昔日奸商身份；文书词 `folio / addendum / holograph / missive / engrossment / nib / acknowledgement` 作为谈判桌上被翻阅、被涂改、被签署的实物；负面状态词 `contentious / excursive / imprudent / uncooperative / ambiguous(ambiguity) / incoherence` 集中在前两幕的对峙里，正面词 `sprightly / stouthearted / reparable / rapport / coalescence` 留给后两幕的转机；16 条搭配全部落进具体动作——`protract the negotiations` 是拖延战术，`draw on` 是凭经验画方案，`enclose...in / with the letter` 是装卷宗与附标书，`settle a dispute / settle up` 是解争议与结费用，`submit to / submit oneself to` 是服从条款与接受年审，`clinch a bargain` 收在签约一幕。

## 二、英文故事　The Bargain at Harrow House

### 1. The eve of the parley

Anna Reyes was an **entrepreneur** who ran a small green-tech firm, and she had one **aspiration**: to win a **partnership** with the giant Harrow Group. Her **mission** was to bring cheap water filters to remote villages, and she believed a fair deal was **accessible** if she could show real value. At dawn she was already **punctual** at the Harrow offices, her **laptop** open and a new **nib** in her pen. Her **deputy**, Marcus Hale, was less hopeful; he was a **contentious** man who had once been a **profiteer** in the scarce-parts market, and he feared Harrow would **outmaneuver** them. Clara, the **coordinator** Harrow had sent, was **punctilious** about every detail: she checked the **composition** of the visiting team, the **offer** in the first **folio**, and the **proposition** on pricing in the **addendum**. Mr. Pike, a **proxy** for an absent investor, sat quietly with a sealed **missive** and a **holograph** from the chairman. Anna had run a quick **errand** to the print shop for the **acknowledgement** of receipt and the signed **engrossment** of their terms. On the whiteboard she sketched the **establishment** of a joint lab and a **fellowship** of young engineers. "This is our **dealing**," she told Marcus, "open and fair." Then Clara opened her notebook to keep the **minutes**.

### 2. The first dialogue

The **parley** began at nine, and at once the air grew cool. Hale **represented** the smaller firm but refused to **meet** Harrow's first price. Harrow's lead, Mr. Vale, was **uncooperative**; he **expressed** doubt and called the proposal **imprudent**. His questions were **excursive**, wandering from price to patents and back, and soon the **dialogue** was full of **ambiguity**. Anna asked for **clarification**, but Vale's answers slid into **incoherence**. Clara's pen kept the record, and she noted the **erasure** where someone had scrubbed a figure; in the margin she **scrawled** a sharp question. Anna had a **hunch** that Vale wanted a longer term, not a lower price, so she chose to **advance** a new idea: a shared patent. "We **rely on** each other for this," she said. But Vale would not **embark** on any joint work that day. He **demonstrated** his power by citing other deals, then moved to **adjourn** the meeting until Friday. As they **negotiated**, Anna felt the **animation** drain from the room.

### 3. The logjam

By Friday the mood had hardened into a **logjam**. Vale **protracted the negotiations** with an **interminable** list of small objections, and each side called the other's terms **irreconcilable**. A rumor spread that a rival buyer had **enclosed** a higher bid **with the letter**, and that the **escalation** was no accident. Worry **environed** the room like smoke. Hale wanted to **desist** and walk away, but Anna was **stouthearted**. "The gap is **reparable**," she said. **Drawing on** her years of field work, she sketched a new **contract** on a napkin; she **formulated some regulations** that would **simplify** the licensing and **meet the needs of** both sides. She **submitted** the sheet to Clara, who **enclosed** it **in** a folder marked "Confidential." But Vale **rescinded** his earlier promise of a joint lab, and the room fell into a long silence. Anna **summarized** her position in one sentence, yet Vale kept **drawing** small circles on the paper and said nothing. Even the **proceedings** seemed to drag.

### 4. The backroom brainstorm

That night Anna called a **brainstorm** in a small back room. "We must **collaborate**, not fight," she told the group. She asked each person to **coordinate with** the others and to **demonstrate her ability** with one concrete number. Even Mr. Pike, the quiet **proxy**, offered a **sprightly** idea: let villages pay in small monthly sums. A telegram said a few angry villagers had **demonstrated against** the water price, so the plan had to be fair. Anna **impelled** the team to **accommodate** that concern. In the morning she called Vale to **meet with** the board, and this time she brought a film of the villages; she **demonstrated** how the filters worked, and the **concurrence** of the engineers was immediate. A **coalescence** of views began: both sides saw a shared future. Vale, once so cold, now smiled and asked Anna to **settle a dispute** over the patent between their lawyers. The room was full of a new **rapport**.

### 5. The engrossment

On the final morning the **engrossment** was laid on the table, and Harrow granted Anna full **admission** to their supplier network. Vale **concluded** the deal in a single sentence, and the **conclusion** everyone had hoped for was at last written into the **contract**. Anna **clinched a bargain** that gave villages a fair price and Harrow a steady market. The lawyers **submitted to** the terms and **submitted themselves to** a yearly review; the two firms **settled up** their legal fees before lunch. Clara, who had kept every **minute**, filed the last **addendum**. Hale, the once **contentious** deputy, now spoke warmly of the joint lab, and even Vale's face lost its cold edge. "Our **aspiration** was never to defeat each other," Anna said. "It was to build something that lasts." The **coalescence** of two proud firms into one lasting **partnership** was complete, and every **errand** of those long weeks had been worth it.

## 四、学习建议

1. **按谈判流程记词**：会前 `entrepreneur / deputy / coordinator / proxy / addendum / engrossment` → 交锋 `contentious / excursive / imprudent / ambiguity / incoherence` → 僵局 `logjam / escalation / interminable / irreconcilable / protract` → 转机 `brainstorm / collaborate / coalescence / concurrence / rapport` → 签约 `clinch a bargain / conclude / settle up / admission`。按幕回忆，比孤立背释义牢固。
2. **文书词成串**：`folio → addendum → holograph → missive → engrossment → acknowledgement → minutes`，正是从草稿、附件、亲笔信、公函到正式签署文件与会议记录的一条文书链；`nib` 与 `erasure / scrawl` 同框，记住"笔尖—涂改—潦草写"的画面。
3. **近义与反义同框**：`negotiation / parley / dialogue` 都指"会谈"，`partnership / fellowship / coalescence / rapport` 都指"联合或融洽"；`reparable`（可补救）与 `irreconcilable`（不可调和）、`punctual / punctilious`（守时/谨小慎微）形成对照，故事里分别落在对手身上与合作达成之后。
4. **搭配优先记动词框架**：`protract the negotiations / draw on / enclose...in / enclose...with / formulate some regulations / meet with / meet the needs of / rely on / settle a dispute / settle up / submit to / submit oneself to / clinch a bargain` 共 16 条，全部嵌在人物动作里， recall 时按"谁对谁做了什么"取词。
5. **冷僻词留痕**：`engrossment`（正式缮写的文件）、`holograph`（亲笔信）、`folio`（一页）、`missive`（公函）、`nib`（笔尖）、`profiteer`（奸商）较书面，均在正文各出现至少一次，结合谈判桌上的实物记忆其行业色彩。
