> 覆盖：名词 37 个 + 动词 18 个。

## 一、编排逻辑

本主题是"任务委派"，核心是一批与"分派工作、明确职责、执行汇报"相关的名词与动词。我用"部门经理把季度制造报表项目分派给三名员工，从下达任务到分头执行再到如期交差"作主线，按"任务下达 → 分担工作 → 办公工具 → 合力执行 → 如期交差"五幕推进，让每个词都落到具体的人物动作或制度细节上：

| 幕 | 场景 | 承载语义场 | 叙事功能 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The assignment 任务下达 | 任务/职责/能力类名词：challenge, role, obligation, account, concern, capacity, skill, means, method, merit | 建立委派场景 |
| 2 | Sharing the load 分担工作 | 分配/调整类：apportionment, displacement, shift, adjustment, layout, form + 委派动词 establish, dedicate, engage, enhance, preclude | 展现分派过程 |
| 3 | Tools of the trade 办公工具 | 办公物件/文档类：photocopier, photocopy, letter, address, booklet, cable, bill, footnote, encryption, obsolescence | 铺垫执行障碍 |
| 4 | Getting it done 合力执行 | 执行动词：compile, proofread, complete, proceed, recommend, wrestle, undertake, rise, sideline, rationalization | 推进因果 |
| 5 | A feat at the finish 如期交差 | 收束类：feat, report, account, merit, routine + 回顾职责词 | 点题升华 |

**设计要点**：`apportionment / displacement / adjustment / shift` 四个"分配变动"词集中在第 2 幕，配合经理画板重排布局的动作；`photocopier / photocopy / booklet / letter / footnote / bill` 等文档物件集中在第 3 幕，由 Mei、Ravi、Tom 分别操作；`wrestle / compile / proofread / complete / proceed` 五个执行动词串起第 4 幕的协作链；结尾用 `feat / merit / challenge / routine` 呼应开头的"艰巨任务"，形成闭环。所有词均含人物动作，绝无清单式罗列。

## 二、英文故事　A Feat for the Quarter

### 1. The assignment

Ms. Ferris gathered Ravi, Mei and Tom for a Monday meeting. "This **quarter** brings a real **challenge**," she began. "We must **compile** a full **report** on our **manufacturing** lines by the end of it." She handed each of them a short **booklet** as an **introduction** to the job. Every person had a clear **role** and a personal **account** to keep. She spoke of the **concern** she felt about the old **routine** and of the **obligation** each worker owed the team. Ravi looked at the **worksheet** and the heavy **workload** it listed, and wondered whether the **workforce** had the **capacity** and the **skill** for so large a task. "We have the **means**," said Ferris, "and a proven **method**. Your **merit** will show in how you **undertake** the work."

### 2. Sharing the load

Ferris made a fair **apportionment** of the tasks on the spot. "No single **form** fits everyone," she said, redrawing the **layout** of the plan on the board. Moving Tom from his usual desk meant a **displacement** that caused a small **shift** in the seating, and she made a careful **adjustment** to the timetable. Mei would **establish** the filing system; Ravi would **dedicate** two whole days to the figures. "I will not let anyone **exploit** a quiet hour," she warned, "or **occupy** himself with small talk." She asked them to **engage** fully with the job and promised to **enhance** the weakest sections herself. A fresh **reassessment** of last year's numbers, she added, would **preclude** any repeat of old errors.

### 3. Tools of the trade

The next morning the office hummed. Mei ran a **photocopy** of the **letter** on the **photocopier** and checked the **address** printed on the envelope. A loose **cable** under the desk threatened to trip anyone, and the old machine was showing signs of **obsolescence**. "Do not **underuse** this equipment," Ferris said. "It still has life in it." For safety, Ravi added **encryption** to the file and put a clear **footnote** under every chart. Tom brought in the **bill** for new supplies and a simple scheme for saving paper.

### 4. Getting it done

For a week the three **wrestled** with the numbers. Ravi would **compile** the tables, Mei would **proofread** every page, and Tom would **complete** the final draft. They **proceeded** step by step, and Ferris **recommended** a shorter layout wherever the text ran long. A quiet **sideline** recycling old paper began to **rise**, and its small profit lifted everyone's mood. The **rationalization** of the workflow meant that no one could **displace** the blame onto a neighbour.

### 5. A feat at the finish

By the last day of the quarter the **report** was finished — a real **feat** for so small a team. The **apportionment** had worked: each **account** balanced, every **form** was filed, and the once crushing **workload** now felt light. Ferris thanked them all. "The **merit** was yours," she said. "You turned a **challenge** into a **routine** you can repeat." Ravi smiled; he no longer doubted the team's **capacity**.

## 四、学习建议

1. **"分配变动"词成组记**：`apportionment`（分派）、`displacement`（调动/取代）、`adjustment`（调整）、`shift`（转移）都出现在经理重排任务的第 2 幕，按"动作链"记忆比孤立背更牢。
2. **名词化后缀 "-ment"**：`adjustment / apportionment / displacement / reassessment / encryption / obsolescence / rationalization` 多为"动词 + 名词后缀"派生，看到 "-ment / -tion / -ence" 即可猜"动作或状态"。
3. **办公文档词集中记**：`photocopier / photocopy / booklet / letter / footnote / bill / worksheet / form` 都是办公室常见物件，可结合第 3 幕的实际操作画面记忆。
4. **委派动词搭配**：`dedicate ... to`、`engage in`、`occupy oneself with`、`preclude sb. from`、`recommend sth. to sb.` 是高频搭配，故事里都用具体情境呈现，复习时可回看对应幕。
5. **近义辨析**：`task` 是具体任务，`workload` 是工作量，`role` 是职责角色，`obligation` 是道义或法律上的义务，`feat` 是完成的壮举——五者在故事中分工清晰，可对照体会。
