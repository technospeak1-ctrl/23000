## 一、编排逻辑

本主题围绕"乐观积极"，可归为 4 个语义场：积极性格形容词、情感与热情名词、positive 心理动词、积极态度搭配。我用"乐观的社区组织者玛雅筹办一场节日庆典"作主线，按"萌生想法 → 做好准备 → 感染众人 → 庆典欢庆"推进，让积极词在情节递进中自然浮现：

| 幕 | 场景 | 承载语义场 | 叙事功能 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | A bright idea 一个明亮的念头 | 积极形容词 + 热情名词 | 建立动机 |
| 2 | Ready to begin 准备就绪 | 态度搭配 + 品质动词 | 展示准备 |
| 3 | Winning people over 感染众人 | 影响动词 + 情感名词 | 展示人际感染 |
| 4 | The celebration 庆典 | 欢庆动词 + 升华 | 收尾欢腾 |

**设计要点**：把"热切"类词（eager、keen、avid、enthusiastically、feverishly、vehement）与"满足/欢欣"类词（content、bliss、delight、grateful、jolly）放在同一条筹备—欢庆线上，形成积极情绪的层次；`be avid for`、`be content with`、`be grateful to/for`、`optimistic attitude`、`positive role` 等搭配承担关键表达。

## 二、英文故事　The Bright Idea

### 1. A bright idea

Maya was an **optimistic** woman with an **optimistic attitude** about everything. She was **ambitious** for her small neighbourhood and **eager for** a project that would bring people together. She had a **fond** **fancy** for old festivals and a **penchant** for music, and she **adore**d the idea of a street celebration. Her **passion** was **zeal** itself; she felt a genuine **ardor** for the task and a **keen interest** in every detail. "This will be **exciting**," she said with a **confident smile**, her **confidence** growing by the hour. She was **avid for** a challenge and **enthusiastically** sketched a plan full of **fantasy** and **bliss**. Even the **agitation** of the first morning felt like **excitement**, and she worked **feverishly**, her **agility** surprising everyone.

### 2. Ready to begin

Maya was **content** and **be content with** nothing less than the best. She wanted a **dynamic personality** on the committee and was **willing** to be **a willing helper** herself. She **ascertain**ed what permits were needed and **authorize**d the purchases, for she had been **be authorized to** act by the council. She would **confirm** the date, **await** the replies, and **persist** through every small delay. "I **convince** myself we can do it," she said, and she would **convince** others **of** the value of the plan too. She **cherish**ed the **affection** of her neighbours and **esteem**ed their **consideration**. As **a faithful friend** to the shy ones, she **console**d the worried and **sympathize**d with the tired; she would **readily** help anyone in need and never **concern** herself with small gossip. Her **briskness** and **detachment** kept her fair.

### 3. Winning people over

With **keen** attention she made a **vehement** plea and **entreat**ed a favour of the local band — **entreat**ing **a favor of** them politely — and they agreed **readily** to play. The **spur** of her enthusiasm began to **stimulate** the whole street. She would **acknowledge** every offer of help, **inspire** the young **to** volunteer, and **fascinate** the children with stories; they were **enthrall**ed. A **sociable couple** next door offered their garden, and **a sociable couple** of teachers made the posters. People **exclaim**ed **at** the colorful **banners**; some **chuckle**d at the funny **mascot**. Maya's **liberality** with praise and her **gratitude** won hearts. She felt **grateful to** everyone and **grateful for** every kindness, and her **appreciation** showed in the **honor** she gave each helper. Her **obsession** with detail was now a **positive role** for the whole team.

### 4. The celebration

On the day, the street was **jolly** and bright. The band played, the children **rejoice**d over the games, and **laughter** rang from every corner. Maya **support**ed by her committee, watched the crowd **be supported by** her neighbours' goodwill — and felt **delight** and pure **bliss**. She **chuckle**d at the **mascot** again and **exclaim**ed at the **banners**. The **celebration** **inspire**d the whole neighbourhood; her **faithful** committee stood beside her, **content** and proud. She **acknowledge**d the **appreciation** of the crowd and **console**d a child who had missed a prize. Looking around, she knew her **optimistic** spirit and **positive role** had turned a simple idea into a feast of **affection**, **gratitude**, and **zeal** — the brightest day the street had ever seen.

## 四、学习建议

1. **情绪层次记**：热切（eager、keen、avid、enthusiastically、feverishly、vehement）→ 欢欣（content、bliss、delight、jolly、grateful）→ 感染力（passion、zeal、ardor、spur、stimulate）。
2. **名词成组**：热情类（passion、zeal、ardor、obsession、penchant、fancy）与欢欣类（bliss、delight、laughter、excitement、agitation）分开记。
3. **积极动词链**：adore → cherish → inspire → stimulate → rejoice，从内心喜爱到外在感染，串成一条线。
4. **搭配优先**：`be avid for`、`be content with`、`be grateful to/for`、`optimistic attitude`、`positive role` 是本主题高频搭配。
5. **词形意识**：副词位置（readily、feverishly、enthusiastically）与形容词位置要分清；过去式与被动（ascertained、authorized、cherished、consoleD、enthralled、exclaimed、fascinated、inspired、persisted、rejoiced、stimulated、sympathized）要会认。
