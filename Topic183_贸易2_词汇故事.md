## 一、编排逻辑

本主题是"贸易 2"，词项覆盖合同、商品规格、付款保险与纠纷处理，抽象形容词和契约动词都很密集，最容易写成"合同条款表"。我用"一位贸易合规官与海外供应商从签约、验货、付款到纠纷化解的一整笔交易"作主线，按"签约 → 货物与规格 → 付款与保险 → 纠纷 → 化解"五幕推进，让每个词和搭配都落在具体的交易动作上：

| 幕 | 场景 | 承载语义场 | 叙事功能 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The contract 签约 | 契约形容词 + 谈判/保证搭配 | 建立人物与规则 |
| 2 | Goods and specifications 货物与规格 | 商品/规格词 + 检验搭配 | 铺陈货品 |
| 3 | Payment and insurance 付款与保险 | 支付/补偿词 + 保险搭配 | 展现资金流 |
| 4 | The dispute 纠纷 | 博弈/毁约动词 + 处罚搭配 | 情节高潮 |
| 5 | Resolution 化解 | 转变/收尾词 + 结算搭配 | 点题升华 |

**设计要点**：`competent / commercial / mutual / reciprocal / patent / ungrudging` 等形容词贴到签约与对手方（胜任、商业、互利、专利、慷慨）上；`feathery / gross / indeterminate / abstentious / impervious / overtly / bootless` 贴到货品描述与谈判姿态上；搭配如 `promote mutual understanding / certify to / insure against / compensate for / terminate the contract / conclude one's speech` 全部嵌入交易流程；`dicker` 类博弈词在此让位于 `impose / outstrip / outweigh / overtrade / roil` 等纠纷动词，形成因果推进。

## 二、英文故事　The Cushion Contract

### 1. The contract

Elena Voss was a **competent** trade officer who believed every deal began with a clear **contract**. She studied **commerce** law and the **premise** of the agreement, then checked the **qualifications** of the overseas supplier. "A **commercial** deal must rest on **mutual** trust," she said, "and a **reciprocal** promise on both sides." She **negotiated** for weeks to **promote mutual understanding** between the two teams. When the supplier's **conduct** turned **overtly** hostile, Elena stayed **abstentious** in her demands and **impervious** to pressure. Her **connection** with a local **commission** helped her read the **indeterminate** clauses, and she signed the **warrant** that bound them both.

### 2. Goods and specifications

The goods were cushions filled with **feathery** down. The supplier had to **import** the down before they could **export** the finished cushions. Elena listed every **item** in the **inventory**, checked the **specifications**, and attached a **tag** to each sample. The **commodity** was light, but the **gross** weight of a full container mattered for shipping. She measured the **scale** of the order and noted the **quota** the supplier could fill. The **merchandise** would carry a **patent** label, and a **slogan** was printed on the box. "A **bootless** description causes disputes," she warned, so she wrote the **specifications** twice.

### 3. Payment and insurance

Money came next. The **payment** **sum** was large, so Elena **insured** the cargo against loss and asked the bank to **certify to** the funds. The supplier demanded a **commission** on top of the price, which she **compensated for** with a smaller discount. "We will **refund a ticket** if the inspection trip is canceled," she wrote, "and **compensate for** any damage." She kept a **surplus** in the budget for generous refunds to good clients.

### 4. The dispute

Trouble came when a rival tried to **overtrade** and **impose** a lower price on the supplier, hoping to **roil** the market. The rival's offer would **outstrip** theirs in volume but **outweigh** it in risk. Elena refused to **scratch** the agreed terms. "This is a **monopoly** in the making," she said, and moved to **terminate the contract** unless the supplier agreed to **shrink** the order to a legal size. She **prognosticated** a decline in quality if they rushed.

### 5. Resolution

The two sides met again, and this time the **transaction** **unfolded** smoothly. Elena **bowdlerized** the few aggressive lines in the draft and spoke plainly. She **concluded her speech** with an apology, and the supplier answered with **ungrudging** praise. The **transformation** of a hostile deal into a partnership lifted **growth** and **profit**. Staff **turnover** fell, and the company put its savings into **estate** near the port. A **retail** partner took the **remainder** of the stock; they would **share** the savings and **distribute** the expenses equally.

## 四、学习建议

1. **签约链**：`contract`（合同）→ `premise`（前提）→ `qualification`（资质）→ `warrant`（委任状）→ `mutual`/`reciprocal`（互利）→ `negotiate`（谈判），一条完整的缔约路径。
2. **商品规格词**：`commodity`（商品）、`merchandise`（货物）、`item`（项目）、`inventory`（库存）、`specification`（规格）、`tag`（标签）、`quota`（配额）、`gross weight`（毛重）一起记，验货场景高频。
3. **付款保险**：`payment`（付款）、`sum`（金额）、`surplus`（盈余）、`commission`（佣金）、`insure against`（投保）、`compensate for`（赔偿）、`refund a ticket`（退票）是结算与理赔的高频搭配。
4. **纠纷动词**：`overtrade`（贸易过度）、`impose on`（强加）、`roil`（搅乱）、`outstrip`/`outweigh`（超过）、`terminate the contract`（解除合同）、`scratch`（撕毁）串成一条冲突升级链。
5. **形容词对照**：`competent`（胜任）/`indeterminate`（不确定）、`abstentious`（节制）/`overtly`（公然）、`impervious`（不为所动）/`ungrudging`（慷慨）、`bootless`（无用）/`patent`（专利），正反性质放进同一笔谈判对照记忆更牢。
