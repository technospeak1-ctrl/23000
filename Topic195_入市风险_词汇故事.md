## 一、编排逻辑

本主题是"入市风险"，词性杂、风险词密集，最易写成清单。我用"年轻教师莉娜带着积蓄入市、遭遇经济学家警告、经历崩盘、被审计、最终学会稳健"作主线，按"入市 → 预警 → 崩盘 → 审计 → 稳健"五幕推进，让每个词落在"人做了什么判断、承担什么后果"的具体动作上。

| 幕 | 场景 | 承载语义场 | 叙事功能 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | A modest start 微薄的本金 | 积蓄/账户/资金流词 | 建立人物与入市动机 |
| 2 | The economist's warning 经济学家的警告 | 通胀/数据/倍数词 | 埋下风险伏笔 |
| 3 | The market turns 市场转向 | 崩盘/贪婪/失控词 | 冲突爆发 |
| 4 | The audit 审计问责 | 审查/违规/失职词 | 揭示根源 |
| 5 | Wiser capital 成熟的资本 | 反弹/稳定/减仓词 | 点题升华 |

**设计要点**：`paycheck / saving / fund / reserve / installment / remittance` 集中在"凑本金"一幕；`inflation / flood / peak / speculation / statistical / likelihood` 与倍数词 `triple / quadruple / tremendous / phenomenal` 集中在"预警"一幕；`frozen / depress / deplete / interrupt / rapacious / containment` 集中在"崩盘"制造冲突；`auditor / exhaustive / improper / inadequate / neglect one's duties` 落在"审计问责"；`rebound / stabilize / stability / lessen / refundable` 在结尾收束，与开篇的浮躁形成对照。

## 二、英文故事　Lena's First Market

### 1. A modest start

Lena, a young teacher, decided to enter the market. She had a **tiny** **saving** account built from every **paycheck**, plus a small **fund** she kept as a **reserve**. She sold some old **belongings** and even cut a planned **donation** to raise cash. Her **borrowing** was limited to one **installment** loan, and a **remittance** from her brother completed the **source** of her capital. At the bank, a **teller** helped her **attach** the papers to an account. The money was **fluid** enough to move, though the **average** return was **minimal** and the fees felt **petty**. "What is the **function** of all this paperwork?" she asked. The teller only smiled.

### 2. The economist's warning

At a seminar, a blunt **economist** warned that **inflation** was hiding in the numbers. A **flood** of cheap credit had pushed prices to a **peak**, and **speculation** in shares was **remarkably** high. He spoke **precisely** and **presumably** knew the figures: the **proportion** of **stockholder**s who **presume** a **tremendous** gain was **evident** in every **statistical** chart. A **triple** rise in one sector could **quadruple** in another, he said, but a **phenomenal** boom could look **insignificant** beside a **stringent** credit squeeze. His warnings, **spanning** decades of **budgetary** data, were hard to ignore. "The **likelihood** of a fall is **relatively** high," he said, "whatever the headlines claim."

### 3. The market turns

Then the crash came. Confidence **depress**ed overnight, and the once-**fluid** market turned **frozen**. A **collective** wave of selling **interrupt**ed trading just as a large **import** order failed. Shareholders who had trusted easy gains now watched their **taxpayer**-backed banks wobble. **Rapacious** traders exploited **exclusion** rules, and regulators struggled with **containment**. Lena held her positions **tightly** and watched **narrowly** as losses began to **deplete** her capital. An official later admitted that a failure to supervise had let a **rapacious** few **neglect** their duties.

### 4. The audit

An **auditor** arrived to examine the wreckage. Her **exhaustive** review found **improper** entries and **inadequate** records at the very fund that had been **frozen**. She showed how loose checks had let insiders profit while small investors like Lena paid the price. "**Irrespective** of who signed the papers," she said, "the rules were broken." Those found to **neglect** their duties faced charges, and the report was sent to every **taxpayer**'s representative.

### 5. Wiser capital

In time the market began to **rebound**, and Lena moved to **stabilize** what remained. She put her money back into a **secure**, **refundable** deposit and chose **stability** over excitement. Slowly she began to **lessen** her exposure and rebuild. **Relatively** wiser now, she no longer presumed a quick gain; she kept only what was **evident** and sound. "I learned to **stabilize** first and **rebound** later," she wrote, "and to hold **secure** assets **irrespective** of the noise."

## 四、学习建议

1. **本金链**：`paycheck`（薪水）→ `saving`（储蓄）→ `fund`（基金）→ `reserve`（储备）→ `installment`（分期）→ `remittance`（汇款），沿"钱从哪来、怎么攒、怎么借"记。
2. **预警数据词**：`inflation / flood / peak / speculation / statistical / likelihood` 与倍数词 `triple / quadruple / tremendous / phenomenal` 同幕，配合 `precisely / presumably / relatively / remarkably / evidently` 一组副词记。
3. **崩盘反面词**：`frozen / depress / deplete / interrupt / rapacious / exclusion / containment` 都与市场下行同框；`tightly / narrowly` 两个副词写"如何守仓"。
4. **审计问责**：`auditor / exhaustive / improper / inadequate / neglect one's duties / irrespective` 落在合规检查场景，`irrespective of` 是高频固定表达。
5. **回归词**：`rebound / stabilize / stability / lessen / secure / refundable` 一组正向词收尾，与开篇浮躁形成对照；注意 `rebound`（回升）与 `stabilize`（趋稳）的细微差别。
