## 一、编排逻辑

本主题收的是描写"空间与定位"的词——东南西北的方位、上下内外的关系、凹凸曲直的形态，以及表示时间先后的副词。这类词最容易写成一张干巴巴的方位表。我的解法是让它成为一场**测绘远征**：制图师 Mira 受命勘测一片人迹罕至的边疆，她在绘制地图、攀越山口、测量河谷、观星定向、归档成图的过程中逐一使用这些词——方位词指引路线，形态词描述地形，副词记录行进的节奏与先后。

| 幕 | 场景 | 承载语义场 | 叙事功能 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The commission 受命 | 方位名词 + 国际/地理形容词 | 建立任务框架 |
| 2 | Into the cold 进入寒区 | 方向副词 + 气候/行进形容词 | 推进旅程 |
| 3 | Measuring the frontier 测量边疆 | 空间形态形容词 + 测量名词 | 核心测绘 |
| 4 | The stars 观星定向 | 时间/性质副词 + 人文名词 | 深化主题 |
| 5 | Home 归来归档 | 剩余词 + 收尾 | 收束全篇 |

**设计要点**：把 `abiding friendship`、`everlasting friendship`、`concave lens`、`convex lens`、`a horizontal position`、`a time-consuming job`、`corresponding period of last year` 七条搭配写进任务书与测绘记录；`lattice / perch / width / amplitude / zenith` 等名词成为 Mira 落笔测量时的具体工具与刻度；方位词 `north / south / east / west` 与八个合成方向词沿路线成组出现，形成一张可读的"文字地图"。

## 二、英文故事　Charting the Northern Reach

### 1. The commission

Mira was a surveyor who had spent years mapping the edges of the known world. One spring the academy asked her to lead an **international** expedition into a remote **geographical** frontier, and she read the **lengthy** brief twice. The work would be **time-consuming**, but she welcomed it. The old charts showed a **northern** coast stretching to the **north**, a **southern** cape reaching to the **south**, highlands rising to the **east**, and flat desert lying to the **west**. Her route would run from the **northeast** camp across the **northwest** pass, down into the **southeast** valley, and out along the **southwest** shore. She drew a **lattice** of reference lines over the map and marked a high **perch** where the team could camp. An **abiding** respect had grown between Mira and her guide Rafi — the steady trust of an **abiding friendship** — and she relied on his **unfailing** judgment. "We will map what no one has measured," she told him, "and the **coherence** of our notes will outlast the journey."

### 2. Into the cold

They set out **ahead** of the storm and pushed **farther** each day than any team before them. The **outdoor** work was hard: the wind was **noisy**, the trail **narrow** and **tortuous**, and the light reached them only **dimly** through the clouds. On the **leeward** side of the ridge the air was still, but beyond it an **arctic** gale blew without pause, as **ceaseless** as the surf of an **antarctic** shore. Snow fell **unseasonably** early that year. Rafi was a **speedy** climber, and he found a **pendulous** curtain of ice hanging from a **platelike** slab of rock. "The **vague** outline beyond is our pass," he said, pointing to a **concave** notch between two peaks. They camped **there**, and **therein** lay the real difficulty: their instruments were made of a **ductile** metal that bent in the cold. When the gale shifted, they travelled **southwards** for two days to escape it. **Afterward**, Mira remembered that night as the coldest of the whole journey.

### 3. Measuring the frontier

At the pass Mira set up her instruments. She levelled the tripod until the bubble showed a true **horizontal** line, then checked the **axial** alignment of the valley. The valley's **width** was greater than the charts claimed, and the **amplitude** of its bends surprised her. At noon the sun climbed to its **zenith**, and she measured the **spatial** relation of each peak to the next. She fitted a **concave lens** and then a **convex lens** to the sighting tube, comparing what each one showed. The **centripetal** pull of the river bent the shoreline into a curve, and she marked its **outermost** point on her sheet. From the **peripheral** villages came reports of a **detectable** tremor in the ground. She noted that the height of the cliff varied **inversely** with its distance from the pass, and that each **corresponding** rock layer matched its neighbour across the gap. The map was taking shape, though on the flat page it still looked **unidimensional**.

### 4. The stars

That night the clouds cleared and a single bright **star** appeared above the camp, steady enough to steer by. Mira climbed onto the **outermost** rock for a clearer view, and from that high **perch** the whole valley lay open below her. She thought about the **ulterior** motive some officials had for wanting the map — they cared less about knowledge than about the coal beneath the hills — and she pushed the thought **aside**. The **ceaseless** wind, the **abiding** loyalty of her guide, the **unfailing** accuracy of their readings: these were the things she trusted. She hoped the **everlasting friendship** she had found on the road would survive the journey home, long after the maps were filed away.

### 5. Home

Back at the academy, Mira climbed **upstairs** to the archive and laid the finished chart on the long table. Surveying the frontier had proved **a time-consuming job**, but every line was in place. She checked the **horizontal position** of each landmark against the original notes and compared them with records of the **corresponding period of last year**. The **concave** and **convex** readings agreed; the **width** of the river, the **amplitude** of the valley, and the **zenith** observations all matched. Where the old charts had shown a **vague** blank, her map now carried a **detectable**, coherent outline — a small, exact record of a **geographical** frontier that no one had bothered to measure before.

## 四、学习建议

1. **方位成组记忆**：`north / south / east / west` 四个基本方位，加上 `northeast / northwest / southeast / southwest` 四个复合方位，再配 `northern / southern` 与副词 `southwards`——按"基本 + 复合 + 派生"三层记忆一张文字地图。
2. **凹凸对子**：`concave`（凹）对 `convex`（凸），`concave lens / convex lens` 是一对；搭配 `centripetal`（向心）、`axial`（轴向）、`horizontal`（水平）一起理解几何与物理中的空间关系。
3. **副词辨向**：`ahead`（向前）、`farther`（更远）、`there / therein`（在那里/其中）、`afterward`（后来）、`aside`（一边）、`upstairs`（楼上）、`inversely`（相反地）——都是描写位置或关系推进的小词，注意其与介词搭配（如 `inversely with`、`therein lay`）。
4. **持久类形容词**：`abiding / everlasting / unfailing / ceaseless` 都含"持续不断"义，但侧重点不同——`abiding` 持久、`everlasting` 永恒、`unfailing` 可靠不断、`ceaseless` 不停，可用 `abiding friendship`、`everlasting friendship` 对比记忆。
5. **工具与刻度名词**：`lattice`（网格）、`perch`（高处）、`width`（宽度）、`amplitude`（幅度）、`zenith`（天顶）是测绘与描述的精确词；`a horizontal position`、`a time-consuming job` 是描写工作状态的地道短语。
6. **搭配再造句**：`corresponding period of last year` 建议自己造一句，例如 "Sales rose compared with the **corresponding period of last year**"，把空间/时间对应的用法迁移到数据描述中。
