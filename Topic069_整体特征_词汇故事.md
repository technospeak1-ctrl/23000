## 一、编排逻辑

本主题收的是描写"事物整体特征"的词——规模大小、时间频率、空间形状与内在性质。这类词最容易沦为清单，所以我用"策展人艾达布置一场世界文物展"作主线，让她在挑选展品、安排顺序、布置空间、收尾检视的过程中逐一使用这些词：规模词形容展品，时间词安排展期，形状名词描述器物，动词记录布展动作。

| 幕 | 场景 | 承载语义场 | 叙事功能 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The collection 藏品 | 规模/数量形容词 + 数量名词 | 盘点藏品 |
| 2 | Time and order 时间与顺序 | 时间/频率形容词 + 顺序词 | 安排展期 |
| 3 | Shape and space 形状与空间 | 空间/性质形容词 + 形状名词 | 布置展厅 |
| 4 | The underlying sense 深层含义 | 内在性质形容词 + 抽象名词 | 提炼主题 |
| 5 | Opening day 开幕 | 动词 + 收尾 | 收束全篇 |

**设计要点**：把 `a gigantic sculpture`、`immense improvement`、`intermittent showers`、`underground water`、`worldwide epidemic`、`simultaneous translation` 等搭配写进布展说明；`curve / oval / loop / fraction / fragment / proportion` 等形状名词成为描述器物的具体语言；7 个动词 `bundle / coil / combat / contrast / dazzle / erect / fade` 分别落在打包、盘绕、防护、对比、炫目、竖立、褪色七个布展动作上。

## 二、英文故事　The Exhibition

### 1. The collection

Ada was asked to organize a **worldwide** exhibition of **ancient** objects, and at once she faced an **enormous** task. The warehouse held an **abundant** supply of pieces: a **gigantic** stone figure, an **immense** bronze door, and a **considerable** number of smaller finds. There was a **bunch** of coins, a **heap** of broken pots, and more than one **fragment** of a **blank** tablet whose **identity** no one knew. Some objects were **exotic**, imported from distant lands; others were strictly **conventional**. Their **magnitude** varied greatly — from a **miniature** statue to a full-size chariot — and the **proportion** of large to small pieces was **excessive**. The **various** styles filled **dense** rows of shelves, yet the small reading room felt **cozy**. "We have **numerous** treasures," she said, "but only a **fraction** can be shown." Safety was her first **priority**. She began a careful **comparison**, sorting each **category** by size and tone.

### 2. Time and order

The plan had to cover a **continuous** twelve months. Ada set a **permanent** hall for the main works and a **periodic** display that changed with the seasons. The **consecutive** dates were fixed, with the **preceding** week reserved for setup and the **following** month for reviews. A **forthcoming** lecture, a **simultaneous** translation service, and a **prompt** opening ceremony were all arranged. The **underlying** schedule was tight; a **gradual** delay was **likely**, and an **intermittent** problem with the lights was **bound to** appear. "The show is **owing to** open on time," she reminded the crew, "so every **subsequent** step matters." She added a **supplementary** guidebook and planned an **overnight** security watch. A **previous** exhibition had failed; this one would not.

### 3. Shape and space

The gallery itself was **circular**, with an **oval** central court and a **loop** of corridors around it. A gentle **ramp** led to the **underground** vault, where the **exterior** wall of an old temple had been rebuilt. Every **curve** and **portion** of the layout was measured; the **extent** of the floor plan was **considerable**. Outside, a **wide** avenue stretched to the **locality** where the museum stood. Some labels were **loose** and had to be refastened; the **liquid** in one glass vessel looked **muddy** under the poor light; a **cohesive** display was what Ada wanted. She checked that each piece was **comparable** to its neighbour in scale, and that no **gap** was left unexplained. The **intermediate** room linked the ancient hall to the modern wing, and the **utmost** care was taken with every **underlying** support.

### 4. The underlying sense

As she worked, Ada searched for the **underlying meaning** of the whole collection. Was it merely **trivial** — pretty objects arranged for show? Or did it carry a **widespread** truth about how people live? The **external** pressures of modern life, she decided, were **comparable** to those of the **ancient** world; human nature was **continuous** across time. She wrote that the exhibition was **practicable** as a business but **inadequate** as a mere display unless it touched something **permanent** in the visitor; its real **continuity** lay in the ideas, not the objects. A **worldwide epidemic** of forgetting, she argued, could be fought by memory; the **likelihood** of understanding was **considerable**. She kept the tone of the labels **appropriate** and sober, resisting the urge to make everything **excessive** or **dazzling**.

### 5. Opening day

On the morning of the opening, the crew worked fast. They **bundled** the last crates away; a worker **coiled** the spare cables up and hung them on a hook. Guards were posted to **combat** theft, and signs invited visitors to **contrast** the ancient tools with the modern ones. The new lights made the gold masks **sparkle** and, for a moment, **dazzle** the crowd. A statue was **erected** in the court, and though the paint would **fade** with years, the shape would remain. Ada stepped back, **bound to** feel proud. From the first **fragment** to the final label, the exhibition held together — a **cohesive**, **continuous** whole, built from a **multitude** of small, careful acts.

## 四、学习建议

1. **规模三档**：`miniature`（微型）＜ `considerable`（相当大）＜ `enormous / gigantic / immense`（巨大）——描写大小时按档选用，`immense improvement`、`a gigantic sculpture` 是常见搭配。
2. **时间链**：`preceding → previous → following → subsequent → forthcoming`，再配 `periodic`（周期）、`intermittent`（间歇）、`consecutive`（连续）、`continuous`（持续）——按"先后 + 频率"两条线记忆。
3. **"在内/在外"前缀**：`exterior / external`（外部）对 `interior / internal`（内部）；`underground / underlying`（下面/潜在）——`underlying meaning`（潜在含义）是高频表达。
4. **形状名词**：`curve`（曲线）、`oval`（椭圆）、`loop`（环）、`ramp`（斜坡）、`fraction`（小部分）、`fragment`（碎片）、`proportion`（比例）——都是描述物体外形的精确词。
5. **动词布展五步**：`bundle`（打包）→ `erect`（竖立）→ `coil`（盘绕）→ `contrast`（对比）→ `dazzle/fade`（炫目/褪色），可套用于"布置/搭建"类场景写作。
6. **未逐一入文的词与搭配**：`dense population`、`priority` 建议复述时补入，例如 "a **dense** crowd filled the hall"、"Safety was our first **priority**"。
