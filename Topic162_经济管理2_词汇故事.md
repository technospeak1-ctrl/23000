## 一、编排逻辑

本主题是"经济管理 2"，词量最大（63 词），且抽象财经词密集。我用"年轻女企业家 Nadia 临危受命拯救一家濒临破产的商行：查账、砍成本、谈合并，最终扭亏为盈"作主线，让每个词落在她经手的一张报表、一次董事会或一场谈判上，按"危机中的商行 → 审计 → 削减成本 → 合并谈判 → 繁荣复苏"五幕推进：

| 幕 | 场景 | 承载语义场 | 叙事功能 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | A firm in trouble 危机中的商行 | 危机/破产/金融类形容词 + 债权人 | 建立困境、点明危机 |
| 2 | The audit 审计 | 账目/借贷/证照/财政名词 | 铺陈财务细节 |
| 3 | Cutting costs 削减成本 | 预算/节约/削减动词 | 展现改革动作 |
| 4 | The merger 合并谈判 | 集团/垄断/授权/谈判词 | 制造博弈冲突 |
| 5 | A booming recovery 繁荣复苏 | 繁荣/汇率/积累类词 | 收束并升华主题 |

**设计要点**：`insolvent / financial crisis / economic crisis / declare bankrupt` 集中在"危机"一幕；`audit / auditor / blotter / debit / invoice / mortgage / licence` 化作审计员桌上的具体单据；`abridge / allocate / economize / deflate / diversify` 这些改革动词各配一个具体对象；`booming / colossal / compact / economic / economical / futile / gross / insecure / mercantile / mercenary / monetary / underdeveloped` 等形容词分别贴到对应场景；`a colossal amount of money / an insecure investment / an efficient government / commercial intercourse / declare bankrupt / economic crisis / financial crisis / gross yield / monetary policy / underdeveloped country / abridge the cost / accumulate experience / allocate money / augment one's prestige / combine ... with ... / convert ... into / discharge ... for / diversify into / economize time / evaluate a person / cultural exchange / fluctuate between` 全部搭配真实嵌入叙事。

## 二、英文故事　The Turnaround

### 1. A firm in trouble

Nadia, a young **entrepreneur**, was hired to save a **commercial** firm that was **insolvent**. Its **board** had spent a **colossal** amount of money on an **insecure** investment in an **underdeveloped** country, and a **financial** crisis had left it unable to pay a single **invoice**. The **economics** of the case were plain: costs were high and the **gross** yield was low. A **creditor** had begun to **dictate** to the managers, and rivals whispered that the firm would soon declare bankrupt. "We are in an **economic** crisis of our own making," Nadia told the staff. She had **accumulated** experience in distant markets and knew that even an **economical** style of management could not cure a **mercenary** culture overnight.

### 2. The audit

Mr. Grant, the **auditor** from the bank, arrived with a **compact** narration of the firm's troubles and a fresh **audit**. He checked the **calendar** for the deadline, read the **blotter** line by line, and balanced every **debit** against the **invoices**. The local **currency** was weak, and the **financing** team's **mortgage** on the factory worried him, as did the **licence** for the main plant and the **property** deeds. "Your **discretion** has been poor," he said, "but your **appraisal** of the market is sharp." He noted the annual income and observed that the central bank's **monetary** policy had turned in their favor. Nadia took over the administrative work herself, hoping that an efficient government would cut the red tape and that **commercial** intercourse with neighbouring firms would revive.

### 3. Cutting costs

Nadia drew up a new **budget** and set about to **abridge** the cost wherever she could. She would **allocate** money to the workshop, **economize** time on paperwork, and set aside a sum appropriate for training. "We will **combine** the repair shop with the store room," she said, "and **convert** the old hall into offices, a **conversion** that would save rent." She refused to **deflate** the workers' bonuses, for she knew that a **futile** saving would only breed resentment. She was not there to **evaluate** a person harshly, but she had to **discharge** one clerk for theft. She also planned to **diversify** into software, a risky **venture** that would **incorporate** the skills of her small team.

### 4. The merger

A rival **magnate**, Mr. Sloane, proposed a **coalition** with a **consortium** of smaller firms. "A **monopoly** would **augment** my prestige as much as yours," he said, but Nadia suspected he only wanted to **manipulate** the **board**. A **protectionist** in the government opposed the deal at once. The **negotiation** was long: she insisted that the new **entity** keep its **licence**, that the **fund** stay intact, and that no one **dictate** to her again. She had **accredited** her own team to sign, and she made every clause **compact** so that no one could hide a **debit** in the small print. When Sloane tried to **commercialize** their shared name, she threatened to **annul** the whole agreement. The **mercantile** habits of the old firm died hard, but she held her ground.

### 5. A booming recovery

Within a year the firm was **booming**. The **boom** in orders came from a cultural **exchange** with a partner in an **underdeveloped** country, and prices no longer **fluctuated** between panic and greed. Nadia had **economized** both time and money, and she had **accumulated** contacts that would **augment** the firm's standing for years. A final **audit** confirmed the **appraisal**: the firm was sound. The **auditor** shook her hand, the **creditor** was paid in full, and the **board** voted her a **colossal** amount of money as a bonus. Looking back, Nadia saw that the **mercantile** lessons of the **commercial** world were simple: an **insecure** investment teaches more than a safe one, and an **economical** style of leadership can turn even a **futile** plan into a **booming** success.

## 四、学习建议

1. **财经词按流程成组记**：审计流程 `audit / auditor / blotter / debit / invoice / appraisal / discretion`；融资抵押 `financing / mortgage / fund / currency / monetary policy`；组织结构 `board / coalition / consortium / monopoly / entity / magnate`。成组记比孤立记牢固。
2. **"-ing / -ed" 形容词辨析**：`booming`（蓬勃的）、`gross`（总的）、`insolvent`（破产的）、`insecure`（不安的）多描述状态；`economic`（经济的）与 `economical`（节约的）形近义异，`economic crisis` 是经济危机，`an economical style` 是简练的作风。
3. **固定搭配整体背**：`a colossal amount of money`、`gross yield`、`monetary policy`、`an efficient government`、`commercial intercourse`、`declare bankrupt`、`underdeveloped country` 都是经济类高频短语，勿拆开记。
4. **动词搭配介词**：`dictate to sb.`（对某人发号施令）、`discharge sb. for sth.`（因某事解雇某人）、`diversify into`（向……多元化）、`convert ... into ...`（把……改成……）、`fluctuate between ... and ...`（在……之间波动）介词固定，故事正文都给了真实例句。
5. **同根词串记**：`economy → economics → economic → economical → economize`；`finance → financial → financing`；`commerce → commercial → commercialize → mercantile`。注意 `mercantile`（商业的）与 `mercenary`（唯利是图的）拼写相近但褒贬不同。
