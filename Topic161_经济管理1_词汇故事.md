## 一、编排逻辑

本主题是"经济管理 1"，词多为贸易、财务与制度名词。我用"女 strategist Clara Vale 奉老 tycoon 之命开拓海外市场，从定策略、过海关、谈合同到化解危机、达成互惠协议"作主线，把每个词落到一份文件、一道手续或一次谈判上，按"定策略 → 过海关 → 后方生变 → 停滞的一季 → 互惠成交"五幕推进：

| 幕 | 场景 | 承载语义场 | 叙事功能 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | A bold strategy 定策略 | 战略/市场/投资 + 市场类形容词 | 建立人物与目标 |
| 2 | Through customs 过海关 | 海关/税费/单证/授权名词 | 落实贸易手续 |
| 3 | Trouble at home 后方生变 | 股东/所有/违约/制裁/法务词 | 制造内部冲突 |
| 4 | A stagnant quarter 停滞一季 | 衰退/收入/闲置/统计词 | 压低情节、埋转机 |
| 5 | A reciprocal deal 互惠成交 | 互惠/减免/收益类词 | 收束并升华主题 |

**设计要点**：`strategy / strategist / venture / stake / surplus` 集中在"定策略"一幕；`customs / tariff / quota / receipt / register / sanction / warrant` 化作海关柜台上的具体单证；`shareholder / ownership / patronage / prosecution / default / loophole` 落在股东会的争执里；`duty-free / up-market / prosperous / stagnant / reciprocal / mutually / sole` 七个形容词（副词）各贴一处场景；全部常用搭配 `duty-free goods/shop / fellow subsidiary / operational mechanism / a prosperous country / reciprocal relationship / sole right / a stagnant economy / a steady demand / up-market malls / excise duty / export tariff / non-tariff barrier / overhead costs` 以及动词搭配 `execute an order / execute the contract / invest in / invest with / exempt ... from taxation / speculate on` 都嵌进叙事动作。

## 二、英文故事　The Trade Mission

### 1. A bold strategy

Clara Vale worked as a **strategist** for a **prosperous** trading house owned by an old **tycoon**. The **socio-economic** report on her desk described a **prosperous** country with a steady demand for tools and new **up-market** malls, and she saw a chance to **invest** in it. Her **strategy** was to form a joint **venture** and hold a controlling **stake**, because a **sole** right to sell would beat a quick **surplus**. "If we **invest** in trust as well as machines," she told the directors, "we will own more than **stock**." The board **invested** her with full authority so she could **execute** the contract. She hired a **statistician** to verify every **statistic**, for **statistics** can flatter as well as inform, and asked him to watch the **valuation** of their shares. A **rival** firm was already sniffing around, and she meant to repair the house's clumsy operational mechanism before it could move.

### 2. Through customs

At the border a **blackout** had plunged the post into darkness, so the **customs** officers worked by lantern light. They passed a **duty-free** shop with shelves of **duty-free** goods, but the **coinage** of that country was unstable, so Clara offered **sterling** instead. She presented the **register** and the **registration** number of the firm, plus the **receipt** for the excise duty she had paid. A young clerk found a **loophole** in the **tariff** rules: an export **tariff** had been charged twice, and a non-**tariff** barrier blocked their crates. "If we sign this, we are in **default**," Clara said, and she refused to put her name to it. The **merchant** who had sold them the **binder**, the temporary contract, waited outside. At last the clerk accepted a letter of **sanction** from the ministry and a **warrant** authorizing the **transaction**. The officers stamped the papers and **reset** the broken lock on the crate. That evening she cabled a **remittance** home to cover the overhead costs.

### 3. Trouble at home

Back home the **shareholder** meeting turned sour. A fellow **subsidiary** in the north had failed to **execute** an order on time, and the angry buyer had begun a **prosecution**. Questions of **ownership** were raised, and some members were only a **puppet** of the old **tycoon**, whose **patronage** had kept the directors too soft. Clara defended the **promotion** campaign and the **quota** system, but the room was **slack** with doubt and the **workload** on the clerks was heavy. Orders were down, and a quiet **recession** had set in. "The new rules **exempted** such goods from **taxation**," she said, "but that will not save us." She knew critics would **speculate** on her next move.

### 4. A stagnant quarter

The next quarter was **stagnant**. With a **stagnant** economy at home and an import **quota** biting, **revenue** fell and the **proceeds** from sales shrank. The **rival** firm offered to buy them out, but Clara saw that their own **strategy** would leave no **relief** for the workers. She called in the **statistician** again and studied each **statistic**. One figure showed that their **surplus** was still growing in the foreign market: the problem was only a **slack** shipping schedule. "We are not in a **recession**," she told the directors, "we are in a **stagnant** patch." She refused to let the firm **speculate** away its good name, and she promised that profits would **swell** again once the crates moved.

### 5. A reciprocal deal

In the end Clara **exempted** the small partners from the fee and offered **mutually** fair terms. "A **reciprocal** relationship is the only **binder** that lasts," she said. The **merchant** signed, the **customs** office eased the non-**tariff** barrier, and even the **rival** agreed to share the **quota**. The **prosperous** house recovered: **proceeds** rose, **revenue swelled**, and the **valuation** of its **stock** climbed. The **shareholder** who had shouted loudest now called her **strategy** brilliant. Clara framed the **receipt** for their first **transaction** — a small **patronage** of a **sole** right won honestly, and proof that **statistics** and trust can **mutually** support each other.

## 四、学习建议

1. **贸易单证成组记**：`customs / tariff / quota / receipt / register / registration / warrant / sanction / transaction / binder` 都是过海关与签约时用得上的词，故事第 2 幕把它们排在同一柜台前，便于整体记忆。
2. **财务名词成对辨析**：`revenue`（收入）对 `proceeds`（收益），`surplus`（盈余）对 `deficit`（赤字），`shareholder`（股东）对 `creditor`（债权人，见 Topic 162），`valuation`（估价）对 `stock`（股票）。
3. **固定搭配整体背**：`duty-free goods/shop`、`a steady demand`、`a stagnant economy`、`up-market malls`、`non-tariff barrier`、`overhead costs`、`reciprocal relationship`、`sole right` 都是经济类高频短语，勿拆开记。
4. **动词搭配与介词**：`invest in`（投资于）与 `invest sb. with sth.`（授予某人某权）意思不同；`execute an order`（执行订单）与 `execute the contract`（使合同生效）各有所指；`exempt ... from ...`（免除……的……）、`speculate on`（推测）介词固定。
5. **同根词串记**：`statistics`（统计学）、`statistic`（统计数字）、`statistician`（统计员）三词同根但词性不同；`prosperous`（繁荣的）对应名词 `prosperity`；`reciprocal`（互惠的）对应副词 `reciprocally`。
