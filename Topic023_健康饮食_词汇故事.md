## 一、编排逻辑

本主题围绕"健康饮食"，可归为 4 个语义场：食物评价形容词、健康与营养词、食物/器具/人物名词、烹饪与进食动词。我用"同一个人前后两场宴席"作主线，按"放纵的宴会 → 身体报警 → 营养师改造 → 健康的新宴"推进，让褒贬词在前后对照中自然浮现：

| 幕 | 场景                           | 承载语义场                 | 叙事功能       |
| - | ---------------------------- | --------------------- | ---------- |
| 1 | The gourmet's banquet 美食家的宴会 | 美食评价形容词 + 酒食名词 + 进食动词 | 建立"放纵"的基线  |
| 2 | The morning after 翌日清晨       | 健康/病理形容词 + 营养名词       | 制造转折（身体报警） |
| 3 | The new kitchen 新厨房          | 食材/烹饪名词 + 烹饪动词        | 展示科学饮食的操作  |
| 4 | A different feast 另一场宴席      | 节制类词 + 剩余名词           | 收尾升华（新旧对照） |

**设计要点**：把正面味觉词（luscious、tasty、yummy、full-bodied）与负面词（cloying、tasteless、brackish、fusty、moldy、pernicious）放进同一条"两场宴席"线，形成价值对照；nutrition 一族（alimentary、gustatory、nutrition、nutritional、nutritious、nourishing、salutary、healthful）集中在诊断与改造两幕，形成语义场聚焦；`a healthful diet`、`nourishing food`、`nutritional value`、`raw material`、`soak up` 等搭配承担关键说明。

## 二、英文故事　The Two Feasts

### 1. The gourmet's banquet

Leo was an **epicure** with a **gourmet**'s pride, and he believed **hospitality** meant excess. For his birthday he loaded his **tableware**—every **tray** and **saucer**—with rich **viand**s: **fat** meat, **cloying confection**ery, **luscious** cakes and **yummy dessert**s. The **entrée** was a **hodgepodge** of **hash** and **stew**, with **pudding** and **popcorn** on the side. He **intermingle**d a **condiment** of sharp **seasoning** and **flavoring**, which gave the **flavor** a **piquant**, almost **brackish** edge and hid the **acerbity** underneath. His guests **gobble**d and **guzzle**d, **slurp**ed their **ale** and **cider**, **inhale**d the fumes of his **champagne**, **cocktail** and **vodka**, and even **eruct**ed politely into their napkins. A **coffeepot** steamed; someone chewed **gum**. To Leo the whole spread was **tasty** and **full-bodied**; he never saw the **pernicious fat** beneath the sugar, or the **causal** link between such nights and the **malaise** he felt each morning.

### 2. The morning after

By spring Leo looked **haggard**; his **health** had quietly failed. A **tepid**, **tasteless** breakfast sat untouched—the **curd** had gone **fusty**, the **margarine** was **odorless** and stale, and a **moldy pear** lay in a **squalid** corner of the **grocery** bag. Dr. Mira, his doctor, called it a **nutritional disturbance**: too much **cholesterol**, too little real **nutrition**. "Your body is **perishable**," she said; "no **nostrum** will save you." She spoke of the **gustatory** pleasure that comes from **alimentary** wisdom, and of the **salutary** power of **a healthful diet** and **nourishing food** with genuine **nutritional value**. Leo had lived **ravenous** yet hollow, mistaking **seamy** indulgence for pleasure and never learning **abstemious**, **abstinent** habits. The **squalor** of his kitchen, she told him, was written on his face.

### 3. The new kitchen

Under Dr. Mira's eye the kitchen became a **culinary** classroom. She bought **asparagus**, **carrot**s and **fig**s from the **huckster** at the market—good **raw material**, she said, not the **chaff** and **rind** Leo used to throw away; it was **cellulose**, the very fiber his diet had lacked. She taught him to **soak** the beans and let the starch **soak up** water, to watch milk **coagulate** and gravy **congeal** as they cooled. She **kipper**ed a fish, **pasteurize**d the milk, **roast**ed vegetables on the **gridiron** and simmered a **stew** in the **casserole**. Nothing went into the **freezer** to lie **frozen** and forgotten: **perishable** food was sealed in **hermetic** jars, and the **fungicide** on the old board was scrubbed away. The water was **potable**, the vegetables still slightly **raw**, and every **refreshment** she mixed had **nutritional value**. Even a plain **potation** of warm barley water felt like a treat.

### 4. A different feast

Months later Leo held a second dinner. This time the table bore **nourishing food**: a **vegetarian entrée**, **asparagus** with fresh **seasoning**, a salad of **carrot** and **fig**. He practiced a sensible **rationing** of both **potation** and **confection**, and kept a **cocoon** of calm in the kitchen where his **vegetarian** guest felt at home. The **hospitality** was warm but **abstemious**. "I used to think **tasty** meant **fat**," Leo told the table. "Now I know a **salutary**, **nutritious** meal can be **luscious** too." He had learned to **prolong** not merely the **flavor** of a dish but the **health** of everyone who sat at it. And when a guest reached for a third helping of **pudding**, he only laughed: even **temperance** leaves room for **yummy** things—so long as the **nutrition** is real.

## 四、学习建议

1. **前后对照记褒贬**：第一幕的放纵词（fat、cloying、guzzle、gobble）↔ 第四幕的节制词（abstemious、rationing、temperance）。两条宴席线并行，褒贬一目了然。
2. **营养语义场集中记**：alimentary、gustatory、nutrition、nutritional、nutritious、nourishing、salutary、healthful 都围绕"营养与健康"，注意 nutritious（有营养成分的）与 nourishing（滋养的）的细微差别。
3. **烹饪动词串成链**：soak（泡）→ coagulate / congeal（凝固）→ roast / kipper / pasteurize（烤/熏/杀菌）→ intermingle（调配），按操作流程记忆。
4. **搭配优先**：`a healthful diet`、`nourishing food`、`nutritional value`、`raw material`、`soak up` 是高频考点，要整块记住而非拆词。
5. **词形意识**：过去式与被动要会认（intermingled、kippered、pasteurized、roasted、soaked、gobbled、guzzled、slurped、inhaled、eructed）。
