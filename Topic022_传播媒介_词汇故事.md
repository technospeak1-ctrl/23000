## 一、编排逻辑

本主题围绕"新闻与传播媒介"，可归为 4 个语义场：新闻评价形容词、媒介与出版物名词、新闻工作者与内容名词、传播动作。我用"一则新闻从报料到见报"作主线，按"接报 → 采访 → 编辑 → 见报与反响"推进：

| 幕 | 场景 | 承载语义场 | 叙事功能 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The tip-off 报料 | 评价形容词 + 媒介名词 | 建立情境（critical moment） |
| 2 | The reporter 记者 | 新闻工作者与内容名词 | 展示采访（journalist 工作） |
| 3 | Editing 编辑 | 出版物名词 + 传播动作 | 制造情节（筛选与核实） |
| 4 | In print 见报 | 剩余名词 + 传播动词 | 收尾升华（媒介的力量） |

**设计要点**：把正面（effective、invaluable、eminent、terrific）与负面（irrelevant、obscene、quaint、off-hand）评价放进同一条新闻线，形成对照；`conceal…from`、`differentiate between`、`appeal to`、`subscribe to` 等搭配承担转折。

## 二、英文故事　The Story

### 1. The tip-off

It was a **critical moment** for the **media**. An **eminent** **journalist** received a **claim** at the **press**: a **terrific** story, with **incredible** **news** and an **exclusive**. The **public** was already in an **ongoing discussion**; **prevalent opinions** ran wild. But much of the **coverage** was **irrelevant information**, and a **popular** but **quaint** **hobby**-blog carried an **obscene** **caption**. "We need **effective measures**," the editor said, "and **invaluable advice**, not an **off-hand** guess." A **spokesman** for the **paper** **announced** that the **newsroom** would **commence** an **enquiry**. The **motto** of the **magazine** was **accuracy**; the **headline** had to be **explanatory**, not sensational.

### 2. The reporter

The **journalist** took a **questionnaire** and a **notebook** to the **bookstall** where the **occurrence** was reported. A **column** in a **periodical** had first **commented** on it; a **commentary** on the **newscast** had **highlighted** it. The reporter would **scan** the **clippings**, **photocopy** the **original** **excerpt**, and **conceal** nothing **from** his source. An **artwork** and a **label** in a **brochure** gave a clue; a **leaflet** and a **handout** gave another. The **telex** brought a **precis** of the **issue**; a **newsreel** showed the **focus** of the **protest**. "Don't **affiliate yourself with** any side," the editor warned; "**differentiate between** fact and **claim**, and **expose** what is **concealed**." By evening the reporter had a **terrific** draft with **originality**.

### 3. Editing

In the **editorial** meeting, the **issue** of the **journal** was planned. The **editorial** itself argued for **effective measures**; an **excerpt** of the **interview** ran beside the **editorial**. The **focus** of the **coverage** was the **claim**; an **exclusive** in the **quarterly** **publication** offered a **different** **option**. A **press** **advertise**ment **appealed to** readers to **subscribe to** the **periodical**. The **postage** for the **booklet** was paid; the **telex** **disseminated** the **news** at **terrific speed**. The **commentator** would **commentate** on the **newscast**, **heighten** awareness, and **tilt** opinion — but there was no **horseplay** in that room: a **critical** sub-editor cut every **irrelevant** line and **concealed** no **enquiry** detail. A **slogan** and a **tag** were chosen; the **label** "exclusive" was **approved**.

### 4. In print

The **issue** hit the **bookstall** at dawn. The **headline** was **critical**, the **editorial** **eminent**, the **advice** **invaluable**; readers **appealed to** the **paper** for more. A **renewal** of **subscription** followed; the **readership** grew **thousandfold**-fast. The **exclusive** was **photocopy**ied and **disseminated** by **multimedia**; even a **quaint** **newsreel** carried the **headline**. The **spokesman** **announced** a **protest**, the **press** printed a **column**, and the **media** **heightened** the **public**'s **awareness**. The **journalist**'s **originality** had turned an **occurrence** into **news**, and the **commentary** had **differentiated between** **claim** and fact. I **subscribed to** the **periodical** that morning, **scanned** the **excerpt**, and thought: the **medium** is only as **effective** as the **accuracy** behind it — an **invaluable** lesson from a **terrific** story.

## 四、学习建议

1. 按新闻流程记：报料（tip-off、claim、exclusive、critical moment）→ 采访（journalist、questionnaire、source、conceal）→ 编辑（editorial、excerpt、headline、differentiate）→ 见报（issue、bookstall、headline、renewal）。
2. 评价对照：正面（effective、invaluable、eminent、terrific）↔ 负面（irrelevant、obscene、quaint、off-hand）。
3. 搭配优先：`conceal…from`、`differentiate between`、`appeal to`、`subscribe to`、`affiliate oneself with` 是高频考点。
4. 词形意识：动词过去式/被动要会认（commenced、announced、disseminated、exposed、heightened、photocopied、tilted）。
