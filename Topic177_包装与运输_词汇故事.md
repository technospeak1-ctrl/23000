## 一、编排逻辑

本主题是"包装与运输"，货物从打包到装船再到交付是一条完整的物流链，最易写成"货运术语表"。我用"一位沿海货站物流主管把一批雨衣布料从车间送抵买方港口"作主线，按"接单 → 打包 → 码头装船 → 事故与延误 → 交付"五幕推进，让每个词落在具体的包装与发运动作上：

| 幕 | 场景 | 承载语义场 | 叙事功能 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | A specified order 指定订单 | 订单形容词 + 包装属性搭配 | 建立任务 |
| 2 | Packing the floor 打包车间 | 包装材料名词 + 包装动作 | 铺陈货物 |
| 3 | Down to the quay 码头装船 | 港口/单证名词 + 装卸动词 | 推进发运 |
| 4 | A crash and a delay 事故与延误 | 风险词 + 期货/保险 + 暂停动词 | 制造冲突 |
| 5 | Delivery at last 终于交付 | 剩余词 + 收束搭配 | 点题升华 |

**设计要点**：`waterproof / durable / tight / cheap / specified / due` 等形容词分别贴到布料属性（防水、耐用）、市场状况（供不应求、低息贷款）与订单要求（精确、到期）上；`waterproof fabric / package deal / a durable friendship / tight market / cheap credit` 五条形容词搭配全部嵌进接单场景；19 条搭配如 `bar code / bill of lading / consignment note / freight forwarder / currency futures / application for insurance` 沿物流链依次出现，成为"她做了什么"的一部分；`abrade / consign / discharge / disconnect / drag on / suspend payment / unload / unpack` 等动词落在打包、装船、抢修与验收的决策里。

## 二、英文故事　The Shipment That Made It

### 1. A specified order

Marta Reyes ran the **logistic** desk at a coastal **depot**, and on a grey Monday a **specified** order landed on her table. The buyer wanted **waterproof fabric** for raincoats and **brown goods** for the home shelf, all to be sent as **package** freight in a single **batch**. "It's a **package deal**," her boss said, "and we must finish by the **due** date." Trade was slow and a **tight market** left buyers short of cash, but **cheap credit** from the bank kept the firm alive. Marta had known the supplier for years — **a durable friendship** — so she trusted his word. On the form she wrote the **conditions**: no damp, no dents, no exceptions.

### 2. Packing the floor

The packing floor smelled of **twine** and glue. Workers wrapped each **parcel** in **waterproof** paper, tucked a **packet** of silica inside, and sealed the **container** with **cord**. They folded the **bulk** rolls in **waterproof fabric** and printed a **bar code** on every **package**. For **protection**, they pushed soft **stuffing** between the layers, yet a loose **cord** could **abrade** the cloth and leave a **fragment** of thread on the floor. Marta counted a **shipload** of raincoats and a **trainload** of cartons waiting on the siding. "A **batch** this size needs steady hands," she said.

### 3. Down to the quay

By Thursday the **cargo** stood on the **quay**. A **bulk carrier** waited at the **wharf**, and the **freight forwarder**, Mr. Haddad, handed Marta the **manifest**, the **bill of lading**, and the **consignment note**, with a **dispatch note** on top. The **carrier**'s vans had come from an **offshore** yard, and the crew kept a **nautical** log as they began to **discharge** the boxes. They **unloaded** the vans at the **wharf** and stacked the goods. "The **port** is crowded," Haddad said. "We **consign** these tonight or we miss the tide." The **haulage** bill was high, but a **shipload** of **freight** was already stacked and the **destination** was clear.

### 4. A crash and a delay

That night a small **crash** on the access road caused a long **delay**. The **delivery** was **due** in three days, but the vans sat in **transit**. Then an **embargo** at the **destination** port was announced, and Marta watched her **currency futures** wobble — she had bought **futures** to hedge the **freight** cost. The **application for insurance** she had filed helped, and the **proximity** of a spare yard saved them. "This **drags** on," she muttered, as a **back order** piled up and a storm **disconnected** the depot phone. After the **crash** the harbor master warned that one more accident could **suspend a driving license**. She had to **suspend** a second **shipment** and **suspend payment** to the carrier until the road was cleared. A note went out: "Goods held; storage **free of charge**." A broken **packet** of food had gone **rancid**, and the checkers turned it out at once.

### 5. Delivery at last

Two days late, the **cargo** reached the **destination**. The buyer came to **unpack** the **parcel**s himself. He ran a hand over the **waterproof** cloth — not a trace of damp, not even a **fragment** of a mark. "The **conditions** held," Marta said. The **packaging** had done its job: the **cord** and **twine** were intact and the **stuffing** dry. He signed the **bill of lading** and praised the **durable friendship** that had begun with one honest **shipment**. What had started as a **provisory** deal now stood firm; the **back order** was gone and the **tight market** had eased.

## 四、学习建议

1. **物流链主线**：`specified order`（指定订单）→ `package deal`（一揽子交易）→ `packaging / stuffing / cord`（包装/填料/细绳）→ `manifest / bill of lading / consignment note / dispatch note`（各类单证）→ `delivery`（交付），按货物走一遍就记住整条链。
2. **包装属性形容词**：`waterproof`（防水）、`durable`（耐用）、`tight`（密封/紧俏）、`cheap`（廉价）、`rancid`（变味）、`provisory`（附条件），都可贴在货物与交易状况上对比记忆。
3. **运输方式词**：`shipload`（一船）、`trainload`（一列车）、`bulk carrier`（散装货船）、`freight forwarder`（货代）、`haulage`（运费）、`cargo / freight`（货物）是一组高频外贸运输词。
4. **风险与金融词**：`embargo`（禁运）、`crash`（碰撞）、`delay`（延误）、`currency futures`（外汇期货）、`futures`（期货）、`application for insurance`（投保单）常在货运保险场景同现。
5. **动词搭配**：`drag on`（拖延）、`suspend payment`（暂停付款）、`suspend a driving license`（吊销执照）、`consign`（发货）、`discharge / unload / unpack`（卸货/拆包）都落在"发运—出事—补救"的动作链里，配套记忆更牢。
