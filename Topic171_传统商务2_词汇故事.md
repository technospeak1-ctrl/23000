## 一、编排逻辑

本主题"传统商务 2"以双边贸易、投标、展会、电报与招股章程为核心，天然有主线。我用"女经销商 Mara 从一张展台做起到签下双边条约、穿越资金危机的经历"作故事，按五幕推进，让每个词与搭配都落在具体经营动作上：

| 幕 | 场景 | 承载语义场 | 叙事功能 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | A dealer's vision 经销商的愿景 | 身份/愿景/招股/合作 | 建立人物与目标 |
| 2 | At the trade stand 展会投标 | 展台/投标/破损/电讯 | 铺陈交易场景 |
| 3 | The contract and the cash 合同与钱款 | 延期/减额/赤字/认购 | 制造资金危机 |
| 4 | Standardizing the line 标准化产品线 | 标准化/修改/维护/出口 | 展现经营改进 |
| 5 | A treaty and a future 条约与未来 | 剩余词 + 点题搭配 | 点题升华 |

**设计要点**：`dealer / business / vision / prospectus / valuable / off-the-peg / bilateral / leverage / benefit` 贴到创业愿景；`stand / square / uniform / ritual / bid / breakage / metal / voucher / wire / telegram / resemblance` 铺陈展会投标；`defer payment / decrease to / demonetize the cash / deficit / depression / underwrite / downsize / wither` 构成资金危机；`standardize / modify / maintain / upkeep / export / inquire / spread over / progress` 落到经营改进；`boost in sales / treaty / viability / burden / decline` 收束全篇。

## 二、英文故事　The Stand on the Square

### 1. A dealer's vision

Mara Voss was a **dealer** who had **started** a small export **business** with a clear **vision**. She wrote a **prospectus** to attract partners and offered them a **valuable** share of the profits. Her plan was **special**: buy **off-the-peg** garments, add a **bilateral** label, and use each partner's **leverage** to open new markets. "Every **business** needs a **benefit** that someone can feel," she said, and fair wages for the makers were written into her first **treaty**.

### 2. At the trade stand

At the spring fair Mara rented a **stand** on the main **square**. Her staff wore a neat **uniform**, and the samples were arranged with almost **ritual** care. A buyer placed a **bid** for the whole line, hoping for a **boost in sales**; her **resemblance** to a famous brand helped, but she refused to trade on it. She noted every **breakage** in the **metal** fittings and issued a **voucher** for the damage. When a **wire** from the factory came through, she sent a **telegram** confirming the order before the **stand** closed.

### 3. The contract and the cash

Trouble came with the foreign payment. A bank warned that a new law might **demonetize the cash** they held, so Mara asked to **defer payment** on the contract. She also had to **decrease to** half the order after a rival's **bid** undercut her, and she watched the figures **decline**. The **risk** of a **deficit** grew, and a **depression** settled over the office. To **cover** the gap she asked an old friend to **underwrite** the remaining shares; when he hesitated she felt the **undue** pressure of debt. Some partners urged her to **downsize** at once, but she chose to wait rather than let the firm **wither**.

### 4. Standardizing the line

Mara decided to **standardize** the product line, and she had to **modify** the pattern twice to get it right. She would **maintain** the old machines even as the **upkeep** rose, just as she tried to **maintain one's health** by walking each morning. To reach new buyers she began to **export** and took care to **inquire** about local tastes. Her catalogue would **spread over** three pages, she joked, but slowly **progress** came.

### 5. A treaty and a future

In the end the **bilateral treaty** with the overseas buyer was signed, and the **boost in sales** that followed wiped out the **deficit**. The **burden** of doubt lifted; the **decline** became growth, and the **viability** of the firm was no longer in question. Mara looked back at the **prospectus** she had written and the **special** faith that had kept her going. From one **stand** on a **square** to a lasting name, her **business** proved that a clear **vision** and steady **progress** are worth more than any sudden gain.

## 四、学习建议

1. **创业愿景词**：`dealer / business / vision / prospectus / valuable / off-the-peg / leverage / benefit` 勾勒出经销商从零起步的蓝图。
2. **展会投标词**：`stand / square / uniform / ritual / bid / breakage / metal / voucher / wire / telegram / resemblance` 是参加交易会时的高频场景词。
3. **资金危机链**：`demonetize the cash`（禁现）→ `defer payment`（延期付款）→ `decrease to`（减额）→ `decline / deficit / depression`（下滑/赤字/消沉）→ `underwrite`（认购）→ `downsize / wither`（紧缩/凋敝），按因果顺序记忆。
4. **经营改进词**：`standardize / modify / maintain / upkeep / export / inquire / spread over / progress` 落在"改进产品线"这一动作上。
5. **动词搭配**：`boost in sales`（销售增长）、`defer payment`（延期付款）、`decrease to`（减少到）、`maintain one's health`（保持健康）、`spread over`（散开）结构固定，宜整串记忆。
