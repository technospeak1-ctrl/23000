## 一、编排逻辑

本主题是"对外交往"，外交机构、礼节和谈判术语密集，最易写成外交辞令表。我用"奥尔德王国与梅里亚联邦就边界与贸易举行一轮谈判"为主线，按"使团抵达 → 论坛开幕 → 交易磋商 → 强硬插曲 → 条约签订"五幕推进，让每个词落在外交流程的具体环节上：谁出使、谁主持、谁让步、谁施压、谁签约。

| 幕 | 场景 | 承载语义场 | 叙事功能 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The delegation arrives 使团抵达 | 使节/王室/外交礼节 | 建立出使背景 |
| 2 | The forum opens 论坛开幕 | 会议/议程/程序动词 | 铺陈谈判规则 |
| 3 | The bargain 交易磋商 | 贸易/割让/友好名词 | 推进实质谈判 |
| 4 | The hard line 强硬插曲 | 保守/爱国/预防形容词 | 制造冲突波折 |
| 5 | A treaty signed 条约签订 | 合作/互补/纪念词 | 点题收束 |

**设计要点**：`embassy / consulate / ambassador / diplomat / emissary / diplomacy` 沿"使馆—大使—特使—外交"的机构链分布；`royal / monarch / emperor / dynasty / kingdom / realm / majesty / obeisance / protocol` 集中在王室礼节场景；`forum / agenda / convention / debate / preside / concur / declare / rebuff` 组成会议流程；`commerce / domestic products / cession / pact / tribute / charter` 落在交易磋商；`Conservative Party / patriotic emotion / a memorial service / constant temperature / swipe` 把国内舆论压力写进第四幕；11 条常用搭配 `a memorial service`、`complementary characters`、`conservative party`、`constant temperature`、`cooperative effort`、`diplomatic relations`、`domestic products`、`federal government`、`local authority`、`patriotic emotion`、`the royal family` 均整串写入对应场景，避免脱离语境的搭配堆砌。

## 二、英文故事　The Treaty of Two Capitals

### 1. The delegation arrives

At dawn the **embassy** doors opened and **Ambassador** Helen Vale stepped out, an **emissary** of the **royal** court of Alden. Before this posting she had often stood behind **the royal family** at state ceremonies, watching the **monarch** and the ancient **dynasty** he represented. A guard was sent to **escort** her to the **chamber**, and a **commander** of the border guard waited at the door, where the **emperor**'s old **realm** was represented by a single seal. Her **diplomatic** mission was simple: keep **diplomatic relations** with the **federal government** of Meria alive. Across the square, Chancellor Grell waited with his own **diplomat**s and a **consulate** clerk. Grell was a **powerful** man who liked to **dominate** every room, but he understood **protocol** and returned every **obeisance** with care. "Your **majesty** sends greetings," Helen said, though the **kingdom** had no crowned head that year — the **lord** who ruled it was a **premier** in all but title.

### 2. The forum opens

At the **forum**, the **chairman** took the **chair** and called the **council** to order. The **agenda** listed a **convention** on trade and a **debate** over the border. Helen was asked to **preside at** the morning session. The **delegate** from the northern **district**, a **mayor** with a **local authority** mandate, rose to **declare** that his province would never discuss **cession** of land. The **spokesman** for Meria tried to **dictate** terms, but the **counterpart** from Alden refused to be **dictated to**. Grell tried to **declare himself** the guardian of the border, yet his own **supervisor** in the **congress** frowned. It **appeared** that the **senate** would **rebuff** any hurry. Grell tried to **exert pressure on** the delegates, but Helen **concurred** with the **commonwealth** observer that haste would **exert** too high a price. "I **believe** in patience," she said, and moved to **postpone** the vote. She would **appal** to the older treaties for guidance. The **state** of the talks was fragile; even the **chairman** agreed to **close** the session early.

### 3. The bargain

Over the following weeks the two sides traded **commerce** figures. Meria wanted Alden's **domestic products** in its markets; Alden wanted a guarantee for its **frontier** villages. Helen spoke of **amity** and **comity**, of **solidarity** between neighbours, and of the **rapprochement** that both capitals now desired. A **pact** was drafted, with a **tribute** to the patience of the clerks. Grell would **bestow** honours **on** any clerk who kept quiet, and he **bestowed** a medal on a **mayor** who had kept the **local authority** calm. The **internal** maps showed an **inland** lake that sat on the **frontier**, and a **tribe** living there **belonged** to no **state** on paper. Helen argued that the new **relation** between the two governments should **embody** the **independence** of that **tribe** as well as the **commerce** of the towns. Meria had won its **independence** a century earlier, she reminded them; a **charter** for the lake would **constitute** a fair **cession** and a lasting **tribute** to **democracy**.

### 4. The hard line

Not everyone welcomed the bargain. A **conservative** newspaper in Alden, close to the **Conservative Party**, called Helen **alien** to her own class. At a **memorial service** for the soldiers of the last war, the **patriotic emotion** in the hall ran high, A **pacifist** in the crowd shouted that no treaty was worth a war, and an old general took a **swipe** at the diplomats. The **constant** cold of the chamber, kept at a **constant temperature** by the clerks, did little to cool tempers. A **discerning** observer might have noticed that Grell's own **premier** had begun to lose patience; Helen noticed it too. She proposed **preventative** measures — a hotline and a second **convention** — to keep **warfare** from returning, and she refused to let the **memorial** gathering become a rally against the treaty.

### 5. A treaty signed

In the end the **convention** produced a treaty. It was a **cooperative effort** in every sense: the **cooperative** clerks of both **consulate**s worked late, and Helen's **counterpart** proved a man of **complementary characters** — where she was cautious, he was bold. The **leadership** of both delegations signed, and Helen **apologized** for the delay. Years later a **monument** stood in the square, and the **relation** between the two states was called a model of **diplomacy**. Even the **conservative** papers called the pact a **tribute** to patient **diplomacy**, and the **commonwealth** observers wrote that **amity**, not **warfare**, had been the true **tribute** of that winter.

## 四、学习建议

1. **外交机构链**：`embassy` → `ambassador` / `emissary` → `diplomat` → `diplomacy` → `consulate` → `protocol` / `obeisance`，按"馆—人—礼"顺序记，配上 `diplomatic relations` 一起复习。
2. **王室词群**：`royal` / `the royal family` / `monarch` / `emperor` / `dynasty` / `kingdom` / `realm` / `majesty` / `lord` / `premier` 集中在第一幕，是外交场合的称谓系统。
3. **会议流程动词**：`preside at` / `debate` / `declare` / `declare oneself` / `dictate` / `dictate to` / `concur` / `rebuff` / `postpone` / `close` 按会议进行的顺序记，动作层次分明。
4. **谈判名词**：`agenda` / `convention` / `forum` / `council` / `delegate` / `counterpart` / `spokesman` / `pact` / `cession` / `charter` / `tribute` 写实质磋商，`commerce` / `domestic products` / `frontier` 写利益焦点。
5. **国内舆论与收束**：`conservative` / `Conservative Party` / `patriotic emotion` / `a memorial service` / `constant temperature` / `swipe` 写外部压力；`cooperative effort` / `complementary characters` / `amity` / `rapprochement` / `monument` 写和解结果，正反两组对照记忆。
