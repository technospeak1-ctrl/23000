## 一、编排逻辑

本主题是"工作技能与方法"，词表既有 portable、typewriter 这类器物词，也有 proficiency、coordination 这类方法词，还有 annex、apportion 这类动作词，容易写成"办公用品清单 + 定义列表"。我用"新员工诺拉在车间从笨手笨脚到独当一面，最后向新人传授工作方法"作主线，按"初入车间 → 计划失败 → 改良方法 → 技艺成熟 → 传授方法"五幕推进，让每个词都落在具体人物动作上：

| 幕 | 场景 | 承载语义场 | 叙事功能 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The first morning 第一天上班 | 人物/器物词 + 描述性形容词 | 建立场景与人物 |
| 2 | A plan that failed 失败的计划 | 故障/阻碍/负面情绪词 | 制造冲突与转折 |
| 3 | A better method 改良的方法 | 方法/抽象名词 + 动作动词 | 展现方法的力量 |
| 4 | A skilled hand 娴熟的手 | 熟练度/成果词 | 人物成长的高潮 |
| 5 | Passing the method on 传授方法 | 全部核心词的回收复盘 | 点题升华 |

**设计要点**：`workshop / typewriter / portable computer / memo / microphone` 集中在"第一天上班"，让器物随人物动作出现；`malfunction / oversight / laceration / impediment / jargon` 集中在"失败的计划"一幕，形成问题链；`revision / correction / coordination / incorporation / innovation / catalyst` 集中在"改良的方法"一幕，表现方法改进的过程；`proficiency / feat / attainment / freshness` 用于"娴熟的手"一幕写成长；结尾用 `collective / empirical / entitled` 等回收主题词，点明"方法是可以传授的共同习惯"。

## 二、英文故事　The Method of the Workshop

### 1. The first morning

On her first morning at the **workshop**, Nora felt the **adjustment** keenly. The old **typewriter** sat beside a shiny **portable computer**; a **memo** lay pinned to the board, and a **microphone** waited for the briefing. Mr. Hale, the **administrator**, gathered a **bunch** of new hires into a **circle** and, **conventionally**, began with the rules. He was **strict** with everyone but fair. "You are all **entitled** to fair pay," he said, "and our **qualified employees** set the standard." Around them a **cluster** of **semi-skilled workers** checked the **machine**, a **jobbing gardener** trimmed the yard, and an old **servant** swept the floor. Nora watched a **handful** of senior hands work with a **skilful** touch. This was her new **circle**, and the work was far from **ease**.

### 2. A plan that failed

By the third week, an **impractical plan** from head office had brought the line to a stop. A **malfunction** in the main **machine** stopped the belts; a small **oversight** in the logbook meant nobody noticed a **laceration** in a worker's glove until he **slit** his thumb on a sharp edge. The **stress** of the **delay** made some workers **overtire**, and a few began to **tattle** about who was to blame. Nora tried to name the **impediment** in plain words, but two supervisors **antagonized** each other with **competing** orders and **jargon** nobody understood; one even tried to **postdate** the fault report. The new filing **cipher** on the forms confused everyone, and the direction of the project seemed lost. An **unpromising future** stretched ahead. Mr. Hale, though, trusted **empirical knowledge** more than guesses. He asked Nora to write a **statement** of what she had actually seen, and her **stubbornness** about accuracy finally worked in her favor.

### 3. A better method

Mr. Hale told her to **apply** those notes. With his support she made a careful **revision** of the old method and a simple **correction** to the logbook rules. She had learned to **apportion** the daily tasks fairly, and she would **annex** a short check-list to each work order. A small **innovation** — a shared board — became the **catalyst** for better **coordination** between shifts. The **incorporation** of short breaks kept the **alternation** of work and rest healthy, and the team's **effectiveness** rose. They **decided** to **employ** two people to check every **operation** together. A modest **appropriation** from the budget bought new tools, and Nora weighed the **weight** of each part before lifting it. Her chance to **proffer** a suggestion **coincided with** a visit from the regional chief. She did not **delay**; she **hastened** to show the **diversification** of tasks that had raised the group's **capacity**. The **necessity** of a good method was now plain to all.

### 4. A skilled hand

Within a year Nora's **proficiency** was obvious. She had once been **reliant** upon others' notes; now **colleagues** came to her for help — a **reliance** she never took lightly. She could take apart a **mechanism** and rebuild it, and she understood the **mobility** of parts between the two lines. The old hands called her quick **feat** of repair a real **attainment**. She brought **freshness** to every meeting, and her **view** of work as a shared craft won respect. The **weight** of responsibility sat easily on her shoulders now.

### 5. Passing the method on

In the end Nora stood before a new **cluster** of trainees in the same **workshop**, the **microphone** in her hand. "A method is not a **cipher** kept by the few," she said. "It is a **collective** habit, learned by doing and checked by **empirical** observation. Whether you are a **jobbing** helper with a **portable computer** or a **semi-skilled** operator at an old **machine**, you are **entitled** to learn. Be **strict** with the small things, be **skilful** with your tools, and never call a plan **impractical** until you have tried it. An **unpromising** start can still become a fine **feat** if you keep a cool head and a **handful** of good rules." The newcomers wrote every word down, and the old **typewriter** clicked in the corner as if it agreed.

## 四、学习建议

1. **按场景成组记器物词**：`workshop / machine / mechanism / typewriter / portable computer / memo / microphone` 都是工作现场的东西，故事里让它们伴随人物动作出现（打字机咔哒响、话筒传到手里），比孤立背单词更牢。
2. **把"方法链"当一条线记**：`impractical plan → malfunction / oversight → revision / correction → coordination / incorporation → innovation / catalyst → proficiency / feat`。这是一条"失败—改进—熟练"的因果链，正好是故事五幕的骨架。
3. **注意词形与搭配**：`be strict with`（对……严格）、`be reliant on/upon`（依赖）、`be skilful in/at`（擅长）、`coincide with`（恰逢）、`employ sb. to do sth.`（雇某人做某事）五条搭配都要连冠词和介词一起记；原文中分别写作 "was **strict** with"、"been **reliant** upon"、"be **skilful** with"、"**coincided with**"、"**employ** two people to check"。
4. **同根词一起辨析**：`reliant`（形容词，依赖的）— `reliance`（名词，依赖）；`skilful`（娴熟的）— `skill`（技巧）；`effective / effectiveness`（有效的/效力）；`innovation / innovative`（革新/革新的）。故事第 4 幕专门让 reliant 与 reliance 同场出现。
5. **动词的书面色彩**：`annex / apportion / proffer / postdate / overtire / tattle` 偏书面或特定场景，已在正文各出现一次并配具体动作（附清单、分任务、提建议、填迟日期、累坏工人、打小报告），复习时回想这些动作画面即可。
