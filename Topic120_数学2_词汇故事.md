## 一、编排逻辑

本主题是"数学 2"，词项集中在几何测量、四则运算、图表数据、概率规则四条线，且不少词与 Topic 119 重叠或近义。我用"数学教练 Mrs. Vance 带队在地区数学挑战赛前集训五天"作主线，按"几何教室 → 代数黑板 → 图表与数据 → 模拟测验 → 成绩揭晓"五幕推进，让每个术语落到教具、步骤或考题上：

| 幕 | 场景 | 承载语义场 | 叙事功能 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The geometry room 几何教室 | 图形/平行/交点/三角测量 | 用水准仪与地砖建立形状词 |
| 2 | The algebra board 代数黑板 | 四则/公式/函数/算法 | 用解题步骤串起运算词 |
| 3 | Graphs and data 图表与数据 | 统计图/数值/比例/微积分 | 把数据词落在图表上 |
| 4 | The practice test 模拟测验 | 奇偶/机会/阶段/目标 | 用考场规则铺陈评价词 |
| 5 | The result 成绩揭晓 | 顶点词 + 复盘动词 | 收束与点题 |

**设计要点**：11 条搭配全部落在真实课堂动作上——`run parallel to` 是两条永不相交的线，`square meter` 是量地砖，`two/three-dimensional` 是教练对平面与立体的对比，`be equivalent to` 是等式关系，`computer graphics` 是学生画的图，`linear algebra`/`mathematical calculation`/`quantitative analysis` 是黑板内容，`odd number`/`even chance`/`fundamental element`/`add up` 是考题与提醒。几何线按 `side→angle→concurrence→triangulation` 展开，运算链按 `plus→minus→addition→subtraction→multiplication→division→dividend→remainder` 展开；`minimal / null / nonentity / unspecified / round / short-range` 各贴在一种"答案状态"上，避免清单感。

## 二、英文故事　The Mathematics Challenge

### 1. The geometry room

Mrs. Vance opened the geometry room for the district mathematics challenge. "Geometry begins with a **side** and an **angle**," she said, drawing an **equilateral** triangle and an **octagonal** tile on the board. She set a **compass** on each desk and asked the class to draw a circle, then used its **diameter** to find the **radius** and then measured the **perimeter** of a **rectangle**. "Two lines that **run parallel to** each other never **intersect**," she explained, "while a **sloping** line will cross them; the point where three lines meet is their **concurrence**." A **square** tile was measured in **square meters**, and a small **cube** beside it was a **cubic** solid. "A **two-dimensional** diagram lies flat; a **three-dimensional** one has depth," she said. An old **geometrician** had drawn a **triangulation** of the field outside, and a print of **cubism** hung on the wall, where flat shapes were broken into many small planes. "Even a painting obeys **mathematical** rules," Vance said.

### 2. The algebra board

On the algebra board Vance wrote a **fundamental** rule first: a **fundamental element** of any expression is its smallest part. "A **plus** sign and a **minus** sign are the marks of **addition** and **subtraction**," she said; "**multiplication** and **division** are the other two. In a **division** the **dividend** is the number being split, and what is left over is the **remainder**." She asked a student to **add** a column and **add up** the **multiple** values, then to **multiply** the sum by three; another was told to **compute** the answer twice. "A **formula** is a rule, an **equation** is a statement that two sides are equal, and a **function** turns one number into another," she went on. Vance wrote a short **algorithm** — a list of **steps** for solving the problem — and said one **time** through was not enough. "Work for a **fortnight** if you must," she laughed, "and weigh each **ounce** of effort. A blank answer is a **nonentity**, not a **null** value; a **minimal** answer can still be **equivalent** to a fuller one — in symbols, x **is equivalent to** y. Check the **norm** for the class, and keep the **range** of your answers reasonable."

### 3. Graphs and data

For the data session Vance pinned a **graph** and a **diagram** on the wall. "These **data** come from last year's contest," she said; "a **statistical** chart shows the spread, and a **quantitative** analysis compares the **ratio** of correct answers." She pointed at a **numerical** label and a **round** number on the axis: "When a value is round, it is given to the nearest whole unit; an **unspecified** value is left open." **Additionally**, a small table listed the **time** spent on each item. A student drew a **graphic** panel on the computer — "**computer graphics**," Vance named it — and plotted a **linear** line beside a curve from **calculus**. "In **linear algebra** lines are studied together," she added, and wrote a **mathematical calculation** under the chart to prove the point. "These results **show** up clearly," she said. "Now **show** me the **short-range** trend for the first week only."

### 4. The practice test

Before the practice test Vance handed out a sheet of **odd** numbers and even ones. "An **odd** number cannot be split into two equal whole parts," she said; "an **even** number can. If you guess between two answers, you have an **even chance** of being right." She set a **short-range** goal for the first hour and a longer one for the whole contest. "The **nature** of a problem matters more than its length," she said; "a **stage** is reached when one **step** is done, and you must **maximize** your score by checking every line. Do not leave an **unspecified** blank; write the **round** answer if the exact one is messy."

### 5. The result

When the results came in, the **culmination** of the term's work was plain: every **mathematical** claim on the board had been tested. Vance drew a final **diagram** and a short **graph** on the board; the **data** spread across a narrow **range**, and the **norm** for the class was high. "I **show** you not only the answer but the **addition**, the **division** and the **subtraction** behind it," she said. A student who had used a **fundamental element** incorrectly saw the error at once. "The whole of **mathematics**," Vance concluded, "is built from small **steps**, and every **side** of a figure has its **angle**."

## 四、学习建议

1. **与 Topic 119 对照记**：`cubic / three-dimensional / radius / diameter / perimeter / rectangle / square / plus / minus / odd / addition / multiplication / division / equation / formula / function / graph / data / range / ratio / step` 等在两篇中复现，把两篇的故事叠着看，能分清哪些是共享核心词、哪些是本篇新词（`algorithm / dividend / remainder / concurrence / triangulation`）。
2. **四则运算一条链**：`plus → minus → addition → subtraction → multiplication → division`，再接 `dividend / remainder / multiple`，配合 `add / add up / compute / multiply / maximize` 五个动词，想象自己按步骤算一道题。
3. **答案状态成对辨析**：`null / nonentity / unspecified` 都指"没有值"，但语气不同；`minimal`（最小）与 `fundamental`（基本）、`round`（取整）与 `numerical`（数值的）、`short-range`（近程）与 `range`（范围）各成一组，放在"批改试卷"的场景里记。
4. **几何测量链**：`side → angle → concurrence → triangulation` 是"边—角—交点—三角测量"的递进；`equilateral / octagonal / square / cubic / sloping / parallel` 各贴一种图形，配 `compass / square meter` 两个工具。
5. **搭配整体记**：`run parallel to`、`be equivalent to`、`even chance`、`square meter` 都是数学写作高频短语，`computer graphics` 和 `linear algebra` 是学科名，整体背诵比拆词更快。
