## 一、编排逻辑

本主题围绕"音乐节与演出录制"，可归为 4 个语义场：演出评价形容词、音乐与乐器名词、影视制作与场次名词、演出录制动作。我用"一场音乐节从排练到发行"作主线，按"筹备 → 演出 → 录制 → 发行"推进：

| 幕 | 场景 | 承载语义场 | 叙事功能 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Before the festival 节前 | 评价形容词 + 场地名词 | 建立情境（leading 阵容） |
| 2 | The performance 演出 | 乐器、音乐名词 | 展示音乐（sonorous 音色） |
| 3 | In the studio 录音室 | 影视制作名词 | 制造情节（录制与分类） |
| 4 | Release 发行 | 剩余名词 + 动作动词 | 收尾升华（娱乐的流传） |

**设计要点**：把 `comic`、`histrionic`、`leading`、`pop`、`sonorous`、`spectacular` 放进同一场演出，形成风格与评价的对照；`be absorbed in`、`be selected`、`scatter strength` 等搭配承担情节转折。

## 二、英文故事　The Festival Recording

### 1. Before the festival

The **festival** was the **leading** event of the **epoch**, and the **attendance** promised to be huge. A **comic actor** and a **spectacular** **magician** were billed; the **cast** included a **violin** virtuoso and a **pop** **vocalist**. A **compact disc** of last year's **repertoire** sold at a **booth**; the **auditorium** was being cleaned, and the **screen** was being set. The **programme** listed a **quartet**, a **solo** and a **vaudeville** **skit**; the **classification** of the **episode** as **histrionic** puzzled no one. A **discrepancy** between the **copyright** date and the **trailer** date was fixed. "We want a **spectacular success**," the manager said, "not **overexposure**." The **audience** was **absorbed** in the **narration** of the **trailer**.

### 2. The performance

The **music** began with a **sonorous** **trumpet** and a **drum**; a **guitar** and a **marionette** show followed. The **melody** of the **symphony** was **pop**-tinged; the **rhythm** and **tempo** were perfect. A **quartet** played a **solo**, a **violin** answered, and the **stereo** **volume** was turned up. The **vocalist** sang a **tune** that caused a **sensation**; the **magician**'s **marionette** danced to the **jazz**. A **rehearsal** of the **rap** number had gone well, and now the **pastime** of the crowd was pure **recreation**. The **sonorous** notes **amused** the children; even a **skyscraper**-tall **screen** could not contain the **spectacle**. An **epoch**-making **episode** of the **programme** was born.

### 3. In the studio

After the show, the **studio** crew got to work. A **projector** and a **screen** were set; the **film** would be **projected** at the next **festival**. The **assortment** of **instruments** — drum, guitar, trumpet, violin — was **classified** by an **assistant**; the **copyright** forms were **fabricated** in triplicate. The **narration** was re-recorded, and the **episode** was **selected** for the **programme**. Some footage showed **overexposure**; the **discrepancy** in the **volume** was corrected, and a **motion** to **classify** the film as a comedy was passed. A **trailer** and a **compact disc** were planned; the **repertoire** was **scattered** across three **discs**. "Don't **scatter strength**," the producer warned; "select the best **solo** and build the **sensation** around it." The **understudy** of the **leading** **actor** was **selected** too.

### 4. Release

The **compact disc** and the **trailer** were released, and the **audience** bought them by the thousand. The **sensation** of the **festival** lived on in the **stereo** **volume** of every car; the **pop** **tune** and the **sonorous** **trumpet** **absorbed** a whole generation. The **magician** and the **comic actor** became **leading** names; the **vaudeville** and the **rap** were **classified** as a new **epoch** of **recreation**. A **skyscraper** **poster** announced the **spectacular success**. Looking back, I **select**ed one **episode** from the **assortment** and played it again: the **histrionic** **marionette**, the **solo** **violin**, the **sonorous** **trumpet**. The **narration** said it best — that **pastime** and **recreation**, rightly made, outlast the **festival** and the **screen** itself.

## 四、学习建议

1. 按录制流程记：节前（festival、attendance、auditorium、programme）→ 演出（trumpet、drum、guitar、symphony、solo）→ 录音室（studio、projector、copyright、narration、trailer）→ 发行（compact disc、audience、sensation、volume）。
2. 声音词成组：sonorous、melody、rhythm、tempo、symphony、stereo 都与音乐相关。
3. 搭配优先：`be absorbed in`、`be selected`、`scatter strength`、`compact disc`、`spectacular success` 是高频考点。
4. 词形意识：动词过去式/被动要会认（absorbed、classified、fabricated、projected、scattered、selected）。
