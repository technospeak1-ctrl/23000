## 一、编排逻辑

本主题延续功能词，但更偏"演讲与评述语篇"——如何在陈述中补充、限定、强调和转折。孤立记忆这些词毫无语境，因此我用"经济学家艾伦研究房价飙升并在研讨会上陈述、回应质疑"作主线，按"背景 → 数据 → 陈述 → 答辩 → 结论"推进：

| 幕 | 场景 | 承载语义场 | 叙事功能 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The brief 课题背景 | 时间/延续/迄今 + 范围形容词 | 铺垫问题 |
| 2 | The numbers 数据 | 近似/程度/频率副词 | 呈现发现 |
| 3 | At the podium 讲台陈述 | 补充/限定/强调标记词 | 正式陈述 |
| 4 | The challenge 答辩 | 让步/转折/对立词 | 回应质疑 |
| 5 | The conclusion 结论 | 核心动词 + 抽象名词 | 收束论点 |

**设计要点**：演讲标记 `namely`（即）、`incidentally`（附带）、`inasmuch`（因为）、`hitherto`（迄今）、`whatsoever`（任何）在陈述与答辩中自然出现；程度词 `approximately`、`fractionally`、`comparatively`、`enormously`、`exclusively` 用于数据描写；`conclude / connect / correlate / emphasize / overshade` 等动词承担结语的推理动作。搭配 `exorbitant price`、`a slight difference`、`upward mobility`、`go nowhere`、`not necessarily` 均嵌入正文。

## 二、英文故事　The Price of a Roof

### 1. The brief

**Hitherto**, no one had explained why the city's rents had risen so sharply. Alan, an economist with a **mere** interest in housing at first, was asked to lead the study. The **primordial** question was simple; within the **confines** of the city, the problem was **obvious**: prices had climbed **continuously** for a decade, and the trend **continued** into the present year. **Nowhere** else in the country had prices risen so fast. The **magnitude** of the change was startling. **Inasmuch** as the council wanted an answer before the election, the work had to be swift. Alan read every report **duly**, noting the **generality** of official claims and the **exception** hidden in the small print. **Anyhow**, he was determined to find the **mainspring** of the crisis, and above all to understand the **preoccupation** of ordinary families: could they still afford a roof?

### 2. The numbers

The figures were striking. Rents had risen **approximately** forty percent, **comparatively** more than in neighbouring towns. A **fractionally** smaller increase appeared in the suburbs, but it was a **slight difference** at best. The growth was **enormously** driven by one factor, **mainly** the shortage of land, and **mostly** by speculation **exclusively** in the downtown core; prices rose **continually**, a pattern that had **invariably** held before. Alan **highly** doubted the official story. In **meantime**, he built a second model; **meanwhile**, a colleague checked it. The **continuity** of the data was **eminently** clear, and the **excess** of demand over supply was **doubtless** real. **Naturally**, prices followed. **Necessarily**, somebody would be hurt. **Inevitably**, the poorest tenants would **go nowhere** — no relief **whatsoever** was in sight. **Notably**, the **opposite** trend appeared in the **peripheral** districts, where rents fell **somewhat** — an **inconspicuous** but real shift that had been **scarcely** noticed. **Seemingly** contradictory, these numbers were in fact **explicable**.

### 3. At the podium

At the seminar, Alan spoke **briefly** at first, then at length. "Three causes," he said, "**namely** land shortage, speculation, and lax lending. **Incidentally**, the third is the least studied." He was **particular** about his wording. "It is **not necessarily** true that wages fell; **rather**, rents simply rose **fractionally** faster than pay." He spoke **wholly** from the data, **immediately** addressing the **exorbitant price** of downtown flats. His **uppermost** concern was fairness. **Presumably**, the council already knew the answer; **doubtless** some did not. "**Hence** the need for action," he concluded. **Likewise**, other cities had seen the same pattern. He was **eminently** qualified to say so, having studied the **exception** in the **meantime**. **For the meantime**, he would leave recommendations open. **Nevertheless**, he made one point **notably** clear: a **wholly satisfactory solution** must address supply, not just price.

### 4. The challenge

A critic rose. "Your model is **converse** to common sense," he said. "You say land is scarce, **whereas** building has boomed." Alan was not **finicky** about being challenged. "**Despite** the boom," he replied, "permits were **nowhere** near enough." The critic tried the **opposite** tack: "Then do exactly the opposite — freeze rents!" "**Notwithstanding** your point," said Alan, "a freeze would **overshadow** the real issue, which is supply." **Nonetheless**, he stayed courteous. **Although** the critic was loud, **even so** the data stood. **Regardless** of the noise, Alan **connected** the dots: speculation, scarcity, and lending were **continuously** linked. He **emphasized** that the **upward mobility** of the young was at stake. **Seemingly** simple, the question was **nowhere** near settled. **Somewhat** frustrated, the critic sat down.

### 5. The conclusion

Alan **concluded** that no single cause sufficed. He **connected** the **magnitude** of the rise to a **correlation** between lending and land prices — the two **correlated** closely in every model. He **emphasized** the point once more: "A **seemingly impossible task** is not impossible; it is merely hard." His **conclusion** **overshadowed** the critic's objections. The **exception** he had found in the small print became the rule. By the **latter** part of the decade, the city had built more homes, and the **preoccupation** of families eased. The **council** adopted his plan, **whereby** supply rose and prices steadied. **Since** then, the **continuity** of that policy has held. **All the same**, Alan knew the work was **nowhere** near finished: housing, like any **generality**, hides **particular** pains, and **whatsoever** the forecast, every family still needs a roof.

## 四、学习建议

1. **演讲三标记**：`namely`（即，用于列举）、`incidentally`（顺便一提，插入新话题）、`inasmuch as`（鉴于，正式版 because）——三者是学术陈述的高频信号词，故事第 3 幕集中演示。
2. **"几乎不"梯度**：`nowhere`（无处）、`scarcely`、`barely`（见 043）、`rarely`、`seldom`——搭配 `nowhere near`（远远不）很实用。
3. **让步三兄弟**：`despite`（+名词）、`notwithstanding`（正式，可 +名词/从句）、`although`（+从句）——注意词性差别，`nonetheless` 则是副词"尽管如此"。
4. **程度强化**：`enormously`、`highly`、`eminently`、`wholly`、`exclusively`、`predominantly`（见043）——越formal的演讲越爱用 `-ly` 副词限定。
5. **数据写作链**：`approximately`（约）→ `fractionally`（极小地）→ `comparatively`（相对）→ `a slight difference`（细微差别）——描写数字时的精确阶梯。
6. **词形对照**：`continuous`（形容词，连续的）与 `continuously`（副词）；`continual`（形容词，频繁的）与 `continually`（副词）——`continuous` 强调不间断，`continual` 强调频繁重复，故事中 prices rose **continually**（屡次上涨）与 climbed **continuously**（持续攀升）恰成对照。
7. **动词五步**：研究结语的固定动作 —— `connect`（关联）→ `correlate`（相关）→ `emphasize`（强调）→ `conclude`（结论）→ `overshadow`（使失色），可套用于任何议论文的写作。
