## 一、编排逻辑

本主题是"技术设备"，词表由设备实物（`apparatus / blade / gear / rotary pump / circuitry / wiring`）、技术状态（`breakdown / fault / impurity / moisture / precision`）、技术人员（`mechanic / electrician / practitioner`）与维护动作（`devise / embed / automate / computerize / intensify`）四条线组成，另有 27 条常用搭配。我以"年轻工程师莉娅受电力公司之托，带上老机械师山姆，检修山丘上一台停摆的风力涡轮机，从故障诊断到改造再到重新运转"为主线，按"技术室 → 齿轮故障 → 加装改造 → 运转测试 → 高科技时代的思考"五幕推进，让每个词都成为一台机器、一次测量或一道工序：

| 幕 | 场景 | 承载语义场 | 叙事功能 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The technical room 技术室 | 设备实物 + 技术/电子形容词 + 人员 | 建立场景与工具谱系 |
| 2 | A fault in the gear 齿轮故障 | 故障/损耗名词 + 精确形容词 + 摩擦动词 | 用一次诊断带出负面状态词 |
| 3 | The retrofit 加装改造 | 设计与自动化动词 + 系统名词 | 把修复落到具体操作 |
| 4 | Testing in operation 运转测试 | 运转/维护搭配 + 寿命与到场名词 | 验证成果 |
| 5 | The high-tech age 高科技时代 | 补充资金/读物 + 收束反思 | 点题升华 |

**设计要点**：`apparatus / assemblage / detector / indicator / instrumentation / circuitry / wiring` 集中在技术室，作为被贴标签、被检查的实物；`abrade / impurity / moisture / calibre / fault / breakdown` 在故障幕串成"磨损—湿气—杂质—口径不对—查出故障—停机"的因果链；`devise / embed / computerize / connect / automate / curb / intensify` 在改造幕成为莉娅的一连串动作；27 条搭配全部落进具体画面——`browse through` 是翻手册与检索系统，`be embedded in` 是传感器嵌入机壳，`precise measurement` 是量间隙，`in operation / regular maintenance / technical standard` 是运转与验收，`high-tech age / supplementary funds / supplementary reading` 收在结尾的反思与后续计划。

## 二、英文故事　The Turbine on the Hill

### 1. The technical room

In the **high-tech age**, a **power company** had asked Leah Tan, a young **practitioner** of **mechanical engineering**, to inspect a **wind turbine** on the hill. The **wind power** it made fed a nearby village, but the **configuration** of its parts was old. In the **technical** room at the foot of the tower, every **apparatus** was labelled: a **rotary pump**, an **electric drill**, an **electric appliance**, and a small **assemblage** of spare **blades**. A **detector** and an **indicator** sat beside the **instrumentation** panel, and the **circuitry** was drawn on a clean **format** of paper. **Auxiliary machinery** stood behind the main frame, an **interactive telephone system** connected the room to the **power company**, and a box of **electronic goods** held spare parts. The old **mechanic**, Sam, was **browsing through** a manual, while an **electrician** checked the **wiring** and the **electrical power** line. "Nothing here stays **in operation** by magic," Sam said, tapping the **control** switch.

### 2. A fault in the gear

On the tower the **blade** had begun to **abrade** against its housing. A **technical hitch** was to blame: a thin **gear** had worn down, and a **parts shortage** meant no new one was in stock. **Moisture** had crept into the **rotary pump**, and the **impurity** in the oil showed on the **indicator**. The **calibre** of the spare bolt was wrong by a hair. Sam used a **detector** to find the **fault**, but the reading was **sophisticated** enough to confuse a beginner. "This is not **precise** enough," Leah said, wiping **moisture** from the **circuitry**. The **breakdown** had cost a full day of **wind power**, and the village had noticed.

### 3. The retrofit

Leah decided to **devise** a fix. A small sensor **was embedded in** the housing — the **know-how** came from her old notes — and she **computerized** the readings so a laptop could map them. She **connected** the sensor to the **control** box, planned to **automate** the alarm, and **curbed** false readings with a simple filter. The **synthetically** blended oil resisted **impurity** better than the old kind. She **intensified efforts** to test the rig. The **coding** was written in a simple **format**, and a **system analysis** showed where the **transmission** lost power. Sam shook his head at the **sophistication** of it all, but he had to admit the **technical standard** was met. "This **tandem technology** is new to me," he said. Leah then took a **precise measurement** of the gap.

### 4. Testing in operation

By noon the **wind turbine** was **in operation** again. The **rotary pump** turned, the **electrical power** flowed, and the **indicator** stayed steady. A **repair technician** from the village arrived with spare **electronic goods**, and an **electric appliance** in the room hummed as Leah paused to **browse through** the **retrieval system** for an old **supplementary reading** on blade wear. The **turnout** at the site was small — only three engineers — but the **longevity** of the machine mattered more than the crowd. "We need **regular maintenance**, not heroics," Leah said.

### 5. The high-tech age

That evening Leah wrote her report. The **high-tech age**, she noted, did not remove the need for a good **mechanic**; it only changed the tools. **Supplementary funds** would pay for a new **detector**, and **regular maintenance** would keep the **wind turbine** running for years. She filed the **technical standard** sheet in the **retrieval system** and sent a copy to the **power company**. The village's **interactive telephone system** carried her voice to the mayor, who promised to **intensify efforts** on training. On her desk lay a **supplementary reading** about **tandem technology**, a **rotary pump** diagram, and a note that the **impurity** in the oil had come from **moisture** in the air. "A **sophisticated** machine," she wrote, "still needs a plain **electric drill** and a steady hand." The **turnout** at the next training session was twice as large.

## 四、学习建议

1. **按检修流程记词**：入场 `technical room / apparatus / detector / instrumentation / wiring` → 诊断 `abrade / impurity / moisture / calibre / fault / breakdown` → 改造 `devise / embed / computerize / automate / curb / intensify` → 验收 `in operation / precise measurement / technical standard / regular maintenance`。沿"检查—找故障—修复—运转"走一遍，词就带上动作。
2. **设备搭配成串**：`rotary pump / electric drill / electric appliance / electronic goods / auxiliary machinery / interactive telephone system / retrieval system / wind turbine / wind power` 都在第一幕的技术室里出现，按"屋里有哪些机器"成组回忆；`repair technician / mechanic / electrician / practitioner` 则是与这些机器打交道的人。
3. **负面状态词同框**：`abrade / impurity / moisture / fault / breakdown / technical hitch / parts shortage` 一起组成"故障现场"，与第四幕的 `in operation / longevity / regular maintenance` 形成停机与恢复运转的对照。
4. **动词搭配记施动者**：`browse through a manual`（山姆翻手册）、`be embedded in the housing`（传感器嵌入机壳）、`intensify efforts to test`（莉娅加大力度）、`computerize the readings / automate the alarm / curb false readings`（莉娅改造读数），每个动词都有明确主语， recall 时按"谁做了什么"取词。
5. **冷僻词留痕**：`calibre`（口径）、`know-how`（技术诀窍）、`longevity`（寿命）、`turnout`（到场人数）、`sophistication`（精密复杂）较书面，均在正文出现，结合涡轮机检修的具体画面记忆其专业色彩。
