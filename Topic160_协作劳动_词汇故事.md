## 一、编排逻辑

本主题是"协作劳动"，39 个词里既有描写劳动态度的形容词（dutiful / perfunctory / restless），又有具体协作动作（carry / interact / rub / simonize）。我把它们编进"一群村民合力翻修旧礼堂、为丰收节做准备"的主线，让每个词都落在某位真实人物的具体动作或一次争执上，按"接下任务 → 第一天上工 → 午后争执 → 重归于好 → 丰收晚宴感悟"五幕推进：

| 幕 | 场景 | 承载语义场 | 叙事功能 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The undertaking 接下任务 | 任务/工作量/装备 + 正面态度形容词 | 建立场景、分配角色 |
| 2 | The first day 第一天上工 | 体力协作动词 + 材质/工具名词 | 铺陈劳动动作 |
| 3 | The quarrel 午后争执 | 负面态度词 + 言说/拒绝动词 | 制造冲突、推动因果 |
| 4 | Making amends 重归于好 | 正面副词 + enjoy/recant 类动词 | 化解矛盾、回落主题 |
| 5 | What we learned 丰收晚宴 | 近义名词/形容词辨析 | 点题升华 |

**设计要点**：`undertaking / workload / outfit / repair` 集中在"接任务"一幕，由协调人 Maria 分配；`carry / rub / simonize / straighten / erase / remove / slide / wag` 这些动作落在施工的村民和老牧羊人的狗身上，每人手上有具体活儿；`dutiful / pellucid / good / tightly` 与 `facile / perfunctory / restless / chutzpah / impuissance / revulsion` 在前后两幕形成态度对照；`explain away / explain oneself / refuse a draft / refuse an application / far removed from / slide into` 六条搭配全部嵌进"争执"一幕的因果里；结尾用 `example / interlocutor / repartee` 收束"协作"主题。

## 二、英文故事　The Village Hall

### 1. The undertaking

The village hall had stood empty for years, and at last the neighbours gathered for the **undertaking**. Maria, our **dutiful** coordinator, unfolded the plan with a **pellucid** clarity that made every task seem **possible**. "Our **overriding** priority is safety," she said, "so the **good** work must be done **tightly**." She handed out the **outfit** — gloves, brushes and wax — and posted the **workload** on the wall. Old Bart the **shepherd** arrived with his flock and brought a thick woven **mat** to wipe our boots. He had seen many a **repair** in his long life, and we took him as our **example** of patient labour. By noon the crew began to **interact**, and the first **synchronous** strokes of the brushes rang through the empty hall.

### 2. The first day

We **carried** the benches inside and set them in rows. Tom, a **restless** young man, tried to wax the floor but did it in a **perfunctory** way, and soon the shine began to **slide** off. "You must **rub** it in circular strokes," Maria said **gently**, showing him how to **simonize** the old wood until it gleamed. We **straightened** the crooked chairs and **erased** the graffiti from the walls. Bart's sheep-dog **wagged** its tail at the door. Everything went **synchronously** now: while some sanded, others **removed** the broken boards and fitted new ones. The job was far **removed** from a quick fix; it was honest, patient work, and we began to share ideas as we worked.

### 3. The quarrel

By afternoon a dispute broke out over money and method. Tom claimed the job was **facile** and called Maria's careful rules mere **chutzpah**. His **impuissance** showed when he tried to lift a beam alone and failed, and a flicker of **revulsion** crossed the older workers' faces. As their **interlocutor**, I urged him to **explain** himself, but he fired back a sharp **repartee** and tried to **explain** away the crooked shelf he had nailed up. The trouble deepened when the supplier **refused** the draft for the lumber and the council **refused** an application for a late permit. Tom **shrugged** and was ready to **recant** his promise to help, but Maria held firm. "We **equate** a promise with a debt," she said, "and we are **indebted** to everyone who trusts us." Slowly the **restless** mood began to **slide** into shame.

### 4. Making amends

The next morning the mood changed. We **removed** the bad shelf, **erased** the marks and **straightened** every board again. This time Tom worked **dutifully** and **gently**, and he even **enjoyed** himself as the floor began to shine. He **carried** the wax and **rubbed** it in slow circles, and together we **simonized** the whole hall until the wood was **pellucid**. Bart's dog **wagged** at the door again. When the bank honoured the draft and the permit came through, everyone cheered. "This is a **good** day's work," Maria said, and we all felt **indebted** to one another.

### 5. What we learned

At the harvest supper, held in the finished hall, Bart the **shepherd** raised a glass. "No **undertaking** is **facile** when many hands share it," he said. "One **example** of **dutiful** work teaches a hundred." We had learned that a **restless** heart can find peace in a **synchronous** rhythm, that **perfunctory** effort breeds **revulsion**, and that patient **repair** earns trust. The floor was **tightly** joined, the plan **pellucid**, and our **repartee** now only kind jokes. We were **indebted** to the **outfit** and to the **mat** at the door, but most of all to the **interlocutor** who kept us talking until the **possible** became real.

## 四、学习建议

1. **协作动词成组记**：`carry / interact / rub / simonize / straighten / erase / remove / slide / wag` 都是看得见的动作，故事里分别配了具体对象（搬长凳、擦地板、扶椅子），比孤立背单词牢固得多。
2. **态度词正反对照**：正面 `dutiful / pellucid / good / tightly / synchronous`，负面 `facile / perfunctory / restless / chutzpah / impuissance / revulsion`；故事第 1、2 幕和第 3 幕正好形成鲜明对照。
3. **搭配整体记**：`explain away`（辩解）与 `explain oneself`（说明原因）意思不同；`refuse a draft`（拒付汇票）与 `refuse an application`（拒绝申请）是固定商务/法务用法；`far removed from`（远非）和 `slide into`（陷入）常接抽象名词。
4. **易混名词辨析**：`undertaking`（承担的任务）、`workload`（工作量）、`repair`（修补活儿）、`outfit`（一整套装备）在"接任务"一幕同时出现，注意各自所指。
5. **词形与副词**：形容词变副词 `dutiful → dutifully`、`gentle → gently`、`tight → tightly`、`synchronous → synchronously`；重读闭音节动词过去式双写尾字母，`rub → rubbed`、`wag → wagged`、`shrug → shrugged`。
