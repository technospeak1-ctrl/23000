## 一、编排逻辑

本主题词量超大（109 词），农具、作物、牲畜、耕作动词高度密集，最易写成"农业名词表"。我用"一位自耕农经营五十英亩混合农场，从整地、播种、养畜、除害到收割入库一整年"的因果线作主线，按"土地与禀赋 → 田地与农具 → 牲畜养殖 → 虫害与辛劳 → 收割入库"五幕推进，并按语义场分层：

| 幕 | 场景 | 承载语义场 | 叙事功能 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The lie of the land 土地 | 土地性质形容词 + 土壤/农场名词 | 建立人物与土地 |
| 2 | Fields and tools 田地与农具 | 作物/农具名词 + 耕作动词 | 铺陈种植 |
| 3 | The living farm 牲畜养殖 | 畜群/牧场名词 + 养殖动词 | 铺陈养殖 |
| 4 | Pests and hard work 虫害辛劳 | 害虫/药剂名词 + 处置动词 | 情节高潮 |
| 5 | The harvest 收割入库 | 收获/仓储名词 + 收束动词 | 点题升华 |

**设计要点**：`arable / fertile / fecund / barren / fallow / productive` 等土地形容词分别贴到可耕地、沃土、贫地、休耕地与高产田上；`plough / harrow / rake / scythe / spade / saw / helve` 等农具按耕作顺序出现，形成农具体系；`drove / flock / herd / poultry / livestock` 与 `shepherd / forage / provender / grazing` 组成养殖谱系；`cultivate → irrigate → fertilize → reap → thresh → grind` 串成农事动词链，`pest / locust / weed` 与 `pesticide / insecticide / shear / fell` 形成虫害与处置对照；动词搭配 `abound with / hatch out / hunt for / shear a sheep` 全部嵌入农活动作。

## 二、英文故事　The Yeoman's Year

### 1. The lie of the land

Thomas was a **yeoman** **farmer** who worked a **farm** of fifty acres. Half the **land** was **arable** and **fertile**, but a **barren** strip by the **hedge** had defeated three generations. The **climate** was kind and **agriculture** was in his blood, so **abundance** seemed certain to a **green** newcomer, yet Thomas knew better: the **fecund** soil needed rest, and a **fallow** field was as vital as a **productive** one. A **gnarled** oak stood by the gate. His **agricultural** neighbor called the ground **affluent**, but Thomas **enclosed** it carefully and spread **compost** and **muck** to make it **fertilized**. "Good **husbandry** is not luck," he said. He kept a **garden** beside the **barn** and hired a **gardener** to tend each young **plant**. He kept careful **benchmarking** notes on every field.

### 2. Fields and tools

In spring Thomas **cultivated** the **cereal** fields, sowing **wheat**, **grain**, **bean** and a **tuber** crop. A small **cotton** patch grew near the **orchard**, and a **plantation** of young trees lined the lane. A **windmill** drove a **pump** from the **levee**, and channels carried **irrigation** water; he **irrigated** the rows at dawn. The old **plough** needed a new **helve**, and the **harrow** and **rake** were worn, so he **reclaimed** a **scythe** and a **saw** from **storage**. He used a **spade** for the **seed** beds and **insolated** the grain on a **heap** of sacks, spreading **fertilizer** where the soil was thin. When the **crop** stood tall he would **reap** it with a **reaper** and **thresh** it; the mill would **grind** the **grain** into **foodstuff**. He also **propagated** cuttings in the garden. "We must **fertilize** before we **reap**," he always said.

### 3. The living farm

The **livestock** were the heart of the place. A **drove** of cattle, a **flock** of sheep and a **herd** of goats moved between the **pasture** and the **meadow**; a **shepherd** with a **whip** and a **saddle** watched them. They **grazed** on the **grass** and ate **forage**, **provender** and **hay** stored in the **barn**, each animal in its own **stall**. The winter **grazing** was thin, so a **forager** cut extra **grass** in summer. In the **hatchery**, eggs would **hatch out** into **poultry**, and his **domesticated** hens were quick to **breed**. Bees hummed in the **apiary**, a small **apiculture** sideline, and a pond held an **aquaculture** trial. "We **abound with** life here," he said, though a **locust** cloud could **diminish** it in a day.

### 4. Pests and hard work

Not every season was kind. A **pest** of **locust**s ate the **crop**, and **weed**s choked the **bean** rows. Thomas used a **pesticide** on the worst patch and an **insecticide** in the **orchard**, though he preferred to **slash** the **weed**s by hand. When a **gnarled** tree died, he had to **fell** it with the **saw**. He **enclosed** the **garden** with a new **hedge** to keep the **drove** out. The **processor** at the mill offered a low price, a late frost would **diminish** the **yield**, and a hailstorm could **slash** the **harvest** in minutes. He **hunted for** a better buyer, and the council agreed to **subsidize** a new **levee**. "We must **shear** the sheep before the heat," he said, and each spring he would **shear a sheep** himself.

### 5. The harvest

By autumn the **harvest** was in. The **reaper** had done its work, the **hay** was stacked in a great **heap**, and the **warehouse** held the **yield**. The **processor** turned the **produce** into **foodstuff**, and the **throughput** of the little plant kept the **supply** steady. Thomas checked his **benchmarking** notes: the **productivity** of the **farm** had risen and the **yield** per acre was the best in years. He **towed** the last cart back to the **barn** and **threshed** the final **grain**. The **yeoman** was tired but content with the **abundance** of the season. He had learned to **fertilize** the **arable** field, to **irrigate** the **fallow** one, to **breed** good **poultry** and to **reap** what he had sown. "A **farmer** who **cultivates** his **land** and **grazes** his **herd** with care," he said, "will never **diminish**."

## 四、学习建议

1. **土地形容词群**：`arable / fertile / fecund / productive / affluent`（可耕的/肥沃的/多产的/高产的/富裕的）是正面，`barren / fallow / green`（贫瘠的/休耕的/无经验的）是反面或待开发状态，放在同一块地上对照。
2. **农具体系**：`plough / harrow / rake / spade / scythe / saw / helve`（犁/耙/钉耙/铁锹/大镰/锯/斧柄）按耕作先后顺序记，配合 `storage / barn / warehouse` 等存放处。
3. **养殖谱系**：`livestock / drove / flock / herd / poultry`（家畜/畜群/羊群/牧群/家禽）由总称到具体，`pasture / meadow / grazing / forage / provender / hay`（牧场/草地/放牧/饲料/草料/干草）是饲喂词。
4. **农事动词链**：`cultivate → irrigate → fertilize → reap → thresh → grind`（耕作→灌溉→施肥→收割→脱粒→碾磨）是一条完整的农事流程，配 `reaper / windmill / pump / levee` 等工具。
5. **虫害与处置**：`pest / locust / weed`（害虫/蝗虫/杂草）对 `pesticide / insecticide / slash / fell / shear`（农药/杀虫剂/砍/伐/剪），正反同场记忆；动词搭配 `abound with / hatch out / hunt for / shear a sheep` 落在真实农活里。
