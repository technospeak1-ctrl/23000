## 一、编排逻辑

本主题是"生产过程"，82 个词横跨原料、酿造、机件与故障四大语义场，最容易写成"工程手册式清单"。我用"诺拉·派克接管河湾酒厂兼制罐车间，从原料场一路修到灌装线"作主线，按"原料场 → 酿造间 → 制罐车间 → 产线故障 → 新线 revert"五幕推进，让每个词成为生产线上的一道工序或一个具体零件：

| 幕 | 场景 | 承载语义场 | 叙事功能 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The raw yard 原料场 | 原料/农田/检验形容词 | 建立场景 |
| 2 | The brewhouse 酿造间 | 酿造/化工/物理变化词 | 铺陈工序 |
| 3 | The can shop 制罐车间 | 形状/机件/连接词 | 展开生产 |
| 4 | The line breaks 产线故障 | 故障/改造/清理动词 | 制造冲突 |
| 5 | The new line 新线 | 剩余词 + 收束 | 点题升华 |

**设计要点**：`raw / material / stuff / stockpile` 落在原料场，`cultivation / irrigation / depletion / reap` 落到农田，避免与机件词混排；`fermentation / distill / condense / dilute / purify / ripen` 串成酿造工序，`intensity / inversion / opacity / odour` 写成工人观察到的现象；`malleable / mold / emboss / jagged / meshed / seam` 一组形状词集中在制罐车间；`leak / detach / calibrate / decompose / distend / redesign / redo / remodel` 一组动词在"产线故障"一幕形成"发现—拆解—改造"的因果链；`counterfeit currency / raw data / intensive training / emit light / smash up / pack up` 等搭配全部成为人物手边真实的东西或动作。

## 二、英文故事　The Riverbend Line

### 1. The raw yard

Nora Pike took over the Riverbend Works, a brewery with its own can shop, and she began in the **raw** yard. Grain arrived in **stack**s beside a **stockpile** of old tin, and every **material** was checked at the **intake** before it entered the plant. Nora set a **marker** on each bin and filled the **brim** of the test cup to the line. Her **methodical** **inspection** covered both the **raw data** on the sheets and a **random** handful from every sack; only a **fraction** of the grain was ever rejected. The farm depended on **irrigation**, and Emil had to **irrigate** the fields; its **cultivation** record showed a slow **depletion** of the soil. "We cannot **reap** more than the land gives," she told Emil Voss, the engineer. She wrote the daily **format** in a notebook and noted how the **stuff** in the bin smelled after rain.

### 2. The brewhouse

In the brewhouse the work grew hotter. Emil helped **brew** the first batch and Nora watched the **fermentation** tanks for three days. A **turbine** drove the pump and a narrow **nozzle** let them **dilute** the syrup with clean water; the vapour would **condense into** a clear liquid. They **distilled** the spirit, **bleached** the tanks each morning, and used a carbon filter to **purify** the water before the mash was left to **ripen**. The **intensity** of the heat rose, so Nora told the crew to **intensify efforts** and to **retain** their self-control; rushing was **counterproductive**. When a sudden **inversion** of the flow forced them to **invert** the valve, the **opacity** of the liquid hid the fault and the sharp **odour** of yeast gave it away. Old lamps still **emit light** but little warmth. The pumps **rotate** in turn, and Nora gave the new hands **intensive training** before the next shift.

### 3. The can shop

The can shop was a world of shapes and joins. Sheets of **malleable** tin were fed into a **mold**, and a press would **emboss** the name into each lid. Emil used a small **device** he had **rigged up** from spare parts to **carve out** the first ring, then helped Nora **carve up** the waste sheet into strips. The **frame** of the old press had **jagged** edges and its **meshed** guard was loose, so a **lateral move** of the wrist could cause injury. On the **mosaic** floor lay a **reel** of wire, a loose **socket**, and a **loop** of belt whose **link** had snapped. Nora studied the **configuration** of the line, the **pattern** of each **seam**, and the marks left by the old **marker**. She applied **polish** to the **rust** on a bracket and lifted the **manhole** cover to check the drain. The crew had to **bind** the wire tightly, and the **varied** orders sometimes pulled them toward a merely **sectional interest**. Still the same **pattern** held on every shift, and the machines could **reproduce** one lid a thousand times.

### 4. The line breaks

One morning the line failed. A gasket began to **leak**, and by noon the news would **leak out** to the whole town. Nora told Emil to **detach** the valve and **dispose of** the spoiled batch, while the foreman **disposed for** a night crew to clear the floor. They had to **calibrate** the gauge again because the spoiled mash had begun to **decompose** and the pressure made an old pipe **distend**. Nora wanted to **expand** the shop, but first they must **trim** the waste and **update** the manual. They would **redesign** the frame, **redo** the wiring, and **remodel** the whole bay, then **install** a new gauge at dawn. A hammer **smashed in** a blocked drum, and in frustration the men nearly **smashed up** the line. They **packed** the good tins into crates and began to **pack up** their tools. A stranger tried to pay them with **counterfeit currency**; Nora refused, and the worst of the scrap went to the **landfill**. The **effete** old press was retired that evening, and the **output** fell by half before the new gauge was fitted.

### 5. The new line

By autumn the **output** was steady again. The **inspection** sheet showed fewer faults, and the **raw** grain came from farms that could **reap** honestly. Nora walked the plant with Emil: the **turbine** hummed, the **nozzle** dripped, and the **meshed** guard sat firmly on the **frame**. The crew had learned that a **random** sample and a **methodical** record were worth more than a fast guess. They had **redesigned** the **configuration**, **remodeled** the bay, and **updated** the manual, so every **link** in the process was clear. "Production is not one big machine," Nora said. "It is a **mosaic** of small honest steps — **bind**, **brew**, **pack**, and **retain** the patience to do them all again." Emil smiled; for the first time the plant seemed to **reproduce** not just tins but care.

## 四、学习建议

1. **按工序分段记**：原料场 `raw / stockpile / intake / cultivation / irrigation / reap`，酿造间 `brew / fermentation / distill / condense / dilute / purify / ripen`，制罐车间 `malleable / mold / emboss / carve / rig up`，故障段 `leak / detach / calibrate / redesign / remodel`。一段一幕，82 个词就不再是一长串。
2. **物理变化动词**：`condense into`（凝结）、`dilute with`（稀释）、`distill`（蒸馏）、`purify`（提纯）、`decompose`（分解）、`distend`（膨胀）都描述物质状态改变，放在酿造间与故障段对照记忆。
3. **形状与连接词**：`malleable / jagged / meshed / mosaic / pattern / seam / socket / loop / link` 属"形状与机件"词族，想象一条制罐线从锡片到成品的构造即可成串回忆。
4. **"re-" 改造词**：`redesign / redo / remodel / reproduce / update` 都含"重新/再"的意味，在"产线故障"一幕里连续出现，适合一起背。
5. **搭配整串背**：`raw data / intensive training / emit light / retain one's self-control / smash up / pack up / dispose of / leak out` 都嵌在具体动作里，务必连短语一起记，不要拆成孤立单词。
