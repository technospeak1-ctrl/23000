## 一、编排逻辑

本主题围绕"体育运动"，可归为 4 个语义场：运动状态形容词、运动项目与器材名词、人物与赛制名词、运动动作。我用"一位全能运动员的赛季"作主线，按"训练 → 赛场 → 竞技 → 冲线"推进：

| 幕 | 场景 | 承载语义场 | 叙事功能 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The training 训练 | 状态形容词 + 器材名词 | 建立情境（athletic 日常） |
| 2 | The events 项目 | 各类运动项目名词 | 展示项目（球类田径） |
| 3 | The competition 竞技 | 人物与赛制名词 | 制造高潮（比赛与对手） |
| 4 | The finish 冲线 | 剩余名词 + 运动动词 | 收尾升华（体育精神） |

**设计要点**：把速度（fast、swiftness、promptness、gallop）与耐力（patient、stamina、marathon、thousandfold）放进同一赛季，形成"快与持久"的对照；`throw out`、`compete in` 等搭配承担情节。

## 二、英文故事　The Season

### 1. The training

Rina was **active** and **athletic** from morning to night. Her training was **functional**, not showy: **jogging** on the **track** at dawn, **aerobics** in the **indoor** hall, and **limber** stretching with **dumbbell-like** weights. She kept her **mood** steady and her body **limber**; a good **sweater** and a **racket** lay by the door. Her **trainer** — a patient **coach** — urged **promptness** and praised her **stamina**. "Speed is **fast**," he said, "but the **ascent** needs a **patient** heart." She **trotted** around the **field**, then **elongated** her stride until it **lengthened** into a **gallop**. In the **gym** a **handicap** matador of a sparring partner tried to match her, but she was **thousandfold** quicker. Each **pulsation** of her heart was **inverse** to her fatigue: the more she gave, the more she had.

### 2. The events

The **competition** listed every **event**. On the **court** a **baseball** **batter** faced a **fast** pitch; on the grass a **cricket** match and a **hockey** game ran side by side. A **golf** player, a **squash** player and a **kung fu** master shared the **group**; a **wrestle**r and a **fighter** warmed up. A **marathon** and a **relay** were scheduled; a **hike** and a **tramp** across the hills were offered for the **membership**. A **dart** was thrown, a **raft** race was paddled; a **time-out** was called when the **referee** saw a **default**. Rina watched a **matador** in the ring and a **sportsman** clear a **hurdle**. "Every **game** is a **match** against yourself," her **coach** said, and the **swiftness** of her **backhanded** return proved it.

### 3. The competition

The **championship** drew a **rival** from every **team**; the **tournament** was fierce. Rina **competed in** the **final** event, her **racket** held steady. The **crowd** began to **acclaim** her; a **stimulant**-free **sportsman** beside her ran clean. Her **rival**'s **backhanded** shot was **inverse** to her own — clever but slow — and Rina's **fast** **forehand** won the point. A **fighter** in the **ring** and a **matador** in the arena **acclaimed** each other; the **referee** kept the **match** fair. When a **hurdle** of a **handicap** threatened, she **regenerated** her **stamina** in a breath. The **tan** of her skin showed the **thousandfold** hours outdoors; the **promptness** of her **final** **throw** settled the score. A **time-out**, a **relay** change, a **sprint** —— and the **crowd** rose.

### 4. The finish

At the **finish** Rina **wheezed** but stood tall. She **surf**ed the wave of applause, **trotted** a victory lap, and **gallop**ed once more down the **track** for pure **fun**. The **swiftness** of her **ascent** and the **patient** **stamina** of her **marathon** were the same virtue seen twice. A **throw** of the **racket** into the air, a **wrestle** with a **rival** in jest — the **competition** had **lengthened** into **friendship**. She **elongated** her cool-down into a **hike** along the **field**, then a **tramp** home. "To **compete** is to **regenerate**," she told the **coach**; the **thousandfold** **pulsation** of a **sportsman**'s heart is worth more than any **tan** or **stimulant**. The **dart**, the **raft**, the **kong fu** — all were one **game**, and the **final** prize was the **swiftness** of a body **limber** with **functional** grace.

## 四、学习建议

1. 按赛季流程记：训练（jogging、aerobics、track、trainer、stamina）→ 项目（baseball、cricket、hockey、marathon、relay）→ 竞技（championship、rival、referee、final）→ 冲线（wheeze、surf、gallop、trot）。
2. 速度 vs 耐力对照：fast、swiftness、promptness、gallop ↔ patient、stamina、marathon、thousandfold。
3. 搭配优先：`compete in`、`throw out`、`time-out` 是高频考点。
4. 词形意识：动词过去式要会认（trotted、elongated、lengthened、regenerated、acclaimed、surfed、wheezed）。
