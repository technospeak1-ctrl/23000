## 一、编排逻辑

本主题是"法律公文、机构"，词条多为制度性名词与公文动词，最易写成法条词汇表。我用"法院书记员处理一桩伪造合同案，从立案、立法背景、听证、文书鉴定到结案"的公文流程主线，按"受理 → 法源 → 听证 → 鉴定 → 结案"五幕推进，让每个词落在法律机构与文书的具体环节上：

| 幕 | 场景 | 承载语义场 | 叙事功能 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | A summons from the court 受理 | 法律形容词 + 法院/文书名词 | 建立案件 |
| 2 | The draft in the legislature 法源 | 立法机关名词 + 立改废动词 | 交代法律依据 |
| 3 | The hearing 听证 | 庭审角色 + 文书/证明名词 | 推进程序 |
| 4 | The forensic report 鉴定 | 法庭科学 + 效力形容词 | 揭示关键信息 |
| 5 | Closing the file 结案 | 遵守/执行/撤销动词 | 点题收束 |

**设计要点**：`legal / legitimate / valid / statutory / prescribed / mandatory` 按"合法—有效—法定—强制"的效力阶梯分布；`court / supreme court / legislature / jury / solicitor / constabulary` 集中在机构与人员场景；`decree / rescript / statute / code / writ / petition / draft` 构成公文链；`enact / amend / repeal / abrogate / abolish / nullify / deactivate / reverse` 一组"立改废"动词按时间顺序出现；`abide by / comply with / conform to / obey / submit to` 一组"遵守"词与前者正反对照，避免清单式罗列。

## 二、英文故事　The Seal Before the Court

### 1. A summons from the court

Clerk Daniel Reed spent his days on **legal affairs** in a quiet office attached to the **court**. One morning a **constable** from the county **constabulary** handed him a **writ** and a **petition** about a **bogus** contract. The **law** was plain enough, yet the **gist** of the complaint was **vague**. "Is this **legal**?" the constable asked. Daniel refused to give **a vague answer**. A **legitimate** claim, he said, must rest on a **valid** foundation, and every **item** in it had to be **provable**. The **justice** of the **court** depended on nothing less.

### 2. The draft in the legislature

The dispute turned on a new **statute** that the **legislature** had just passed. Daniel read the **draft** twice, for a **decree** and a **rescript** had already been issued and the **enactment** altered the old **penal system**. He had to **define** the disputed **terms** in the **code**: what did "delivery" mean, and what **obligation** did it create? The **code** fixed the **jurisdiction** of the court and the **terms** of every filing. In the **legislature**, members would **amend** a clause, **repeal** an outdated rule, or **abrogate** a section outright. Daniel studied **jurisprudence** at night; he knew an **illegal** clause could not survive, and that a **statutory holiday** did not excuse a late **petition**. The **implementation** of the **law** would need clear **terms**.

### 3. The hearing

At the **hearing**, a **solicitor** named Ms. Hale read the **petition** aloud while the **jury** watched. A clerk produced the **prescribed** form and a **patent** certificate; another held up the **packing** list and a signed **waiver**. The dates were **corresponding**, but the **authenticity** of the seal was in doubt. "The **validity** of this paper is **provable**," Ms. Hale said, and she **cited** an old **statute**. Daniel saw the **efficacy** of the **writ** turn on that single **point**. Outside the room, a crowd that included a disabled veteran waited for the **court** to **convene**.

### 4. The forensic report

A **forensic** examiner came to **validate** the seal. She would **cite** the **constitution** and the **legislation** behind the case. "This is not a **valid contract**," she said; the **supreme court** had held that a **titular** signature binds no one. The **vested** rights of the owner made a quick transfer **prohibitive**. She confirmed that the county's **penal system** treated a forged seal as a serious matter, and that an **illegal immigrant** seen near the dock had been **arrested** that morning. The **mandatory** rule allowed no exception.

### 5. Closing the file

Daniel had to **submit** a report and **abide by** the rules; he would **submit to** the judgment of the **court**. He chose to **adopt a suggestion** from the bench and **define** the disputed clause **as** void. The parties were **obligated** to speak truthfully and were **obliged to** appear. Daniel would **obey** the **court**, **comply** with the **decree**, and **conform to** the **code**. Both sides had to **covenant** to keep the peace. He would **implement a policy** of clarity: a dormant clause would be **deactivated**, the old rule **abolished**, a second clause **nullified**, and the new **enactment** **implemented**. When the judge chose to **reverse** the earlier ruling, the **redress** was swift. The owner threatened to **sue** for damages, but the law would **forbid** such claims without proof. "We **enact** laws so that **justice** is **provable**," Daniel wrote in the margin.

## 四、学习建议

1. **法律效力阶梯**：`legal`（合法）→ `legitimate`（正当）→ `valid`（有效）→ `statutory`（法定）→ `prescribed`（规定）→ `mandatory`（强制），按效力强弱记，是判断公文表述是否严谨的关键。
2. **机构与人员**：`court` / `supreme court`（法院/最高法院）、`legislature`（立法机关）、`jury`（陪审团）、`solicitor`（事务律师）、`constable` / `constabulary`（巡官/警察部门），一次记全一套法律机构词。
3. **公文链**：`draft` → `enactment` → `decree` / `rescript` → `statute` / `code` → `writ` / `petition` → `waiver`，按"起草—颁布—成文—送达—放弃"顺序记忆。
4. **立改废动词**：`enact`（制定）、`amend`（修正）、`repeal`（废止）、`abrogate`（废除）、`abolish`（取消）、`nullify` / `deactivate`（使无效）、`reverse`（推翻），与 `abide by` / `comply with` / `conform to` / `obey` / `submit to` 的"遵守"义正反对照。
5. **鉴定与效力**：`forensic`、`authenticity`、`validity`、`provable`、`efficacy`、`vested`、`titular` 围绕"真伪与效力"串联，配合 `valid contract`、`a vague answer` 一起记。
