## 一、编排逻辑

本主题是"理论技术 1"，词项横跨科学方法论、物理装置、计算设备与生命/空间科学，极易写成"科技术语清单"。我用"技术展的导览员 Ms. Vale 带一名学生从入口的理论展板走到物理台、计算角、生命科学区，最后听赠言"作主线，按"理论框架 → 物理台 → 计算角 → 生命与天空 → 演示与赠言"五幕推进，让每个术语落在具体的仪器、读数或操作上：

| 幕 | 场景 | 承载语义场 | 叙事功能 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The framework 理论框架 | 方法论形容词 + 科学抽象名词 | 建立"精确与近似"的对照 |
| 2 | The physics bench 物理台 | 力学/核能/化学装置名词 + 实验动词 | 把术语落到可操作的仪器上 |
| 3 | The computing corner 计算角 | 计算设备 + 数据处理动词 | 用打字与读盘串起操作词 |
| 4 | Life and sky 生命与天空 | 学科名词 + 专家与探测名词 | 群像铺陈、扩展视野 |
| 5 | The demonstration 演示与赠言 | 收束动词 + 全部搭配 | 点题与升华 |

**设计要点**：`exact / imprecise / accuracy / precision` 通过"精确数字与粗略估计"一次对照；`hypothesis / supposition / definition / fallacy / notional` 集中在入口展板上，形成"假设—推想—定义—谬误"的认知链。9 条常用搭配如 `an accurate description / an exact number / a fundamental principle / a nuclear power station / physical endurance / a qualitative analysis / scientific management / statistical data / unrealistic ideas` 全部写进展板文字与导览原话；`define sth. as sth. / quantify the risks of / solve a problem / specialize in science` 四条动词搭配变成学生的真实操作，而非孤立短语。

## 二、英文故事　The Applied Science Exhibition

### 1. The framework

At the entrance of the exhibition Ms. Vale, the senior **technician**, drew a **framework** on a board. "**Science** begins with a **hypothesis**," she said, "but a **supposition** is not yet a **definition**." A poster beside her showed a diagram of **relativity**. She wrote an **exact** number on one side and an **imprecise** estimate on the other. "**Accuracy** is the goal; **precision** is the tool," she said. "A **fundamental** principle must survive a **trial**, and a common **fallacy** is to mistake a **notional** model for the world." She added that theories are **dynamic**, not fixed, and that an **applied** answer must work both in the **laboratory** and in the field. She handed us a **chart** of **statistical data** and asked for **an accurate description** of the display. One student gave **a qualitative analysis** in words; another checked **statistical data** against **an exact number**. She crossed out a plan full of **unrealistic ideas** and told us to use **arithmetic** to verify every figure. "A **scientific** mind also needs **scientific management** of time," she said, and we began to take notes.

### 2. The physics bench

At the **engineering** bench a metal **constrictor** narrowed a tube, and a **gauge** showed the change. A small **accelerator** sent a ball racing along a track; we measured its **acceleration** against the **friction** of the surface. "A **helix** of wire becomes a **transformer**," Ms. Vale said, switching on a **high-tech** lamp that she had **burnished** to a shine. The lamp failed to **illuminate** the corner until she **pinpointed** a loose **device** behind the panel. Nearby a **flask** of **dioxide** sat beside a jar of **petroleum**; lowering the pressure in a sealed chamber produced sudden **decompression**. "We verify, then we publish," she said, recording the **verification** of every reading. A poster showed a single **nucleus** and the **nuclei** of heavier atoms, and a small model of a **nuclear** core stood beside a picture of a **nuclear power station**.

### 3. The computing corner

In the computing corner a **chip** the size of a nail ran the whole **database**. Ms. Vale typed a **code** into a terminal to **input** the readings, then watched a **modem** send them to the main room. "**Automation** saves hands," she said, "and an **automatic** valve closes when the **outfit** is unattended." I had to **retrieve** an earlier file, **revise** two lines of **code**, and **define** a **parameter** as a limit we set ourselves. We tried to **quantify the risks of** a bad connection, and after three attempts we **solved** the problem. A screen printed the **outcome** in clear digits, and the whole class cheered.

### 4. Life and sky

The last wing was devoted to life and sky. An **anthropologist** who studied **anthropology** shared a table with a scholar of **ethnology** and a student of **archaeology** brushing dust from a tray. A **microbiology** poster showed cells; a **genetics** chart showed inherited traits; a **zoology** display kept a moth under glass next to a **graphology** sample, the study of handwriting. Dr. Rao, a visitor with real **expertise**, explained that even **alchemy** had once guessed at the **spectrum** of metals. An **astronaut** who had trained in **aeronautics** and **astronomy** pointed to a **probe** on a model flight path and gave us a **parameter** for its fuel.

### 5. The demonstration

For the final demonstration Ms. Vale asked each of us to choose a path. "If you **specialize in science**, you will meet all of these again," she said. She would **illustrate** each idea with a diagram and **deduce** the answer step by step. "New tools **alleviate** the hard work of measuring," she said, "but a field test still needs **physical endurance** and a careful **outfit**." She reminded us that an **accurate** description and **an exact number** are both required in a report, and that a **scientific** habit of mind turns **a fundamental principle** into a working **device**. When the lights came up, the **high-tech** hall was quiet. I had come for the machines, but I left with a method: ask a question, **define** the terms, measure, **quantify the risks of** being wrong, and **solve** what can be solved.

## 四、学习建议

1. **按五幕分块记**：方法论（`hypothesis / supposition / definition / fallacy / accuracy / precision / notional`）、物理装置（`constrictor / gauge / accelerator / helix / transformer / flask / nucleus / nuclei`）、计算设备（`chip / code / database / modem / automation / input / retrieve`）、生命与空间（`anthropology / archaeology / microbiology / genetics / zoology / astronaut / probe`）、实验动词（`deduce / illustrate / pinpoint / quantify / solve`）。
2. **近义与对照辨析**：`hypothesis`（假设）与 `supposition`（推想）与 `definition`（定义）构成认知链；`accuracy`（准确度）与 `precision`（精密度）是科学写作常见的一对；`exact` 与 `imprecise` 反义，故事里放在同一块展板上对照。
3. **按操作顺序记动词**：`input → retrieve → revise → define → quantify → solve → verify → publish`，想象一次完整的"输入—修改—定义—量化—解题—查证"流程；`burnish / illuminate / pinpoint` 分别对应"磨光—照亮—定位"三种具体动作。
4. **搭配整串背**：`an accurate description / an exact number / a fundamental principle / a nuclear power station / physical endurance / a qualitative analysis / scientific management / statistical data / unrealistic ideas` 以及 `define...as...`、`quantify the risks of`、`solve a problem`、`specialize in science`，都要连同导览原句一起记，避免只背中文释义。
5. **冷词留痕**：`graphology`（笔迹学）、`ethnology`（民族学）、`alchemy`（炼金术）、`constrictor`（压缩器）、`high-tech`（高科技）较书面或专业，已在正文 concretely 出现，注意其学科归属。
