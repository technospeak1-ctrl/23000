## 一、编排逻辑

本主题是"军事冲突"，武器、伤亡与战术动词密集，最易写成军械清单或血腥场面。我用"河谷哨所的一次族群冲突从升温到围困再到平静"为主线，按"断层 → 军械 → 爆发 → 围困 → 平静"五幕推进，全程以指挥官的调度与战术动作为主，不渲染血腥。让每个词落在冲突升级的具体环节上：谁点燃、谁装备、谁拦截、谁坚守、谁收场。

| 幕 | 场景 | 承载语义场 | 叙事功能 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The fault line 断层 | 族群/好战/情绪形容词 | 建立冲突根源 |
| 2 | The arsenal 军械 | 武器/弹药/兵力名词 | 铺陈物质条件 |
| 3 | The outbreak 爆发 | 冲突/袭击/战术动词 | 冲突升级 |
| 4 | The siege 围困 | 围困/坚守/纪律动词 | 危机高潮 |
| 5 | The lull 平静 | 喘息/反思/收束词 | 点题完成 |

**设计要点**：`ethnic / racial / ethnic conflicts / militant / militant group / bellicose / resentful` 集中在第一幕写冲突的社会根源；`armament / firearm / pistol / rifle / bullet / bomb / blast / barrel / arrow / missile / submarine / naval force` 沿"轻武器—重武器—兵力"链分布，配 `massive debts` 写装备困境；`outbreak / clash / combat / contend with / intercept / deviate / deviation / deepen` 写成爆发时的战术动作；`aggressor / perpetrator / casualty / conquest` 写冲突中的角色与后果；`siege / shelter / a vulnerable point / repel / retreat / capitulate / annihilate` 落到围困场景；5 条常用搭配 `a vulnerable point`、`ethnic conflicts`、`massive debts`、`militant group`、`naval force` 整串写入正文，`contend with`、`intrude into`、`rebel against`、`familiarize sb.with` 也各归其位。

## 二、英文故事　The Valley Held

### 1. The fault line

For years the **ethnic** map of Karsk had been a fault line. Two **racial** communities shared the valley, and **ethnic conflicts** had flared every spring. The **militant group** on the ridge was **bellicose** in its posters and **resentful** of every **massive** government grant to the other side. Captain Rusk, who had to **command** a mixed **military** post, knew the **strife** was **inextricably** tied to old land claims. When a market **disturbance** turned into an **incident** with a **bang** and a broken window, he spoke **harshly** to both crowds. The **mass** of angry people did not disperse, and a **menace** hung over the valley. "This will not end with a **lull**," he wrote in his log.

### 2. The arsenal

The quartermaster opened the **armament** store. There were **rifles** and **pistols** for the guard, a crate of **bullet**s, two **barrel**s of fuel, and a **bomb** whose **blast** had once shaken the whole post. An old **sword** hung beside the **arrow**s of the border patrol, and a **helmet** waited for every man. A **missile** was listed on a separate sheet. The **naval force** upstream had a **submarine** that never surfaced in daylight. Rusk had to **equip** the post before the next **outbreak**, but the treasury had **massive debts** and could not pay. A **trick** of accounting kept the **firearm** licences valid. On the range, the **target** was a painted board, and a nervous recruit squeezed a **trigger** too early; the **bang** made everyone jump.

### 3. The outbreak

The **outbreak** came at dawn. A **perpetrator** with a **pistol** fired at the **shelter** gate, and an **aggressor** group tried to **intrude into** the post. Rusk ordered his men to **combat** them without panic. Two officers had to **contend with** a crowd that **intercepted** the supply road. The two sides would **clash** again at noon. Someone tried to **deviate** from the **strategy** agreed the night before; that **deviation** cost them the bridge. A **casualty** was carried to the **shelter**, and the **disposition** of the guards was tested. Rusk would not **capitulate**, and he refused to **jeopardize** the truce. He vowed to **annihilate** the plot, to **conquer** the panic, and to **crush** the **menace** by daylight. The **conquest** of the ridge was not glorious, but it was necessary. A **scout** sent to **demonstrate** the post's strength came back with a report that **deepened** the worry.

### 4. The siege

By noon the **siege** had begun. The **militant group** demanded that the guard **disband** and that Rusk **rebel against** his own government. He did neither. Instead he asked the capital to **recruit** two more men, ordered the **retreat** of non-combatants to the **shelter**, and told his soldiers to **repel** every attack. One **vulnerable point** in the wall was patched with sandbags, and no order was allowed to **impair** a prisoner's rights. No soldier was permitted to **molest** a captive, however **resentful** he felt. When the **siege** broke at dusk, Rusk chose not to **annihilate** the remnants; he had read too many **casualty** lists.

### 5. The lull

In the **lull** that followed, an **escapist** journalist wrote that the valley was tired of **strife**. The **presidency** sent an order to **familiarize** new officers **with** the local **disposition**. Rusk was called **victorious** in the papers, but he knew the **strategy** had only bought time. The **massive debts** were still there, the **ethnic conflicts** had not vanished, and the **militant group** had merely gone quiet. He typed one line in his report: "We held a **vulnerable point**; we did not end the **strife**." Then he looked at the **target** on the wall and wondered how many **bang**s a valley could hear before it stopped listening.

## 四、学习建议

1. **族群冲突链**：`ethnic` / `racial` → `ethnic conflicts` → `militant group` → `bellicose` / `resentful` → `disturbance` / `incident` → `outbreak` → `siege` → `lull`，按"根源—升温—爆发—围困—平静"串记。
2. **武器装备链**：`armament`（总称）→ `firearm` → `rifle` / `pistol` / `bullet` / `trigger` → `bomb` / `blast` / `missile` → `naval force` / `submarine`，再配上 `helmet`、`sword`、`arrow` 与 `massive debts` 写装备困境。
3. **战术动词组**：`combat` / `contend with` / `intercept` / `clash` 写交锋；`deviate` / `deviation` / `deepen` / `demonstrate` 写指挥判断；`annihilate` / `crush` / `conquer` / `repel` / `retreat` / `capitulate` 写战斗结局。
4. **角色与后果**：`aggressor` / `perpetrator` / `casualty` / `scout` / `escapist` 写冲突中的各色人等，配合 `command`、`disposition`、`presidency` 理解指挥体系。
5. **常用搭配整串记**：`a vulnerable point`、`ethnic conflicts`、`massive debts`、`militant group`、`naval force` 五条务必整串记忆，`contend with`、`intrude into`、`rebel against`、`familiarize sb.with` 则结合文中动作一起复习。
