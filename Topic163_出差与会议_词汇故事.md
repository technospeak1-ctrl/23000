## 一、编排逻辑

本主题是"出差与会议"，36 个词围绕出差流程与会议场景。我用"公司代表 Daniel 出差参加年度会议：订票、路上波折、到会场、会上辩论、达成一致"作主线，把每个词落在他手里的一张单据或眼前的一个人物上，按"安排行程 → 一路颠簸 → 会议场地 → 会上辩论 → 一致收场"五幕推进：

| 幕 | 场景 | 承载语义场 | 叙事功能 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Planning the trip 安排行程 | 订票/舱位/证件/津贴词 | 建立出差任务 |
| 2 | The journey 一路颠簸 | 交通/途中/住宿词 | 铺陈旅途波折 |
| 3 | The venue 会议场地 | 会场/与会者/会务词 | 转入会议场景 |
| 4 | The debate 会上辩论 | 争论/现实/集会计论词 | 制造会议冲突 |
| 5 | A unanimous close 一致收场 | 决议/相关回顾词 | 收束并升华主题 |

**设计要点**：`travel agency / reservation / economy class / airline / passport / luggage / schedule / departure / per diem / traveler's check` 化作旅行社柜台前的具体手续；`ferry / traffic / guide / trail / tramp / hostel` 落在途中的具体见闻；`venue / carpet / attendee / president / session / memorandum / subcommittee / flip chart / audio-conference / minutes / conciseness` 摆进会场；`outgoing / seasonal / specific / tense / unanimous` 五个形容词各贴一处人物或决议；全部搭配 `seasonal demand / a tense situation / an unanimous decision / assembly point / audio-conference / economy class / finance meeting / flip chart / frequent flyer / per diem / resort hotel / seasonal fluctuation / seasonal rate / travel agency / traveler's check` 都真实嵌入叙事。

## 二、英文故事　The Conference Trip

### 1. Planning the trip

Daniel, a **businessman** who was also the firm's **representative**, asked the travel agency to book a trip to the annual conference. The **seasonal** demand for flights was high, so the agent found him an economy class seat on a morning **airline** and a **reservation** at a modest resort hotel. As a frequent flyer, Daniel knew to keep his **passport** ready and to pack light **luggage**. The **schedule** listed a **departure** at six and a per diem for meals. He carried a traveler's check and studied the **specific** rules for the finance meeting. "The **seasonal** rate is fair," he said, "but I want a quiet room." The agent confirmed the booking, and Daniel **signed** the form.

### 2. The journey

The **airline** flight was smooth, but at the coast a **ferry** waited in heavy **traffic**, and the sailing was delayed. A **guide** with a clip-board called the assembly point, and Daniel followed the **trail** down to the dock, where a **tramp** slept on a bench. The man was **outgoing** and told stories of **seasonal** fluctuation in the tourist trade. Daniel felt a **tense** edge in the air when the boat pitched. He spent the night in a small **hostel** rather than the hotel, because the **seasonal** demand had filled every room. By morning the sky was clear and the **reality** of the trip — long queues, cold coffee — finally sank in.

### 3. The venue

The conference **venue** was a glass hall with a thick red **carpet** and a **sign** above the door. Every **attendee** took a seat, and the **president** opened the **session** with a **memorandum** on the year's accounts. A **subcommittee** was formed to run the finance meeting, and a flip chart was wheeled in. At noon they ate in the **cafeteria**. When the room went quiet, someone started an **audio-conference** with the overseas branch. The **minutes** were taken by a careful clerk who prided herself on **conciseness**. Yet the **debate** over a new **tour** of the northern ports soon turned into a tense situation.

### 4. The debate

The argument was about money. The president asked for **specific** figures, but the speakers offered only a **busywork** of charts. Daniel, still the firm's **representative**, stood up with the **reality** of the matter: "We cannot sell **seasonal** demand as a permanent trend." A **rally** of young members and one elderly **businessman** cheered him on, while the **president** tapped the **seal** on the table and called for order. For a moment the **reality** of the numbers seemed lost in the **debate**.

### 5. A unanimous close

By evening the **debate** had burned itself out, and the president asked for a vote. The room reached a **unanimous** decision: the **tour** would go ahead with a **specific** budget and a **seasonal** rate for the quiet months. Daniel pressed the **seal** into the **memorandum** and pocketed the **minutes**. Walking back to the **hostel**, past the **trail** where the **tramp** had slept, he felt that the **reality** of the meeting was better than the **busywork** of the afternoon. The **conciseness** of the final **session** had surprised even the **president**. Back home he would tell the travel agency to book a room nearer the **venue** next time — but for now, the **airline** tickets in his **luggage** were enough.

## 四、学习建议

1. **出差流程成串记**：`travel agency → reservation → airline → economy class → frequent flyer → passport → luggage → departure → schedule → per diem → traveler's check`，故事第 1 幕把整条链条排在他的订票动作里，顺着流程记最牢。
2. **会务词按实物记**：`venue / carpet / sign / flip chart / audio-conference / minutes / memorandum / seal / session / subcommittee / president / attendee` 都是会场里看得见摸得着的东西，第 3 幕逐一摆进会议室，配合实物画面记。
3. **固定搭配整体背**：`seasonal demand`、`seasonal fluctuation`、`seasonal rate`、`economy class`、`frequent flyer`、`per diem`、`traveler's check`、`resort hotel`、`assembly point`、`finance meeting`、`flip chart`、`audio-conference` 都是出差会议高频短语，尤其 `seasonal` 一词串起三条搭配，可集中记。
4. **近义与搭配辨析**：`a tense situation`（紧张的局势）中 `tense` 形容气氛；`a unanimous decision`（一致的决定）强调无异议；`specific`（具体的）与 `general`（笼统的）相对，故事里主席正是要具体数字。注意书中 `an unanimous decision` 的写法，规范英语因 `unanimous` 首音为辅音，通常写作 `a unanimous decision`。
5. **多义词留意语境**：`sign`（标记/签名）、` seal`（印章/封条）、`tour`（旅行/周转）、`tramp`（流浪者/不定期货船）、`guide`（导游/路标）都有多个义项，故事各给了具体画面，阅读时结合上下文判断词义。
