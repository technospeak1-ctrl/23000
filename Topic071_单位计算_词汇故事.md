## 一、编排逻辑

本主题是"单位名词 + 计算动词 + 度量形容词"的混合。单位词（metre / gram / litre / watt / volt / ohm……）天生就是清单，所以我把它放进一场**学校科学展的测量比赛**：裁判 Mr. Vale 宣布规则、选手 Ken 与 Lena 依次完成测量、换算、纠错三轮任务，最后公布成绩。单位名词成为比赛器材，方位与比例形容词描述被测的木箱，8 个动词 `consist / convert / descend / encompass / expand / extract / regard / simplify` 分别落在"由……组成、换算、下降、包含、放大、提取、视为、简化"八个比赛动作上，常用搭配自然嵌进裁判的口令和选手的操作里。

| 幕 | 场景 | 承载语义场 | 叙事功能 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The rules 规则 | 单位名词 + 标准/衡量词 | 建立比赛框架 |
| 2 | The first task 第一题 | 方位/比例形容词 + 度量修饰词 | 描写被测物 |
| 3 | The conversion 换算题 | 8 个动词中的主体 | 推进情节 |
| 4 | The tricky round 纠错题 | 副词 + 剩余动词 | 制造波折 |
| 5 | The result 结果 | 搭配收束 + 升华 | 收束全篇 |

**设计要点**：把 `as a whole`、`at large`、`a couple of`、`at intervals`、`at one session`、`in quantity`、`in scale`、`in the red`、`just about`、`head and shoulders above`、`consist of`、`regard as`、`add up`、`leave out`、`count in`、`narrow down`、`tell apart`、`compensate for`、`have nothing to do with` 等搭配写进裁判口令与选手动作；`inside / outside / outermost / overlapping / projecting / rear / subscript / proportional / uniformly / slightly / partially` 集中描写同一只木箱，形成空间与程度的对照。

## 二、英文故事　The Measurement Contest

### 1. The rules of the contest

On the morning of the school science fair, Mr. Vale, the judge, explained the **standard** rules of the **measurement** contest. The event was open **at large** to every class, and the storehouse **abounded in** rulers and scales. "Every good measurement needs a **yardstick**," he said, holding up a metre rule. Ken and Lena, his two contestants, studied the **surface** of the long table. They would have to record the **size** and every **dimension** of the objects set before them. The **metre** rule was marked off in **centimeter** and **millimeter** divisions, and a longer tape could measure a **kilometre** of the school track. On the scale lay a one-**gram** weight and a one-**kilo** bag of sand; beside them stood a one-**litre** jar, a **pint** of milk and a **quart** of oil. A **pound** of apples completed the set. This collection **was composed of** more than twenty pieces. For the electrical round the team had a one-**watt** bulb, a **kilowatt** heater, a **volt** meter and an **ohm** resistor. "Nothing here is **combined** by chance," Mr. Vale said. "Judge the whole set **as a whole**."

### 2. The first task

The first object was a small wooden box. "What lies **inside**?" asked Mr. Vale. Lena measured the **inside** wall and the **outside** edge; the **outermost** layer was painted red. Two boards were **overlapping**, and a **projecting** nail stuck out at the **rear**. Ken wrote a small **subscript** below each number and checked that the **proportional** scale was fair. The marks were **uniformly** spaced — **slightly** closer in one corner — and he **partially** covered the label with his hand to keep it clean. "Read the instructions **herein**," said the judge, tapping the rule card. "A metre is longer **than** a centimetre, remember. **Thirdly**, always show the unit **via** a clear label."

### 3. The conversion round

Round two tested conversion. "Please **convert** this length," said Mr. Vale. Ken began to **expand** the scale of his drawing; the **expansion** made the tiny marks readable. "**Combine** the ruler **with** the scale," the judge added, "and **count in** every trial." Lena asked what the kit **consisted of**, and Mr. Vale answered that it **consisted of** a ruler, a scale and a calculator. A single figure could **encompass** a whole page of data, and these small sums **made up** the total. When they had to **extract** a value from the table, Ken noticed a sudden **descent** in the readings — the numbers **descended** steadily after the third trial. To **simplify** the sum, Lena chose a round figure and **regarded** each unit **as** a separate step. "**Add up** the parts," Mr. Vale reminded them, "and do not **leave out** the small ones."

### 4. The tricky round

In the last round the judges hid a fault. The scale was **slightly** off, and the **outside** case was heavier **than** the **inside** one. A broken nail **had nothing to do with** the error. "You must **regard** this **as** a test of patience," said Mr. Vale. Lena **narrowed down** the cause and could **tell apart** the two similar units; her answer, **combined** from three trials, was only **partially** correct. Ken, meanwhile, was still hunting for the **rear** label, which **added to** the confusion. A **couple of** students laughed, but the judge asked everyone to work **at one session** and to **compare with** the official table **at intervals**.

### 5. The result

When the scores were read out, Lena stood **head and shoulders above** the others. "Her answer was **just about** perfect," said Mr. Vale. Ken had **cut down on** his errors in the final round and even managed to **make ends meet** on his budget of paper. The judges **distributed** the result sheets **to** the class and supplied extra paper **in quantity**; Ken **saved up** his best figures for the last page and used them to **compensate for** the earlier slip. "The contest was **in scale** with the difficulty we promised," the judge concluded, "and no one finished **in the red**." As they packed the **kilowatt** heater and the **ohm** resistor away, the two friends agreed that a good **measurement** is simply a careful story about **size**.

## 四、学习建议

1. **单位词成组记**：长度 `millimeter → centimeter → metre → kilometre`，质量 `gram → kilo → pound`，容量 `pint → quart → litre`，电学 `watt / kilowatt / volt / ohm`——同一组放进一次比赛里记，比单背单词牢固。
2. **"整体与部分"公式**：`be composed of / consist of / make up / combine with / add up` 都描述"部分如何组成整体"，`as a whole` 则从整体角度看问题——这五个短语是本主题的高频句型。
3. **空间形容词链**：`inside → outside → outermost → projecting → overlapping → rear` 描写同一只木箱；`subscript`（下标）与 `proportional`（成比例的）则关联刻度与图表，适合配合一张测量图记忆。
4. **程度副词别混**：`slightly`（轻微）、`partially`（部分）、`uniformly`（一律地）都修饰度量结果；`than` 是比较级的固定搭档，`via` 表示"通过某种方式"。
5. **8 个动词对应 8 个动作**：`consist（组成）→ convert（换算）→ expand（放大）→ encompass（涵盖）→ extract（提取）→ descend（下降）→ simplify（简化）→ regard（视为）`，可按比赛流程复述一遍。
6. **搭配再造句**：`in the red`（亏损）、`just about`（几乎）、`head and shoulders above`（远超）、`at intervals`（不时）、`in quantity`（大量）等建议各造一句；这些短语在说明文与议论文里都很常用。
