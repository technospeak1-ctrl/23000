## 一、编排逻辑

本主题是"日常事务"，词项可归为三组：描写状态与方式的形容词/副词（`faulty / hasty / insouciant / shipshape / overtime` 等）、办公室里日复一日碰到的物件与抽象名词（`xerox / schedule / paperwork / deadline / routine` 等）、以及处理杂务的动词（`adjust / enquire / supervise / submit / occupy` 等）。若照词表平铺，只会是一张"杂事流水账"。我用"办公室助理的一周"作主线，按"周一早晨 → 故障复印机 → 难缠的同事 → 截止日期大战 → 新秩序"五幕推进：每一件事都因前一件而起——复印机坏掉引发抱怨，抱怨拖慢进度，进度告急逼出加班，加班之后迎来改组。

| 幕 | 场景 | 承载语义场 | 叙事功能 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Monday morning 周一早晨 | 工作日常 + 物品（weekday / routine / backup / xerox） | 建立场景 |
| 2 | The faulty copier 故障复印机 | 故障与修理（faulty / maintenance / install / adjust） | 制造第一个麻烦 |
| 3 | The difficult people 难缠的人 | 性格与态度（jaundiced / insouciant / fastidious / reproof） | 铺垫冲突 |
| 4 | The deadline 截止日期 | 忙碌与推进（deadline / overtime / headway / submit） | 推向高潮 |
| 5 | The new order 新秩序 | 回扣与总结（actuary / implementation / trivia / headway） | 点题收束 |

**设计要点**：态度词同框对照——`fastidious / wizened / jaundiced` 的挑剔与 `amiably / shipshape` 的善意有序并置，`insouciant / hasty` 的草率与 `efficacious / unanimity` 的高效形成因果反差。常用搭配全部落进真实情节：`burdensome responsibilities / faulty reasoning / intermittent showers / practical experience / secondary education / adjust oneself to / be busy with / externalize one's thoughts and emotions / inform sb. of / keep sb. informed / occupy oneself with / occupy a prominent position / submit to / do sth. for corporate good` 均在正文以完整短语出现。词表中被排版吞掉的 `appoint / fix / gloss over` 也自然补进第 2、3、5 幕。

## 二、英文故事　A Week in the Office

### 1. Monday morning

On a grey **weekday** the **office** woke slowly. As the **caretaker** unlocked the door, I set my **briefcase** down and opened the **schedule**; the **routine** was clear enough, but the **paperwork** on my desk had grown. I made a **backup** of the files, printed an **attachment** on the **xerox**, and stamped each **document**, while the **section** head wanted everything **shipshape** before nine. I ran an **errand** for the caretaker before the first mail. Nothing about that morning was **unexceptional**.

### 2. The faulty copier

Outside, **intermittent** showers darkened the window; inside, the **xerox** was **faulty** again. A **fastidious** engineer came to **install** a new part, but the **maintenance** note said the **fix** would not hold. I **adjusted** the tray and **enquired** about a replacement while he **cogitated** over the **faulty** reasoning in the manual. A **hasty** decision to ignore the noise cost us an hour, so the engineer worked **overtime** until the machine ran **efficaciously** again and we made real **headway** with the reports.

### 3. The difficult people

Not everyone took the delay well. Old Mr. Vale, a **wizened** clerk, held a **jaundiced** view of every new rule, would **quibble** over a comma, and **opined** that the old ways were better. Another **fastidious** auditor **bandied** complaints around the corridor, while an **insouciant** intern **feigned** interest and **glossed** over mistakes in his notes. The manager **customarily** began the day with a joke, but today he **informed** me of a **malpractice** complaint, spoke **amiably**, and still delivered a sharp **reproof**. The intern tried to **externalize** his thoughts and emotions, saying that a **pretence** of calm was easier than honest work. **Amiss** as that sounded, the **lassitude** in the team was the real problem.

### 4. The deadline

By Thursday the **deadline** on the annual report loomed. The work was **burdensome**, and **burdensome** responsibilities sat on every desk. I jotted down the key figures, **reviewed** the draft, and **rearranged** the **schedule** so we could **submit** it on time. A colleague tried to **scamp** the last section, another began to **scribble** notes in the margin, and someone wanted to **shift** the blame; I refused to **submit** to such habits. I was **busy** with the final checks, kept the director **informed** of each change, and **dispatched** the file after the chair came to **supervise** the last pass. Somehow we **managed**; the chair would **mete** out praise where it was due. We worked **overtime** again, and this time the effort was **efficacious**: by Friday the report was done, and the team's **unanimity** surprised even the **treasurer**.

### 5. The new order

On Monday the **office** was quieter. A young **actuary** was **appointed** to **occupy** a prominent position in the new **corporate** plan, and an **actuarial** table replaced the old guesses; the **treasurer** explained that even a **secondary** role mattered, and that **secondary** education alone was not enough without practical experience. The **implementation** of the plan went smoothly. The old caretaker's **successor** took over the keys, a short **suspension** of the coffee service ended, and the **dole** queues were a distant memory for us all. I had to **adjust** myself to the new rhythm and **occupied** myself with the **routine** once more, filing, stamping, checking the **backup**. Looking back at the week's **trivia**, I could see how a team does its work for **corporate** good and turns small tasks into real **headway**.

## 四、学习建议

1. **按"一天的流程"串记名词**：`weekday → schedule → routine → paperwork → document → backup → xerox → deadline → headway`。第 1、2、4 幕正是顺着这条时间线走的。
2. **态度词成组辨析**：负面一端 `jaundiced / insouciant / hasty / fastidious / amiss`，正面一端 `amiably / efficacious / shipshape / unanimity`；故事第 3、4 幕让两端在同一办公室里正面交锋。
3. **介词搭配整体背**：`inform sb. of sth.`、`keep sb. informed of`、`be busy with`、`occupy oneself with`、`submit to`、`adjust oneself to`、`externalize one's thoughts and emotions`、`do sth. for corporate good`。
4. **容易混淆的三个"写"**：`jot`（快速记要点）、`scribble`（潦草乱写）、`review`（审阅回看）；第 4 幕里三词同场出现，可对照记忆。
5. **排版遗漏词要留痕**：`appoint`（任命）、`fix`（修补）、`gloss over`（掩盖）在原词表中常被前后例句吞掉，这里分别落在第 2 幕的"修复印机"、第 3 幕的"实习生掩饰错误"和第 5 幕的"任命精算师"。
