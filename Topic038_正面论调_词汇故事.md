## 一、编排逻辑

本主题围绕"正面论调"，可归为 4 个语义场：褒扬评价形容词、肯定与信任名词、赞许与支持动词、正面评价搭配。我用"小镇为模范公民举办表彰会"作主线，按"提名 → 陈述功绩 → 一致通过 → 颂词与响应"推进，让褒义词和肯定动词在颁奖流程中成组出现：

| 幕 | 场景 | 承载语义场 | 叙事功能 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The nomination 提名 | 品格褒扬形容词 + 信任名词 | 建立人选 |
| 2 | The citation 事迹陈述 | 肯定名词 + 支持动词 | 展示功绩 |
| 3 | Unanimous approval 一致通过 | 赞许动词 + 评价搭配 | 推进表决 |
| 4 | The tribute 颂词 | 剩余褒义词 + 升华 | 收尾颂扬 |

**设计要点**：把"品格"褒扬（altruistic、loyal、sincere、considerate、committed）与"能力"褒扬（nimble、exceptional、reliable、tip-top、super）放进同一位模范人物身上；`an ardent supporter`、`be loyal to one's duty`、`a reliable friend`、`unanimous approval`、`be successful in/at` 等搭配承担关键表达；肯定动词按"adopt → approve → confirm → commend → revere"形成赞许递进。

## 二、英文故事　The Citation

### 1. The nomination

The town gathered to honour its **beloved** citizen, Mrs. Hale. She was an **altruistic** woman, known for **altruistic act**s of kindness, and **committed** to the community with **determination**. The mayor, **assured** of her worth, called her **a man of exceptional talent** in all but name. "She is **loyal**," he said, "**loyal to her duty** and to her neighbours." She was **considerate** and **sincere**, **reasonable** and **sound** in judgment, **punctual for** every appointment, and **willing** to help. A **reliable friend** to many, she was also **wary** of waste and **intent** on doing good. Her **supreme** quality was a **sincere friend**'s heart; she had **appreciable** influence and **unanimous approval** from the start. Even the **urgent maintenance** of the hall she had organized herself.

### 2. The citation

The secretary read the **citation** with a **clear**, **assured** voice. Mrs. Hale had shown **perseverance** and made a real **contribution**; her **commitment** was beyond question. The council had **ascertain**ed the facts, **certify**ied the accounts, and **confirm**ed that no one could **allege** any wrongdoing — every **allegation** was false. The mayor could **apprehend** her worth at once and spoke in **complimentary** terms. They would **adhere to** the truth and **assert** it publicly. An **ardent supporter** rose to **advocate** the award; another **cited** her bravery. The **credit** was hers alone. Her **anticipation** of others' needs, her **consensus**-building, and her **advantage** to the town were **telling** — **a telling blow** against cynicism. People **appreciate**d her and **approve**d the choice; the **approval** in the room was **assured**. A **favor** was done to all by honouring her.

### 3. Unanimous approval

One by one the members **agree**d; the vote was **unanimous**, and **by mutual consent** the motion passed. The chair would **adopt** the resolution, **approve** the **authorization** of funds, and **ensure quality** in the ceremony. A colleague moved to **diversify** the tribute — to **diversify** the programme with music and a **super annuity fund** in her name — and the idea was **correct**, **sensible**, and **convenient** for all. "This is a **tip-top**, **terrific** plan," he said, and the **appeal** of it won **approval**. They **insist**ed on **insisting on** excellence; they **resolve**d **to resolve** to honour her every year. The **agreement** was complete, the **certainty** absolute; her **promise** to keep serving drew **respect** and **trust** from everyone. She was, in short, a **paragon** — **exceptional**, **reputable**, **successful in** everything she attempted.

### 4. The tribute

At the podium the mayor **commended** her, **recommended** her as a model, and **revered** her name. "She **advised** us wisely, **allowed** us to dream, and **underpinned** every good cause," he said. He **recognized** her **recognition** from distant towns, **donated** to her favourite charity in her honour, and **favored** no one above her. The crowd **consented** with one voice. Mrs. Hale, **nimble** in mind and **optimistic** to the last, **assured** them that **recognition** belonged to the whole town. Her **altruistic**, **considerate**, **loyal** spirit, her **crucial** and **paramount** service, her **sincere**, **sound**, **reliable** character — all earned **respect** and **support**. As the **treasure** of the town, she was **supreme**, **super**, and **beloved**, and the **unanimous approval** that day was a **telling** tribute to a life well lived.

## 四、学习建议

1. **品格 vs 能力褒扬**：品格（altruistic、loyal、sincere、considerate、committed、wary）↔ 能力（nimble、exceptional、reliable、tip-top、super、sound）。表彰会主线把两类褒扬集于一身。
2. **"重要"三词辨异**：crucial（关键的）、paramount（至高的）、supreme（最高的）——程度递增，常修饰 service、quality、moment。
3. **肯定动词链**：ascertain（查明）→ certify（证明）→ confirm（证实）→ approve（批准）→ commend（称赞）→ revere（崇敬），是表彰流程的递进。
4. **搭配优先**：`an ardent supporter`、`be loyal to one's duty`、`a reliable friend`、`unanimous approval`、`be successful in/at` 是高频搭配。
5. **词形意识**：过去式与被动（ascertained、certified、confirmed、adopted、approved、asserted、consented、diversified、donated、insisted、recognized、resolved、revered、underpinned、commended、commended）要会认；注意 cite→cited、certify→certified 的拼写变化。
