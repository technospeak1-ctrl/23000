## 一、编排逻辑

本主题围绕"艺术与绘画"，可归为 4 个语义场：艺术评价形容词、艺术作品与技法名词、艺术家与机构名词、创作动作。我用"一次画廊新展从筹备到开幕"作主线，按"布置 → 真伪之辨 → 开幕演出 → 感悟"推进：

| 幕 | 场景 | 承载语义场 | 叙事功能 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Hanging the show 布展 | 艺术评价形容词 + 作品名词 | 建立情境（aesthetic 布置） |
| 2 | Authentic or phony 真伪之辨 | 真伪形容词 + 技法名词 | 制造冲突（authentic vs phony） |
| 3 | The opening 开幕 | 艺术家/机构名词 + 演出动词 | 展示人物（artistry） |
| 4 | The verve of it 神韵 | 剩余名词 + 创作动词 | 收尾升华（艺术的意义） |

**设计要点**：把.authentic/phony、exquisite/amateurish、vivid/toneless、marvelous/invisible 放进同一场展览，形成真伪与品质对照；`transcribe…into…`、`accompany` 等搭配承担情节。

## 二、英文故事　The Exhibition

### 1. Hanging the show

The **gallery** was hanging a new **exposition** of **art**. The **curator** — an experienced **portraitist** — arranged each **painting** with an **aesthetic** eye; a **brush** and **paint** lay on a **tripod** nearby. An **exquisite** **sculpture** of **crystal** stood in the center, its **vivid** colors **finely** cut; a **collage** of **stripe** patterns and a **cameo** hung nearby, and each **instrument** of the small **band** was **stipple**d with gold. The **showpiece** of the **showroom** was a **manuscript** with a **touching** **fable** in the margin. A **photographer** devoted to **photography** and **photographic** **pixel** art adjusted the **flash lamp**; a **stencil** and a **montage** waited on the wall. Even the **graffito** in the corner looked **marvelous** in the soft light. On the **stage** a **surrealism** piece challenged the **canon**, its **brim** of color **finely** layered; the **assimilation** of styles was **dramatic** but not **toneless**, and the **composition** had a **tempo** of its own.

### 2. Authentic or phony

A **musicologist** and an **apprentice** studied a **roll** said to be an **authentic** **canon** by a **master**. "It is either **authentic** or **phony**," the **musicologist** said; "there is no **invisible** middle." An **effigy** in **statuary** and a **badge** with a **rebus** were displayed; a **hyperbole** in the **ballad** on the label made the **apprentice** smile. Some **artifacts** from a **finale** dig were **dramatic** in their **simplicity**. A **phonetic**—no, a **phony** **coloration** of a **painting** fooled no expert; the **dual** nature of the **brushwork** — **fine** yet **amateurish** — gave it away. The **characterization** of the **model** was **startling** in its honesty. Only an **authentic** work has such **verve**.

### 3. The opening

At the **opening**, a **cellist** and a **quartet** arrived; a **conductor** raised his hand, and the **accompaniment** began. A **troupe** of **acrobats** performed; a **mime** in white **mimed** climbing an invisible stair. A **band** played an **interlude**, and a **debut** **violinist** gave a **solo** that **transcribed** the **painting** into sound. The **performance** was **marvelous**; the **drawer** of the **art** spoke of **artistry** and **endowment**. A **performance** of a **ballad** was **accompanied** by the **cellist**, and the **finale** brought **enthusiastic** applause. The **photographer** took a **photograph** for the **exposition** catalogue; the **badge** of the **gallery** gleamed. An **amateurish** **artistry** it was not.

### 4. The verve of it

Walking home, I thought of what makes a work **authentic**. It is not the **photographic** precision or the **startling** **hyperbole**, nor the **exquisite** **coloration** alone; it is the **verve** — the **touching**, **vivid** honesty that **transcribes** feeling into **art**. A **phony** copy may have the **stripe** and the **cameo**, but it lacks the **invisible** **endowment** of the **portraitist**. The **musicologist** was right: **art** is **dual** — **fine** craft and **marvelous** truth. I **rehearsed** the **ballad** in my head, **mimed** the **acrobat**'s leap, and **flashed** a smile at the **model** in the **showroom**. The **exhibition** had **accompanied** me into a **startling** awareness: **authentic** **artistry**, however **amateurish** its first **debut**, outlives the **toneless** and the **phony**.

## 四、学习建议

1. 按布展流程记：布置（gallery、exposition、painting、brush、tripod）→ 真伪（authentic、phony、canon、manuscript、verve）→ 开幕（cellist、conductor、accompaniment、troupe、mime）→ 感悟（artistry、endowment、transcribe）。
2. 品质对照：authentic ↔ phony；exquisite/fine ↔ amateurish；vivid/touching ↔ toneless；marvelous ↔ invisible middle。
3. 搭配优先：`transcribe…into…`、`flash lamp`、`accompany` 是高频考点。
4. 词形意识：动词过去式要会认（transcribed、accompanied、mimed、rehearsed、flashed）。
