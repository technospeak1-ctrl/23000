## 一、编排逻辑

本主题是"军事 1"，兼具政体词与军语词，容易写成军衔表。我用"卡尔斯克共和国战后重建军队、在边界举行演习并平定一次兵变"为主线，按"战后会议 → 联盟与预算 → 边界演习 → 兵变 → 脆弱的和平"五幕推进，让每个词落在真实的部队调动与战术动作上，以军人视角为主，避免血腥渲染。

| 幕 | 场景 | 承载语义场 | 叙事功能 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | A post-war assembly 战后会议 | 政体/王权/社会形容词 | 建立军改背景 |
| 2 | The alliance and the budget 联盟与预算 | 军种/编制/动员名词 | 铺陈部队建设 |
| 3 | The border exercise 边界演习 | 边界/演习/侦察词 | 推进战术动作 |
| 4 | The rebellion 兵变 | 叛乱/夺取/镇压动词 | 冲突高潮 |
| 5 | A fragile peace 脆弱的和平 | 和解/忠诚/晋升词 | 点题收束 |

**设计要点**：`post-war / republican / autonomous / revolutionary / sovereign / throne / royalty` 集中第一幕铺陈"从王国到共和国"的政体转换；`alliance / coalition / collaboration / navy / artillery / cannon / ammunition / armor / troop / regiment / squad` 沿"联盟—军种—编制—装备"的链条分布；`marshal / sergeant / rank / liaison` 构成指挥与联络链；`border / boundary / maneuver / campaign / spy / boycott` 落在第三幕的演习与侦察场景；`rebel / rebellion / betrayal / seize / pummel / proclaim / quit` 写成第四幕的兵变推进；`to implement changes`、`manipulate public opinion`、`proclaim war`、`quit doing sth.` 四条动词搭配分别嵌进预算、舆论、宣战与抗命的语境，避免清单式罗列。

## 二、英文故事　The Border Held

### 1. A post-war assembly

In the **post-war** winter the **assembly** met for its first **session**. The old **throne** had been abolished, and **royalty** survived only in the portraits on the wall. A **republican** constitution had made every province **autonomous**, but a **revolutionary** faction wanted more: an end to **segregation**, an end to **racism** in the ranks, and a clear **statement** of rights for every **activist** in the capital. Senator Hale, who had once served a **sovereign** of the old line, read the **committee** report slowly. The **downfall** of the monarchy had been quick, he said, but the **hegemony** of one party had merely taken its place. A **reconciliation** of the two sides would not come cheaply, and no one in the room **purposely** raised the question of who had been guilty of **betrayal**.

### 2. The alliance and the budget

The new **marshal** needed a budget. He spoke of an **alliance** with the northern **navy**, a **coalition** of small provinces, and close **collaboration** with the **artillery** school. Each **regiment** would receive new **cannon**, each **squad** new **armor** and **ammunition**. Sergeants like Della knew that paper **maneuver**s did not win wars — she had seen a **breakdown** of supply lines in the last campaign — but she kept her **rank** and said little. A young **liaison** officer, **eligible** for promotion, was asked to **implement changes** in the supply office. The **marshal** even asked him to **manipulate public opinion** in favour of the budget, a task the young officer accepted **terribly** reluctantly.

### 3. The border exercise

On the **border**, where the **boundary** had been drawn by a map nobody trusted, the **troops** began a **campaign** of drills. The **marshal** had **purposely** chosen a cold week **whereby** both sides could test their **maneuver**s. A **spy** in a civilian coat watched from the ridge. Della's **squad** moved **terribly** slowly through the snow, but no one **quit**; they had been told to **quit doing** anything until the exercise ended. When a **boycott** of the supply convoy threatened the drill, the order came to **mobilize** the reserve, and the wagons rolled again before dawn.

### 4. The rebellion

Then the **rebellion** began. A **rebel** officer tried to **seize** the radio station, and someone began to **pummel** the doors of the **assembly**. The **marshal** refused to **proclaim war** on his own people; instead he went on the radio to **proclaim** a ceasefire. Della, who had never **quit** a post, held the **boundary** bridge. The **betrayal** of one **liaison** officer cost them an hour, but the **campaign** was saved. In his **statement** to the press, a colonel took a **swipe** at the **rebel** leaders without naming them.

### 5. A fragile peace

In the end the **triumph** was modest: the **border** held, the **retention** of the province was secured, and **loyalty** to the new constitution became more than a slogan. Senator Hale asked the **senator** from the north to **mediate** between the **rebel** families and the **assembly**. The **marshal** was promoted, and Della finally received the **rank** she had earned. No one spoke of **hegemony** now. The **reconciliation** they had wanted in winter arrived in spring, and the **republican** experiment survived one more **session**.

## 四、学习建议

1. **政体转换链**：`throne` / `royalty` / `sovereign` → `downfall` → `republican` / `autonomous` → `revolutionary` → `hegemony` → `reconciliation`，记成一条"王国垮台—共和实验—和解"的时间线。
2. **部队编制链**：`alliance` / `coalition` → `navy` / `artillery` → `regiment` / `squad` → `marshal` / `sergeant` / `rank` / `liaison`，按"结盟—军种—编制—指挥"顺序记全一套军语。
3. **装备与动员**：`cannon` / `armor` / `ammunition` 是装备三件套；`mobilize` / `implement changes` / `manipulate public opinion` 写成预算与舆论战的三个动作。
4. **战术动作词**：`maneuver` / `campaign` / `border` / `boundary` / `boycott` / `spy` 落在演习场景；`seize` / `pummel` / `proclaim war` / `quit` 落在兵变场景，正反对照记忆。
5. **收束词**：`triumph` / `retention` / `loyalty` / `mediate` / `reconciliation` 用于写战后安置，和第一幕的 `reconciliation` 首尾呼应，形成完整闭环。
