## 一、编排逻辑

本主题是"部门职位"，词项可分为三层：描述层级关系的形容词（`subordinate / supervisory / managerial / white-collar / decent`）、公司里大大小小的部门与头衔（从 `chairperson / chief / directorship` 到 `clerk / copyist / typist`）、以及推动组织变化的动词（`reshuffle / outsource / affiliate / fill / instruct`）。若按字母顺序抄一遍，就是一张干巴巴的通讯录。我用"Halcyon 公司的一次大改组"作主线，按"总部层级 → 白领楼层 → 大规模裁员 → 补缺与晋升 → 新秩序"五幕推进，让每个职位对应一个具体的人在做具体的事：主席主持会议、总会计师核账、工头盯流水线、口译员给经纪人翻译。

| 幕 | 场景 | 承载语义场 | 叙事功能 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The head office 总部 | 高层头衔 + 监督关系（chairperson / chief / subordinate / supervisory） | 建立层级 |
| 2 | The white-collar floor 白领楼层 | 专业岗位（accountant / barrister / editor / stenographer） | 铺陈职位谱系 |
| 3 | Big cuts 大规模裁员 | 组织变动（reshuffle / outsource / affiliate / big cuts） | 制造冲突 |
| 4 | Filling the positions 补缺晋升 | 招聘与领导（fill a position / leadership / managerial talent） | 推进转折 |
| 5 | The new order 新秩序 | 主题回扣（position / hierarchy / decent job） | 点题收束 |

**设计要点**：上下级关系词同场对照——`subordinate / supervisory / managerial` 用来写 `line manager` 与 `general manager` 的从属、`foreman` 对 `line worker` 的监督；`white-collar` 与 `line worker` 分置两层，暗示白领与一线的分野。常用搭配 `a decent job / be subordinate to / managerial talent / fill a position / instruct sb. to do sth. / qualify for` 均在正文以完整短语出现，其余机构类搭配（如 `executive committee / collective bargaining / team building`）也自然嵌入改组情节，避免词典式列举。

## 二、英文故事　The Great Reshuffle at Halcyon

### 1. The head office

At Halcyon Goods the **hierarchy** was drawn on the wall. At the top sat the **chairperson**, Lady Pike, and the **chief** executive; beneath them each **director** headed a **department** and held a **directorship** that the executive committee had approved. A general manager ran the daily work, a Finance Manager watched the books, and a line manager was **subordinate** to that general manager. The **foreman**, who had a **supervisory** role on the floor, knew every **staff** member by name and directed each line worker. Farther out, a **bureau** in the **district** reported to the **governor**; a **superintendent** visited the branch and an **inspector** checked the **agency** license. The **union** met on Fridays.

### 2. The white-collar floor

Each **white-collar** worker filled the next floor. In the Accounts Department an **accountant** kept the **accountancy** books while a **controller** checked every figure; a **barrister** drafted the contracts and an **adjuster** calculated the claims. An **editor** revised the brochure, a **copyist** and a **typist** rewrote it, and a **stenographer** took dictation. An **interpreter** translated for a visiting **agent**, while an **adman** argued with a **middleman** about the campaign. A **clerk** stamped the forms. Every **interviewee** wanted a **decent** job, and each learned that you needed more than **seniority** to **qualify** for the post.

### 3. Big cuts

In autumn the executive committee ordered big cuts. The firm would **outsource** the mailroom and **affiliate** two small offices to a partner; a marketing consultancy took over the ads and a marketing director arrived from outside. The redeemable payment was announced and collective bargaining began with the **union**. One **department** would **reshuffle** its teams, and a few managers had to **create** new roles to keep everyone busy. A **requisition** for new desks was approved, and a **relocation** to a quieter **district** office was offered to volunteers. A sales executive was hired to steady the clients.

### 4. Filling the positions

To **fill** a **position** on the new team, the selection board interviewed twelve people. The **chairperson** wanted **managerial** talent, so she **instructed** the **director** to arrange team building for the newcomers. A senior manager spoke about **leadership**, a **pacesetter** in sales showed his figures, and the **mastermind** behind the plan explained how the **practice** worked. By spring the **recruitment** was over. A line worker became a line manager, a **staff** favourite joined the **committee** and the **chairperson** welcomed her, and even the **adman** won a place on the **council**. The **assistance** of the **interpreter** and the **copyist** had been essential, and their **membership** in the **union** was never in doubt.

### 5. The new order

Looking back, everyone agreed the **reshuffle** had been hard but fair. The **hierarchy** was flatter, the **directorship** clearer, and the **position** of every **white-collar** worker was written down. The **adman** and the **middleman** still argued, the **barrister** still read the small print, and the **controller** still checked the **accountancy** books. Nobody missed the old **bureau**; even the **governor** and the **inspector** came to the party. The **chief** raised a glass to a **decent** job well done, and the **foreman** to the **superintendent** who had kept the **district** office running.

## 四、学习建议

1. **按层级链记头衔**：`chairperson → chief executive → director → general manager → line manager → foreman → line worker`，故事第 1 幕把这条链完整走了一遍，比孤立背单词好记。
2. **上下级关系形容词三兄弟**：`subordinate to`（从属于）、`supervisory`（监督的）、`managerial`（管理的）分别描述"对上、对下、管全局"，可结合第 1 幕的 `line manager / general manager / foreman` 一起体会。
3. **机构类搭配整体记**：`executive committee`（执行委员会）、`selection board`（选拔委员会）、`collective bargaining`（集体协定）、`team building`（团队建设）、`redeemable payment`（遣散费）、`big cuts`（大规模裁员）都是职场高频短语。
4. "-ist/-er/-or" 职业后缀再辨析：`accountant / assistant`（-ant）、`barrister / copyist / typist / stenographer / interpreter`（-ist/-er 多为专业人员）、`controller / director / inspector / supervisor`（-or 多为管理或检查角色）。
5. **动词搭配落实用法**：`fill a position`（补缺）、`instruct sb. to do sth.`（指示某人做事）、`qualify for`（胜任）、`reshuffle`（改组）、`outsource`（外包）、`affiliate`（使隶属）。第 3、4 幕把这六个动词串进"裁员—外包—招聘"的完整因果链。
