## 一、编排逻辑

本主题"商务招待"是一场宴请的礼节与言行，天然有场景主线。我用"商人 Vale 筹办一场决定大生意的商务招待会，从发通知、布置大厅、迎接宾客到宴席应对与收尾"的经历作故事，按五幕推进，让每个词与搭配都落在具体的招待动作上：

| 幕 | 场景 | 承载语义场 | 叙事功能 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The invitation 筹备邀请 | 文书/日程/席位/分派 | 建立事件 |
| 2 | The hall is dressed 布置大厅 | 陈列/餐具/典雅/礼节 | 铺陈场面 |
| 3 | The guests arrive 宾客到场 | 人物/举止/失礼/应对 | 制造冲突 |
| 4 | At the table 宴席之上 | 款待/言谈/口译/职责 | 情节高潮 |
| 5 | After the banquet 宴会之后 | 剩余词 + 收束搭配 | 点题升华 |

**设计要点**：`memorandum / documentation / scheme / reception / receptionist / admittance / be entitled to / depute / prescribe / specify by / consolidate / speaker / seminar / occasion` 落到筹备邀请；`decorate with / array / dress / cutlery / napkin / chalice / elegance / magnificence / genteel / formality / courtesy / politeness / transient` 铺陈场面；`retinue / trader / tradesman / vintner / pub / tobacco / energetic / incumbent / akimbo / hauteur / apposite / loutish / dumb / blurt / displease / disconcert / disgruntle` 制造并化解失礼；`regale / carouse / unstinting / sybaritic / blandishment / jest / digress / interpret as / interpret out of context / illuminate / concur / describe / perform a task / perform official duties / dessert / nap / fuddle / tenderness / whit` 构成宴席高潮；`spoilsport / inexpedient / fulfill oneself / consultation / totter` 收束全篇。

## 二、英文故事　A Night to Remember

### 1. The invitation

Mr. Vale was hosting a **reception** to seal a major deal, and he began with a **memorandum** to his staff. The **documentation** had to be perfect: a guest list, a **scheme** for seating, and a note that every invited buyer was **entitled to** a place. He would **depute** his neat **receptionist** to answer the door and check **admittance** at the entrance. A **seminar** on trade was held that morning, and the **speaker** was asked to **specify by** name the firms attending. Vale planned to **consolidate** the guest list, **prescribe** a strict timetable, and **specify** the dress code in the invitations. "This **occasion** matters," he said.

### 2. The hall is dressed

The banquet hall was **decorated with** garlands. Vale's wife arranged a fine **array** of plates and chose elegant **dress** for the staff; the **cutlery** shone, and a folded **napkin** lay by each **chalice**. Everything spoke of **elegance** and **magnificence**, a **genteel** show in which **formality** was softened by warm **courtesy** and simple **politeness**. The glory of the room felt **transient**, but for one night it was perfect.

### 3. The guests arrive

The guests came with **retinue** and pomp: a **trader**, a **tradesman**, and a **vintner** who had come from the **pub** with samples of **tobacco**. One young guest was **energetic** and spoke at length; another stood **incumbent** over the door, arms **akimbo**, with an air of **hauteur**. Vale made an **apposite** joke to ease the mood, but a **loutish** remark from the young man began to **displease** the older buyers. Before Vale could speak, the boy **blurted** out an apology. When he was told that the head seat was not **allowed** for him he looked **dumb**, and his awkwardness went on to **disconcert** the room, while the delay **disgruntled** Vale's partner.

### 4. At the table

At dinner Vale moved to **regale** his guests with stories and a **carouse** that grew lively. The food was **unstinting**, almost **sybaritic**, and a flattering **blandishment** or two went down well. When an envoy began to **digress**, Vale would **jest** and steer him back. He was careful not to **interpret as** an insult a careless word, nor to **interpret out of context** a rival's **jest**; a bright remark could **illuminate** the whole deal. His aide sat quietly, ready to **describe** the goods and to **perform a task** or two, while the senior man rose to **perform official duties** with grace. Everyone **concurred** that the wine was excellent, though a glass too many began to **fuddle** the younger guests. After **dessert** Vale took a brief **nap**, and his **tenderness** toward the guests won them over; not a **whit** of doubt remained.

### 5. After the banquet

When the doors closed, one **spoilsport** said the show was **inexpedient**, but Vale knew that a host who **fulfills** every duty gives a young man a chance to **fulfill oneself**. After a long **consultation** with his partner, the deal was signed; the firm's credit did not **totter** but stood firm. Vale looked at the empty **chalice** and the quiet hall, and felt the warmth of a night well spent.

## 四、学习建议

1. **筹备链**：`memorandum → documentation → scheme → reception → receptionist → admittance → be entitled to → depute → prescribe → specify by → consolidate`，是一条完整的宴会筹备线。
2. **场面与礼节**：`decorate with / array / dress / cutlery / napkin / chalice / elegance / magnificence / genteel / formality / courtesy / politeness / transient` 描写招待会的排场与礼数。
3. **失礼与应对**：`loutish / hauteur / dumb / blurt / displease / disconcert / disgruntle` 是负面言行，而 `apposite / courtesy / tenderness` 是正面礼节，同篇形成对照。
4. **宴席动词**：`regale / carouse / unstinting / sybaritic / blandishment / jest / digress / illuminate / concur / fuddle / dessert / nap` 围绕宴席展开，场景感强。
5. **动词搭配**：`perform a task / perform official duties`（执行任务/公务）、`interpret as / interpret out of context`（理解为/断章取义）、`be entitled to`（有权）、`fulfill oneself`（实现自我）、`specify by`（用……说明）结构固定，宜整串记忆。
