## 一、编排逻辑

本主题是"物理 1"，词项横跨力学、热学、电磁、声光、原子五大块，且夹着大量实验动词，极易写成术语清单。我用"物理老师 Mr. York 主持校园物理开放实验室，带学生从杠杆走到原子展台"作主线，按"力与运动 → 热与物质 → 电与磁 → 光与声 → 原子与收尾"五幕推进，让每个术语成为一件仪器、一次操作或一句讲解：

| 幕 | 场景 | 承载语义场 | 叙事功能 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Forces and motion 力与运动 | 杠杆/浮力/形变/振动/流体 | 用可操作的仪器建立力学词 |
| 2 | Heat and matter 热与物质 | 温度/相变/混合/净化 | 让热学词落在烧杯操作上 |
| 3 | Electricity and magnetism 电与磁 | 电路/电磁/电压/发电机 | 用接线动作串起电磁词 |
| 4 | Light and sound 光与声 | 光谱/显微镜/射线/超声 | 铺陈成像与波动词 |
| 5 | The atomic corner 原子展台 | 原子/粒子/实验方法 | 收束并点题 |

**设计要点**：搭配全部落在真实实验动作上——`thermal energy` 由热水传递、`magnetic field` 用铁屑显示、`a vertical line` 画在读数图上、`ultraviolet radiation` 是胶片记录的光带、`atomic bomb` 是墙上的示意图、`experimental design` 与 `multiple choice` 是考试提醒。力学线按 `fulcrum→pivot→rod→pressure→buoyant` 展开，电学链按 `battery→charge→circuit→resistance→volt→voltage→generator` 展开；`static / stationary / immobility` 三者通过"静止的木块"一次辨析，`mottled / mingling / intermix / agglomerate / inspissate` 通过桌上的粉末与蒸汽成串出现，11 个动词全部变成学生的操作步骤。

## 二、英文故事　The Open Physics Laboratory

### 1. Forces and motion

Mr. York began the open laboratory with a lever. "A **rod** resting on a **fulcrum** turns about a **pivot**," he said, pressing one end; "the **pressure** under the short arm can lift a heavy **vessel**." A block stayed **stationary** on the bench until he pushed it, while a **buoyant** cork and a block of **softwood** rose in a tank of water. "A **static** load does not move," he said, "but a **compressed** spring stores energy, and a **contraction** of the air can make a loud sound." He waved a strip of metal until it began to vibrate; "waves **undulate** along the strip," he said, and the class watched the **vibration** travel. "There is no **immobility** in this room," he joked. On a screen he showed how **aerodynamics** shapes a car and how **biomechanics** explains a stride; a small rocket used **propellant** to reach **supersonic** speed, and a dog whistle blew at an **ultrasonic** **frequency**. "The **physiology** of the ear explains why we cannot hear that," he added.

### 2. Heat and matter

The next bench dealt with heat and matter. A **thermal** blanket kept a beaker warm, a **thermometer** in the water gave the reading, and a **thermostat** switched the heater off. "**Thermal energy** moves from hot to cold," York said; "**evaporation** lowers the level, and milk will **ferment** into **fermented milk**." He let a frozen sample **defrost** before reading the scale, stirred a powder that would **agglomerate** into lumps, and poured a syrup that would **inspissate** over time — at the **molecular** level, he said, the change was already under way. Water had to **percolate through** a bed of sand before he could **purify** it, and a **permeable** membrane let the clean water pass. He warmed a strip of **foil** made of two metals until the **bimetallic** sheet bent — "two metals, one curve," he said — and a drop of oil began to **outgas** under the lamp. "Some liquids are **volatile**," he warned, "and an **igneous** rock keeps the heat of its making." A **mottled** stone and a cloud of **mingling** vapour sat beside him; he **intermixed** two powders and watched a **substantial** residue settle.

### 3. Electricity and magnetism

At the electrical bench the class built a simple **circuit**. A **battery** supplied the **charge**, and a small **electromagnet** with an iron core had a north and south **pole**. "A **magnetic field** surrounds every magnet," York said, laying iron filings on the paper; "a **dielectric** such as glass will not carry the current, but the wire fights back with **resistance**." A **transistor** in the line controlled the flow, and a **valve** in an older set glowed warm. The wire was **spun** from copper; the meter read twelve **volts** and the **voltage** stayed steady. A **generator** nearby turned motion into **electricity**, while a strip of **rustproof** steel stayed bright beside a rusty nail.

### 4. Light and sound

On the optics bench a **prism** split white light into a **spectrum**, and a **microscope** revealed grains too small for the eye. "A **stereoscopic** pair of pictures gives depth," York said, handing round a viewer; "a lens can **blear** the image if it is out of focus, and the **proximate** cause is always a loose screw." He drew a **vertical** line through the reading on the chart. A special camera took an **X-ray** of a sealed tube, and a **radiography** plate showed the **radiation** passing through; a **radar** dish picked up a returning pulse at a fixed frequency. "The **exposure** was short," he said, "but the film recorded a band of **ultraviolet radiation** beyond violet." A **microwave** oven hummed in the corner, and an **offbeat** tapping on the glass turned out to be a loose clip.

### 5. The atomic corner

Last they reached the atomic display. "An **atomic** nucleus can release a **neutron**," York said, moving a model ball along its **orbit**; "the **atomization** of a liquid makes a fine spray, and each **particle** in a **particulate** cloud is still a world of its own." The wall carried a drawing of an **atomic bomb** and a note that its energy comes from the same force. "For the exam," he said, "use good **experimental design**: write a clear **multiple choice** answer, label every reading, and let a colleague **buffer** the results before you publish. Physics is not a list of names — it is a way of asking what happens next."

## 四、学习建议

1. **按五幕分块记**：力学（`fulcrum / pivot / rod / pressure / buoyant / static / stationary / immobility / vibration / undulate`）、热学（`thermal / thermometer / thermostat / evaporation / ferment / inspissate / percolate / purify`）、电磁（`battery / charge / circuit / electromagnet / pole / dielectric / resistance / volt / voltage / generator`）、声光（`frequency / prism / spectrum / microscope / stereoscopic / radiation / radar / X-ray`）、原子（`atomic / neutron / orbit / particle / particulate`），按场景回忆比按字母表快。
2. **近义词成组辨析**：`static`（静态的）与 `stationary`（静止的）与 `immobility`（固定）；`mottled`（斑驳的）、`mingling`（混合的）、`substantial`（物质的）都是描述物质外观的词，放在第 2 幕的烧杯桌一起记。
3. **实验动词按操作顺序记**：`percolate through → purify → agglomerate → inspissate → outgas → ferment → intermix → buffer`，想象一次完整的"过滤—净化—混合—发酵"实验流程；`undulate / blear` 分别对应"波动"和"模糊"两种现象。
4. **易混辨析**：`volt`（伏特，单位）与 `voltage`（电压，物理量）；`thermal`（热的）与 `thermometer`（温度计）、`thermostat`（调温器）同根不同词性；`ultraviolet`（紫外线的）与 `ultrasonic`（超声的）前缀 ultra- 表"超"，但一个属光、一个属声。
5. **搭配整体记**：`magnetic field / thermal energy / ultraviolet radiation / atomic bomb / experimental design / multiple choice / a vertical line` 是物理阅读高频短语，连同 `X-ray`、`percolate through`、`fermented milk` 一起在故事原句里背，避免只记中文释义。
