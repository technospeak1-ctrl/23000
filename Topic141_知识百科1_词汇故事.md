## 一、编排逻辑

本主题是"知识百科 1"，词表横跨天文、化学、物理、生物与数学，最像一部小型百科全书，也最容易写成分类词条。好在 16 条搭配本身提供了现成的场景线索：`cosmic rays / outer space / infinite space` 属于宇宙厅，`optical instrument / horizontal surface / vertical line` 属于光学与力学厅，`electrical equipment / electronic impulse / static electricity / a stationary engine` 属于电学厅，`vocal organ / audio equipment / aural skills / visual field` 属于生命与感知厅。我以"馆长带初中生梅参观科学博物馆的四个展厅"为主线，按"星空厅 → 物质厅 → 光学台 → 力学厅 → 生命与声音厅"五幕推进：

| 幕 | 场景 | 承载语义场 | 叙事功能 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The sky room 星空厅 | 宇宙名词 + 空间/宇宙形容词 | 建立人物与尺度感 |
| 2 | The matter room 物质厅 | 元素/物质名词 + 化学动词 | 铺陈物质谱系 |
| 3 | The light table 光学台 | 形状/光学名词 + 状态动词 | 用演示串起操作词 |
| 4 | The forces room 力学厅 | 力/电/运动名词 + 方向形容词 | 把物理落到装置 |
| 5 | The life and sound room 生命与声音厅 | 生物/感知名词 + 数字词 + 收束 | 收束、点题、回收搭配 |

**设计要点**：`horizontal surface / vertical line / a stationary engine / static electricity` 全部落在可触摸的展品上，而非抽象定义；`static` 与 `stationary` 同幕出现（静电 vs 固定式发动机），`electrical` 与 `electronic`、`decimal` 与 `digital` 也在同一幕形成易混对照。`gay / bile / vapor / colander` 等与"百科"看似无关的词，分别安放在参观的人群、生物标本与厨房器具展台，确保每个词都有具体落点而非悬空。

## 二、英文故事　A Day at the Museum of Knowledge

### 1. The sky room

At the door of the Museum of Knowledge, Mei met Curator Hale, who had spent thirty years turning hard science into plain wonder. In the first room a **galaxy** turned slowly on a screen, and she learned that the **universe** is far larger than the **globe** we live on. A model showed an **eclipse**: the moon's dark disc crossed the sun and the **aureole** flared. "The **horizon** is only where our sight stops," said Hale. Beyond it lay **outer space** and **infinite space**, full of **cosmic rays**. Stars **twinkled** across the ceiling, and a wire **sphere** hung above a model of the solar system. As the lights dimmed, the **dimension** of the small room seemed to grow. "**Cosmic** questions begin with a single question," she smiled, "and the answer is usually bigger than the question."

### 2. The matter room

In the chemistry room rows of jars held every **element**: **carbon**, **hydrogen**, **oxygen** and **nitrogen** in gas tubes, **sulfur** and **silicon** in grey powders, and small coins of **nickel** and **zinc**. A jar of **oxide** sat beside a bottle of **ozone**, and Mei watched a thin **vapor** rise from a **mixture** of ice and salt. "Matter is never still," said the curator. "You can **concentrate** it, **distill** it, or let light **penetrate** it." She pointed to a bottle labelled with a chemical **symbol**, and Mei saw that even a letter on a jar could stand for a whole **substance**. The air smelled faintly of a **blaze** of reagent colours, and the lesson felt as bright as a **spark**.

### 3. The light table

On a low table the curator laid a glass **cube**, a **triangle** of lenses and a **prism**; a beam split into colours, and a **blaze** of red and gold crossed the wall. The **symmetry** of a **crystal** amazed Mei, and a thin **fiber** of glass carried the light around a bend. A chain of **polymer** beads ran the length of the table. Beside it lay a **fossil** in stone, a shallow tray of **gravel** and a kitchen **colander** — "even a sieve is a tool of knowledge," Hale smiled. Rubber gloves **insulated** their hands as they worked; one lens let light **penetrate** a drop of coloured water. She showed how to **concentrate** a beam and how to **distill** a clear **fluid** from a cloudy **mixture**. Inside a glass tube a tiny **electron** circled the nucleus. "Every mark here is a **symbol**," she said, "and every **substance** has a story."

### 4. The forces room

The next hall was about force and motion. A **magnet** held a nail in the air, and a **microwave** oven demonstrated heat. A model of **a stationary engine** stood on a **horizontal surface**, with **a vertical line** painted on the wall beside it. "Electricity can be **static** or moving," said the curator, rubbing a rod until **static electricity** made a faint crackle. Rows of **electrical equipment** and a screen of **electronic impulse** traces filled one wall; a radio **antenna** reached toward the ceiling. A **compass** needle turned toward the **magnet**. On a globe the **equator** divided the northern **hemisphere** from the southern, and a spinning wheel showed **torque** and **gravity** at work. "An engine's **horsepower** is only a number," she said, "but the **momentum** of a moving train is real." **Electrical** wires hummed, and every **electronic** display counted the turns.

### 5. The life and sound room

In the last room a model **cell** floated in resin, and a chart showed how the body burns **carbohydrate**. A small glass jar held **bile**, and the curator explained the **expertise** needed to read a blood test. "Your **vision** covers only a small **visual field**," she said, handing Mei an **optical instrument**; "a lens can widen it." In the sound corner a model of the **vocal organ** stood beside **audio equipment**, and an exercise in **aural skills** asked them to name a note. A **gay** couple in the group sang along, and the **vocal** guide laughed. A computer desk showed **cyberspace** and **digital automation**, and a chart of the **decimal system** lay open. A lamp gave off **ultraviolet** light, and Mei learned that even **oxygen** can turn into **ozone** under it. "Knowledge," the curator said at the door, "is a **mixture** of many rooms — and every room has a window onto the **universe**."

## 四、学习建议

1. **按展厅记搭配，而不是按字母**：星空厅 `cosmic rays / outer space / infinite space`，光学厅 `optical instrument / horizontal surface / vertical line`，电学厅 `electrical equipment / electronic impulse / static electricity / a stationary engine`，感知厅 `vocal organ / audio equipment / aural skills / visual field`——一幕一组，Recall 时按画面取词。
2. **"静"字两兄弟**：`static`（静态的，配 `static electricity` 静电）与 `stationary`（静止的、固定的，配 `a stationary engine` 固定式发动机）同在力学厅出现；`electrical`（电的）与 `electronic`（电子的）、`decimal`（小数的）与 `digital`（数字的）也各成一对，注意搭配不同。
3. **元素谱系一条线**：`carbon → hydrogen → oxygen → nitrogen → sulfur → silicon → nickel / zinc → oxide / ozone`，沿"气体—非金属—金属—化合物"在物质厅走一遍，比背化学符号表牢得多。
4. **几何与光线链**：`cube → triangle → prism → crystal → symmetry → fiber → polymer`，配 `concentrate / penetrate / insulate / distill` 四个动词，正好是光学台的演示动作。
5. **冷词留痕**：`aureole`（日冕）、`bile`（胆汁）、`colander`（漏勺）、`gay`（同性恋的）、`vapor`（蒸气）都在具体展品或人群中露过面，注意它们与主题"百科"的连接方式，不要当成孤立的怪词。
