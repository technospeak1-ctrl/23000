## 一、编排逻辑

本主题是"学校教育 2"，词项横跨校规评价、语言学课堂、学籍管理、院系争议与治学态度，极易写成"校务术语清单"。我用"一名学生经历新校规公布、语法课、注册大厅、院系会议，最后在花园里听老教授赠言"作主线，按"校规公示 → 语言实验室 → 注册大厅 → 院系会议 → 花园里的赠言"五幕推进，让每个词成为一张告示、一次登记、一场争论或一句叮嘱：

| 幕 | 场景 | 承载语义场 | 叙事功能 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The notice 校规公示 | 评价性形容词 + 校务名词 | 建立规矩与冲突 |
| 2 | The lab 语言实验室 | 语言学名词 + 语音/语法形容词 | 把术语落到板书上 |
| 3 | Registration 注册大厅 | 学籍身份名词 + 管理动词 | 群像铺陈 |
| 4 | The council 院系会议 | 争论动词 + 意见名词 + 搭配 | 情节高潮 |
| 5 | In the garden 花园赠言 | 治学动词 + 剩余名词 + 收束 | 点题与升华 |

**设计要点**：`conflicting / divergent / offensive / drastic` 与 `disciplined / normal / obligatory` 放进同一张校规告示形成正反对照；`prefix / suffix / morphology / acronym / terminology / transitive / unaccented` 集中在语言学课堂的板书上，避免"词典条目"式罗列。8 条常用搭配如 `literal translation / normal education / in duplicate / ponderous burden / tedious lecture / offensive remarks / conflicting views / divergent ideas` 全部写进真实场景；`disperse doubts / excel in/at / forbid sb. to do sth. / modify the approach / be motivated to do sth. / verge on` 等动词搭配变成会议发言与教授叮嘱。

## 二、英文故事　A Term at the Institution

### 1. The notice

On Monday a **normal** morning at our **institution** began with a notice from the **dean**. It said that **normal education** must train both the mind and the character. The new **syllabus** was **obligatory** for every **level** of the school, and the rules were **drastic**: late papers would cost a **diploma** point. My roommate called the change **problematic**; the **monitor** of our class read it with an **unquestioning** face. I handed my form **in duplicate**, since two of the notices were **duplicate** and one had to come down. A **ponderous** paragraph described the **ponderous** burden of dormitory **surveillance**, followed by a **tedious** list of **offensive** remarks that would not be tolerated. "A **disciplined** school is not a silent one," Ms. Pratt said, **emphasizing** that the **conflicting** and **divergent** views in the corridor were welcome.

### 2. The lab

In the language lab we met the quiet side of words. Dr. Fenn wrote a **terminology** list on the board and showed how a **prefix** and a **suffix** change a word's **morphology**. "An **acronym** like NASA is short," he said, "but its history is **elusive**." He asked us to build an **analogy** — teacher is to school as doctor is to clinic — and to expand a short note by **amplification**. A **literal** translation, he warned, can sound strange; an **unaccented** syllable and a **transitive** verb need a careful ear, yet every word must stay **pronounceable**. I wrote new **vocabulary** in my notebook and felt my **speciality** beginning to take shape.

### 3. Registration

At registration the halls were crowded. A **freshman** from the north asked where the **nursery** and the **kindergarten** were; a **boarder** showed him the way. A **novice** at the desk asked how to use the school **platform**, and an **auditor** sat quietly at the back with no right to vote. A **postgraduate** in a worn coat checked her **score** on the screen. The registrar spoke of a student on **probation** and another facing **expulsion** after a **contest** went wrong; the **contestant** who won still apologized to the loser. When a **dropout** returned to ask for advice, no one turned him away. A map on the wall gave the **direction** of every office. "Rules change," the registrar said, "but a door stays open."

### 4. The council

In the afternoon the council met. The **contention** was sharp: some members held **conflicting views**, while the younger teachers offered **divergent ideas**. Dean Marlow did not **quash** the argument; he spoke to **disperse doubts** instead. He would **allude** to an old rule without naming it, then **reiterate** the main point twice. "We **forbid** students to leave this hall in anger," he said, "and we forbid no honest question." The university would **confer** an honorary title on a retiring teacher, and he tried to **elicit** a reply from the quietest member. His praise did much to **motivate** her; she was **motivated to** serve on the committee. She had **excelled in** her field for years and hoped for **tenure** there; she urged me to **excel** myself too. Even the **tedious** lecture on regulations ended in applause.

### 5. In the garden

Before I left, an old professor in the garden was lifting a heavy box; he would **lug** it to the archive himself. He did not **grudge** the years he had spent here, and he warned me never to **degrade** a colleague to win an argument. "Facts **diffuse** slowly," he said; "a good **thesis** proves what it claims." He told me to **memorize** the lovely lines but to **modify** the approach when life changes, and to **modify** a demand before it becomes stubborn. He had **specialized** in **meteorology** before turning to history, and his **theme** was always curiosity. His advice was simple: **verge on** no extreme, **demonstrate** patience, and let each small act **motivate** the next. I walked home under a sky he would have called changing.

## 四、学习建议

1. **按五幕分块记**：校规评价（`drastic / obligatory / problematic / offensive / ponderous / tedious`）、语言学（`prefix / suffix / morphology / acronym / terminology / transitive / unaccented`）、学籍管理（`freshman / boarder / auditor / postgraduate / probation / expulsion`）、争议处理（`contention / conflicting views / divergent ideas / quash / reiterate / allude`）、治学态度（`memorize / modify / specialize / demonstrate / verge on`）。
2. **正反对照记**：`normal / disciplined / obligatory` 与 `conflicting / divergent / drastic / offensive` 同在公示牌一幕，记色彩比记释义牢；`literal translation` 与 `amplification` 又是一对翻译方法的对照。
3. **近义与构词辨析**：`prefix / suffix` 一前一后，`acronym / terminology / vocabulary / morphology` 都属语言学术语；`dean / monitor / instructor / auditor / postgraduate` 按校园身份归类；`excel in/at` 与 `excel oneself` 一个是横向比较，一个是自我超越。
4. **搭配整串背**：`conflicting views / divergent ideas / in duplicate / literal translation / normal education / offensive remarks / ponderous burden / tedious lecture` 连同 `disperse doubts / forbid sb. to do sth. / be motivated to do sth. / verge on` 都应在故事原句里记忆，不要只背中文。
5. **冷词留痕**：`quash`（取消）、`allude`（暗指）、`lug`（拖）、`grudge`（吝惜）、`elusive`（难懂的）较书面或易混，已在正文出现，注意其语域与搭配。
