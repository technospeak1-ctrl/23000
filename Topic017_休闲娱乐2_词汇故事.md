## 一、编排逻辑

本主题围绕"阅读、游戏与竞技中的休闲"，可归为 4 个语义场：评价与态度形容词、阅读与游戏名词、竞技与杂物名词、创作与表达动词。我用"一场校园读书与才艺节"作主线，按"筹备 → 阅读角 → 才艺赛 → 感悟"推进：

| 幕 | 场景 | 承载语义场 | 叙事功能 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The preliminary rounds 筹备 | 态度形容词 + 初步搭配 | 建立情境（prize 前的准备） |
| 2 | The reading corner 阅读角 | 阅读与文学名词 | 展示阅读（书与诗） |
| 3 | The contest 才艺赛 | 竞技名词 + 夸张形容词 | 制造高潮（比赛与荣誉） |
| 4 | What remained 余韵 | 剩余名词 + 表达动词 | 收尾升华（休闲的真谛） |

**设计要点**：把褒义（fabulous、splendid、inspiring、incredible）与贬义（trite、insignificant、extravagant）放进同一节，形成评价对照；`be prone to`、`be relevant to`、`culminate in`、`relate to` 等搭配承担转折。

## 二、英文故事　The Reading and Talent Festival

### 1. The preliminary rounds

The school's festival began with **preliminary** rounds, and the committee was **pleased** with the **willingness** of so many to **volunteer**. A **well-known** poet had promised an **inspiring** talk; an **intense** buzz filled the hall. We were **prone to** expect **incredible** things, and under the **given** condition of a tight budget, the **presentation** had to be **legible** and **relevant to** real **interests**. A **selective** reading list included a **biography**, an **autobiography**, a **pastiche** of **poetry**, and a **serial** from a **news-stand** **reprint**. "Keep it **realistic**," the teacher said; "a **splendid scene** is fine, but don't be **extravagant in living** or **omit** the **rubric**." Someone offered **banter** about **fabulous heroes**, which **aptly** set the tone.

### 2. The reading corner

In the reading corner a **bibliophile**-like student turned the pages of a **kaleidoscope** of **poetry** and a **crossword** book. A **cartoon** of a **prank** and a **cathedral** drew laughs; an **insight** in the **biography** gave a **judgment** worth keeping. A child traced a **feat** in a **comic** and dreamed of a **medal**. The **fidelity** of an old **reprint** impressed the **personality** of the group; a **fondness** for **poetry** was clearly **stimulating** minds. On the **screen** a **token** of **turquoise** light played; a **slot** machine waited by the **swing** and the **trapeze**. A **vault** of old **photographs** — no, a **repetition** of **pornography**-free **posters** — lined the **playground** wall, and a **supplementary** **news-stand** **release** of the **serial** sold out. Outside, children played **hopscotch** and **natation**-style games near a **surfboard** propped by the **slot** machine; the **relaxation** of the hour was plain, and the **inspiration** of the poet's words lingered.

### 3. The contest

The **contest** reached its height when a **finalist**—a **well-known** **finalist**—took the **trapeze**. The crowd's **zeal** and **zest** were **incredible**; an **inspiring** story in the **program** had **primed** everyone. A **medal** gleamed, a **vault** was cleared, a **pirouette**-like **trapeze** turn **transcended** the ordinary. The **judgment** of the **referee**-panel was **legible** in their **faces**: a **splendid**, **fabulous** performance. Yet a **trite** **serial** and an **insignificant** **pastiche** were booed; a **prone**-to-error **juggler** dropped a **token**. "Not every **pursuit** is **relevant to** glory," the poet said, "but every **feat** has a **mode**." An **intense** **persuasion** campaign by the organizers **recruited** more volunteers; the day **culminated in** a **procession** to the **church** and the **centennial** **cathedral**.

### 4. What remained

After the **Christmas** and **centennial** decorations came down, I **reassured** myself that the **insight** was **realistic**: **leisure** is a **realm** where a **repetition** of small **feats** becomes a **triumph** of the **utmost** kind. A **rehearse**d **persuasion** by a friend **prevailed** on me to join the **reading** group; I **envisioned** a **supplementary** list of **biographies** and **poetry**. We **composed** a **pastiche** of **poetry**, **portrayed** a **fabulous hero** in a **cartoon**, and **summarized** the **insight** in a **rubric**-friendly **summary**. A **mar** on the **fidelity** of an old **record** didn't matter; a **prank** with a **lash**-like **ribbon** made us **willingly** laugh. When a rival **retorted** blow for blow, the poet would **persuade** both sides to **relate to** one another, and we **reckoned on** his calm; no quarrel could **weaken** the day. The **willingness** to **relate to** one another — that, more than any **medal**, was the **triumph**. The **release** of the **serial** and the **repetition** of the **feat** faded; the **zest** and the **insight** remained.

## 四、学习建议

1. 按节庆流程记：筹备（preliminary、presentation、rubric）→ 阅读（biography、autobiography、poetry、pastiche）→ 竞赛（contest、medal、trapeze、vault、triumph）→ 余韵（insight、leisure、realm、zest）。
2. 评价对照：褒（fabulous、splendid、inspiring、incredible、legible）↔ 贬（trite、insignificant、extravagant）。
3. 搭配优先：`be prone to`、`be relevant to`、`culminate in`、`relate to`、`reckon on` 是高频考点。
4. 词形意识：动词过去式要会认（composed、envisioned、portrayed、summarized、reassured）。
