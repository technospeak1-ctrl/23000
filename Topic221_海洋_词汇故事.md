## 一、编排逻辑

本主题是"海洋"，海岸、水循环、降水、盐度与水下工程术语密集，最易写成地理词条表。我用"一位海洋学家沿海岸与河流做一次从入海口到源头的野外调查并写成日志"作主线，按"海岸初到 → 河流水循环 → 地貌与沉积 → 实验室里的水 → 河口黄昏"五幕推进，让每个词与搭配落在测量、采样、记录和观察的具体动作上：

| 幕 | 场景 | 承载语义场 | 叙事功能 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The coast at dawn 海岸初到 | 海岸/气候/潮汐词 | 建立人物与场景 |
| 2 | Following the water 追水溯源 | 河流/降水/天气词 | 展开水循环 |
| 3 | Delta and stone 三角洲与岩石 | 地貌/沉积/冰川词 | 展示地质时间 |
| 4 | The water lab 水样实验室 | 盐度/处理/渗透词 | 落到科学操作 |
| 5 | The living shore 有生命的岸 | 生态/湿地/收束词 | 点题升华 |

**设计要点**：`coastal areas / maritime climate / humid climate / foggy / offshore current / tidal current / prevailing winds / bay / harbor / dock / inlet / seashore / seaside / isle` 集中在"海岸初到"一幕；`source / brook / creek / stream / waterfall / springwater / dew / droplet / bead / mist / shower / downpour / monsoon / hurricane / deluge / torrent / surging / foam / spray / drought / scorching day / corrugate` 落在"追水溯源"一幕；`delta / sediment / glacial period / alluvial gold / porous / dripstone / drift / inland / moat` 归入"地貌与沉积"；`saline / absorption / saturation / transparent / effluent / sewage / sewerage / drain / sluice / reservoir / intake / influx / moisten / saturate / seep / submerge / trickle` 归入"水样实验室"；`coral / seaweed / dolphin / salmon / pond / quagmire / swampland / abundance / splendid scene / tranquil life / in vast numbers` 收在"有生命的岸"一幕。

## 二、英文故事　The Coastline Journal

### 1. The coast at dawn

As an **oceanographer** I keep a field journal, and I opened it on the quay at first light. The **coastal areas** here are shaped by a **maritime climate** and a **humid climate**: the air was **humid**, the **humidity** high enough to leave drops on my lens, and a **foggy** bank hung over the **bay**. From the **dock** I watched an **offshore current** pull away from a **tidal current** running into the **harbor**; the two branches of the **current** met at the **inlet** in a line of foam. **Prevailing winds** pushed a small boat **landward**, and beyond the **seashore** an **isle** rose out of the mist. My hotel stands at the **seaside**, and from its window I can watch the whole coast wake up. I wrote down the date and the weather, then set out to follow the water inland.

### 2. Following the water

On a **scorching day** the **source** of the river was still cold. I followed a **brook** down to a **creek**, where a **stream** ran over stones, and a **waterfall** fed a pool of **springwater** so clear that every **droplet** held a **bead** of light. At dawn **dew** lay on the grass and **mist** hung over the valley; by noon a **breeze** had begun to **corrugate** the surface of the pool. Then the sky broke: a **shower** became a **downpour**, and the **monsoon** arrived with the force of a **hurricane**. A **deluge** poured into the **torrent**, and **surging** water carried **foam** and **spray** over the bank while **bubbles** rose in the eddies. The following week brought a **drought**, and the brook fell to a thin thread. I wrote both extremes in the same column: a river is a record of its weather.

### 3. Delta and stone

At the river mouth I found the **delta**, built from **sediment** carried down over centuries. A guide told me that in the last **glacial period** this valley had been solid ice, and that **alluvial gold** still washes out of the banks. The **alluvial** soil here is **porous**, so rain disappears into it quickly; in a limestone cave I saw a **dripstone** that had grown one drop at a time. Fragments of rock **drift** downstream and settle where the water slows. Farther **inland** the old **moat** around a hill fort had gone dry, and the stones of its **exterior** wall were warm to the touch. I took a sample from each layer and labelled it carefully; a coast, I was learning, keeps its history in the ground as well as in the charts.

### 4. The water lab

Back in the lab I tested the samples. A **saline** drop tasted bitter, and the **absorption** of light through it was easy to measure; the **transparent** water in a glass tube showed every speck. Water began to **seep** through a sand core, and to **saturate** a dry stone I used a **reservoir** of seawater; the **saturation** point came sooner than I expected. The town's **intake** pipe draws from upstream, while a **sluice** controls the **influx** into the reservoir; **effluent** and **sewage** pass through the **sewerage** system before they reach the **drain**. I watched a drop **trickle** down the glass, used a sponge to **moisten** the porous rock, and covered the samples with a **moist** cloth. When the tide rose, the lower deck was **submerged**. I also noted the **shallow river** upstream, whose bed I had walked the day before.

### 5. The living shore

At sunset I climbed the cliff for one last look. The view was **imposing**: an **extensive** sheet of water, its **exterior surface** catching the light, and a **splendid scene** of **coral** and **seaweed** in the clear shallows. A **dolphin** rose and a **salmon** leaped; **in vast numbers** the small fish turned together, and I thought of the **abundance** this coast still holds. Behind the dunes a **pond** caught the last light, while farther on a **quagmire** led into **swampland** where the mud was **wet** and deep; at high tide the path would be **swamped**. My boots came back **externally** clean but soaked inside. Under such **circumstances** a person learns to read the **tranquility** of the water: a **tranquil life** here is never still, only balanced. I closed the journal. The sea is older than our maps and will outlast them, and every river I had followed was only one of its many beginnings.

## 四、学习建议

1. **气候与海流**：`maritime climate`（海洋性气候）、`humid climate`（湿润气候）、`humidity`（湿度）、`foggy`（有雾的）、`prevailing winds`（盛行风）、`offshore current`（离岸海流）、`tidal current`（洋流）是海岸描写的六件套，配合 `coastal areas / seashore / seaside / bay / harbor / dock / inlet / isle` 记空间层级。
2. **水循环链**：`source`（源头）→ `brook`（小溪）→ `creek`（小河）→ `stream`（溪流）→ `waterfall`（瀑布）→ `springwater`（泉水）→ `dew / droplet / bead / mist` → `shower / downpour / monsoon / hurricane / deluge / torrent / surging / foam / spray` → `drought / scorching day`，按"从源头到暴雨再到干旱"一条时间线记。
3. **地貌与沉积**：`delta`（三角洲）、`sediment`（沉积物）、`glacial period`（冰川期）、`alluvial gold`（沙金）、`porous`（多孔的）、`dripstone`（滴水石）、`drift`（漂流）、`inland`（内陆）、`moat`（壕沟）属于地质时间尺度，与前一条的天气尺度形成对照。
4. **水的实验词**：`saline / absorption / transparent / saturation / saturate / seep / moisten / moist / submerge / drain / sluice / reservoir / intake / influx / sewage / sewerage / effluent` 是实验室与市政水务词，`corrugate`（起波纹）、`trickle`（滴淌）、`swamp`（淹没）三个动词补上动作。
5. **收尾的生态词**：`coral / seaweed / dolphin / salmon / pond / quagmire / swampland / wet / extensive / imposing / splendid scene / abundance / in vast numbers / tranquility / tranquil life` 把物理的海洋写成有生命的海洋，`exterior surface / externally / circumstance` 则在观察视角上收束全文。
