## 一、编排逻辑

本主题是"待遇、福利"，词表几乎全是薪酬 name 与福利条目，若逐个罗列就成了"工资单术语表"。我用"护士克拉拉从社区诊所跳槽到一家著名医院，逐项读懂薪水与福利，最终谈成更好待遇"作主线，按"接到聘书 → 研究工资条 → 读懂福利手册 → 谈成公平待遇 → 回望整体报酬"五幕推进，让每个词都成为她实际读到、算到或谈到的东西：

| 幕 | 场景 | 承载语义场 | 叙事功能 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The offer 接到聘书 | 人物态度词 + 公司/前景词 | 建立人物与动机 |
| 2 | The payslip 研究工资条 | 薪酬钱款词 + 扣除/通知动词 | 呈现报酬细节 |
| 3 | The benefits 读懂福利手册 | 保险/假期/补贴词 | 铺陈福利体系 |
| 4 | A fair deal 谈成公平待遇 | 声誉/能力词 + 谈判动词 | 冲突与解决 |
| 5 | The whole package 回望整体报酬 | 核心词回收 | 点题升华 |

**设计要点**：`paycheck / payroll / deduct / counterfoil / pay slip` 类的钱款词集中在"工资条"一幕，让她逐行读、逐项算；`whole life insurance / endowment scheme / disability benefit / paid leave / holiday entitlement` 集中在"福利手册"一幕，形成完整福利清单；`reputation / prowess / flexible / provisory` 用于"谈判"一幕，体现她凭能力争取；结尾用 `remuneration / reward / rewarding` 点出"报酬不只是钱"的主题。

## 二、英文故事　The Whole Package

### 1. The offer

Clara had spent three years as a **dedicated** nurse at a small clinic, and she was **delighted** when a **renowned** hospital called about a **vacancy**. The director was **respectful** and spoke of a bright **prospect** for someone with her skills. "Our **remuneration** package is generous," he said, and he promised that the **company** would fund her **education**. Clara listened carefully; the term of the contract sounded fair, and the government rules on staffing were clear. She signed the letter that afternoon.

### 2. The payslip

On her first payday, Clara studied her **paycheck** line by line. The **payroll** office had listed every item: tax was **deducted**, and the **procedure** was almost **self-explanatory**. She worked the **core time** from nine to three, but extra **overtime** was recorded separately, and the **overtime allowance** arrived with the next **paycheck**. The **bonus** for the winter shift was listed as well. Her **working hours** were printed on the back, next to a **counterfoil** she had to keep. At the **checkout** in the staff shop she saw a **commission** note for the pharmacist and a **dividend** slip from the staff savings plan. A **labor union** leaflet warned that a **kickback** scheme was under investigation, and she wondered whether her own **allowance** was really a **pittance**. Nothing was **omitted**, and a brief note said that any change would be **notified** in advance. Her **labor** was worth more than she had thought.

### 3. The benefits

The handbook listed the real **benefit** of the job. There was a **whole life insurance** policy, an **endowment scheme** that would pay out at retirement, a **pension** and a separate **annuity** for long service. A **disability benefit** covered accidents on the ward, and **danger money** applied to the emergency team. New parents received **paid leave**, and every nurse had a **holiday entitlement** of five weeks. The **weighting** for night shifts was higher, and a **severance** payment was guaranteed if the unit closed. Clara had heard of **workfare** schemes elsewhere, but here the **welfare** office genuinely helped staff. For the first time she felt that her **education** had been an investment, not an expense.

### 4. A fair deal

When the hospital proposed a new grade, Clara asked for a review. She had built a **reputation** for calm work under pressure, and her **prowess** with difficult patients was well known. The director offered a **flexible** schedule with a **provisory** six-month review, which she accepted, and her union gave it full **backing**. He agreed to **alter** two clauses, promised not to **escalate** the workload, and said the training budget would be **optimized**. Within a month she was **elevated** to senior nurse. The **reward** was not just money; it was a **rewarding** sense of **job satisfaction** that she had never expected. She had **requited** their trust with steady service.

### 5. The whole package

Looking back, Clara realized that fair **remuneration** is more than a **paycheck**. She told new hires to read every **benefit** line, keep the **counterfoil**, and ask about **paid leave** before signing. A good **reward** **requites** effort, she said, and a **rewarding** job pays in more ways than one.

## 四、学习建议

1. **把福利清单按功能分组记**：保险与养老 `whole life insurance / endowment scheme / pension / annuity / disability benefit`；假期与工时 `paid leave / holiday entitlement / core time / working hours / overtime`；补贴与奖金 `bonus / commission / allowance / weighting / danger money / severance`。故事第 2、3 幕正是按这条线读工资条和福利手册的。
2. **辨析近义的"钱"**：`paycheck`（薪水支票）→ `payroll`（薪水册/薪资部门）→ `remuneration`（正式说法，报酬）→ `reward`（回报，可指金钱也可指精神）→ `allowance`（津贴）→ `bonus`（奖金）→ `pittance`（微薄薪水，含贬义）。
3. **注意易混的"回扣"词**：`commission`（佣金，正当）与 `kickback`（回扣，常含非法意味）在故事第 2 幕同场出现，语气一正一负，对比记忆。
4. **搭配整体记**：`core time`（固定工作时间）、`overtime allowance`（加班补助）、`labor union`（工会）、`paid leave`（带薪假期）、`job satisfaction`（工作满意度）、`holiday entitlement`（休假权利）都是固定名词短语，不要拆开逐个翻译。
5. **动词的书面色彩**：`alter / deduct / elevate / escalate / notify / omit / optimize / requite` 中 `requite` 最书面（回报善意），`escalate` 常用于价格或 workload 上升；故事分别用"修改条款""扣税""晋升""加剧工作量""提前通知""没有遗漏""优化预算""回报信任"八个动作帮它们各归其位。
