## 一、编排逻辑

本主题是"工作实践"，词表围绕新人上手、办公工具、团队协作、复盘改进与职业远见展开。我用"一名新员工从入职培训到独立完成流程改进，最终被有远见的领导采纳建议"作主线，按"入职第一天 → 工具与记录 → 团队协作 → 复盘与改进 → 有远见的收尾"五幕推进，让每个词落到具体人物动作或实务流程上：

| 幕 | 场景 | 承载语义场 | 叙事功能 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Monday morning 入职第一天 | 身份/状态形容词：annual, beforehand, vocational, achievable, overqualified, qualified, optimal, temporary + temp | 建立入职场景 |
| 2 | Tools of the trade 工具与记录 | 办公实务名词：spreadsheet, dictaphone, dossier, checklist, cross-reference, listing, duplicate, information, input, fixture, benchmark, technician + inspect, authenticate, countersign, divide | 展现上手过程 |
| 3 | Working together 团队协作 | 协作动词：align, amalgamate, associate, facilitate, initiate + counterpart, consultancy, expertise, experience, feedback, availability, painstaking, appraiser | 推进协作链 |
| 4 | The review 复盘与改进 | 复盘词：accountability, affair, amelioration, praise, unanimously + admit, arise, anticipate, obligate, appraise, antedate | 完成改进闭环 |
| 5 | A visionary finish 有远见的收尾 | 愿景词：visionary, staple, efficient, monotonous, freelance, founder, activist, contribute, disposition + overall checkup 等搭配 | 点题升华 |

**设计要点**：`spreadsheet / dictaphone / dossier / checklist / cross-reference / listing / duplicate` 等办公物件集中在第 2 幕，由 Lin 逐一操作；`align with / amalgamate / associate with / facilitate / initiate` 五个协作动词串成第 3 幕；`arise from / admit to / obligated to` 等搭配落在第 4 幕的复盘场景；`annual budget / vocational qualification / qualified employees / visionary leader / overall checkup / double prime / temporary assurance` 七条常用搭配全部自然嵌入对应幕；结尾以 `visionary / efficient / contribution` 升华"工作实践出真知"的主题。

## 二、英文故事　Learning the Ropes

### 1. Monday morning

On **Monday** morning the manager **announced** a training course for all the **newcomers**. Lin, a **temp** from an agency, had studied the **annual** report **beforehand**. She was **overqualified** for the job, yet she treated the **vocational** qualification of her colleagues with respect. "We want **qualified** employees, not just paper certificates," the trainer said, "and every goal must be **achievable**." Lin nodded; the **optimal** way to learn, she felt, was to watch and ask.

### 2. Tools of the trade

At her desk Lin opened a **spreadsheet** and switched on a **dictaphone**. The **dossier** from the previous clerk lay open, with a **checklist** clipped to it and a **cross-reference** to an old **listing** in the margin. When a **duplicate** of the file appeared, she checked the **information** against the original **input**. A loose **fixture** on the shelf made her nervous, so she asked the **technician** to **inspect** it. Before anything left the office, a senior clerk had to **authenticate** and **countersign** it. Lin **divided** her tasks into small steps and set a **benchmark** for her own speed.

### 3. Working together

The team had to **align** with the new procedure and **amalgamate** two old forms into one. Lin **associated** with her **counterpart** in the finance office, and a **consultancy** was hired to share its **expertise**. Her **experience** was thin, but the **feedback** she gave was sharp. "A good checklist will **facilitate** the whole process," she said. She **initiated** a weekly meeting and posted her **availability** on the door. The work was **painstaking**, yet an outside **appraiser** came to **appraise** it and praised her care.

### 4. The review

At the monthly review the **accountability** for every **affair** was made clear. Lin listened **carefully** as the team discussed the **problems** in the old system and the **amelioration** they wanted. One old file even **antedated** the new rules. When a mistake did **arise** from a rushed form, she was the first to **admit** to it. She did not **anticipate** any further trouble, and said so. The manager was **obligated** to act; he **praised** her honesty, and the room agreed **unanimously**.

### 5. A visionary finish

The **founder** of the company was a **visionary** leader who wanted **efficient**, not **monotonous**, work. He called the changes a **staple** part of the new culture and ordered an **overall** checkup of every desk. Lin, once a **freelance** writer, now saw the **double** prime benefit of a steady job. An **activist** in the staff union welcomed the **temporary** assurance the firm offered. After long **hours** of practice, she had **contributed** a simple idea: keep the best tools and train everyone to use them. Her **disposition** was quiet, but the results spoke loudly.

## 四、学习建议

1. **办公实务名词成组记**：`spreadsheet / dictaphone / dossier / checklist / cross-reference / listing / duplicate / fixture / technician` 全在第 2 幕 Lin 的桌面上，按"一次文件处理流程"整体记忆。
2. **协作动词搭配**：`align with / amalgamate ... into / associate with / facilitate / initiate / appraise` 串成第 3 幕的协作链，其中 `align with`、`associate with` 是高频固定搭配。
3. **七条常用搭配整体掌握**：`annual budget / vocational qualification / qualified employees / visionary leader / overall checkup / double prime / temporary assurance` 分别在故事里有真实情境，注意 `qualified employees`（合格的员工）与 `overqualified`（资历过高）的对照。
4. **"-tion/-ence/-cy" 名词后缀**：`accountability / availability / amelioration / consultancy / expertise / feedback` 多位抽象名词，结合第 3、4 幕的复盘场景记忆其"状态或过程"含义。
5. **近义与词形**：`appraise`（评估）与 `appraiser`（评估人）、`contribute`（贡献）与 `contribution`、`temp`（临时雇员）与 `temporary`（临时的）词形相近，注意词性与搭配差别；`arise from`（源于）、`admit to`（承认）、`obligated to`（有义务做）三处搭配务必整体记忆。
