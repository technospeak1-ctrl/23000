## 一、编排逻辑

本主题是"程度·逻辑·强调"三步曲的收官，最大的特点是**逻辑连接短语极多**（`as long as`、`by virtue of`、`in spite of`、`not only...but also`、`now that`、`thanks to` 等 51 条）。这些短语只有在完整的议论语篇中才能显示差别。因此我用"建筑师伊莱亚斯回顾老城改造项目、为纪念册撰文"作主线，按"缘起 → 抉择 → 波折 → 落成 → 回顾"推进：

| 幕 | 场景 | 承载语义场 | 叙事功能 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The old square 老广场 | 初始/时间/程度副词 | 交代缘起 |
| 2 | The pivotal decision 关键抉择 | pivotal / sole / foremost + 条件短语 | 做出决定 |
| 3 | Rough going 波折 | 否定/让步/转折短语 | 应对阻力 |
| 4 | A new threshold 新的门槛 | 结果/因果短语 + 程度词 | 项目落成 |
| 5 | Looking back 回顾 | 总结短语 + 升华 | 收束全篇 |

**设计要点**：`first and foremost`、`by no means`、`little by little`、`in other words`、`in short`、`now that`、`thanks to`、`ever since` 等连接短语分别承担列举、否定、渐进、换言、总结、因果等真实篇章功能，而非堆砌；程度词 `considerably`、`exceedingly`、`exceptionally`、`superbly`、`overpowering` 描写规模与情感。

## 二、英文故事　A Square Reborn

### 1. The old square

**Initially**, the old square was a **piddling** patch of cracked stone that nobody valued. **To date**, no plan had ever gone **over** the wall of neglect. **Ever since** he was a boy, Elias had known every **irregular** cobble and the **innermost** alley behind the market. He had walked **past** its worn edge a thousand times, noticing how the old stones and the new shopfronts lay in odd **juxtaposition**. **From time to time** he would stand there, **every once in a while** sketching an idea. The place was, **in general**, forgotten — **by no means** a priority. **Little by little**, though, a notion took hold: the square could be a **pivotal figure** in the city's renewal. The idea grew **considerably** after the council published an **approximately** forty-page survey, **exceedingly** thorough in its detail. **Even** the **piddling** budget could not kill it. **In other words**, a **sole** chance had arrived, and Elias meant to **directly** seize it.

### 2. The pivotal decision

**First and foremost**, he had to define the goal. **Specifically**, the square would be a **progressive** public space, **not only** beautiful **but also** usable. **As long as** the design served the people, **either** a garden **or** a market could work. Beauty was **secondary to** use. **In terms of** cost, the plan was **exceptionally** lean, its paths **intricately** drawn to fit the old stones. **By virtue of** its simplicity, it won support. **On account of** years of delay, the council was eager. **Due to** careful planning, funds arrived **in advance**. **In view of** the risk, Elias still asked for a review — **unless** the engineers signed off, he would not break ground. The old bridge, he argued, was **distinct from** the modern span; **unlike** the new one, it carried memory. **Not so much** a traffic route **as** a landmark, it had to stay. **That is**, it had to be preserved. **For the sake of** that memory, he refused to pull it down.

### 3. Rough going

Then came the objections. **Even if** the plan was sound, critics doubted it. **Even though** he had data, they resisted. **In spite of** his evidence, the press was **unfit** to be fair, and the row grew **overpowering**. **Off and on**, meetings dragged for **a while**. **Hardly** had he presented the budget **when** a councillor quit. **Now that** the tide had turned, Elias had to act. **No longer** could he wait; **not as yet** had he lost, but time was short. **Or else** the project would fail. **In the meantime**, he rewrote the case. **Rather** than fight, he listened. **Somewhat** humbler, the critics **at any rate** began to soften. **At most** a dozen objections remained. **Each other** they convinced, **one another** they won over — **both** the council **and** the public came round.

### 4. A new threshold

Work began, and the square crossed a new **threshold**. **Thanks to** the crew, the stone was relaid **superbly**. **Subsequently**, trees appeared; **thereafter**, families came. **From now on**, the place was alive. **Henceforth**, it would be cared for. **For ever**, Elias hoped, it would last. **In short**, the **repetition** of small acts — sweeping, planting, mending — built something large. **That is to say**, patience was the true material. The changes were **irreversible**; an **irreversible order** of the old council had been overturned by patient work, and further change was **unavoidable**. **With respect to** the bridge, it stood, restored. **With the exception of** one cracked bench, everything was renewed. **In the meantime**, the city had learned a lesson.

### 5. Looking back

**Looking back**, Elias saw the project as the **top** of a long effort. The old certainties were **not so much** wrong **as** merely incomplete. **Now that** the square was full of life, no one doubted the choice. **In short**, a **piddling** patch had become a **progressive** beacon. **By no means** the work of one man, it was **due to** many hands. **Owing to** their care, the square endures **all over** the city's memory. **Every other** week he still visits, **off and on**, and watches the children cross the **sole** bridge that leads home.

## 四、学习建议

1. **因果短语四兄弟**：`due to`、`owing to`、`thanks to`、`on account of`、`by virtue of` 都表"因为"，但 `thanks to` 偏褒义（幸亏），`owing to`/`due to` 中性，`by virtue of` 强调"凭借某种性质"。
2. **让步与条件**：`even if/though`（即使）、`in spite of`（尽管+名词）、`as long as`（只要）、`unless`（除非，故事中 "he would not break ground **unless** the engineers signed off"）——注意 `in spite of` 后接名词，`even though` 后接从句。
3. **总结换言**：`in other words`（换句话说）、`that is`（即）、`in short`（简言之）——写议论文转折衔接的三个层次。
4. **时间推进**：`initially → from time to time → little by little → subsequently → thereafter → from now on → henceforth → for ever`，构成完整的时间链条。
5. **互相代词**：`each other` 与 `one another` 都表"互相"，现代英语中常可互换，故事第 3 幕并列使用以示范。
6. **程度描写的"高级词"**：`considerably`（相当大地）、`exceptionally`（格外）、`superbly`（极好地）、`overpowering`（压倒性的）——比 very 更精准。
7. **"与其……不如"结构**：`not so much...as...`（故事中 "**not so much** a traffic route **as** a landmark"）是高级表达，用来纠正或精确化某个说法，比 `rather than` 更文雅；`not...so much as` 在词表中的写法即源于此结构。
8. **尚需造句的搭配**：`in case of`（万一）与 `be secondary to`（仅次于）虽已在表格列出，建议各造一句巩固，例如 "Carry an umbrella **in case of** rain"、"Safety is **secondary to** speed here"。
