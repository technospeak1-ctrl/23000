## 一、编排逻辑

本主题是"计划宣传"，词表由三条线交织：计划与可行性词（`project / blueprint / feasible / practicable / priority / allocation`），宣传与修辞词（`advertisement / ballyhoo / puffery / quip / vehicle / understated`），以及表达与论证动词（`propose / expound / adduce / declare / promulgate / prove`）。我以"品牌负责人玛雅为一款折叠电动车策划上市，先做可行方案，再遇对手夸张炒作，最后以克制而真实的宣传赢得市场"为主线，按"初步方案 → 提案会议 → 炒作攻势 → 克制宣传 → 上市结果"五幕推进，让每个词都落在一次测算、一张图表或一句发布辞上：

| 幕 | 场景 | 承载语义场 | 叙事功能 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The inchoate project 初步方案 | 计划/可行性/设计形容词 | 建立人物与任务 |
| 2 | The proposal 提案会议 | 论证动词 + 计划名词 + 分歧表达 | 用一次会议串起说理词 |
| 3 | A campaign of ballyhoo 炒作攻势 | 夸张/虚假名词 + 即席修辞动词 | 制造对手这一反面镜 |
| 4 | Promoting with restraint 克制宣传 | 发布动词 + 媒介名词 + 低调形容词 | 推出正面做法 |
| 5 | The result 上市结果 | 证实/继续动词 + 气度名词 | 点题收束 |

**设计要点**：可行性三词 `feasible / practicable / allocation` 放在第一幕的预算测算里，`inchoate → novelty → bristling → arresting` 写出草图从平淡到醒目的过程；第二幕的 `adduced / expounded / expository / delineated / particularized / elaboration` 是一条完整的"摆数字—画图—逐项细说"论证链，`differ from / differ about / declare oneself / declare against / declare one's intention immediately` 把会议上的赞同与分歧写活；对手一幕集中 `ballyhoo / puffery / bunkum / mendacity / effrontery / sidesplitting`，与第四幕的 `understated / magnanimity` 形成张扬与克制、虚假与真实的对照；12 条搭配全部落进动作——`attend to the numbers / release news / release the reporter from / resume testing / design a car` 等都在正文有具体施动者。

## 二、英文故事　The Honest Launch

### 1. The inchoate project

Maya Trent had been handed an **inchoate** **project**: launch a folding electric bike for a small firm. Her first **priority** was to turn the bare idea into a **blueprint**, and she treated every **allocation** of money as a promise to keep. The plan had to be **feasible**, not a dream, and she asked whether it was **practicable** to build two hundred bikes in a month. A **deliberate** reading of the market data showed a real need, and she **determinedly** refused to rush the launch. The design had a certain **novelty** — the frame folded twice — but the first sketch was **inanimate** and flat. A young designer who could **design a car** from a sketch brought it to life with **bristling** lines and an **arresting** cover. "A **sequential** set of steps will get us there," Maya said, and she **designed** the schedule that night.

### 2. The proposal

Next morning she **proposed** a **proposal** to the board and asked them to **discuss** it. She **adduced** three figures to show demand, then **expounded** the idea; her **expository** charts **delineated** the market. She **particularized** each cost, and her **elaboration** ran to ten pages. A quiet accountant **associated** the plan **with** a rise in sales, but the sales chief **differed from** her on the timing and **differed about** the budget. "I **declare** myself in favour," said the chairman, but he wanted the launch **postponed** by a week. The delay would **affect** every later step, so Maya **perused** every page again and **estimated** the cost at forty thousand. A rival's early **announcement** had already reached the press, so she had to **disclose** her own date at once. She **consented to** a small delay, but **declared against** any cut in quality, and **declared her intention immediately**: the bike would be honest, not hyped.

### 3. A campaign of ballyhoo

Across town, a rival firm began a **ballyhoo** that was hard to ignore. Its **advertisement** ran through every local **vehicle**, from radio to the town newspaper, and the **insertion** of a loud jingle made the ads **sidesplitting** to some and annoying to others. The rival's **puffery** knew no bounds: "The fastest bike ever made!" The claims were **bunkum**, and the **mendacity** behind them showed an **effrontery** that shocked Maya. At a press lunch the rival's manager **extemporized** a speech full of **quips** and empty praise, but when Maya asked for proof he could only **resemble** a cornered animal. His **rejoinder** was a **sleight** of hand, and his **ratiocination** led nowhere. Maya's team was asked to **attend** the event, and the quiet accountant **attended to** the numbers while the sales chief stormed out.

### 4. Promoting with restraint

Maya chose an **understated** tone for her own message: a quiet film of real riders, not loud claims. She **billboarded** the launch date across the city and asked a radio host to **release news** of the test results. When a false report threatened to hurt the bike, she **disclosed** the truth and **released** the reporter **from** her promise of secrecy. A rival tried to **promote** a cheaper copy, but Maya would not **promulgate** a single **unreliable** figure. She **expedited** the final **modification** to the frame, and a brief **interaction** with a rider at the shop gave her the best line of all. The team's **flexibility** kept the campaign alive when the venue changed. "We do not need **puffery**," she said. "We need proof."

### 5. The result

On launch day the bike did not **resemble** the glossy rival at all; it was plain and sturdy. A hundred riders turned up, and the **interaction** between them and the engineers gave Maya her best **advertisement**. When a reporter asked about the rival's loud claims, she answered with **magnanimity**: "Let them shout; we will show." The sales figures **proved** the plan **feasible** and the **estimate** correct. By evening the rival had gone quiet, and Maya was ready to **resume** work on the next model; she would **resume testing** the frame the very next morning. The **novelty** of a folding bike was no longer a trick — it was a promise kept.

## 四、学习建议

1. **按上市流程记词**：策划 `inchoate / blueprint / priority / allocation / feasible / practicable` → 提案 `proposal / adduce / expound / delineate / particularize / elaboration` → 对手 `ballyhoo / puffery / bunkum / mendacity / effrontery` → 正宣传 `understated / release news / billboard / magnanimity` → 结果 `prove / resume`。沿故事线回忆比孤立背词更牢。
2. **论证动词成链**：`adduce → expound → expound with expository charts → delineate → particularize → estimate → prove` 正是玛雅说服董事会的全过程，配 `elaboration / ratiocination` 一起记，体会"摆证据—画图—细说—证实"的层次。
3. **张扬与克制对照记**：`ballyhoo / puffery / bunkum / effrontery / sidesplitting` 是对手的夸张话术，`understated / magnanimity / honest` 是玛雅的克制回应；`unreliable` 与 `prove` 一头一尾，呼应"虚假终需证据"。
4. **近义辨析**：`feasible`（可行，偏想法）与 `practicable`（行得通，偏做法）在第一幕同框；`advertisement`（广告）/ `announcement`（通告）/ `billboard`（大字宣传）/ `promulgate`（颁布宣传）覆盖"宣传"的不同手段；`differ from`（与……不同）与 `differ about/on/over`（在……上分歧）在会议上一并出现。
5. **搭配按施动者记**：`associate with / attend to / consent to / declare against / declare oneself / declare one's intention immediately / design a car / differ from / differ about / release news / release sb. from / resume doing sth.` 共 12 条，每条都有明确的动作发出者，按"谁做了什么"检索即可。
