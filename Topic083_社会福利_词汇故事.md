## 一、编排逻辑

本主题围绕"社会福利"，词可归为四类：福利理念形容词、社会角色名词、救助行动动词、制度搭配。我用"福利工作者克莱尔调查社区救助基金、推动改革"作主线，按"发现需求 → 查明问题 → 动员力量 → 制度改善"推进，让人物、机构、行动在因果链中依次出现：

| 幕 | 场景 | 承载语义场 | 叙事功能 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The case 个案 | 弱势者名词 + 福利理念形容词 | 发现需求 |
| 2 | The inquiry 调查 | 揭露/批评动词 + 负面角色名词 | 查明问题 |
| 3 | The campaign 动员 | 捐助动词 + 正面角色名词 | 动员力量 |
| 4 | The reform 改革 | 制度名词 + 规范动词 | 制度改善 |
| 5 | A justly society 公正社会 | 剩余词 + 升华 | 收束主题 |

**设计要点**：`a harmonious society`、`mutual respect`、`job security`、`Civil Rights`、`be bound to`、`go nowhere` 等搭配自然嵌入；`benefactor / beneficiary / provider / victim / orphanage` 构成"救助关系网"；`donate / endow / endorse / disclose / abhor / compensate` 等动词落在"调查—募捐—立法"的具体动作上。

## 二、英文故事　The Safety Net

### 1. The case

Clare worked for a **community** charity in a poor **zone** of the city, where **poverty** touched every family and **ignorance** kept many from asking for help. Her first case was a **couple** with an **adoptive** child whose **pedigree** was unknown; the boy's **kin** had vanished, and his only **outlet** was the local **orphanage**. Clare believed every **individual** deserved **security** and a **humane** life, and that help must be **consistent**, not **occasional**. She knew that **unequal** treatment and an **ostensible** concern for the poor were not enough; the warmth of real **intimacy** and trust mattered too. "We are **bound to** protect the **victim**," she told her team, "and to act **ethically** and **justly**." Her **compassionate** manner made the **prospective** donor feel at ease, and she spoke in a calm, **conversational** tone. But the **intrinsic** dignity of the poor was often ignored, and the **impact** of one small **impulse** of kindness could not last.

### 2. The inquiry

As she dug deeper, Clare found **inequality** where the **populace** had been told there was none. A **hypocrite** ran the local **institution**, an **aristocrat** who **abhorred** the poor in private while praising **charity** in public. He would **asperse** and **defame** anyone who complained, and **berate** the staff for speaking up. A **rumor** spread that funds were **abused**, that donations meant for the **beneficiary** never arrived. Clare **disclosed** the truth to a **notary**, who urged her not to **decry** the matter in public before she had proof. An old **antagonist** of hers tried to **interfere** and **nullify** the inquiry, and for a while the investigation **went nowhere**; doubts **lingered**, and the delay would **incur** the anger of the donors. But an **omen** of change came when a **voter** **avowed** what he had seen: the **obstruction** was deliberate, and the **consequence** would be serious.

### 3. The campaign

Clare would not let one failure **discourage** her. She **roused** herself and the whole neighbourhood. A wealthy **benefactor** came forward and **donated** a large sum; another promised to **endow** a new fund. A **distinguished** professor agreed to **endorse** the plan, and a **voluntary** group of students joined the **initiative**. They wrote to every **provider** and ensured the money would **compensate** families fairly. Clare asked the council to **stipulate** clear rules and to **regulate** how funds were spent. "**Mutual respect**," she said, "is the basis of a **harmonious society**." Slowly the **variability** of aid gave way to a fair **consensus**; even the **ingratitude** of a few could not **impinge** on the common good. Their **consideration** for others became the **influence** that changed the town.

### 4. The reform

New laws were written to **protect** **Civil Rights** and to **penetrate into** every **obstruction** of the old system. The **deadline** for reform was tight, and Clare's team had to **reconsider** every **contradictory** clause. An **intermediate** body was set up to **reconcile** the two sides, and they **circumscribed** the powers of the corrupt official. The **infrastructure** of welfare improved: a new **outlet** for complaints, a **consistent policy** for **job security**, and a public record of every **oath** and **adoption**. The **era** of quiet neglect was ending. Clare **repudiated** the old ways and **involved** more citizens in the work; **be involved in** the community became a point of pride. She knew that a **justly** run society needed **numerous** small acts, not one grand gesture.

### 5. A justly society

By the following spring, the fund was sound. The **voluntary** helpers still met each week, and **harmonious** relations returned to the **zone**. Clare looked at the **beneficiary** families, at the **prospective** plans for a new clinic, and at the **mutual** trust where suspicion had been. She had **abhorred** the injustice and **disclosed** it; she had **endorsed** reform and **endowed** hope. The **intrinsic nature** of charity, she wrote in her report, is not pity but **justice** — a **consistent policy** of care, a **security** that lasts, and the **impact** of ordinary people who refuse to let the vulnerable **go nowhere**. That was the **safety net** a **civil** society weaves for all its **kin**.

## 四、学习建议

1. **救助关系网**：`benefactor`（捐助者）→ `donate/endow`（捐赠）→ `charity/institution`（机构）→ `beneficiary/victim`（受益者/受害者）→ `compensate`（补偿）——按"钱从谁来到谁去"串记。
2. **揭恶动词群**：`abhor`（憎恶）→ `disclose`（揭露）→ `asperse/defame/decry`（诽谤责难）→ `berate`（痛斥）→ `repudiate`（摒弃），程度与对象各有不同。
3. **制度动词**：`stipulate`（规定）、`regulate`（规范）、`circumscribe`（限制）、`reconsider`（复议）——都是"制度建设"场景的高频词。
4. **福利理念搭配**：`a harmonious society`（和谐社会）、`mutual respect`（相互尊重）、`job security`（工作保障）、`Civil Rights`（公民权）——是讨论社会福利的核心短语。
5. **易混词**：`bound`（有义务的）常接 `be bound to do`；`ostensible`（表面的）与 `consistent`（一贯的）在故事中代表"表面/实质"之别。
6. **表面与实质**：`ostensible`（表面上的）常指"名义上如此、实则不然"，与 `consistent`（一贯的）、`intrinsic`（固有的）对照——故事中"ostensible concern for the poor"（表面关心穷人）正是伪君子的特征。`unequal treatment`（不平等待遇）与 `equality`（平等）互为反义。
