## 一、编排逻辑

本主题围绕"社会热点"，词可归为三类：热点立场形容词、人物与议题名词、交锋行动动词。我用"议员维尔主持一场关于福利改革的国会听证会"作主线，按"开场 → 证人 → 交锋 → 表决 → 余波"推进，让女权者、有神论者、愤世者、失业者、移民在听证席上依次发言：

| 幕 | 场景 | 承载语义场 | 叙事功能 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The hearing opens 开场 | 制度名词 + 宪政形容词 | 设定议题 |
| 2 | The witnesses 证人 | 人物名词 + 立场形容词 | 多元发声 |
| 3 | The clash 交锋 | 冲突名词 + 掩饰/破坏动词 | 矛盾激化 |
| 4 | The vote 表决 | 立法名词 + 表决动词 | 达成决议 |
| 5 | After the vote 余波 | 剩余词 + 升华 | 收束主题 |

**设计要点**：`a congressional bill`、`an unobstructed road`、`be eager to`、`patriotic emotion`、`perverted thought`、`put a premium on`、`rural life`、`significant difference`、`unemployed on benefit` 九条搭配全部嵌入听证场景；`dissemble / dissimulate / breach / meddle` 与 `counterbalance / relieve / reinforce` 构成"破坏—补救"动词对照；`feminist / theist / cynic / egoist / democrat` 构成"立场光谱"。

## 二、英文故事　The Hearing

### 1. The hearing opens

On a rainy morning Congresswoman Vale opened the hearing in the **assembly** hall. The **setting** was formal: **a congressional bill** lay on the table, and every **congressman** knew the **government** was watching. The **constitutional** question was simple — could the assembly **reform** the benefit system? Vale had **dedicated** herself to the **priority** of **equality** for every **generation**, and she would not **deign** to answer the **cynic** who shouted from the back. **Faithfulness** to the constitution, she said, was not **ostentation** but daily work.

### 2. The witnesses

A long line of witnesses followed. A **feminist** argued that **gender** had shaped the debate for too long. An **orthodox** pastor and a **theist** spoke of **loyalty** and **morale** in **rural life**. An **egocentric** **egoist** spoke only of himself, while a **dieter** brought charts on **dietetics** and an **entertainer** complained that **popular** taste was ignored. An **emigrant** described his escape from the **slum**; an **onlooker** in the gallery took notes. A **democrat** proposed an **egalitarian** scheme, and a **sophisticated** lawyer replied that the country must **put a premium on** skill. Someone spoke **extraordinarily** well about **patriotic emotion**, and a worker **unemployed on benefit** asked for **an unobstructed road** to a job. Vale warned that **perverted thought** would only deepen **disrespect**.

### 3. The clash

Then the mood changed. A witness tried to **dissemble** and **dissimulate** about **counterfeit** documents, but Vale would not let him **meddle** with the record. He had **breached** the rules of the **association** that invited him, and **disrepute** fell on the whole hearing. Another speaker began to **discriminate** against those who had **emigrated**, and a **trespasser** outside waved a flag of **supremacy**. A third tried to **tempt** the committee with **adventure** stories and **dumped** waste in a river to make a point; he tried to **tint** the **discourse** with fear and even threatened **suicide**. A **dupe** who had been tricked sat in **confinement**. Vale worked to **prevent** a collapse, to **counterbalance** fear with **aid**, and to **relieve** the dupe. She asked the clerk to **disassemble** the old filing system so the truth could be found. The **overflow** of angry letters revealed the **emergence** of a new **phenomenon**: a **rally** in the square. There was a **variance** between their words and deeds; the **conditioning** of public opinion by images had made **feminism** a target.

### 4. The vote

By evening the committee had drafted an **amendment**. There was a **significant difference** between the two drafts, yet both sides were **eager to** compromise. Vale asked everyone to **vote for** the change; the democrat moved to **ratify** it at once. To **reinforce** the new **equality** clause, they added money for rural clinics, and Vale **renounced** the old **priority** of appearances.

### 5. After the vote

Late that night Vale walked home past the **rally**. The **discourse** would continue tomorrow, but the **amendment** had changed the **setting**. She thought of the theist and the feminist, of the onlooker and the dupe, of the emigrant who wanted only **an unobstructed road**. A **phenomenon** is never one person's doing; it is the **emergence** of many small voices. The **government** had listened, and for one evening **disrespect** gave way to **faithfulness** to the **constitutional** promise.

## 四、学习建议

1. **立场光谱**：`feminist / democrat / theist / orthodox / cynic / egoist / egalitarian` 代表听证会上不同立场，可按"progressive ↔ traditional / 公 ↔ 私"两组对照记忆。
2. **掩饰与揭露**：`dissemble / dissimulate / counterfeit` 是"伪装造假"，`breach / discriminate / meddle / dump` 是"违规破坏"，`counterbalance / relieve / reinforce` 是"补救建设"——三群动词串成一条情节线。
3. **国会搭配**：`a congressional bill`、`ratify an amendment`、`vote for a change`、`be eager to compromise` 是政治新闻高频短语，建议整串朗读。
4. **抽象名词**：`emergence / phenomenon / variance / conditioning / disrepute / ostentation` 是社论常用抽象词，注意它们各自搭配的介词（of / between / in / on）。
5. **易混词**：`emigrant`（移出者）与 `immigrant`（移入者）方向相反；`disrespect`（失礼）与 `disrepute`（坏名声）都以 dis- 开头，注意区分。
