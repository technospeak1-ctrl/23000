## 一、编排逻辑

本主题是"商界法文"，词条集中在公司、债务、破产与商贸文书，搭配数量尤多，最易写成财经术语清单。我用"哈尔登贸易公司从财务失衡、暗箱交易、被调查、宣告破产到清算收尾"的商业败局主线，按"报表 → 交易 → 调查 → 破产 → 清算"五幕推进，让每个词与搭配落在商贸文书与清算动作上：

| 幕 | 场景 | 承载语义场 | 叙事功能 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The balance sheet 报表 | 财务形容词 + 公司/债务名词 | 建立危局 |
| 2 | A deal in bad faith 交易 | 信用证/税务/担保搭配 | 铺陈违规 |
| 3 | The detective's inquiry 调查 | 侦查动词 + 违法名词 | 揭示真相 |
| 4 | Bankruptcy 破产 | 破产程序名词 + 撤销动词 | 程序推进 |
| 5 | Settlement and a new rule 清算 | 赔偿/赎回动词 | 点题收束 |

**设计要点**：`legal entity` / `artificial person` / `unlimited liability` / `debt ratio` / `equity ratio` 集中在报表一幕；`revocable letter of credit` / `warrant money` / `warrant of attorney` / `tax avoidance` / `tax evasion` / `tax loophole` 一组商贸搭配按交易流程出现；`adjudication of bankruptcy` / `receiver` / `receivership` / `affidavit of means` 落在破产程序一幕；`cancel` / `annul` / `confiscate` / `embezzle` / `renege` 与 `redeem` / `atone` / `restitution` / `remission` 正反对照，避免清单式罗列。

## 二、英文故事　The Fall of Halden Trading

### 1. The balance sheet

The **law** was not new, but Halden Trading had tested it for years. On paper the firm was a **legal entity**, an **artificial person** able to sign contracts. Yet its **debt ratio** rose while its **equity ratio** fell. The auditors called the trend **adverse** — **be adverse to** any hope of credit. A clerk noted that the **equity method** had been applied wrongly, and that **unlimited liability** hung over the partners. Nobody believed the firm would **go bankrupt** so soon. The **valid period** of the main contract had not expired, and an **international dispute** with a supplier was already public. The **economic sanctions** on one partner made matters worse.

### 2. A deal in bad faith

The export deal was done in **bad faith**. A **backhander** changed hands, and the **illegal profit** was hidden in a **watered** account. The buyer sent a **revocable letter of credit** and **warrant money**, while an agent carried a **warrant of attorney**; the **warrantee** received nothing. The **licit** part of the trade was small; the rest was **runaway**. Managers spoke of **tax avoidance**, then crossed into **tax evasion** through a **tax loophole**. An **uncommitted** director signed anyway, and a **safe**-looking **guarantee** proved worthless. The **pecuniary** motive was clear. A small **adjunct** to the contract listed the **proviso** that shipment was optional. A **certification** of origin was forged, and the **object** of the shipment was misdeclared. The **abstention** of two board members did not stop the vote.

### 3. The detective's inquiry

A **detective** was hired. She found that a **burglar** had entered the warehouse and that crates were **smuggled** across the border. Two managers **embezzled** funds; a rival practiced **dumping** to cut prices. She **confiscated** the false papers and showed that the fake **certification** would **damage** the **damages** claim. A **fine** was certain, and the workers threatened to **strike**; a **walk-out** followed. The owners took **counsel** with a lawyer, who told them to **observe** the terms. The contract did not **entitle** them to a bonus, and the supplier would **renege** on the deal. No signature could **validate** a false **decree**. The whole plan would **go bust**.

### 4. Bankruptcy

**Adjudication of bankruptcy** came in the spring. The court appointed a **receiver** to run the firm under **receivership**. The partners filed an **affidavit of means**; their **debt** was larger than the **debtee** had expected. A **debtor** in the same group, a manager on **probation**, tried to hide assets. An **umpire** settled a dispute over those assets, and the board's **ratification** of the deal came too late. The judge signed a **cancellation** of the old charter, and an **annulment** of the last contract followed. The **trial** was brief: the judge would **annul** the license.

### 5. Settlement and a new rule

To **atone** for the fraud, the owners paid **restitution** and won **remission** of part of the fine. A **quittance** of the debt was signed, and the **safety** inspectors returned. In the receiver's **opinion**, the firm had been **bankrupt** for a year. The gain from the sale would **cancel out** the loss, but no one could **redeem** the promise made to the workers; a government might **redeem national debt**, yet this firm could not **redeem its promise**. The **damage to** its name remained. When the **international dispute** finally ended, the partners kept one rule: a **legal** contract is only as good as the **valid period** printed on it.

## 四、学习建议

1. **公司身份与财务**：`legal entity` / `artificial person`（法人）、`unlimited liability`（无限责任）、`debt ratio` / `equity ratio`（负债/产权比率）、`equity method`（净值法），围绕资产负债表一起记。
2. **商贸文书搭配**：`revocable letter of credit`、`warrant money`、`warrant of attorney`、`guarantee`、`certification`、`proviso`、`quittance`，按"付款—担保—证明—免除"流程记忆。
3. **税务三分**：`tax avoidance`（避税）→ `tax loophole`（合法避税漏洞）→ `tax evasion`（逃税），配合 `bad faith`、`backhander`、`illegal profit` 理解违规的递进。
4. **破产程序**：`adjudication of bankruptcy` → `receiver` / `receivership` → `affidavit of means` → `cancellation` / `annulment` → `restitution` / `remission` / `quittance`，按清算顺序串成一条线。
5. **正反动词**：`embezzle` / `smuggle` / `renege` / `confiscate` / `annul` 是"违规与撤销"，`redeem` / `atone` / `observe` / `validate` 是"补救与遵守"，两组对照记忆更牢。
