## 一、编排逻辑

本主题是"企业发展"，词表覆盖创业者与创业精神（`entrepreneur / entrepreneurial / career / speciality`）、企业形态与场所（`unlisted / headquarters / location / premises / license`）、成长状态（`incipient / gradual / promising / sustaining / upcoming`）与经营决策动词（`found / foresee / envisage / plot / proceed / substantiate / exceed / suspend`）。我以"诺拉的面包店从一间后屋成长为十二家连锁店"为主线，按"有希望的青年 → 未上市的小公司 → 预见转折 → 连锁扩张 → 十年回望"五幕推进，写一部微观企业成长史，让每个词都落在一次选址、一张执照或一条经营决策上：

| 幕 | 场景 | 承载语义场 | 叙事功能 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | A promising youth 有希望的青年 | 创业形容词 + 店铺名词 + 起步动词 | 建立人物与起点 |
| 2 | The unlisted firm 未上市的小公司 | 企业形态/场所名词 + 渐进时间词 | 铺陈早期经营 |
| 3 | Foreseeing the turn 预见转折 | 预见与辨析动词 + 市场状态词 | 用一次战略转向推进情节 |
| 4 | The chain grows 连锁扩张 | 连锁/纪念/运转搭配 + 和谐名词 | 展现成长高峰 |
| 5 | Looking back 十年回望 | 收束形容词 + 反思动词与搭配 | 点题升华 |

**设计要点**：`promising / incipient / secondhand / foundation` 写创业之初的寒酸与希望，`unlisted / location / headquarters / license / premises` 是企业一寸寸长大的空间印记；`foresee / envisage / plot / substantiate / distinguish from` 是诺拉在转折关头的决策链，`impair / discourage / suspend / exceed` 写出风险与突破；`driving force / concord / perspective / gradual / sustaining` 收在十年回望的反思里，回答"企业靠什么长久"这一主题问题。14 条搭配全部在正文有真实动作：`a promising youth` 是开场人物，`be beneficial to / in full / prior to` 是经营原则，`in commemoration of / in transit / high value item / takeout service / retail chain` 是扩张画面，`further more` 是一句朴素的判断。

## 二、英文故事　The Bread That Became a Chain

### 1. A promising youth

Nora Vale was a **promising** youth who worked every summer **vacation** in her aunt's bakery. Even then she had **entrepreneurial** **skills**: she kept the **premises** clean, tracked the **regularity** of each order, and saved for a **secondhand** oven. Her **career** began on the day she bought that oven with money from a **volunteer** stall at the town fair. The **foundation** of her future firm was laid in that small back room, and the **incipient** idea was simple: good bread, sold fresh. "Quality must **be beneficial to** the buyer," she told her aunt, and she **allotted** every hour to the dough.

### 2. The unlisted firm

Five years later the bakery was an **unlisted** company with a single **location** and no **headquarters** to speak of. Nora was the **entrepreneur** who **founded** it, and her **license** hung above the counter. She **allotted** a small budget to a new **location** and **preceded** her rivals by opening at dawn. A **sustaining** grant from a local fund kept the doors open through the first winter. She **proceeded** slowly, and the **duration** of that quiet period tested her patience. Her staff had a **benefits package** that was modest but fair, and a **driving force** in the kitchen was an old baker who had **preceded** her in the trade. "We grow by **gradual** steps," Nora said.

### 3. Foreseeing the turn

Nora could **foresee** a change: people wanted bread in the evening, not just at dawn. She **envisaged** a **retail chain** with a **takeout service** in every shop. To **distinguish** her brand **from** the imitators, she **substantiated** her claim with a **high value item** — a sourdough loaf made over two days; sourdough was her **speciality**. A consultant warned that a rival's price war might **impair** her margins and **discourage** her staff; for a moment she thought to **suspend** the plan. But the **tendency** of the market was clear, and her **perspective** widened. A new **location** would **exceed** the old shop's sales, she believed. She **plotted** the route on a map and **proceeded** at once.

### 4. The chain grows

Within three years the **retail chain** had twelve shops, and the **upcoming** opening would be the biggest. Nora moved the **headquarters** to an old warehouse where the **glitter** of new ovens filled the room. A **monument** of glass and brick rose at the site of the first bakery, built **in commemoration of** the aunt who had taught her. Every morning bread was **in transit** between the kitchens and the shops, and the **regularity** of those deliveries became a promise customers trusted. The **concord** between Nora and her old baker was rare in business; they argued about dough, never about money. She kept the **benefits package** **in full** for every worker — "**Further more**, a happy baker makes better bread," she said — and her next **location** would **exceed** them all.

### 5. Looking back

On the tenth anniversary Nora walked through the first shop. Her **career** had begun with a **secondhand** oven, and now a **monument** stood where the old bakery had been. She **envisaged** an **upcoming** generation of bakers with the same **entrepreneurial** **skills**. "We **founded** this on one rule," she said: **prior to** every opening, train the staff; keep the recipe **in full**, never cut it. The **driving force** was never money but **concord** and a **perspective** that reached beyond the next quarter. She **substantiated** that belief with the numbers: a **gradual** rise, year after year, **beneficial to** every worker and customer. The **sustaining** fund had long been repaid, and the **license** on the wall still bore the date of that first small **location**. The **glitter** of the new ovens was nothing beside the **regularity** of the bread.

## 四、学习建议

1. **按成长阶段记词**：起步 `a promising youth / vacation / volunteer / secondhand / incipient / foundation` → 立足 `unlisted / location / license / premises / sustaining / gradual` → 转型 `foresee / envisage / distinguish from / substantiate / exceed` → 扩张 `retail chain / takeout service / in transit / monument / in commemoration of` → 回望 `concord / perspective / driving force / in full / prior to`。沿企业成长史回忆，词自动带上时间顺序。
2. **人物与空间词成组**：`entrepreneur / entrepreneur ial skills / career / volunteer` 写人；`premises / location / headquarters / license / monument` 写店铺与场所；`secondhand oven / benefits package / high value item` 写物。三组各归其位，避免混淆。
3. **决策动词一条线**：`foresee → envisage → distinguish from → substantiate → plot → proceed → exceed` 正是诺拉的转折决策链；`impair / discourage / suspend` 是这条链上的阻力，`driving force / concord / perspective` 是支撑她的内在力量。
4. **近义与搭配辨析**：`foresee`（预见）与 `envisage`（设想）在第三幕连续出现；`prior to`（在……之前）、`in transit`（运输中）、`in full`（全部地）、`in commemoration of`（纪念）四个介词短语各有明确场景；`further more` 为书中写法（相当于 furthermore），用于补充理由。
5. **冷僻词留痕**：`incipient`（初始的）、`concord`（和谐）、`speciality`（特色）、`unlisted`（未上市的）、`sustaining`（资助的）较书面，均在正文出现，结合面包店成长的具体画面记忆其商业色彩。
