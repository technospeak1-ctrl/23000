## 一、编排逻辑

本主题是"学科课程"，几乎全是各门学科的专名与学术形容词，极易写成术语清单。我用"一位老教授退休前带学生参观学科博览会、布置'写一页学科概览'的作业"作主线，按"教室开场 → 数理科学 → 生命与地球科学 → 语言人文学科 → 结课考核"五幕推进，让每门学科作为展台、教材或教授的举例自然出现：

| 幕 | 场景 | 承载语义场 | 叙事功能 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The last lecture 最后一课 | 课堂器物 + 教学角色 + 教学动词 | 建立人物与任务 |
| 2 | The sciences 数理科学 | 数学/几何 + 物理/力学 + 化学 + 学术形容词 | 铺陈理科谱系 |
| 3 | The living world 生命与地球 | 生物各分支 + 地理地质 + 农学园艺 | 铺陈生命地球学科 |
| 4 | Words and ideas 语言人文 | 语言学/文学 + 历史/社会 + 思维形容词 | 铺陈人文学科 |
| 5 | The final task 结课作业 | 考核动词 + 剩余学术词 + 搭配 | 收束与点题 |

**设计要点**：`kinetic energy`、`geometric configuration`、`a logical argument`、`optional courses` 四条搭配落在教授的举例里；`-logy` 学科后缀词（biology/geology/ecology/neurology…）按"生命→地球→语言"分组依次出现，形成词族感；`analytic / logical / metaphorical / lexical / psychological` 等学术形容词分别贴到对应的学科展台；`parse / indite / assay / sift / sermonize` 等动词体现在Professor布置作业与批改的动作中。所有学科名都是"被讲到的内容"，不是孤立罗列。

## 二、英文故事　The Last Lecture

### 1. The last lecture

On the last Friday of term, Professor Vale stood at the **rostrum** of the old lecture hall, with a piece of **chalk** in his hand and a wooden **ruler** lying on the **podium**. He had been a **teacher** for forty years, first a young **instructor**, later a **professor**, and for one student a patient **mentor**. "Today," he said, "there will be no **lecture** and no **homework**. Your only **task** is to write one page — a **conspectus** of everything you have learned." On the **blackboard** he drew a **geometric configuration** of a **disc** inside a **rectangular** frame, then a **cubic** box and a **parabola**. "Even a **geometric** shape," he murmured, "holds a **logical argument** if you know how to read it." The students opened their **text** books. He asked them to **parse** a long sentence **verbatim**, to **study** the **catalog** and **bibliography** on the desk, and to **teach** the idea back to a neighbour before the bell.

### 2. The sciences

When the class moved into the science wing, the **science** of **maths** came first: **algebra**, **calculus**, and **statistics** covered the boards, and the **geometry** board carried a **geometric** proof built on one clean **axiom**. Next came **physics** and **mechanics**, where a spinning wheel demonstrated **kinetic energy** and a falling weight showed the force of **hydraulic** engines. A small **capacitor** held a **capacitance** charge; a lamp was rated in **lumen**. In the **chemistry** lab a student used an **assay** to test a **hydrate** of **glutamate**, while a model of **shipbuilding** and a **disc** of alloy sat on the shelf. "**Acquired** knowledge is a good beginning," Vale said, "but an **analytic** mind asks why." He turned to the **dynamic** equations of motion and then to the **formulation** of a single law, showing how one idea supports a whole branch of learning.

### 3. The living world

In the biology rooms the branches unfolded like pages of a **monograph**. **Biology** led to **botany** and **cytology**, to **anatomy** and **histology**, and to **physiology** and **neurology**; a poster on **gerontology** showed how cells age. An **ornithologist** had pinned up the **zoological** chart of a bird, while a case of rocks belonged to **geology** and **petrology**. A large **geographical** map unfolded across the table: **geography** explained the **ecology** of a river, **agronomy** the fields beside it, and **horticulture** the gardens of the **history** department. "A **logical** mind sees the link," Vale said, "from the soil to the cell to the star." Outside, the **hieroglyphic** carving over the old gate and a **sonnet** cut in stone reminded them that even a university has its **annals**.

### 4. Words and ideas

The last wing was given to words. A linguist traced an **etymology** back to its root, marking each **comma** and each **locution**; a **lexical** notebook listed the **terminology** of the trade, and a **glossary** translated the **metaphorical** phrase that a poet had used in a **simile**. A student of **pedagogy** and **sociology** studied the **psychological** roots of belief, while a reader of **speleology** traced an old **precept** and an **apothegm** carved beside a cave **sonnet**. On the wall hung an **encomium** and a **screed** of praise beside a **versemonger**'s rhyme. A student rose to **indite** a verse of his own; another chose to **sermonize** about the **agnostic** and the **hieroglyphic** signs no one now read. When a visitor asked for the original manuscript, the curator **sifted** through the box and smiled. "A **logical argument**," Vale noted, "is built the same way — claim, reason, and a **conspectus** of the whole." What **actuates** the scholar, he added, is not the **optional** course but the question behind it.

### 5. The final task

By evening the one-page **conspectus** lay on every desk. Vale read a few aloud: one began with **cubic** space, another with the **dynamic** cell, a third with a **verbal** proof — each **formulation** different, each **logical** in its own way. He had marked them all, and no student had **flunked**; every name had **passed**. "The point of a **lesson**," he said, closing the **catalog**, "is not that you **study** a subject and **teach** it back, but that you learn to **parse** the world **verbatim** and still find the **geometric** order inside it." He left the **chalk** on the **podium**, took his **text** and **glossary**, and walked out. The **holiday** had begun, but the **task** of learning, he said at the door, is **optional** only if you have already decided to stop.

## 四、学习建议

1. **学科后缀词族**：`-logy`（…学）是最 productive 的一组——`biology / geology / ecology / neurology / physiology / cytology / histology / gerontology / petrology / horticulture / pedagogy / sociology / speleology / zoological`；记住后缀就能成串识别。
2. **数理链**：`maths → algebra → calculus → statistics`，几何一条线是 `geometric / geometry / rectangular / cubic / disc / parabola / axiom`，物理一条线是 `physics / mechanics / dynamic / kinetic / hydraulic / capacitor / capacitance / lumen`。
3. **学术形容词配套记**：`logical`（逻辑的）配 `a logical argument`，`geometric`（几何的）配 `geometric configuration`，`kinetic`（动力的）配 `kinetic energy`；`analytic / metaphorical / lexical / psychological / agnostic` 各贴一个学科场景。
4. **易混辨析**：`capacitor`（电容器，实物）与 `capacitance`（电容，物理量）；`physical`（身体的/物理的）与 `physiology`（生理学）、`physics`（物理学）词根同源但词性不同。
5. **冷门学科词**：`pedagogy`（教育学）、`sociology`（社会学）、`speleology`（洞穴学）、`gerontology`（老年学）、`petrology`（岩石学）、`cytology`（细胞学）都收在第 4、3 幕，靠 `-logy` 后缀成串记忆；`versemonger`（打油诗人）、`apothegm`（格言）、`encomium`（赞辞）属文学冷词，放在同一面"人文墙"上一次记牢。
