## 一、编排逻辑

本主题是"政治体制"，机构与制度名词密集，最容易写成政治机构名录。我用"一名学生记者参观首都、旁听两院制议会、见证一项政策从提出到执行、并跟随外交使者了解边疆"作为主线，按"抵达首都 → 两院议事 → 政令与执行 → 外交与边疆 → 代议与改革"五幕推进，把 `parliament / premier / ministry / embassy` 等机构落到记者的具体参访动作上：

| 幕 | 场景 | 承载语义场 | 叙事功能 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | A city of institutions 机构之城 | 地理/行政/人口名词 | 建立场景 |
| 2 | The two chambers 两院议事 | 政体/议会/声明名词与形容词 | 铺陈制度 |
| 3 | The decree 政令 | 政策/强制/镇压动词与名词 | 推进冲突 |
| 4 | The foreign mission 外交使命 | 外交/边疆/安全名词 | 切换视角 |
| 5 | Representation and reform 代议与改革 | 代议/改革动词与形容词 | 点题收束 |

**设计要点**：`capital / metropolitan / territory / frontier / county / colony / region` 按"首都—都市—领土—边疆—郡—殖民地—地区"空间层级落在第一幕；`parliament / bicameral legislature / peerage / premier / opposition / committee / commissioner` 集中在议会一幕，让 `bicameral`、`politic` 人格化到议长的发言；`policy / mandate / decree` 与 `implement / impose / curtail / censor / overrule / deploy / inspect / isolate / organize` 组成"政令—执行"链；`embassy / ambassador / compatriot / permit / toll / homestay / security` 落在外交一幕；`represent / symbolize / undertake / unify / facilitate / plead` 收束于代议改革，配合 `bicameral legislature`、`captive market`、`internal injuries` 三条搭配，避免清单式罗列。

## 二、英文故事　Two Houses at Bellport

### 1. A city of institutions

Lena arrived in the **capital** on a grey morning. The **metropolitan** trains were crowded, and a **clutter** of paper covered every desk in the **ministry** where she registered. A clerk stamped her **document** and handed her a **guideline** and a **sanitation** report for visitors. On the wall a map showed the **territory** and the **frontier** beyond the last **county** and the hill **region**, with an old **colony** marked in faint ink. A **demography** chart explained why the **distribution** of seats favored the cities, and the **formation** of the new **council** was already being debated. "This is how a state sees itself," the clerk said, pointing to the **institution** badges. Lena wrote down every **measure** she could not yet name.

### 2. The two chambers

The next day she entered the **parliament**. It was a **bicameral legislature**: an upper house of **peerage** and a lower elected house. After a **harangue** from the **opposition** bench, the **premier** read a **declaration** and a **statement** from the **commissioner** to open the **conference**. A **Cantonese** member corrected the wording in the **charter**, and Lena noticed that the **premier**'s **politic** silence told her more than the speeches. The **committee** chair called the roll, and the **committee** fell quiet when the **premier** rose again.

### 3. The decree

By noon a new **policy** was on the floor. The minister would **implement a policy** of tax reform and **impose** a levy on grain; a **mandate** from the council would **curtail** old privileges. When the upper house tried to **censor** the debate and **overrule** the vote, the **premier** said the **regime** would not retreat. Soldiers were **deployed** to **inspect** the checkpoints, and **armed forces** were **in force** along the border. Officials tried to **isolate** the ringleaders and **organize** a loyal **procession**, but a **siege** of the palace gate **complicated** matters. A **regent** was named during the king's absence. An older **republic** had once **overthrown** its own king, and from the steps a leader **proclaimed** that the people would **reign** again.

### 4. The foreign mission

At the **embassy** an **ambassador** received a **compatriot** who had come to ask for a **permit**. The visitor spoke of a **captive market** where **captive** workers paid a **toll** at every gate, and of a **homestay** where the **inspection** was harsh. A guard showed **internal injuries** from a beating, and the **security** officer called a doctor. A new **denomination** of coin had been issued, and the **ambassador** promised to raise the case at the next **conference**.

### 5. Representation and reform

Back in the chamber, a young **deputy** asked how a citizen could **represent** his village. The **statesman** answered that a **commissioner** must **symbolize** the whole **county**, not just the capital, and that officers of every **rank** answer to the **council**. They would **undertake** a **rationalization** of the **institution**, **unify** the two **discrete** houses, and **distribute** grants to **facilitate** fair development in **agriculture**. The grants would be **widely distributed**. The **mandatory** rules on **territorial** and **internal** affairs were clear. A **plea** from a border **region** was read aloud, and the **premier** rose to **plead** for patience. Lena closed her notes: a **bicameral** **parliament** could **represent** many voices, but only if it kept the **charter** honest.

## 四、学习建议

1. **空间层级词**：`capital`（首都）→ `metropolitan`（大城市的）→ `territory`（领土）→ `frontier`（边疆）→ `county`（郡）→ `region`（地区）→ `colony`（殖民地），按治理地理范围记，配合 `demography`、`distribution` 一起学。
2. **议会词群**：`parliament`、`bicameral legislature`、`peerage`、`premier`、`opposition`、`committee`、`commissioner`、`conference` 集中在"两院议事"场景，一次记全议会人物与机构。
3. **政令动词链**：`implement a policy`（执行）→ `impose`（征税）→ `curtail`（缩减）→ `censor`（审查）→ `overrule`（驳回）→ `deploy`（部署）→ `inspect`（检查）→ `isolate`（隔离）→ `organize`（组织），按"立法—执行—强制"顺序记。
4. **外交与边疆**：`embassy`、`ambassador`、`compatriot`、`permit`、`toll`、`homestay`、`inspection`、`security`、`captive market`、`internal injuries` 落在使者来访的具体场景，避免抽象记忆。
5. **代议与改革**：`represent`、`symbolize`、`undertake`、`unify`、`facilitate`、`plead`、`distribute` 收束于改革一幕，配合 `rationalization`、`mandatory`、`territorial`、`internal`、`discrete` 等形容词理解制度变革的语言。
